# FIT-SPR-007 --- Dance

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Batch:** CLU-01 --- Core Launch Utility\
**Document type:** Canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Activity Code:** `FIT-SPR-007`\
**Domain:** Fitness\
**Category:** Sports & Recreation\
**Classification:** Movement / Recreation\
**Baseline-supported measurements:** Duration → seconds/minutes/hours;
Completion → completion

## 1. Canonical Meaning

### Canonical title

**Dance**

### Description

A movement and recreational Activity in which the participant
intentionally dances for a defined activity occurrence.

Dance is a broad but recognizable undertaking. The canonical Activity
does not prescribe one dance style, choreography, intensity, music,
setting or purpose.

## 2. One Canonical Activity, Many Expressions

Different dance styles do not automatically require separate Tiizi
canonical Activities.

Examples may include: - freestyle; - social dance; -
traditional/cultural dance; - choreographed dance; - dance-fitness
expressions; - individual or group dance.

These can remain expressions, context or governed Variants of **Dance**
where the participant-facing undertaking and measurement contract remain
materially the same.

A particular dance form should receive independent canonical identity
only where product evidence shows that its undertaking, measurement,
guidance or governance materially requires separate treatment.

## 3. Cultural and Context Boundary

Tiizi should avoid flattening culturally distinct dance forms into
inaccurate instructional content.

Generic canonical Dance therefore describes the broad undertaking
without claiming to define the technique or cultural meaning of every
dance tradition.

Where a Challenge names a particular style or cultural form as context,
that does not automatically make Tiizi the authority on that form's
technique, tradition or meaning.

More specific canonical Knowledge should be introduced only where it can
be responsibly defined.

## 4. Duration Measurement

**Metric:** Duration\
**Units:** seconds, minutes, hours

### Reporting meaning

The participant reports elapsed time intentionally spent dancing for the
applicable Activity occurrence.

### Participant instruction

> Enter how long you danced.

The Challenge supplies the Duration target.

Canonical Dance does not prescribe session length or intensity.

## 5. Completion Measurement

**Metric:** Completion\
**Unit:** completion\
**Participant-facing action:** Done

Completion is valid where the Challenge or Template defines an
intelligible Dance occurrence.

The participant affirmation is:

> **I completed the Dance activity required for this Challenge
> occurrence.**

This does not mean: - Tiizi judged the dance performance; - a
choreography was technically correct; - a fitness threshold was
achieved; - the entire Challenge is complete.

## 6. Completion Requires an Intelligible Occurrence

Because Dance is broad, an unqualified generic Done button must not
become meaningless.

A Challenge or Template may define the occurrence through context such
as: - complete the configured Dance session; - complete the identified
dance practice/activity; - participate in the configured Dance
occurrence.

Canonical Knowledge authorizes Completion as a legitimate reporting
path. The downstream configuration gives the occurrence its concrete
context.

## 7. Duration and Completion Are Independent

A Challenge may use:

`Dance → Duration → minutes`

or:

`Dance → Completion → Done`

Supporting both does not require both to be reported.

Completion is not automatically derived from any arbitrary Duration
unless the Challenge explicitly defines that relationship.

## 8. Choreography Is Not Automatically an Activity Component Graph

A Dance occurrence may contain many steps, movements or sections.

Those do **not** automatically become Tiizi Activity Components.

Activity Components are used when separately attributable constituent
truth is necessary to define completion of the canonical Activity, as
with Side Plank and Single-Leg Balance.

A choreography sequence remains internal content/context unless the
product explicitly needs its constituent parts to be governed and
reported separately.

## 9. Setup and Participation

Dance generally requires little universal procedural instruction.

For an intentional Dance occurrence:

1.  use a space appropriate to the intended movement;
2.  begin the applicable dance activity;
3.  participate for the configured occurrence;
4.  report the configured measurement.

Specific choreography or style instruction belongs to the applicable
context, Template or separately governed Knowledge where needed.

## 10. Equipment and Environment

Music, footwear, floor type, partner, instructor, equipment or venue may
be relevant to particular Dance expressions but are not universally
required canonical fields.

The Activity should not invent equipment requirements that do not apply
across Dance generally.

## 11. Safety and Practical Guidance

Use a space appropriate to the intended movement and remain aware of
surrounding people, objects and surfaces.

Perform movements under reasonable control and stop if the Activity
causes sharp pain or significant discomfort.

Tiizi should not prescribe individualized movement restrictions,
rehabilitation advice or universal intensity thresholds.

## 12. Verification Boundary

Canonical Dance Knowledge defines: - the broad undertaking; - Duration
and Completion reporting; - what Done affirms; - ordinary participation
guidance.

It does not establish that Tiizi independently verified: - dance
style; - choreography; - technique; - intensity; - Duration; -
Completion; - quality of performance.

Verification remains separately governed.

## 13. Activity / Challenge Separation

Canonical Activity Knowledge owns:

``` text
Dance
Duration → seconds/minutes/hours
Completion → completion
broad undertaking
ordinary guidance
```

Challenge or Template owns:

``` text
style/context where applicable
specific session or occurrence
Duration target
frequency/schedule
Challenge type
scoring/ranking where applicable
```

## 14. Cross-Domain Use

Dance remains one Fitness canonical Activity even where a Challenge uses
it for: - social connection; - recreation; - creativity; - wellbeing; -
cultural participation.

Purpose does not automatically create duplicate Wellness or Social
canonical Activities.

## 16. CLU-01 Finding

Dance confirms that Tiizi can retain broad, recognizable Activities
without turning every style or internal movement into a separate
canonical record.

It reinforces three rules:

1.  **Style/context does not automatically create canonical identity.**
2.  **Broad Activities may support Completion only when the configured
    occurrence makes Done intelligible.**
3.  **Internal sequences do not become Activity Components merely
    because they contain multiple movements.**

This allows Tiizi to support a useful recreation catalogue without
trying to become an exhaustive authority on every dance form.
