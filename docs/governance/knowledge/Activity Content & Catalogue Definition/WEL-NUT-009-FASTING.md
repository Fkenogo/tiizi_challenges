# WEL-NUT-009 --- Fasting

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Document type:** Representative canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working finding --- remain Draft; not
Challenge Eligible\
**Activity Code:** `WEL-NUT-009`\
**Domain:** Wellness\
**Category:** Nutrition & Hydration\
**Classification:** Eating Pattern\
**Baseline-supported measurement:** Duration → seconds/minutes/hours\
**Baseline publication dependency:** Appropriate safety/caution content

## 1. Source-Baseline Position

The Founder Working Baseline identifies:

`WEL-NUT-009 — Fasting — Eating Pattern — Duration → seconds/minutes/hours`

and explicitly records an **appropriate safety/caution** publication
dependency.

This exemplar therefore does not treat Fasting as an ordinary
publication candidate. Its purpose is to determine how far Tiizi can
define the canonical meaning without inventing health policy or
prematurely making the Activity Challenge Eligible.

## 2. Canonical Meaning --- Working Definition

### Canonical title

**Fasting**

### Working description

A time-bounded eating pattern in which the participant intentionally
goes without food for a defined period.

### Purpose / context

This definition identifies the basic undertaking represented by Fasting
in the working catalogue.

It does not: - prescribe a fasting duration; - recommend fasting; -
claim fasting is appropriate for every participant; - define a medical
or therapeutic fasting protocol; - prescribe what a particular
participant should consume; - claim a health outcome.

## 3. Measurement Contract

### Duration → seconds/minutes/hours

**Metric:** Duration\
**Baseline Units:** seconds, minutes, hours

**Working meaning:**\
The elapsed period the participant reports as the applicable fasting
period.

A future participant-facing reporting instruction could be:

> Enter how long you fasted.

However, this reporting copy should **not be promoted to production
merely because it is technically understandable**. The Activity's safety
and eligibility dependencies remain unresolved.

Compatible time units may be converted deterministically, but unit
conversion does not resolve whether a given fasting configuration is
safe or appropriate.

## 4. Duration Semantics Need More Than a Clock

For Fasting, Duration is not equivalent to a neutral stopwatch
measurement.

A Challenge that asks for a fasting period also raises questions such
as: - what begins the governed fasting period; - what ends it; - whether
the canonical meaning is limited to food or also constrains particular
drinks; - whether different fasting protocols are materially different
undertakings; - whether Tiizi should permit creator-selected durations
without additional constraints.

The current Founder Working Baseline does not, by itself, settle all of
these product/content questions.

Tiizi should not silently invent answers during implementation.

## 5. Protocol Boundary

The canonical Activity should not become a container for every fasting
protocol.

Different protocols may impose materially different rules about: -
timing; - intake; - frequency; - permitted substances; - purpose; -
participant suitability.

If Tiizi later supports particular fasting protocols, they require
explicit governed representation through Activity variants, Templates,
Challenge configuration, or separate canonical identities as
appropriate.

The generic Fasting Activity should not make those decisions implicitly.

## 6. Health and Safety Boundary

Fasting is health-adjacent and can be inappropriate for some people or
circumstances.

The Founder Working Baseline already recognizes that additional
safety/caution content is required before publication.

Accordingly, this exemplar deliberately does **not** invent: - medically
safe fasting durations; - age thresholds; - disease-specific rules; -
pregnancy-related rules; - medication rules; - clinical contraindication
lists; - therapeutic fasting recommendations; - individualized nutrition
advice.

Those would require an appropriate evidence and review basis.

## 7. Claims Tiizi Must Not Make From This Draft

This draft does not support claims that Fasting: - causes weight loss; -
improves metabolic health; - treats disease; - detoxifies the body; -
improves mental performance; - is safe for everyone; - is medically
recommended.

The fact that an Activity exists in the working catalogue does not
establish any of those claims.

## 8. Challenge-Configuration Risk

Fasting demonstrates why **Published** and **Challenge Eligible** must
remain separate.

Even if Tiizi later publishes an authoritative definition of Fasting,
unrestricted Challenge creation could introduce materially different
risk through: - creator-selected Duration; - repeated frequency; -
Streak pressure; - Competitive pressure; - combinations with other
Challenge requirements.

Therefore canonical publication alone should not automatically authorize
arbitrary Fasting Challenge configurations.

PF-03 Challenge Definition must determine whether health-adjacent
Activities need additional eligibility/configuration constraints.

## 9. Competition and Streak Pressure

The Activity layer does not decide which Challenge types may use
Fasting.

However, the product should explicitly evaluate whether Competition or
Streak mechanics can create inappropriate incentives around
health-adjacent Activities.

This is a Challenge Definition/eligibility question, not something to
conceal inside generic safety copy.

No Competitive, Streak or other Challenge use is authorized by this
exemplar.

## 10. Verification Boundary

This draft does not establish that Tiizi independently verified: -
whether the participant consumed food; - when the fasting period started
or ended; - reported Duration; - adherence to a particular protocol; -
physiological effects.

Any future evidence or verification capability remains separately
governed.

## 11. Activity / Challenge Separation Check

The draft contains no canonical: - fasting target; - minimum duration; -
maximum duration; - schedule; - frequency; - eating window; - Challenge
duration; - Challenge type; - scoring; - ranking; - recognition; -
reward; - therapeutic objective.

Those are downstream or separately governed concerns.

## 12. KCS / Publication Assessment

Fasting is exactly the kind of Activity for which the KCS safety/caution
requirement must operate as a real publication gate rather than a
documentation formality.

The baseline identifies a safety/caution dependency, and this product
exercise has not supplied an authoritative evidence basis sufficient to
resolve it.

Therefore:

### Content status

**Draft**

### Publication status

**Do not publish yet**

### Challenge eligibility

**Not Eligible**

### Reason

The canonical concept is understandable, but its publication-quality
safety/caution content and Challenge-use constraints are not yet
sufficiently governed.

## 13. What Is Needed Before Reconsideration

Before Fasting is reconsidered for publication, Tiizi should have:

1.  a clear canonical protocol boundary;
2.  supported, proportionate participant safety/caution content;
3.  an explicit decision about whether generic Fasting is suitable as
    one canonical Activity or requires narrower governed forms;
4.  Challenge Definition rules for permitted use, including whether
    Duration ranges or Challenge-type restrictions are required;
5.  appropriate source/review support for health-sensitive statements.

This work should not be delegated to a coding agent as content
invention.

## 14. Representative-Catalogue Finding

Fasting validates a critical property of the Tiizi Knowledge system:

> A candidate Activity can have a stable identity and technically valid
> Metric/Unit relationship while still being correctly blocked from
> publication and Challenge use.

That is not an incomplete implementation. It is the publication gate
working as intended.

It also demonstrates that: - technical compatibility is not safety
approval; - canonical definition is not recommendation; - publication is
not unrestricted Challenge eligibility; - a Challenge creator's freedom
must remain bounded by governed product rules where necessary.

## 15. Disposition

`WEL-NUT-009 Fasting`

**Canonical identity:** Retain as working candidate\
**Measurement capability:** Duration is technically meaningful\
**Content:** Draft\
**Published:** No\
**Challenge Eligible:** No\
**Implementation authorization:** None

The Activity should remain unavailable to new V2 Challenge establishment
until its publication and eligibility dependencies are deliberately
resolved.
