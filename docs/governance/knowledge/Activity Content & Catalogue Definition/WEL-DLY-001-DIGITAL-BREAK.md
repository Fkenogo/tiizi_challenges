# WEL-DLY-001 --- Digital Break

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Batch:** CLU-01 --- Core Launch Utility\
**Document type:** Canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Activity Code:** `WEL-DLY-001`\
**Domain:** Wellness\
**Category:** Daily Living\
**Classification:** Digital Wellbeing\
**Baseline-supported measurements:** Duration → seconds/minutes/hours;
Completion → completion

## 1. Canonical Meaning

### Canonical title

**Digital Break**

### Description

A defined period during which the participant intentionally steps away
from ordinary discretionary use of digital screens or devices for the
applicable Activity occurrence.

Digital Break is a concrete Wellness practice. It is not a claim that
all device use is harmful, nor a universal prescription for how long
someone should avoid digital devices.

## 2. Positive Undertaking Versus Vague Avoidance

Digital Break is framed as an intentional practice:

> **Take a defined break from ordinary discretionary digital-device
> use.**

This is preferable to an unbounded rule such as: - "do not use your
phone"; - "avoid screens"; - "use less technology."

The canonical Activity needs an understandable beginning and end so that
Duration and Completion have reportable meaning.

## 3. Scope of Digital Use

The ordinary canonical meaning concerns discretionary
digital-device/screen use during the configured break.

Tiizi should not imply that a participant must ignore: - emergencies; -
necessary safety communication; - essential work or caregiving
responsibilities; - other circumstances where device use may reasonably
be necessary.

The Challenge or Template may provide more specific context, but it
should not silently redefine the canonical Activity into an unsafe or
impractical absolute prohibition.

## 4. Duration Measurement

**Metric:** Duration\
**Units:** seconds, minutes, hours

### Reporting meaning

The participant reports the elapsed period for which they intentionally
undertook the Digital Break.

### Participant instruction

> Enter how long you took a Digital Break.

The Challenge supplies the target Duration.

Canonical Knowledge does not prescribe a universal healthy screen-free
duration.

## 5. Duration Semantics

A Digital Break Duration represents the configured intentional break
period.

The Activity should not silently aggregate unrelated short moments of
non-use across a day into one total unless a Challenge explicitly
defines that behavior.

For example, a Challenge asking for a **30-minute Digital Break**
ordinarily means one governed occurrence of the configured practice, not
every moment the phone happened to be unused.

PF-03 should define whether a Challenge may allow accumulated versus
continuous Duration where that distinction matters.

## 6. Completion Measurement

**Metric:** Completion\
**Unit:** completion\
**Participant-facing action:** Done

Completion means:

> **I completed the Digital Break occurrence required by this
> Challenge.**

It does not mean: - "I used technology appropriately today"; - "I met a
healthy screen-time threshold"; - "I avoided every digital device"; - "I
completed today's entire Challenge."

## 7. Completion Requires a Defined Occurrence

A Completion-based Digital Break Challenge must make the occurrence
intelligible.

For example, a Template or Challenge may define the intended break
context or occurrence.

Canonical Knowledge establishes that completion of a defined Digital
Break is a legitimate reporting form. It does not create the target,
schedule or daily threshold.

## 8. Duration and Completion Are Independent

A Challenge may use:

`Digital Break → Duration → minutes`

or:

`Digital Break → Completion → Done`

Supporting both does not require both.

Completion is not automatically derived from an arbitrary Duration
unless the Challenge explicitly defines that relationship.

## 9. Time-of-Day Is Challenge Configuration

A Challenge may ask for a Digital Break: - in the evening; - during a
particular period; - before another activity; - within another
configured window.

Those clock-time or scheduling conditions belong to Challenge
Definition.

They must not be embedded as permanent canonical Digital Break identity.

This is consistent with the unresolved Bedtime/Wake Time finding:
temporal conditions should be governed explicitly rather than hidden
inside Activity names.

## 10. Activity Components

Digital Break does not need Activity Components merely because multiple
device types may exist.

Phone, tablet, computer, television or another digital screen are not
automatically separate constituent Activities.

If a Challenge needs to define which device use is in scope, that
belongs to governed Challenge/Template context unless later product
evidence requires a stronger canonical model.

## 11. Practice Guidance

A participant should understand: 1. when the intended break begins; 2.
the ordinary digital use being stepped away from; 3. when the break
ends; 4. what they are expected to report.

Tiizi does not need to prescribe what the participant must do instead
during the break.

Reading, walking, socializing or another Activity may happen during the
same period, but Digital Break remains the canonical undertaking being
reported.

## 12. Overlap With Other Activities

A Digital Break can coexist with another Activity without becoming that
Activity.

For example, someone may take a Digital Break while Walking or Reading.

That does not mean: - Walking automatically counts as Digital Break; -
Reading automatically counts as Digital Break; - one Activity Evidence
record should automatically be reused across Challenges.

Each governed Activity/Challenge application remains distinct according
to Tiizi's accepted evidence/application model.

## 13. Safety and Guidance Boundary

Digital Break is ordinary Wellness content, not medical or psychological
treatment.

Tiizi should not claim that a Digital Break: - treats anxiety; - cures
sleep problems; - improves a clinical condition; - guarantees
mental-health outcomes.

It may be described as an intentional digital-wellbeing practice without
making therapeutic claims.

## 14. Verification Boundary

Canonical Digital Break Knowledge defines: - the undertaking; - Duration
and Completion meaning; - ordinary scope; - reporting expectations.

It does not establish that Tiizi independently verified: - whether a
device was used; - which applications were opened; - Duration; -
Completion.

Tiizi should not imply device surveillance merely because the Activity
concerns digital use.

Verification remains separately governed.

## 15. Activity / Challenge Separation

Canonical Activity Knowledge owns:

``` text
Digital Break
Duration → seconds/minutes/hours
Completion → completion
ordinary discretionary digital-use boundary
```

Challenge or Template owns:

``` text
target Duration
specific occurrence/context
time window
frequency
Challenge type
continuous/accumulated semantics if supported
```

## 17. CLU-01 Finding

Digital Break confirms that an avoidance-shaped Wellness concept can be
a valid canonical Activity when it is reframed as a **bounded
intentional practice with reportable start/end meaning**.

It also exposes a useful PF-03 question:

> For Duration-based practices, does the configured target require one
> continuous occurrence, or may Duration accumulate across multiple
> occurrences?

That relationship should be explicit in Challenge Definition rather than
guessed by the Activity layer.

The canonical Activity remains neutral: it defines what Digital Break
means and what Duration/Completion report, while the Challenge defines
the required occurrence.
