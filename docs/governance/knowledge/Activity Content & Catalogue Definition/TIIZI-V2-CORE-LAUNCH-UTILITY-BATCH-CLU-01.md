## 1. Purpose

CLU-01 is the first bounded catalogue-production batch selected from the
authoritative 118-candidate baseline.

It is not intended to represent the entire launch catalogue. Its purpose
is to establish enough real canonical breadth to support PF-02
compatibility/catalogue engineering, PF-03 Challenge Definition, early
Templates and the V2 Challenge Wizard without waiting for all 118
candidates.

The batch deliberately prioritizes: - recognizable participant
Activities; - common Challenge use; - Fitness and Wellness breadth; -
all six governed Metrics; - simple and multi-Metric cases; - the new
Activity Component pattern; - representative protocol, Completion and
Quantity semantics; - limited equipment/Weight coverage without making
Weight readiness a blocker.

## 3. Why 15

Fifteen is the upper end of the planned 10--15 Activity batch size but
justified here because the set covers:

-   all six Fitness categories;
-   five of six Wellness categories;
-   all six governed Metrics;
-   simple and multi-Metric Activities;
-   physical and non-physical Activities;
-   one bilateral component case;
-   several already-authored exemplars.

This gives PF-02/PF-03 useful breadth without turning CLU-01 into bulk
catalogue production.

## 4. Metric Coverage

CLU-01 exercises every governed Metric:

-   **Completion:** Stretching, Dance, Sleep, Breathing Practice,
    Digital Break, Reading
-   **Repetitions:** Push-Up, Squat, Box Jump
-   **Duration:** Plank, Side Plank, Running, Walking, Stretching,
    Single-Leg Balance, Dance, Sleep, Breathing Practice, Digital Break,
    Reading
-   **Distance:** Running, Walking
-   **Weight:** Squat where applicable
-   **Quantity:** Walking (steps), Water Intake (ml/L), Reading (pages)

Weight is intentionally present but not dominant. The Load Reporting
Convention can be resolved without blocking the rest of CLU-01.

## 5. Category Coverage

### Fitness --- all six initial categories

-   Strength
-   Cardio & Conditioning
-   Mobility & Flexibility
-   Balance & Stability
-   Power, Speed & Agility
-   Sports & Recreation

### Wellness --- five of six initial categories

-   Sleep & Rest
-   Mind & Emotional Wellbeing
-   Nutrition & Hydration
-   Daily Living
-   Personal Growth

**Social Wellbeing is deliberately deferred to CLU-02.**

This is not because Social Wellbeing is unimportant. Its candidate
Activities---Social Connection, Family Time, Acts of Kindness, Community
Participation---benefit from an additional pass on Completion/Quantity
semantics and the boundary between canonical Activity and social
context. CLU-01 already has sufficient Wellness breadth for foundation
engineering.

## 6. Existing Exemplars Reused

Existing representative exemplars directly support: - Push-Up - Plank -
Side Plank - Running - Sleep - Breathing Practice - Water Intake

Their findings should be reconciled into the batch records rather than

## 8. Deliberate Exclusions

### Bench Press and Farmer Carry

Already useful representative exemplars, but not required in CLU-01
because their principal unresolved contribution is Weight/load
reporting. Preserve them as product references and bring Weight-heavy
catalogue expansion into a later batch after the Load Reporting
Convention.

### Bedtime / Wake Time

Do not include. Their canonical identity versus Challenge time-condition
boundary remains pending PF-03.

### Fasting

Do not include. It remains Draft/not Challenge Eligible pending
safety/product dependencies.

### Fruit / Vegetable Intake

Do not include yet. The authoritative baseline requires serving guidance
before runtime publication.

### Avoid Added Sugar / Avoid Sugary Drinks

Do not include yet. The authoritative baseline requires participant
guidance before runtime publication.

### Avoid Late-Night Snacking

Do not include yet. Its time window is explicitly Challenge-configured
and should follow the PF-03 temporal-condition work.

## 9. Candidate-Specific Working Notes

### Squat

Useful because the baseline supports Repetitions and Weight where
applicable. CLU-01 can make Repetitions the first eligible path while
Weight remains subject to the Load Reporting Convention.

### Walking

High utility and unusually valuable for the model because it supports
steps, Distance and Duration without requiring equipment. It also
validates the baseline's cross-domain principle: Walking remains one
Fitness canonical Activity even when used in Wellness contexts.

### Stretching

Tests whether a broad Practice-like Fitness Activity can support
Duration and Completion without becoming vague. Completion must state
what Done affirms.

### Single-Leg Balance

Tests unilateral execution. Unlike Side Plank, the initial question is
whether left/right are required components, Challenge configuration, or
an execution choice. Authoring should not assume the answer before
examining the canonical undertaking.

### Box Jump

Provides Power/Speed/Agility coverage with a simple Repetitions contract
while exercising equipment/environment and ordinary safety guidance.

### Dance

Provides Sports/Recreation coverage while testing a broad recognizable
Activity whose specific style can remain context/variant rather than
separate canonical identity.

### Digital Break

Provides Daily Living coverage and a clear Duration/Completion case. The
Activity should define disengagement from digital-device use without
hardcoding Challenge duration.

### Reading

Provides Personal Growth coverage and three Metrics: Duration,
Quantity/pages and Completion. It is a useful non-physical multi-Metric
case for PF-03.

## 10. CLU-01 Publication Strategy

Do not wait until all 15 are publication-ready before using the batch
for engineering.

Each candidate moves independently through:

`BASELINE → TRIAGED → AUTHORED → REVIEWED → PUBLICATION_READY → PUBLISHED`

Challenge eligibility is tracked separately by configuration.

A blocked Metric or candidate must not stall the rest of the batch.

## 11. Engineering Gate

CLU-01 is sufficient input for PF-02 once: - existing exemplar decisions
are reconciled; - the eight new candidates receive bounded authoring; -
Activity Component semantics from Side Plank are incorporated into the
standards; - exact compatibility/reporting contracts are captured; -
unresolved configurations are explicitly marked rather than guessed.

PF-02 does not need CLU-01 to be fully published in production before
implementation begins.
