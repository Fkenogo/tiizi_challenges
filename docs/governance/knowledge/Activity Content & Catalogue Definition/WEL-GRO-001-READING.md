# WEL-GRO-001 --- Reading

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Batch:** CLU-01 --- Core Launch Utility\
**Document type:** Canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Activity Code:** `WEL-GRO-001`\
**Domain:** Wellness\
**Category:** Personal Growth\
**Classification:** Learning / Personal Development\
**Baseline-supported measurements:** Duration → seconds/minutes/hours;
Quantity → pages; Completion → completion

## 1. Canonical Meaning

### Canonical title

**Reading**

### Description

A personal-growth Activity in which the participant intentionally reads
written material for a defined activity occurrence.

Reading is the canonical undertaking. The particular book, article,
document, subject, format or purpose does not automatically create a
different Activity identity.

## 2. Content and Format Do Not Normally Change Identity

Reading may involve: - a printed book; - an e-book; - an article; -
another written document or publication.

Those formats do not automatically create separate canonical Activities.

Likewise, reading fiction, professional material, educational material
or another subject does not by itself change the canonical undertaking.

A Challenge or Template may identify specific reading material where
relevant.

## 3. Supported Measurement Paths

Reading supports three legitimate measurement paths:

1.  **Duration → seconds/minutes/hours**
2.  **Quantity → pages**
3.  **Completion → completion**

These paths measure different aspects of one Activity and must remain
semantically distinct.

## 4. Duration Measurement

**Metric:** Duration\
**Units:** seconds, minutes, hours

### Reporting meaning

The participant reports elapsed time intentionally spent reading during
the applicable Activity occurrence.

### Participant instruction

> Enter how long you spent reading.

The Challenge supplies the Duration target.

Canonical Reading does not prescribe a universal daily reading duration.

## 5. Quantity Measurement --- Pages

**Metric:** Quantity\
**Unit:** pages

### Reporting meaning

The participant reports the number of pages read during the applicable
Activity occurrence.

### Participant instruction

> Enter the number of pages you read.

A page count is a participant-facing quantity, not a quality or
comprehension score.

Tiizi does not imply that: - every page has equivalent length or
difficulty; - reading more pages means better comprehension; - pages
from different formats are perfectly comparable; - a page target is
universally beneficial.

Those limitations do not prevent pages from being a useful ordinary
self-reported Quantity.

## 6. Digital and Non-Standard Page Formats

Some digital material may not have stable conventional page numbers.

Tiizi should not invent page equivalence for scrolling content,
variable-layout e-books or other formats where `pages` is not
meaningful.

Where pages cannot be meaningfully reported, a Reading Challenge should
use another compatible path such as Duration or Completion.

This is a configuration/measurement-fit issue rather than a reason to
create a separate Digital Reading Activity.

## 7. Completion Measurement

**Metric:** Completion\
**Unit:** completion\
**Participant-facing action:** Done

Completion means:

> **I completed the Reading occurrence required by this Challenge.**

For Completion to be meaningful, the Challenge or Template must identify
an intelligible occurrence.

Examples may include completing: - the configured reading session; - the
identified chapter or section; - the specified reading material where
the Challenge defines it.

Canonical Reading does not itself define which book, chapter or material
must be completed.

## 8. Completion Is Not Comprehension

Marking Reading Done does not mean: - the participant understood or
retained the material; - Tiizi assessed comprehension; - a learning
outcome was achieved; - a book was necessarily finished; - the whole
Challenge is complete.

If Tiizi later introduces learning assessment, that would be a separate
governed capability.

## 9. Multiple Metrics Do Not Create a Compound Score

Supporting Duration, pages and Completion does not authorize Tiizi to
infer: - pages per minute; - reading speed; - points; - comprehension; -
a combined Duration × Quantity score.

PF-03 must explicitly govern any Challenge that uses more than one
measurement dimension.

Ordinary Challenge creation can select one compatible path.

## 10. Continuous Versus Accumulated Duration

Reading also reinforces the Duration question exposed by Digital Break.

A Challenge may need to distinguish: - one continuous 30-minute reading
occurrence; from - 30 accumulated minutes across several occurrences.

Canonical Reading defines what Reading Duration represents.

PF-03 defines whether the Challenge requires continuous or accumulated
Duration where the distinction matters.

## 11. Quantity Accumulation

Likewise, a Challenge may define whether a page target applies: - to one
reading occurrence; - cumulatively across a day; - cumulatively across
the Challenge period.

That aggregation belongs to Challenge Definition and the Challenge
Engine.

Canonical Reading only defines what one reported page Quantity means.

## 12. Activity Components

A book containing chapters, sections or pages does not make those items
Tiizi Activity Components.

They are content structure, not automatically constituent Activity
truth.

Formal Activity Components remain reserved for cases where separately
attributable constituent performance is inherent to completing the
canonical Activity, such as Side Plank and Single-Leg Balance.

## 13. Practice Guidance

Reading requires minimal universal procedural guidance.

The participant should: 1. identify the applicable reading material; 2.
intentionally undertake the configured reading occurrence; 3. report the
configured measurement.

Tiizi should not add artificial technique content merely to make the
Activity record longer.

## 14. Context and Related Activities

Reading can occur for: - learning; - recreation; - reflection; -
professional development; - another legitimate purpose.

Purpose does not create duplicate canonical identities.

Other distinct undertakings such as Journaling, Writing or Studying may
remain separate Activities where governed.

## 15. Safety and Guidance Boundary

Reading is ordinary low-risk Wellness content.

Tiizi does not need to manufacture generic safety warnings where no
material Activity-specific caution is required.

This follows the Safety Standard rule that safety content should be
proportionate rather than boilerplate.

## 16. Verification Boundary

Canonical Reading Knowledge defines: - the undertaking; - Duration,
Quantity/pages and Completion meaning; - ordinary reporting
expectations.

It does not establish that Tiizi independently verified: - time spent
reading; - pages read; - material read; - completion; - comprehension.

Verification remains separately governed.

## 17. Activity / Challenge Separation

Canonical Activity Knowledge owns:

``` text
Reading
Duration → seconds/minutes/hours
Quantity → pages
Completion → completion
reporting meaning
```

Challenge or Template owns:

``` text
selected reading material where applicable
selected Metric and Unit
target
continuous/accumulated semantics
frequency
schedule
Challenge type
any multi-Metric relationship
```

## 18. Readiness Assessment

### Canonical identity

**Retain `WEL-GRO-001 Reading`.**

### Duration

**Challenge Eligible candidate:**

### Quantity

**Challenge Eligible candidate:** `Reading → Quantity → pages`

where the selected reading format provides meaningful page units.

### Completion

### Multi-Metric use

Not implied by compatibility; PF-03 governs any combined relationship.

### Verification

No Verification Authority established.

## 19. CLU-01 Finding

Reading confirms that one non-physical canonical Activity can
legitimately support several independent measurement paths without
becoming several Activities.

It also demonstrates that **measurement eligibility can depend on the
configured context**:

-   pages are valid when the material has meaningful page units;
-   Duration remains usable when page Quantity is unsuitable;
-   Completion remains usable when the occurrence is clearly defined.

Therefore PF-02/PF-03 should support configuration-sensitive measurement
eligibility rather than assuming every compatible Metric is valid in
every context.

Reading also confirms that Tiizi should not manufacture safety or
instructional content merely for schema completeness. Canonical content
depth remains Activity-specific.
