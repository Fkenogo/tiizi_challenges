# FIT-STR-001 --- Push-Up

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Document type:** Representative canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Activity Code:** `FIT-STR-001`\
**Domain:** Fitness\
**Category:** Strength\
**Classification:** Push\
**Primary measurement:** Repetitions → reps

## 1. Canonical Meaning

### Canonical title

**Push-Up**

### Description

A bodyweight pushing exercise performed from a supported plank position
by lowering the body toward the floor and pressing back to the starting
position.

### Purpose / context

Push-Up is a bodyweight strength activity built around a repeated
horizontal pushing movement.

This description identifies the Activity and its movement pattern. It
does not prescribe how many Push-Ups a participant should perform or how
frequently they should perform them.

### Semantic distinction

Push-Up is distinct from **Push-Up Hold**, which is a separate canonical
Activity measured by Duration.

Variations that preserve the essential Push-Up undertaking may be
represented as governed adaptations or variants. A variation that
materially changes the undertaking or measurement semantics may require
a separate canonical Activity.

## 2. Measurement Contract

### Repetitions → reps

**Metric:** Repetitions\
**Unit:** reps

**What one reported repetition means:**\
For self-reporting purposes, one repetition is one complete
lowering-and-press cycle: the participant begins in the Push-Up starting
position, lowers the body with control, and presses back to the starting
position.

**Participant reporting instruction:**\
\> Enter the number of Push-Up repetitions you completed.

### Reporting boundary

The participant reports the repetitions they completed.

The reporting contract tells the participant what to count. It does not
mean Tiizi independently observed or verified the technique of each
reported repetition.

### Challenge boundary

The canonical Activity does **not** define: - a required number of
repetitions; - sets; - repetitions per set; - daily frequency; -
Challenge duration; - Streak requirements; - scoring; - ranking.

A Challenge may later select `Push-Up → Repetitions → reps` and define
its own governed target or condition.

## 3. Setup

1.  Place the hands on a stable floor surface, approximately under the
    shoulders.
2.  Extend the legs behind the body and support the lower body on the
    toes for the standard expression of the Activity.
3.  Establish a supported plank position with the trunk held relatively
    straight and the body under control.

The participant should have enough clear space to lower and press
without obstruction.

## 4. Execution

1.  Begin in the supported plank starting position.
2.  Bend the elbows and lower the body toward the floor under control.
3.  Keep the trunk supported rather than deliberately allowing the hips
    to collapse toward the floor.
4.  Press through the hands to return to the starting position.
5.  Repeat the lowering-and-press cycle for the repetitions the
    participant chooses or the Challenge requires.

The final step above describes repetition of the Activity; it does not
place a target inside canonical Activity Knowledge.

## 5. Form Guidance

Useful participant-facing cues include:

-   Keep the body supported and controlled through the movement.
-   Lower under control rather than dropping toward the floor.
-   Keep the hands in a stable position.
-   Press back to the starting position before beginning the next
    repetition.

These are performance cues, not a Tiizi verification rubric.

## 6. Common Mistakes

Common execution issues that may help a participant understand the
intended Activity include:

-   allowing the hips to sag substantially during the movement;
-   raising the hips excessively instead of maintaining the supported
    plank shape;
-   dropping toward the floor without control;
-   beginning the next repetition before returning to the starting
    position.

These examples provide technique guidance. Tiizi does not claim that it
detects these mistakes during self-reported participation.

## 7. Equipment and Environment

### Required equipment

No exercise equipment is required for the standard bodyweight Push-Up.

### Environment

A stable surface and sufficient clear floor space are appropriate for
performing the Activity.

## 8. Adaptations and Variants

An easier Push-Up expression may use a raised stable surface, reducing
the amount of bodyweight supported through the hands.

A knee-supported expression may also reduce the loading of the standard
Push-Up movement.

These are useful working examples of Push-Up adaptations, but their
final catalogue relationship should remain governed. Tiizi should not
assume that every Push-Up variation belongs inside this canonical
Activity.

In particular:

-   **Push-Up Hold** is already a separate canonical Activity because
    its undertaking and measurement contract differ: Duration rather
    than Repetitions.
-   weighted Push-Up does not automatically require a separate canonical
    Activity merely because resistance is added; its eventual
    relationship should follow the governed canonical-versus-variant
    rule.
-   materially different movement patterns should be assessed
    independently rather than hidden as adaptations.

## 9. Safety and Practical Caution

Use a stable surface with enough clear space to perform the movement.

Stop the Activity if it causes sharp pain or significant discomfort.

Choose an appropriate easier expression where needed rather than forcing
a movement that cannot be performed under reasonable control.

This is ordinary participation guidance. It is not injury diagnosis,
rehabilitation advice or individualized medical instruction.

## 10. Verification Boundary

Canonical Push-Up Knowledge defines:

-   what Push-Up means in Tiizi;
-   the expected way to perform it;
-   what a participant should count as a repetition;
-   how the participant reports repetitions.

It does not establish that Tiizi independently verified:

-   movement depth;
-   body alignment;
-   repetition quality;
-   the number reported.

Any future verified Push-Up Challenge would require a separately
governed Verification Authority and evidence mechanism.
