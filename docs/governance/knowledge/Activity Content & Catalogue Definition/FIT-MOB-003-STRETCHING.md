# FIT-MOB-003 --- Stretching

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Batch:** CLU-01 --- Core Launch Utility\
**Document type:** Canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Activity Code:** `FIT-MOB-003`\
**Domain:** Fitness\
**Category:** Mobility & Flexibility\
**Classification:** Flexibility\
**Baseline-supported measurements:** Duration → seconds/minutes;
Completion → completion

## 1. Canonical Meaning

### Canonical title

**Stretching**

### Description

A flexibility practice in which the participant intentionally performs
one or more stretching movements or positions during a defined activity
occurrence.

Stretching is a broad but recognizable canonical practice. The
particular body area, stretch selection, sequence and routine can vary
without automatically changing the canonical Activity.

## 2. Why a Broad Practice Can Be Canonical

Tiizi does not need a separate canonical Activity for every possible
stretch merely to support ordinary Stretching Challenges.

Stretching remains useful as one canonical Activity where: - the
participant understands the undertaking; - the Challenge can configure
the required occurrence; - Duration or Completion can be meaningfully
reported; - a specific named stretch is not necessary to interpret the
Challenge.

Specific stretches may still exist as separate canonical Activities
where their undertaking is sufficiently distinct. The authoritative
baseline already contains examples such as Hamstring Stretch, Quadriceps
Stretch, Hip Flexor Stretch and Calf Stretch.

Therefore:

> Broad Stretching and specific named stretches can coexist without
> treating one as a duplicate of the other.

## 3. Duration Measurement

**Metric:** Duration\
**Units:** seconds, minutes

### Reporting meaning

The participant reports the elapsed time intentionally spent performing
the applicable Stretching occurrence.

### Participant instruction

> Enter how long you spent stretching.

Duration may represent a sequence containing several stretches. Tiizi
does not require the participant to report each individual stretch
merely because the overall Activity contains several movements.

The Challenge supplies the Duration target.

## 4. Completion Measurement

**Metric:** Completion\
**Unit:** completion\
**Participant-facing action:** Done

Stretching passes the Completion semantic test only if the configured
occurrence is sufficiently defined for the participant to know what they
are affirming.

A valid Completion meaning is:

> **I completed the Stretching practice required for this Challenge
> occurrence.**

This is not the same as: - "I stretched enough"; - "I achieved the
flexibility target"; - "I completed today's Challenge"; - an undefined
tap of Done with no configured practice meaning.

## 5. Completion Requires a Configured Occurrence

Because Stretching is intentionally broad, Completion cannot mean that
canonical Knowledge secretly defines one universal Stretching routine.

The Challenge or Template must make the applicable occurrence
understandable.

Examples could include: - complete the configured Stretching routine; -
complete the Stretching practice identified by the Challenge.

The specific routine or target is downstream configuration.

Canonical Knowledge establishes that **completion of a defined
Stretching occurrence is a legitimate reporting form**.

## 6. Duration and Completion Are Independent Paths

A Challenge may use:

`Stretching → Duration → minutes`

or:

`Stretching → Completion → Done`

Supporting both does not mean a participant must submit both.

It also does not mean Completion is automatically derived from any
arbitrary Duration unless the Challenge explicitly defines that
relationship.

## 7. Activity Components

A Stretching practice may contain several stretches, body areas or
sides.

Those internal movements do **not** automatically become governed
Activity Components.

For example, a routine containing hamstring, quadriceps and calf
stretches can still be reported as one Stretching occurrence.

Use formal Activity Components only where separately attributable
constituent truth must be preserved for the canonical undertaking, as
established by the Side Plank pattern.

Do not turn every exercise sequence into a component graph.

## 8. Setup and Preparation

1.  Use a space suitable for the selected stretching movements.
2.  Position the body and any ordinary support/equipment needed for the
    practice.
3.  Begin the applicable Stretching occurrence.

No universal sequence is prescribed by canonical Stretching.

## 9. Practice Guidance

During Stretching: - move into positions under reasonable control; -
avoid deliberately forcing a range that cannot be maintained
comfortably; - transition between positions under control; - follow the
applicable Challenge, Template or selected practice where one is
provided.

The content should remain useful without pretending there is one
universal Stretching protocol.

## 10. Specific Stretch Versus Broad Stretching

Where a Challenge specifically requires a canonical named stretch, it
should select that Activity rather than using generic Stretching merely
as a label.

For example, if the intended undertaking is specifically **Hamstring
Stretch**, the canonical named Activity can provide more precise
meaning.

Generic Stretching is appropriate when the intended undertaking is the
broader flexibility practice.

## 11. Variants and Context

The following do not automatically create separate Stretching
identities: - different body areas within a general routine; - different
sequences; - use of ordinary supports; - group versus individual
context; - warm-up versus cooldown purpose.

Purpose/context belongs downstream unless it materially changes the
undertaking.

## 12. Safety and Guidance Boundary

Stretching should be performed under reasonable control and should not
be forced through sharp pain or significant discomfort.

Tiizi should not prescribe: - rehabilitation programmes; - injury
treatment; - clinical range-of-motion targets; - individualized
therapeutic stretching; - medically specific restrictions without an
appropriate governed basis.

Ordinary participation guidance is sufficient for the canonical
Activity.

## 13. Verification Boundary

Canonical Stretching Knowledge defines: - the broad undertaking; - valid
Duration and Completion reporting; - what Done affirms; - ordinary
practice guidance.

It does not establish that Tiizi independently verified: - which
stretches were performed; - technique; - range of motion; - Duration; -
completion.

Verification remains separately governed.

## 14. Activity / Challenge Separation

Canonical Activity Knowledge owns:

``` text

Duration → seconds/minutes
Completion → completion
ordinary practice meaning
```

Challenge or Template owns:

``` text
specific routine where applicable
Duration target
frequency
schedule
required occurrence
Challenge type
```

Challenge Engine owns derived Challenge truth.

## 16. CLU-01 Finding

Stretching confirms that a broad Practice Activity can be canonical
without enumerating every internal movement.

It also sharpens the Completion rule:

> **Completion is legitimate only when the participant can state what
> configured Activity occurrence they are affirming as Done.**

For broad practice Activities, canonical Knowledge can authorize
Completion as a measurement form while the Challenge or Template
supplies the bounded occurrence that gives Done its concrete meaning.

This finding should carry into PF-03 and the V2 Wizard so that
Completion never becomes an ungoverned generic fallback.
