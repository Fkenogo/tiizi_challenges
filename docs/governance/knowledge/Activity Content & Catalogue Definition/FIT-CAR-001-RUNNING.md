# FIT-CAR-001 --- Running

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Document type:** Representative canonical Activity exemplar\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Activity Code:** `FIT-CAR-001`\
**Domain:** Fitness\
**Category:** Cardio & Conditioning\
**Classification:** Locomotion\
**Supported measurements:** Distance → metres/kilometres; Duration →
seconds/minutes/hours

## 1. Canonical Meaning

### Canonical title

**Running**

### Description

A locomotion activity in which the participant moves continuously or
intermittently at a running pace under their own movement.

### Purpose / context

Running gives Tiizi a canonical way to represent running as a reusable
physical Activity across different Challenge structures.

The canonical Activity describes running itself. It does not prescribe
pace, distance, duration, route, frequency or training objective.

### Semantic distinction

Running is distinct from Walking as a separately selectable canonical
Activity in the Tiizi baseline.

Running may occur outdoors, indoors or on suitable equipment without
requiring separate canonical identities merely because the environment
changes.

A materially different undertaking should be evaluated separately rather
than hidden inside Running solely because it involves locomotion.

## 2. Measurement Contract

Running supports two distinct governed measurement dimensions.

### 2.1 Distance → metres/kilometres

**Metric:** Distance\
**Valid Units:** metres, kilometres

**What the reported value means:**\
The distance the participant completed while Running.

**Participant reporting instruction:**\
\> Enter the distance you ran.

Compatible units may be normalized deterministically for Challenge
calculation while preserving the Challenge-configured participant-facing
unit.

### 2.2 Duration → seconds/minutes/hours

**Metric:** Duration\
**Valid Units:** seconds, minutes, hours

**What the reported value means:**\
The elapsed time the participant spent Running for the reported
activity.

**Participant reporting instruction:**\
\> Enter how long you ran.

Duration describes time spent performing the Activity. It does not
itself prescribe a pace or distance.

## 3. Multiple Measurement Paths

Running may legitimately be configured by Distance or Duration.

Examples of downstream Challenge configurations could use: - a Distance
requirement; or - a Duration requirement.

The canonical Activity merely permits those measurements.

Supporting both does not mean the participant chooses between them at
logging time. The Challenge pins the applicable Activity/Metric/Unit
configuration.

Pace, speed, calories and other possible running statistics are **not**
automatically canonical Tiizi Metrics merely because another fitness
product might track them. They require a separate governed product
decision if Tiizi later needs them.

## 4. Preparation

Running does not require a detailed universal setup protocol.

Before starting, the participant should: - use an environment suitable
for running; - have enough space to move safely; - use any ordinary
clothing or footwear they consider appropriate for the setting.

Tiizi should not manufacture a complex preparation routine simply to
fill the canonical content model.

## 5. Execution

Run through the chosen environment or on suitable equipment for the
activity period.

Maintain awareness of the environment and remain in control of movement.

The canonical Activity does not prescribe: - running technique; - stride
pattern; - pace; - heart-rate zone; - training intensity; - route.

Those are not necessary to establish the basic canonical meaning of
Running.

## 6. Technique Guidance

Running is intentionally a **lighter-guidance exemplar**.

Tiizi may provide concise practical guidance where useful, but the
canonical Activity does not need an exhaustive running-form tutorial to
be understandable or reportable.

Appropriate general cues include: - move at a pace you can control; -
remain aware of the running surface and surroundings.

More specialized coaching content should not be invented merely because
the Activity schema can store guidance.

## 7. Reporting Guidance

### Distance-based Challenge

> Enter the distance you ran in the unit used by your Challenge.

### Duration-based Challenge

> Enter how long you ran.

The reporting contract does not require the participant to report pace,
calories, route or physiological measurements.

## 8. Equipment and Environment

### Required equipment

No specialized exercise equipment is inherently required for canonical
Running.

### Environment

Running may take place in an appropriate outdoor or indoor environment,
including suitable stationary running equipment.

Outdoor and treadmill Running do not automatically become separate
canonical Activities merely because the environment differs.

If future product evidence shows that a particular running form has
materially different meaning or measurement semantics, Tiizi can assess
it under the canonical-versus-variant rule.

## 9. Safety and Practical Caution

Run in an environment appropriate for the Activity and remain aware of
surfaces, obstacles, traffic or other relevant surroundings.

Stop if Running causes sharp pain or significant discomfort.

The canonical Activity does not prescribe individualized pace,
heart-rate zones, injury rehabilitation or medical exercise thresholds.

## 10. Verification Boundary

Canonical Running Knowledge defines: - what Running means in Tiizi; -
valid Distance and Duration reporting; - ordinary environmental
guidance.

It does not establish that Tiizi independently verified: - the route; -
distance; - duration; - running form; - pace; - physical exertion.

Evidence and verification, if later required for a particular Challenge,
remain separately governed.

## 11. Activity / Challenge Separation Check

The exemplar contains no canonical: - target distance; - target
duration; - pace; - route; - frequency; - schedule; - Challenge
duration; - Challenge type; - streak rule; - collective target; -
competitive ranking rule; - scoring; - recognition; - reward.

Those remain downstream concerns.

## 12. KCS / Content Coverage

Running exercises: - universal canonical meaning; - Distance
measurement; - Duration measurement; - deterministic compatible-unit
handling; - locomotion/environment guidance; - proportionate physical
safety guidance; - deliberate restraint in content depth.

It demonstrates that publication quality does not require every Activity
to have the same instructional density as a technique-heavy strength
movement.

## 13. Readiness Assessment

### Content status

**Publication-ready candidate**, subject to Founder approval of this
wording and reconciliation with the canonical Knowledge record.

### Challenge eligibility

**Eligible candidate** for governed configurations using: -
`Running → Distance → metres/kilometres` -
`Running → Duration → seconds/minutes/hours`

provided the Activity is Published and the normal Tiizi
publication/compatibility gates are satisfied.

### Unresolved product dependency

None material for the core canonical Activity.

Future questions such as pace/speed metrics, route evidence or
specialized running variants should not block the current Running
definition and should not be invented during this content exercise.

### Verification

No Verification Authority is established or required for ordinary
self-reported Running participation.

-   a Streak Challenge requiring a configured Running distance each day;
-   a Collective Challenge accumulating accepted Running distance;
-   a Competitive Challenge using a governed Running target;
-   a Challenge requiring a configured amount of Running time.

The canonical Activity remains `FIT-CAR-001`. Challenge Definition and
the applicable engine determine the undertaking and result.
