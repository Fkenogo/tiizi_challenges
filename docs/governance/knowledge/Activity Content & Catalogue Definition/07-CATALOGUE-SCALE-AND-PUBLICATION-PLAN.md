# 07 --- Catalogue Scale & Publication Plan

**Working package:** Tiizi V2 Activity Content & Catalogue Definition\
**Document type:** Product catalogue production and publication plan\
**Status:** Founder/Product working baseline\
**Source candidate baseline:** 118 Activities --- 84 Fitness / 34
Wellness\
**Purpose:** Define how Tiizi converts the governed candidate baseline
into a progressively authored, reviewed, publication-ready V2 Activity
catalogue without making completion of all 118 a prerequisite for
continued product engineering.

## 1. Objective

Tiizi should not treat the 118-candidate Founder Working Baseline as
either:

-   a database seed that should be made live immediately; or
-   a content project that must be completely authored before V2 product
    work can continue.

The correct model is progressive catalogue production.

The programme should:

1.  preserve the governed candidate baseline;
2.  assess each candidate against the validated Activity Content
    Standard;
3.  author sufficient canonical content for the candidate's needs;
4.  define valid measurement/reporting semantics;
5.  resolve applicable safety and product dependencies;
6.  publish only candidates that satisfy their applicable gate;
7.  make only governed configurations Challenge Eligible;
8.  allow the catalogue to expand after launch through the same
    controlled lifecycle.

## 2. Governing Principle

The catalogue pipeline is:

``` text
Founder Working Baseline Candidate
        ↓
Candidate Triage
        ↓
Canonical Content Authoring
        ↓
Measurement / Reporting Reconciliation
        ↓
Safety / Product Dependency Review
        ↓
Publication Assessment
        ↓
Draft | Published | Retired
        ↓
Challenge-Eligibility Assessment
        ↓
Eligible configurations only
```

Publication and Challenge eligibility are separate decisions.

## 3. The 118 Candidates Are a Working Population, Not a Launch Quota

The current working population contains:

-   84 Fitness candidates;
-   34 Wellness candidates.

This provides broad coverage for catalogue development.

It does not establish that: - all 118 must launch; - every candidate
must survive unchanged; - all candidates require the same content
depth; - all Published Activities must be Challenge Eligible; -
catalogue completion must block PF-02/PF-03 or other V2 foundation work.

The launch catalogue should be determined by product usefulness,
semantic readiness, measurement coverage, safety readiness and
Challenge-system needs.

## 4. Candidate Dispositions

Every candidate should receive one explicit working disposition during
catalogue production.

### A --- Retain

The candidate represents a sound canonical Activity and can proceed
through normal authoring.

### B --- Retain with bounded content/measurement correction

Identity is sound, but description, measurement semantics,
compatibility, guidance or classification needs correction.

### C --- Reframe / Rename

The underlying undertaking is valid but the current canonical name or
framing is misleading.

### D --- Variant / Relationship candidate

The concept may be better represented as a governed Variant or
relationship of another canonical Activity.

### E --- Split / Merge assessment

The candidate is too broad, duplicates another undertaking, or contains
materially distinct undertakings.

### F --- Challenge-layer concept

The concept appears to be primarily a target, schedule, temporal
condition, scoring rule or other Challenge configuration rather than an
independent Activity.

### G --- Draft dependency

Canonical identity is acceptable, but publication is blocked by
unresolved content, safety, evidence or product dependencies.

### H --- Retire / Reject candidate

The concept does not justify a canonical Activity identity after review.

A disposition is a catalogue decision, not automatically an
implementation change.

## 5. Production Should Be Batch-Based

Do not author 118 records in one undifferentiated pass.

Use bounded batches that deliberately cover different content and
measurement patterns.

Recommended working batch size:

**10--15 Activities per authoring batch**

This is large enough to make meaningful catalogue progress while small
enough to detect systematic problems before they are copied across
dozens of records.

A batch may be smaller for health-sensitive or semantically difficult
Wellness content.

## 6. Batch Selection Strategy

Batches should not simply follow numerical Activity ID order.

Each batch should provide useful product coverage while exercising the
catalogue model.

Selection should consider:

-   domain and category coverage;
-   Metric coverage;
-   equipment versus no-equipment;
-   simple versus multi-Metric Activities;
-   protocol versus technique content;
-   ordinary versus health-adjacent Wellness;
-   likely Challenge usefulness;
-   variant/relationship questions;
-   safety complexity;
-   content-authoring effort.

## 7. Recommended Catalogue Production Waves

### Wave 0 --- Representative Validation --- COMPLETE

The representative set has already tested the model through:

-   Push-Up
-   Breathing Practice
-   Running
-   Water Intake
-   Plank
-   Sleep
-   Bench Press
-   Farmer Carry
-   Bedtime
-   Wake Time
-   Fasting

This wave produced the bounded amendments incorporated into Sections
01--05.

Do not repeat Wave 0.

### Wave 1 --- Core Launch Utility

Author approximately 10--15 high-utility Activities spanning the most
common Challenge measurement patterns.

Objectives: - establish useful early Fitness and Wellness breadth; -
cover all ordinary Metrics that are ready; - create enough catalogue
depth for PF-03, Templates and Wizard design; - avoid unresolved
high-risk content as a dependency.

Wave 1 should include a balanced selection from: - repetition-based
Fitness; - duration-based Fitness; - distance/cardio; - simple
completion-based Wellness; - quantity-based Wellness; - protocol-based
Wellness.

Representative exemplars already completed can count toward this product
coverage where approved.

### Wave 2 --- Category Breadth

Expand coverage across all approved Fitness and Wellness categories.

Objective: Tiizi should not appear complete in one category while major
categories have no usable canonical Activities.

This wave should prioritize breadth rather than catalogue volume.

### Wave 3 --- Equipment, Variants and Multi-Metric Activities

Focus on candidates where: - Weight is relevant; - equipment
relationships matter; - multiple Metrics are compatible; - variants need
reconciliation.

This wave should occur after the Load Reporting Convention is settled
sufficiently for Weight-based eligibility.

### Wave 4 --- Health-Adjacent and Higher-Sensitivity Wellness

Focus on candidates requiring stronger safety/product consideration.

Examples may include nutrition, fasting and other Activities where
arbitrary Challenge targets or competitive/streak pressure require
explicit evaluation.

These Activities should not be rushed merely to increase catalogue
count.

### Wave 5 --- Remaining Baseline Reconciliation

Review the remaining candidate population for: - genuine
launch/post-launch value; - duplicates; - variants; - Challenge-layer
concepts; - low-priority content; - candidates that should remain Draft
or be retired.

The goal is a coherent catalogue, not 118 Published records for its own
sake.

## 8. Launch Catalogue Minimum

Do not define launch readiness by a fixed percentage of 118.

Instead, a launch-ready catalogue should provide:

1.  meaningful coverage across the approved Fitness and Wellness
    domains;
2.  enough Activities to support the intended initial Challenge
    Templates and Wizard journeys;
3.  representation of the ordinary measurement patterns Tiizi supports;
4.  no reliance on Draft Activities for advertised launch journeys;
5.  responsible treatment of health-adjacent content;
6.  no unresolved identity or reporting ambiguity in Challenge-eligible
    configurations.

A precise launch count can be set later once Challenge Definition,
Templates and Wizard requirements make the necessary product breadth
visible.

## 9. Relationship to PF-02

PF-02 Metric/Unit Compatibility and Catalogue Authoring does **not**
need all 118 Activities fully authored before it begins.

PF-02 can proceed from: - the validated content standards; - the
representative exemplars; - the six governed Metrics; - exact
Activity/Metric/Unit tuple rules; - reporting/evidence semantics; - the
first approved catalogue-production batch.

PF-02 should provide the governed machinery through which later batches
are progressively reconciled.

Bulk seeding must not bypass publication or eligibility gates.

## 10. Relationship to PF-03

PF-03 Challenge Definition should proceed once the Activity/measurement
contract is sufficiently stable to answer its downstream questions.

PF-03 specifically needs the catalogue work to establish:

-   valid canonical Activity identity;
-   valid Metric/Unit compatibility;
-   participant reporting meaning;
-   configuration-sensitive eligibility;
-   known multi-Metric cases;
-   known temporal-condition cases;
-   known health-adjacent constraint cases.

The representative set already provides examples for each of these.

Therefore completion of all 118 is not a PF-03 prerequisite.

## 11. Relationship to Templates and Wizard

Challenge Templates and the V2 Wizard must consume only governed
catalogue truth.

They should not: - recreate their own Activity catalogues; - fuzzy-match
display names; - invent Metrics or Units; - expose Draft candidates; -
assume every Published Activity supports every Challenge type; - infer
compound scoring from multiple compatible Metrics.

The catalogue should tell the Wizard which Activity/measurement
configurations are available.

Challenge Definition should tell it which configurations are valid for
the selected Challenge structure.

## 12. Authoring Record for Each Candidate

Catalogue production should use a concise structured working record.

At minimum:

### Identity

-   Activity Code
-   canonical title
-   domain
-   category/classification
-   candidate disposition

### Meaning

-   canonical description
-   undertaking boundary
-   material variant/relationship notes

### Measurement

-   compatible Metrics
-   exact Units
-   participant reporting meaning
-   Completion meaning where applicable
-   evidence/precision notes where material

### Guidance

-   applicable setup/preparation
-   execution/protocol
-   technique/common mistakes where useful
-   equipment/environment
-   adaptations/variants where useful

### Safety

-   ordinary caution
-   supported avoidance/caution where required
-   unresolved dependency if any

### Product Boundary

-   what remains Challenge configuration
-   any PF-03 dependency
-   any Template/variant dependency

### Readiness

-   Draft / Published candidate / Retired candidate
-   eligible Activity/Metric/Unit configurations
-   blocked configurations
-   unresolved items

Not every Activity needs every guidance subsection.

## 13. Review Model

Catalogue review should be proportionate.

### Ordinary low-risk candidate

One product/content pass plus bounded correction if required.

### Measurement/identity ambiguity

Targeted product review of the ambiguity.

### Health-sensitive candidate

Appropriate content/safety review before publication.

### No repeated review loops

Minor wording, formatting or non-material metadata issues should be
corrected in the same batch and should not trigger new governance
cycles.

Escalate only when a finding affects: - canonical identity; -
measurement truth; - participant reporting meaning; - safety; -
Challenge eligibility; - material downstream product architecture.

## 14. Publication Gate

A candidate may move from Draft to Published only when its applicable
content minima are satisfied.

Publication should confirm:

-   stable canonical identity;
-   responsible description;
-   applicable KCS content;
-   valid measurement semantics;
-   required reporting meaning;
-   applicable guidance;
-   applicable safety/caution;
-   lifecycle/version metadata;
-   no unresolved material contradiction.

Publication does not itself mean unrestricted Challenge use.

## 15. Challenge-Eligibility Gate

Challenge eligibility should be assessed against a specific governed
configuration.

At minimum:

``` text
Activity
+ Metric
+ Unit
+ reporting meaning
+ applicable Challenge/product constraints
= eligible configuration
```

Where necessary, eligibility may additionally depend on: - Challenge
type; - target/condition form; - temporal condition; - load
convention; - safety/product constraint; - another governed definition.

This should remain explicit rather than being inferred from publication.

## 16. Progressive Publication

Tiizi should support catalogue evolution after launch.

New or improved Activities can move through:

`Draft → Published → Retired`

with append-only version history and historical Challenge
intelligibility.

This means the initial launch catalogue can be deliberately bounded
while the broader Knowledge library continues to mature.

## 17. Localization During Scale

Canonical identity remains language-independent.

For catalogue-scale authoring: - establish authoritative source-language
content first; - keep localizable text structurally separate from
identity; - do not make full translation coverage a prerequisite for
defining canonical Activity truth unless launch language requirements
require it; - attach historical localizable content sufficiently to
preserve Challenge intelligibility.

Translations should not create duplicate canonical Activities.

## 18. Automation and Coding-Agent Boundary

Coding agents may assist with: - schema enforcement; - batch tooling; -
validation; - detecting missing required fields; - duplicate/identity
checks; - compatibility checks; - import/export; - lifecycle
enforcement; - reporting of unresolved candidates.

Coding agents must not independently invent: - authoritative Activity
meaning; - medical/safety thresholds; - measurement semantics where
product meaning is unresolved; - canonical identity decisions; -
Challenge-use safety policy.

Product/Founder authority supplies those decisions.

## 19. Working Catalogue Progress States

For programme tracking, each candidate may use a simple production
state:

1.  `BASELINE`
2.  `TRIAGED`
3.  `AUTHORED`
4.  `REVIEWED`
5.  `PUBLICATION_READY`
6.  `PUBLISHED`
7.  `RETIRED`

Challenge eligibility should be tracked separately from this lifecycle.

Do not create dozens of governance statuses.

## 20. Recommended Immediate Catalogue Batch

The next content-production batch should be a **Core Launch Utility
Batch** of approximately 10--15 Activities.

It should deliberately include: - several simple repetition Fitness
Activities; - at least one additional Duration Fitness Activity; - at
least one cardio/distance Activity; - at least one simple Completion
Wellness Activity; - at least one Quantity Wellness Activity; - at least
one protocol Wellness Activity; - at least one candidate that tests
variant/equipment relationships.

Do not include Fasting merely to satisfy category count.

Bedtime/Wake Time should remain outside publication until PF-03 resolves
the temporal-condition model.

Weight-heavy candidates can be authored, but unrestricted Weight
eligibility should wait for the Load Reporting Convention.

## 21. Gate to Engineering Reconciliation

The Activity Content & Catalogue Definition package is sufficiently
mature for engineering planning when:

-   Sections 01--07 are internally consistent;
-   representative exemplars are preserved;
-   first catalogue batch is identified;
-   known PF-02/PF-03 dependencies are explicit;
-   no unresolved issue requires rewriting the Activity foundation.

At that point the package can be reconciled into the Tiizi repository by
the coding agent as controlled product authority/reference material.

## 22. Programme Disposition

**CATALOGUE SCALE MODEL DEFINED --- PROGRESSIVE AUTHORING AUTHORIZED AS
PRODUCT WORK; BULK PUBLICATION NOT AUTHORIZED.**

The 118-candidate baseline remains the working population.

The next programme step is not to author all remaining candidates.

The next step is to: 1. finalize the package-level overview; 2. identify
the first Core Launch Utility Batch; 3. reconcile the completed Activity
Content & Catalogue Definition package into the repository; 4. then
authorize the bounded PF-02 engineering task against this product
foundation.

This preserves forward movement while allowing the catalogue and
engineering tracks to proceed in parallel.
