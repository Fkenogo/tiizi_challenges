# Document 00A — Future Standards Reference Recommendations

## 1. Purpose

This document recommends where future product, experience and engineering standards should reference [Document 00A — Tiizi Founder Philosophy and Design Doctrine](00A-TIIZI-FOUNDER-PHILOSOPHY-AND-DESIGN-DOCTRINE.md).

The recommendations create no standard, feature, workflow or implementation obligation. They describe appropriate doctrinal dependencies if and when those standards are approved for creation.

## 2. Reference Rule

Document 00A should guide judgement among constitutionally valid choices. It should never be cited to override:

- [Document 00](00-TIIZI-CONSTITUTIONAL-ONTOLOGY-AND-FOUNDATIONAL-PRODUCT-CONCEPTS.md);
- the [Platform Constitution](platform/01-TIIZI-PLATFORM-CONSTITUTION.md);
- approved Platform Principles;
- the Platform Authority Model;
- a Domain Standard;
- an approved Policy;
- a later lifecycle, privacy, security or other governing standard.

A future standard should cite the Doctrine only where it can name the design judgement the Doctrine informs.

## 6. Recommended Reference Language

Future standards may use:

> This standard is governed by applicable constitutional documents and is informed by Document 00A — Tiizi Founder Philosophy and Design Doctrine. The Doctrine guides choices among compliant alternatives; it does not create Authority or override approved governance.

Product standards may add:

> The capability shall be evaluated against the seven founder questions, including whether Tiizi would still recognise itself if the capability became widely used.

Experience standards may add:

> Simplicity shall reduce avoidable complexity without concealing material meaning, choice, uncertainty or consequence.

Engineering standards may add:

> Technology shall preserve the identity and boundaries of the constitutional concepts it expresses.

## 7. Recommended Adoption Sequence

After founder approval of Document 00A:

1. record its status, approval date and relationship to Document 00;
2. reference it from a future Product Strategy and Capability Admission Standard;
3. establish a Tiizi Experience Principles document;
4. establish technology-neutral Engineering Principles;
5. incorporate the Doctrine into future Community, Challenge, Motivation, Analytics and domain-admission standards;
6. include doctrine-fit review in future quality and governance acceptance work;
7. review references periodically for substance rather than ceremonial citation.

## 8. Reference Validation Questions

Before adding a reference, ask:

1. Which actual judgement does the Doctrine guide here?
2. Is the reference subordinate to constitutional governance?
3. Does it preserve pending founder decisions?
4. Does it avoid converting philosophy into an unapproved requirement?
5. Does it keep future possibilities outside active scope?
6. Would removing the reference change the document's interpretation? If not, the reference may be merely ceremonial.
