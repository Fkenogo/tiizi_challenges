# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 3 — Final Recommendation Confirmation

**Status:** Recommendations confirmed after independent challenge; pending founder approval

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Confirmation Result

Independent challenge did not identify a constitutionally stronger option or package. The Phase 2 recommendations remain:

| Question | Confirmed recommendation | Challenge outcome |
|---|---|---|
| GFC-01 | **GFC-01-B — bounded Administrative Authority creation authentication** | Survives with creation-only scope |
| GFC-02 | **GFC-02-B — successor-continuous reassignment plus Charter-governed exceptional succession** | Survives conditionally; Charter supplies rule, not Authority |
| GFC-03 | **GFC-03-A — founding binding Charter** | Survives with narrow binding-effect boundary |
| GFC-04 | **GFC-04-B — Council capable of bounded Assignments of Responsibility** | Survives narrowly; attribution and non-inference are essential |
| GFC-05 | **GFC-05-A — permanent independent Council inventory entity** | Survives; classification must remain non-allocation |

Every entry remains **Recommended — pending founder approval**.

## 2. Required Founder-awareness Conditions

Founder review should consider the package with these conditions visible:

1. Administrative Authority authenticates only the Group-creation event and its founding facts; it does not become continuing Group Authority.
2. A Charter succession principle does not establish Stewardship Reassignment truth by itself.
3. Founding Charter applicability is subordinate to Platform governance and Policy and creates no permission or inferred consent.
4. Council responsibility never becomes Authority, shared stewardship, Administration, Custody or an implied member relationship.
5. Permanent C-GRP-08 classification allocates no EOG-01 relationship and does not resolve its Information Subject boundary by implication.

These conditions preserve approved governance. They are not implementation requirements or new decisions.

## 3. Principal Alternatives Retained for Founder Review

| Question | Principal alternative | When it would become stronger |
|---|---|---|
| GFC-01 | GFC-01-A | Only if higher governance deliberately expands Participation Authority without making participation the basis of existence. |
| GFC-02 | GFC-02-C | If no existing Authority can establish exceptional reassignment coherently from an applicable Charter rule. |
| GFC-03 | GFC-03-B | If founder preference gives post-creation collective adoption priority over immediate Charter continuity. |
| GFC-04 | GFC-04-C | If Council-body responsibility cannot remain attributable or separate from singular stewardship. |
| GFC-05 | GFC-05-C | If founder judges current Council identity evidence insufficient for permanent inventory treatment. |

## 4. Governance Effect

This confirmation:

- does not approve GFC-01 through GFC-05;
- does not modify EOG-01, EOG-02 or EOG-05;
- does not allocate Authority, Accountable Stewardship, responsibility or any other EOG-01 relationship;
- does not resolve CG-08;
- does not create lifecycle, role, permission, privacy, security or implementation governance; and
- does not authorize an EOG-05 amendment.

## 5. Final Phase 3 Verdict

**The Phase 2 preferred package is confirmed as the constitutionally strongest package for founder review.**

The package is coherent but not self-sufficient: later Charter, reassignment, Council and inventory governance remain necessary. Its remaining dependencies are visible and controlled rather than silently resolved.
