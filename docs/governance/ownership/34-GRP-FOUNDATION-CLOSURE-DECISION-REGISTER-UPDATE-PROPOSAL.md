# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 4 — Decision Register Update Proposal

**Status:** Draft proposal only; do not apply before founder decision

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This document proposes how a later recording task could update the consolidated governance decision register after an attributable founder decision. It does not modify the register, approve a recommendation or authorize an update.

## 2. Current Decision Treatment

GFC-01 through GFC-05 are pack-local traceability identifiers created for GRP Foundation Closure. They remain pending founder approval. CG-08 remains a separate EOG-05 classification gate and is not resolved by this proposal.

## 3. Proposed Register Entries If Approved Without Amendment

| Proposed ID | Proposed title | Proposed status | Proposed decision summary | Mandatory trace note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GFC-01 | Group creation authentication | Approved | GFC-01-B: Administrative Authority authenticates the Group-creation event within a creation-only scope. | No continuing Group Authority; origin, Membership and Stewardship remain distinct. |

These rows are proposed text only. None may be marked Approved unless the founder expressly approves the corresponding decision and its conditions.

## 4. Rejection and Amendment Handling

- A rejected recommendation should be recorded as **Rejected**, with the rejected option and founder notes attributable.
- An amended recommendation should remain **Pending amendment** until exact replacement wording is approved.
- Approval of a principal alternative requires its own exact decision summary and conditions; the Phase 2 recommendation text must not be reused.
- A package-level approval cannot override an individual amendment recorded by the founder.
- No decision-register entry may state that CG-08 is resolved until an attributable EOG-05 amendment is separately approved.

## 5. Register Boundaries

A later register update must not:

- amend EOG-01, EOG-02 or EOG-05 by implication;
- allocate an actor, role or EOG-01 relationship;
- treat the Charter as an Authority;
- treat Council responsibility as shared stewardship;
- treat permanent inventory direction as a completed inventory amendment; or
- introduce lifecycle, permission or implementation detail.

## 6. Proposal Status

No decision register was modified by Phase 4. This proposal remains non-operative until a later task is authorized from an issued founder decision.
