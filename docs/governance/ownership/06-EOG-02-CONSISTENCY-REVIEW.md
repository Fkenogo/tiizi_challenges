# EOG-02 — Constitutional Consistency Review

## 1. Purpose

This review validates the [Constitutional Accountability Framework for Groups](06-EOG-02-CONSTITUTIONAL-ACCOUNTABILITY-FRAMEWORK-FOR-GROUPS.md) and [Group Accountability Options Paper](06-EOG-02-GROUP-ACCOUNTABILITY-OPTIONS-PAPER.md) against Tiizi's approved conceptual and governance baseline.

It reports conflicts and later alignment needs. It does not amend a governing source or approve a recommendation.

## 3. Overall Verdict

**Verdict:** Constitutionally coherent as refined founder decision material, with creation authentication and other listed recommendations still pending.

The EOG-02 documents preserve the ontology, Group boundaries, eleven Platform Authorities and EOG-01 distinctions. They introduce no lifecycle, permission or implementation model.

The founder refinement preserves exactly one singular Accountable Steward relationship for every Group. A Stewardship Council may support that relationship but cannot hold, replace or multiply Accountable Stewardship. This removes the prior shared-stewardship tension without changing EOG-01.

The current Authority Model does not expressly state which Authority authenticates a creator-established Group creation event. EOG-02 now distinguishes creation from participation and evaluates Administrative, Identity and Participation Authority without inventing a new Authority. Administrative Authority is recommended for authentication only; the recommendation remains pending founder approval.

Council membership does not create Accountable Stewardship, Platform Authority, Administration or Custody. The singular Accountable Steward remains ultimately answerable. This treatment is fully consistent with EOG-01.

## 11. Charter Consistency Review

Treating the Group Charter as a Governed Subject is consistent with the constitutional baseline if:

1. the Charter remains distinct from the Group;
2. its contents retain their applicable information categories and Authorities;
3. it does not become a new Platform Authority;
4. presentation does not replace authoritative Charter meaning;
5. material changes remain attributable and historically intelligible;
6. binding effect is established by approved Policy rather than assumed from display or stewardship.

The recommended composite-content model satisfies these conditions. A mandatory Charter, amendment method, versioning method and binding-effect rule remain pending.

## 12. Moderation Consistency Review

The EOG-02 moderation analysis is constitutionally coherent because it treats moderation as the coordinated exercise of existing relationships:

- Policy Authority supplies the approved basis;
- Administrative Authority establishes an in-scope moderation decision;
- Participation Authority establishes any resulting membership relationship truth;
- Accountable Stewardship remains answerability for coherent governance;
- custody preserves applicable records;
- presentation communicates without redefining the decision.

No separate Platform Authority for moderation or default role power is created. The relationship map defines constitutional justification, not permissions.

## 13. Dormant Membership Review

The statement that inactivity is not misconduct by itself follows Truthful User Experience and People Before Engagement. It does not prevent later Policy from making a defined inactivity condition relevant to a legitimate Group purpose.

The refined documents also state that a Group may be inactive without being constitutionally unhealthy. Governance integrity and activity level remain distinct.

No inactivity threshold, notice, removal state, duration, appeal or workflow is approved.

**Result:** Pass.

## 14. Future Governance Concepts Review

Group Governance Health is explicitly identified as future governance only. Its possible considerations are non-governing and create no metric, score, determination or implementation.

Governance Maturity is explicitly conceptual only and distinct from Community Growth. No maturity model, level, assessment or lifecycle is approved.

**Result:** Pass.

## 16. Allocation Restraint Review

The documents make only the founder-established founding consequences:

- the creator becomes the initial Accountable Steward;
- the creator becomes the first Member.

They do not:

- identify an actual person;
- assign the Platform Authority authenticating Group creation;
- assign a later Accountable Steward or Stewardship Council;
- assign a Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter or Delegate;
- allocate Charter or moderation accountability;
- change an Entity Ownership Register row.

**Result:** Pass.
