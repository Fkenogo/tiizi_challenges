# EOG-01 — Governance Baseline Update Note

## 1. Purpose

This note records the governance effect of the founder approvals documented in the [EOG-01 Founder Approval Record](05-EOG-01-FOUNDER-APPROVAL-RECORD.md).

It does not edit or restate the constitutional baseline. It identifies the approved semantic guidance that future Entity Ownership Governance work must now apply.

## 2. What Changed

On 2026-07-19, the founder approved EOG-01-FW-01 through EOG-01-FW-08.

The following accountability semantics are now approved:

- the complete bounded accountability relationship set;
- Accountable Steward as the formal constitutional term;
- the distinction between Governed Subject and Information Subject;
- the separation of Delegation of Authority, Assignment of Responsibility and Stewardship Reassignment;
- explicit relationship stacking with separate purpose and scope declarations;
- Contributor and Participant as canonical non-Authority contextual relationships;
- purpose-limited custody that cannot arise from storage or possession alone;
- mandatory use of the approved vocabulary as semantic guidance in future Entity Ownership Governance work.

EOG-01 is formally closed.

## 3. What Did Not Change

The approval did not change:

- any accountability allocation;
- any Platform Authority or Authority boundary;
- Document 00 or Document 00A;
- the Platform Constitution;
- the Platform Principles;
- the Platform Authority Model;
- the Entity Ownership Foundation;
- any Domain Standard;
- any Entity Ownership Register row;
- any pending decision in EOG-02 through EOG-06;
- any lifecycle, permission, privacy, security or implementation rule.

## 5. Governance Effect

The EOG-01 vocabulary is now mandatory semantic guidance for later Entity Ownership Governance work. Later phases may propose or approve allocations only through their own declared decisions.

This update therefore changes the status of the EOG-01 relationship vocabulary from pending recommendation to founder-approved guidance. It does not convert a relationship definition into an allocation.
