## 2. Constitutional Boundaries Preserved

Any approval must preserve all four mandatory boundaries:

1. **Creation-only Administrative Authority.** Administrative Authority may authenticate the Group-creation event and its founding facts only. It does not become continuing Authority over Group identity, purpose, Membership, Charter truth or later Group change.
2. **Charter supplies rule, not Authority.** A Charter may supply an applicable succession or governance rule. It does not act, authenticate, approve or establish Stewardship Reassignment truth.
3. **Council responsibility is not shared stewardship.** A Stewardship Council may later receive expressly bounded Assignments of Responsibility. The Council never becomes a second Accountable Steward, and Council membership grants no relationship by implication.
4. **Inventory is not allocation.** Permanent inventory classification records constitutional identity only. It grants no Authority, Accountable Stewardship, responsibility, Custody, Administration, Operation or other EOG-01 relationship.

## 3. GFC-01 — Group Creation Authentication

### Approved baseline

A Group is created, not proposed. Creation establishes existence, initial identity and founding purpose. The creator becomes first Member and first Accountable Steward. Participation is voluntary and does not establish Group existence.

### Recommended model

**Recommended — pending founder approval: GFC-01-B.** Administrative Authority authenticates the attributable Group-creation event within a creation-only declared purpose and scope. The creator remains the Attributable originator. Identity and Policy supply their own governed contexts, while first Membership and initial Accountable Stewardship remain separate consequences.

### Principal alternative considered

**GFC-01-A:** Participation Authority establishes authoritative Group creation truth. It offers domain proximity but conflicts with the approved separation between Community existence and participation.

### Constitutional conditions accompanying approval

- Authentication is confined to the creation event and founding facts expressed through it.
- Administrative Authority acquires no continuing Group Authority.
- Creator origin is not Authority.
- First Membership and initial Accountable Stewardship are not administrative allocations.
- No Administrator title, interface or mechanism acquires Authority by implication.

## 4. GFC-02 — Stewardship Continuity

### Approved baseline

Every Group has exactly one Accountable Steward relationship at any given time. The creator initially holds it. Stewardship may be reassigned or voluntarily relinquished through approved governance, and the Group must be capable of continuity beyond its creator.

### Recommended model

**Recommended — pending founder approval: GFC-02-B.** Voluntary change uses successor-continuous Stewardship Reassignment. Incapacity, abandonment or failure may be addressed through an applicable Charter succession principle, while a separately declared existing Platform Authority establishes any authoritative reassignment truth.

### Principal alternatives considered

- **GFC-02-A:** successor-continuous reassignment only; constitutionally simple but incomplete for stewardship failure.
- **GFC-02-C:** governance-protected exceptional determination; resilient but creates greater central-intervention and oversight burden.

### Constitutional conditions accompanying approval

- Exactly one Accountable Steward relationship exists at every relevant time.
- Relinquishment does not become effective through vacancy by implication.
- Charter supplies a rule, not Authority or automatic transfer.
- Stewardship Reassignment remains distinct from Delegation of Authority and Assignment of Responsibility.
- Council membership, Administration and inactivity do not establish succession.
- The Authority for exceptional reassignment remains for later founder-approved governance.

## 5. GFC-03 — Group Charter Foundations

### Approved baseline

The Group Charter is a Governed Subject distinct from the Group, Platform governance, a role bundle and implementation configuration. Creation establishes founding purpose, while Charter binding effect, amendment and historical governance remain pending.

### Recommended model

**Recommended — pending founder approval: GFC-03-A.** A founding Charter becomes constitutionally applicable at Group creation and may evolve only through later attributable governance preserving historically intelligible meaning.

### Principal alternatives considered

- **GFC-03-B:** binding Charter adopted after creation; improves deliberative legitimacy but creates a pre-adoption applicability and continuity gap.
- **GFC-03-C:** layered Charter; flexible but creates the highest interpretive and historical burden.
- **GFC-03-D:** declarative Charter; simple but unable to supply binding succession or Member-governance meaning.

### Constitutional conditions accompanying approval

- Binding effect remains subordinate to Platform governance and applicable Policy.
- Charter supplies governed rules and commitments but does not exercise Authority.
- Creator authorship does not establish every Charter truth.
- Charter applicability grants no permission, technical enforcement or inferred consent.
- Later amendment must preserve attributable and historically intelligible prior meaning.
- No lifecycle, versioning mechanism or amendment procedure is approved here.

## 6. GFC-04 — Stewardship Council Treatment

### Approved baseline

A Stewardship Council is a governed body established to support the singular Accountable Steward relationship. It is not a Platform Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Administration or Custody and does not replace the Accountable Steward.

### Recommended model

**Recommended — pending founder approval: GFC-04-B.** The Council may later receive expressly bounded Assignments of Responsibility supporting stewardship, subject to separate governance for each assignment.

### Principal alternatives considered

- **GFC-04-A:** advisory-only Council; lowest leakage risk but limited constitutional utility.
- **GFC-04-C:** coordination-context Council with substantive relationships attached only to separately identified actors; strong attribution but weakens the Council body's durable supporting function.

### Constitutional conditions accompanying approval

- Council responsibility is not shared Accountable Stewardship.
- A Council assignment creates no Authority, Custody, Administration or relationship for a Council member.
- Every later responsibility requires declared purpose, scope, attribution and review boundary.
- The singular Accountable Steward remains ultimately answerable.
- Composition, appointment, decision-making and responsibility content remain deferred.
- If body-level responsibility cannot remain attributable, GFC-04-C remains the constitutional fallback for later founder review.
