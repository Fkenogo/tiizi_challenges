# EOG-03 Drafting Notes — Founder Review Draft 2

## 1. Purpose

These notes record only the constitutional observations that remain genuinely unresolved after the precision refinements applied in [EOG-03 Founder Review Draft 2](13-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-DRAFT-2.md).

They are not constitutional provisions. They do not resolve founder questions, amend approved governance or allocate accountability.

Resolved Draft 1 observations and Phase 3 findings are recorded in the [Draft 2 Change Log](13-EOG-03-DRAFT-2-CHANGE-LOG.md), not retained here as open questions.

## 2. Remaining Drafting Notes

### DN-02 — Layered Meaning terminology

**Observation:** Draft 2 preserves Platform Meaning, Policy Meaning and Community Meaning as distinct non-overriding layers. Historical Meaning is clarified as the historically applicable preservation of those layers rather than an independent fourth Authority or source input.

**Unresolved point:** Founder confirmation remains required that the three source-layer names and the temporal meaning of Historical Meaning express the intended philosophy.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm the Layered Meaning terminology and clarified historical relationship.

### DN-04 — Knowledge-provided logic

**Observation:** Founder clarification states that Platform Knowledge provides validation, scoring, Progress and Recognition logic. Approved governance assigns authoritative meaning to Knowledge Authority, behavior rules to Policy Authority and Derived Truth to Calculation Authority.

**Draft 2 treatment:** Knowledge supplies Authoritative Meaning used by applicable Policy and Calculation. It does not establish Policy, calculate outcomes or qualify Recognition.

**Unresolved point:** Founder confirmation remains required that “provides logic” refers to governed Knowledge and applicable Policy inputs, not a transfer of Policy or Calculation Authority to Knowledge Authority.

### DN-05 — Supporting Knowledge Asset types

**Observation:** Exercise Assets and Wellness Activity Assets are the primary Platform Knowledge Assets. Existing governance also recognises supporting Metric, Unit, Instructional, Safety, Relationship, Taxonomy and Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge meaning.

**Draft 2 treatment:** Primary status remains non-exclusive. Supporting Knowledge Assets may bear meaning established by Knowledge Authority without creating a final classification system.

**Unresolved point:** A later Knowledge classification standard must determine the final asset boundaries and classifications.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that “primary” permits supporting governed Knowledge under later standards.

### DN-07 — Trusted Contributor condition

**Observation:** The future-direction phrase uses Trusted Contributors, but no contributor eligibility, role or trust decision is approved.

**Draft 2 treatment:** “Trusted” remains a possible future governed condition and does not name a role, create Authority or allocate accountability.

**Unresolved point:** Eligibility, qualification, appointment, review and removal require later governance.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that the phrase may remain as future direction without implying a present contributor capability.

### DN-08 — Governed Review boundary

**Observation:** Future contribution requires review between attributable proposed material and canonical truth.

**Draft 2 treatment:** Governed Review may supply an approved basis to Knowledge Authority. Knowledge Authority alone establishes Authoritative Meaning. Review establishes no truth and creates no role, stage, workflow or allocation.

**Unresolved point:** Review actors, criteria, evidence and process remain deferred. The truth-establishing Authority is not deferred.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm this minimal boundary for future governed contribution.

### DN-09 — Historical Representation

**Observation:** Existing materials use “snapshot,” “version,” “copy” and “historical record” inconsistently. KNW-02 and KNW-03 remain pending.

**Draft 2 treatment:** Historical Representation remains a constitutional integrity concept. It may preserve the broader composition, not merely a Runtime Projection, without prescribing snapshot content, versions, retention, deletion or lifecycle.

**Unresolved point:** Future historical and lifecycle standards must determine the representation contract and preservation obligations.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm Historical Representation as the preferred constitutional term.
