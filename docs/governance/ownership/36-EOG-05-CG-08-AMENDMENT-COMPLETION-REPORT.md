## 3. Founder Refinements Implemented

The implementation recognizes that:

- Groups may develop governance progressively;
- Groups choose whether and when to establish governance bodies;
- a Group may establish one or more governance bodies;
- Groups may determine operational titles;
- exactly one Accountable Steward relationship remains constitutionally required;
- constitutional recognition is not operational prescription; and
- inventory remains distinct from allocation.

The absence of a governance body shall not, by itself, prevent a Group from being created, operating, accepting members or participating in Platform activities unless expressly provided by later approved governance.

## 4. Information Separation Preserved

The amended row separately treats:

- Group Information;
- Governance Body Information; and
- Relationship Information.

Relationship Information remains information about the separately governed relationship and its relevant subjects. It is not absorbed into Governance Body Information.
