**Status:** Phase 0 planning review; not an approved register or allocation instrument

## 1. Executive Verdict

**Verdict: Ready with prerequisites.**

The approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 corpus is sufficient to prepare a reconciled Entity Ownership Register draft that records approved treatment and preserves explicit gaps. It is not sufficient to approve a fully allocated register.

The principal prerequisites are:

- classify eight candidate-row questions without inventing new Governed Subjects;
- reconcile the existing 63-row draft against later approved EOG-02 through EOG-04 treatment;
- preserve CHL, ACT, GRP, KNW and RSK dependencies as unresolved;
- retain the full EOG-01 relationship vocabulary, including Attributable Originator; and
- control the historical EOG-05 identifier collision by using the full programme title.

No approved constitutional contradiction prevents drafting. No new Authority is required.

## 3. Review Method

### 3.1 Candidate identification

The 63 subjects already present in the draft register were retained as audit candidates. Later EOG governance was then reviewed for explicit Governed Subjects, governed information objects, functions, representations and composition inputs missing from that draft.

The resulting [Candidate Entity Inventory](21-EOG-05-CANDIDATE-ENTITY-INVENTORY.md) contains 72 candidates. Inclusion does not approve a permanent register row.

### 3.2 Relationship classification

Every candidate was assessed against:

- Governed Subject;
- Information Subject;
- Authority to Establish Truth;
- Accountable Steward;
- Custodian;
- Administrator;
- Operator;
- Contributor;
- Participant;
- Delegate;
- Presenter;
- Downstream User; and
- Attributable Originator.

Attributable Originator was included because the approved EOG-01 complete bounded relationship set makes it mandatory semantic guidance.

Each cell uses only Approved, Deferred, Undefined or Out of Scope. “Approved” records constitutional treatment, not a newly named actor. “Deferred” and “Undefined” remain unresolved.

## 6. Approved Treatment That Must Be Preserved

The future register must carry forward, without extension:

1. EOG-01's complete bounded relationship semantics and non-conflation rules.
2. Group creation establishes existence, identity, founding purpose, first Member and initial Accountable Steward.
3. Exactly one Accountable Steward relationship exists per Group at a time.
4. Group Charter is a distinct Governed Subject; its contents may involve more than one source Authority.
5. Stewardship Council is a supporting Governed Subject and never a replacement Accountable Steward, Platform Authority, Administrator or Custodian.
6. Knowledge Authority establishes Authoritative Meaning for Knowledge Assets.
7. Runtime Catalogue is a governed function within the Knowledge Authority boundary, not an Authority, actor or role.
8. Runtime Projection and Historical Representation are subordinate representations.
9. Challenge is a distinct Governed Subject and Information Subject within a persistent Group context.
10. Challenge itself is not an Authority, and the Authority establishing individual Challenge Identity, Purpose and Goal remains pending.
11. Participant Authority establishes Submission Intent; Acceptance Authority establishes Accepted Activity Events; Policy Authority establishes Evidence Eligibility; Calculation Authority establishes Derived Truth.
12. Presentation and downstream use do not transfer Authority or accountability.

## 7. Reconciliation Required Before Draft 1

The existing draft register predates approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 treatment. It remains useful evidence but cannot be adopted unchanged.

Material reconciliation points include:

- replacing the old Group “proposal” premise with approved creation consequences;
- recording the approved initial Group Accountable Steward and first Member consequence;
- adding Group Charter and Stewardship Council treatment;
- preserving EOG-03 Platform Knowledge, Template, Runtime and Historical Representation boundaries;
- preserving EOG-04 Challenge Governed Subject, Information Subject, Authority-chain and continuity treatment;
- revisiting the draft statement that all 63 Accountable Steward relationships remain pending;
- separating Historical Knowledge reference from Historical Representation;
- determining whether Exercise and Wellness Activity Assets are rows or subtypes;
- distinguishing entity rows from governed functions and mechanisms; and
- retaining all unresolved CHL, ACT, GRP, KNW and RSK cells.

This Phase 0 review does not apply those changes to the draft register.

## 8. Dependencies and Blockers

The [Dependency Map](21-EOG-05-DEPENDENCY-MAP.md) identifies the effect of every GRP, CHL, ACT, KNW and RSK decision.

### Drafting blockers

No pending decision prevents creation of a transparent Draft 1 if every unresolved cell remains Deferred or Undefined.

### Final-approval blockers

A fully allocated register remains blocked by:

- Challenge truth and stewardship decisions, especially CHL-01;
- Verification and correction Authority decisions under ACT-03 and ACT-04;
- continuing Group stewardship, custody, administration and lifecycle governance;
- Knowledge classification, lifecycle, historical and runtime-detail decisions;
- Ranking and Streak Policy where specific derived and presentation accountability depends on it;
- additional identity, Recognition, social, notification, analytics, administration and operations governance outside the five required families; and
- purpose-limited custody allocation for every candidate.
