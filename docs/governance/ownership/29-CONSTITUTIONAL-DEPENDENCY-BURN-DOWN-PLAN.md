## 6. Burn-down Controls

A phase removes a blocker only after:

1. the detailed founder decision or constitutional standard is explicitly approved;
2. its status is updated through the normal approval process;
3. affected CG treatment is separately recorded where required;
4. EOG-05 impact is assessed without silent amendment; and
5. the allocation-readiness matrix is revalidated.

Preparation, recommendation or founder review alone does not burn down a dependency.

## 7. Burn-down Result

The shortest constitutionally safe path to Milestone A is G1 → G2 → CK1 → HK1 → ACT1 → MOT1. RSK1 follows because it influences final allocation completeness but does not need to delay the safe-start decision once core truth, classification and Recognition blockers are resolved.
