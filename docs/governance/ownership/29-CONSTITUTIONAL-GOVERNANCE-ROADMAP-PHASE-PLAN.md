# Post-EOG-05 — Constitutional Governance Roadmap Phase Plan

**Status:** Recommended programme plan; pending Governance Authority approval

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This plan orders the next constitutional governance work following approval of the [EOG-05 Entity Ownership Register](27-EOG-05-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER-APPROVED.md). It applies the [Dependency Matrix](28-ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP-ALLOCATION-DEPENDENCY-MATRIX.md) and [Recommended Governance Sequence](28-ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP-ALLOCATION-GOVERNANCE-SEQUENCE.md).

It does not resolve a decision, change a decision status, amend an approved EOG standard or authorize the Entity Relationship Allocation Register.

## 2. Programme Objective

The programme should mature constitutional dependencies in the order that minimizes rework and reaches two separate outcomes:

1. the earliest safe milestone for allocation-register Draft 1 authorization; and
2. the later maturity required for final approval of a complete allocation baseline.

The ordering rule is:

```text
Community governance
        ↓
Challenge and Knowledge identity
        ↓
Lifecycle and historical integrity
        ↓
Activity truth integrity
        ↓
Recognition boundary
        ↓
Ranking and Streak detail
```

## 3. Recommended Phases

### Phase G1 — Group constitutional continuity

**Scope**

- Platform Authority authenticating Group creation;
- GRP-02 stewardship reassignment, relinquishment and continuity;
- Group Charter constitutional governance;
- Stewardship Council constitutional treatment; and
- CG-08 permanent inventory classification.

**Why first**

Group is the persistent constitutional Community and the context for every Version 2 Challenge. Challenge accountability cannot be made durable while Group existence and continuing stewardship remain unresolved.

**Explicit exclusions**

Membership lifecycle, moderation workflow, permissions, technical roles and implementation.

**Exit gate**

Group creation and continuing Accountable Stewardship can be described using EOG-01 relationships without relying on role titles, and CG-08 has attributable classification treatment.

### Phase G2 — Group roles, membership and administration boundaries

**Scope**

- GRP-01 canonical role vocabulary and authority boundaries;
- GRP-03 Group and membership lifecycle principles;
- GRP-04 invitation, removal and appeal principles; and
- the constitutional separation of Administration, Delegation, Membership and Stewardship.

**Why second**

Role vocabulary must follow the foundational stewardship model. Membership boundaries should mature before Challenge participation and Challenge lifecycle governance are finalized.

**Explicit exclusions**

Permission matrices, moderation workflow, interfaces and enforcement design.

**Exit gate**

Group role names cannot imply EOG-01 relationships, and later Challenge governance can rely on stable membership and administrative boundaries.

### Phase CK1 — Challenge core and Knowledge classification

**Scope**

- CHL-01 Challenge identity, purpose, Goal, constitutional authority and stewardship;
- KNW-04 Discovery, taxonomy, controlled-vocabulary and Runtime Projection boundaries;
- CG-01, CG-02, CG-04, CG-06 and CG-07; and
- the reciprocal boundary among Challenge, Group configuration, Community context, Template, Creation Mechanism and Platform Knowledge.

**Why combined**

CHL-01 and KNW-04 meet at Goal, taxonomy and Constitutional Composition. Resolving them separately risks treating Group context as Knowledge or Knowledge as Challenge truth.

**Explicit exclusions**

Challenge lifecycle, scoring, Knowledge lifecycle, creation workflow and implementation.

**Exit gate**

Core Challenge truth and stewardship have approved constitutional treatment, and the five associated inventory gates have attributable dispositions.

### Phase HK1 — Historical and lifecycle coherence

**Scope**

- CHL-02 Challenge lifecycle;
- CHL-03 amendment and historical integrity;
- CHL-04 withdrawal, late activity, completion and reopening boundaries;
- KNW-02 versioning and historical representation;
- KNW-03 deprecation, deletion and historical reference; and
- CG-03 and CG-05.

**Why after core identity**

Lifecycle and history must preserve an already-defined Challenge identity and approved Knowledge meaning. They cannot supply those foundations themselves.

**Explicit exclusions**

State-machine implementation, storage, migration, retention periods and interface behavior.

**Exit gate**

Challenge and Knowledge historical treatment can support custody, administration and downstream-use analysis without silent reinterpretation, and all CG gates are closed.

### Phase ACT1 — Activity integrity

**Scope**

- ACT-04 Verification meaning, Authority and downstream effects;
- ACT-03 correction, duplicate, deletion and historical-effect boundaries; and
- preservation of the complete Submission Intent, acceptance, Evidence Eligibility and Calculation separation.

**Internal order**

Review Verification meaning before correction effects, while retaining the authoritative decision identifiers ACT-03 and ACT-04. The two decisions should be approved as one coordinated integrity package.

**Why here**

Activity integrity depends on stable Challenge context and historical boundaries. Ranking, Streak and Recognition must consume governed truth rather than define it retroactively.

**Explicit exclusions**

Workflow, fraud algorithms, technical validation, storage and implementation controls.

**Exit gate**

Verification and correction can receive distinct constitutional allocation treatment without merging Acceptance, Policy or Calculation Authority.

### Phase MOT1 — Motivation and Recognition boundary

**Scope**

- MOT-01 constitutional distinction among daily goals, milestones, Streak, Recognition and Reward;
- qualifying source-truth boundary for Recognition;
- relationship between Recognition and Presentation Authority; and
- final inventory treatment of DRV-09 Recognition.

**Why before detailed RSK governance**

The allocation safe-start milestone requires Recognition to cease being Provisional. MOT-01 can define the constitutional qualification boundary without deciding Ranking formulas, tie rules or Streak calculations.

**Explicit exclusions**

Reward system design, formulas, visual presentation and implementation.

**Exit gate**

Recognition has attributable constitutional treatment without creating evidence or replacing Calculation Authority.

### Milestone A — Allocation Draft 1 readiness review

After ACT1 and MOT1, assess the complete [Classification Closure and Core Allocation Authority Milestone](28-ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP-ALLOCATION-READINESS-DECISION.md#3-earliest-safe-milestone).

If every start-critical condition passes, Governance Authority may consider a separate drafting-authorization package. This roadmap does not grant that authorization.

### Phase RSK1 — Ranking, scoring and Streak governance

**Scope and order**

1. RSK-01 authoritative Ranking source and Metric;
2. RSK-02 normalization and terminology;
3. RSK-03 tie, fairness and finalization; and
4. RSK-04 Streak qualification, time, grace and correction.

**Why after ACT1 and MOT1**

RSK governance should refine Calculation and Presentation treatment only after activity integrity is fixed and Recognition is constitutionally distinct. It must not establish evidence or Recognition by implication.

**Exit gate**

Derived, finalization, Administration, Presentation and downstream-use allocation influences are sufficiently mature for final allocation reconciliation.

### Milestone B — Allocation final-approval readiness

After RSK1, reconcile remaining row-relevant GRP, CHL, KNW, NTF, ANL, OPS, identity, social, roles and permissions, privacy and security dependencies identified in the [Readiness Assessment](28-ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP-ALLOCATION-READINESS-ASSESSMENT.md#6-completion-blockers-and-influences).

Only an independently reviewed allocation register should then proceed to founder approval.

## 4. Parallel Work Boundaries

The following preparation may run in parallel without resolving governance:

- evidence collection for GRP and CHL options;
- KNW corpus traceability for KNW-02 through KNW-04;
- disputed-event scenarios for ACT-03 and ACT-04;
- Ranking, tie and Streak examples for later RSK review; and
- recognition examples that distinguish truthful acknowledgement from Reward.

Decision approval must follow the dependency order even when research runs concurrently.

## 5. Programme Recommendation

Begin with **Phase G1 — Group Constitutional Continuity**. It clears the earliest unresolved constitutional foundation, supplies prerequisites for CHL-01 and removes the direct dependency behind CG-08 without reopening EOG-02.
