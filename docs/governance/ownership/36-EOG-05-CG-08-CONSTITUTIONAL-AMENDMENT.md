# EOG-05 CG-08 — Constitutional Amendment

**Status:** Approved and applied

**Approval date:** 2026-07-21

**Constitutional capacity:** Founder

**Approval reference:**

**Amendment target:** [EOG-05 — Entity Ownership Register](27-EOG-05-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER-APPROVED.md)

## 1. Purpose

This attributable amendment resolves CG-08 by establishing permanent constitutional inventory treatment for C-GRP-08 Group Governance Body. It implements GFC-05-A and the founder-approved refinements recorded in the [Founder Approval Package](36-EOG-05-CG-08-FOUNDER-APPROVAL-PACKAGE.md).

This amendment is constitutional inventory governance only. It allocates no EOG-01 relationship and introduces no operational governance, product behaviour or implementation.

## 2. Founder-approved Constitutional Direction

The amendment establishes that:

1. Groups may develop governance progressively as their needs evolve;
2. each Group chooses whether and when to establish governance bodies;
3. a Group may establish one or more governance bodies;
4. Groups may determine the operational titles of their governance bodies;
5. Stewardship Council remains an approved constitutional alias and example, not a mandatory name;
6. the singular Accountable Steward relationship remains unchanged;
7. inventory remains distinct from allocation; and
8. permanent recognition is constitutional recognition only.

The absence of a governance body shall not, by itself, prevent a Group from being created, operating, accepting members or participating in Platform activities unless expressly provided by later approved governance.

## 4. Information Subject Separation

The amendment preserves three distinct information treatments:

- **Group Information** concerns the Group and does not become Governance Body Information merely because a body acts in Group context.
- **Governance Body Information** concerns a particular governance body where established.
- **Relationship Information** concerns the separately governed relationship and its relevant subjects; it does not become Governance Body Information merely because the body participates in that relationship.

Inventory recognition does not create a relationship entity, relationship holder or allocation.

## 6. Explicit Non-effects

This amendment does not:

- allocate any EOG-01 relationship;
- prescribe governance composition;
- prescribe appointments;
- prescribe elections;
- prescribe governance procedures;
- prescribe governance Authority;
- prescribe governance tenure;
- require Groups to establish governance bodies;
- limit Groups to a single governance body;
- create any Group Health or Group Governance Health framework;
- create governance incentives;
- make governance a prerequisite for participation;
- make a Governance Body an Accountable Steward, Platform Authority, Administrator or Custodian;
- make Governance Body responsibility shared stewardship;
- amend another EOG-05 entity row; or
- introduce lifecycle or implementation governance.

## 7. Deferred Governance Preserved

The following remain deferred:

- governance-body composition;
- appointment and election;
- procedures and decision-making;
- responsibilities and relationship allocations;
- delegation;
- governance tenure and lifecycle;
- operational titles;
- roles and permissions;
- Group Governance Health;
- governance incentives; and
- technical implementation.
