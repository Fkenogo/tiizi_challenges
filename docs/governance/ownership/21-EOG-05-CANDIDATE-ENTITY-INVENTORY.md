# EOG-05 Phase 0 — Candidate Entity Inventory

**Programme:** EOG-05 — Entity Ownership Register Readiness and Scope Review

**Status:** Planning inventory; not an approved register or accountability allocation

**Date:** 2026-07-20

## 1. Purpose

This inventory identifies candidate Governed Subjects for a future reconciled Entity Ownership Register. It compares the 63 subjects in the existing [Phase E1 draft register](01-TIIZI-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER.md) with the later approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 corpus.

Inclusion records a subject for drafting review. It does not approve a new subject classification, relationship allocation, lifecycle, role or implementation structure.

## 3. Disposition Terms

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Retain and reconcile | Existing draft-register subject remains a candidate, but its row must later be reconciled against EOG-01 through EOG-04. |
| Add candidate | Later approved governance expressly defines a distinct Governed Subject or governed information object absent from the draft register. |
| Classification decision required | Approved governance defines the concept, but does not yet establish whether it needs an independent register row or belongs under an existing subject. |
| Exclude as non-entity | The concept is a doctrine, aggregate, relationship label, fact, condition or future idea rather than an independent register subject. |

## 5. Concepts Excluded from Independent Rows

| Concept | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Platform Knowledge | Exclude as aggregate | It is the governed body of authoritative reusable meaning expressed through Knowledge Assets, not an additional actor or automatic register row. |
| Governed Knowledge | Exclude as constitutional condition | EOG-03 expressly defines it as a condition, not an Authority or competing asset class. |
| Authoritative Meaning | Exclude as authoritative fact | It is truth established for a Knowledge Asset, not a separate accountable entity. |
| Constitutional Composition | Exclude as doctrine | It governs how distinct inputs form a distinct experience; it is not an entity. |
| Group Experience | Exclude as domain boundary | The phrase identifies the Group-owned experience boundary and allocates no EOG-01 relationship. |
| Applicable Policy | Consolidate with Policy | “Applicable” describes scope in context; it does not create a second Policy entity. |
| Governed Review | Exclude as governance requirement | It is a required condition before Knowledge Authority may establish meaning, not a role, stage or independent Authority. |
| Undertaking | Consolidate with Challenge for Version 2 | Undertaking is the ontological concept for which the Group Challenge is the governed product expression; EOG-04 forbids a second Challenge entity. |
| Challenge Identity | Consolidate with Challenge | It is an authoritative account concerning the Challenge, not a second Governed Subject. |
| Challenge Information | Exclude as information grouping | It identifies information whose primary Information Subject is the Challenge. |
| Challenge Integrity | Exclude as constitutional condition | It is not a score, state, entity or operational status. |
| Challenge Identity Continuity | Exclude as interpretive principle | It is a bounded principle, not an entity or lifecycle doctrine. |
| Group Governance Health | Exclude as future non-governing concept | EOG-02 explicitly creates no standard, metric or determination. |
| Governance Maturity | Exclude as future conceptual distinction | EOG-02 explicitly approves no maturity model or governed entity. |

## 6. Inventory Result

- Existing draft-register subjects reviewed: **63**.
- Additional candidates introduced or materially clarified by approved EOG-02 through EOG-04: **9**.
- Total candidates requiring relationship-coverage assessment: **72**.
- Concepts explicitly excluded or consolidated as non-independent rows: **14**.

The 72 candidates are an audit universe, not an approved final register scope. Four new candidates—Group Configuration, Community Context, Exercise Asset/Wellness Activity Asset specialization and Creation Mechanism—still require a classification decision before an EOG-05 Draft 1 register can assign permanent row treatment.
