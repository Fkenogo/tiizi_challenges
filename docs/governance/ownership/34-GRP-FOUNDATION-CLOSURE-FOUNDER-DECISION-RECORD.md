## 2. Mandatory Package Boundaries

The following boundaries apply to every approval:

- creation-only Administrative Authority;
- Charter supplies rule, not Authority;
- Council responsibility is not shared stewardship; and
- inventory is not allocation.

No selection in this record may silently remove these boundaries. Any proposed removal requires separate constitutional review.

## 3. GFC-01 Decision

**Approved baseline:** Groups are created; creation establishes founding facts; participation does not establish existence; creator becomes first Member and first Accountable Steward.

**Recommended model:** GFC-01-B — creation-only Administrative Authority authentication.

**Principal alternative:** GFC-01-A — Participation Authority establishment of creation truth.

**Conditions:** authentication is creation-only; no continuing Group Authority; creator origin remains distinct; Membership and Stewardship remain separate; no role or mechanism implies Authority.

- **Founder decision:** Approve GFC-01-B

- **Founder Note:** Administrative Authority refers to the Platform’s constitutional Administrative Authority rather than a specific operational role or individual. The Authority may be exercised through whatever administrative actor or governed mechanism the Platform later authorizes. Its constitutional scope remains limited to authenticating the Group-creation event and its founding facts.

## 6. GFC-04 Decision

**Approved baseline:** Council supports but never replaces the singular Accountable Steward and is not Authority, Administration or Custody.

**Recommended model:** GFC-04-B — Council capable of later bounded Assignments of Responsibility.

**Principal alternatives:** GFC-04-A — advisory only; GFC-04-C — coordination context with separately declared actor relationships.

**Conditions:** Council responsibility is not shared stewardship; no relationship arises from Council membership; later assignments require purpose, scope, attribution and review; singular steward remains answerable; Council operations remain deferred.

- **Founder decision:** Approve GFC-04-B
- [x] Approve
- [ ] Reject
- [ ] Amend
- **Founder Rationale:** Tiizi Groups are intended to remain voluntary, self-regulating communities built on mutual respect, shared purpose and accountability to one another. Governance should remain intentionally lightweight. The Stewardship Council exists only to support the singular Accountable Steward where appropriate and must never introduce unnecessary governance layers or reduce the voluntary nature of Group participation.
- **Approval date:** 2026-07-21

## 7. GFC-05 Decision

**Approved baseline:** Council is an approved supporting Governed Subject; C-GRP-08 remains Provisional under CG-08.

**Recommended model:** GFC-05-A — permanent independent C-GRP-08 inventory treatment through a later attributable EOG-05 amendment.

**Principal alternatives:** GFC-05-B — consolidation under Group; GFC-05-C — continued Provisional treatment.

**Conditions:** inventory is not allocation; no EOG-01 relationship is granted; Information Subject boundaries remain explicit; no composition, lifecycle or responsibility is decided; CG-08 remains unresolved until a separately approved amendment.

- **Founder decision:** Approve GFC-05-A
- [x] Approve
- [ ] Reject
- [ ] Amend
- **Founder Rationale:** Permanent constitutional recognition of the Stewardship Council exists solely to preserve constitutional consistency whenever a Council is established. It does not imply that every Group must establish, maintain or continuously operate a Stewardship Council throughout its lifecycle.
- **Approval date:** 2026-07-21
