# Tiizi Entity Ownership Register

## 1. Purpose

This register identifies the constitutional accountability relationships that apply to governed Tiizi subjects. It records what is governed, whose information is concerned, where information originates, which approved Platform Authority may establish authoritative truth, and which stewardship, custody, administration, operation, presentation and downstream-use allocations are approved or remain pending.

The register does not treat possession, access, visibility, authorship, administration or presentation as Authority to establish truth. It does not create lifecycle states, roles, permissions, privacy rules, security controls or implementation structures.

## 2. Scope

The register covers the five foundational domains—Profile, Group, Challenge, Activity Event and Knowledge Asset—and the cross-platform control, derived and presentation subjects required by the [Tiizi Foundational Governance Baseline v1.0](../platform/01-TIIZI-PLATFORM-CONSTITUTION.md).

It registers 63 governed subjects. It does not add an unapproved Domain Standard or treat a future capability as Version 2 launch scope. Fitness and Wellness remain the launch Knowledge domains, Group Challenges remain the active product model, and individual challenges remain excluded.

## 4. Interpretation Rules

1. **One primary category.** Each row has one primary constitutional information category. References to information governed elsewhere do not change that category.
2. **One truth authority per fact.** A row may name more than one Platform Authority only where it explicitly separates different facts. It never creates competing authority for the same fact.
3. **Approved authorities only.** Governance, Identity, Participation, Participant, Knowledge, Policy, Acceptance, Calculation, Administrative, Operational and Presentation Authority are the only Platform Authority types used.
4. **Runtime Catalogue authority is a function, not a twelfth type.** It is the approved single catalogue-availability function operating under the Knowledge authority boundary established by KNW-01. It establishes runtime availability, not canonical Knowledge meaning.
5. **Pending is explicit.** “Pending founder decision,” “Pending later lifecycle standard,” and “Pending roles and permissions standard” identify an unresolved allocation and its governing dependency.
6. **Accountable stewardship is not Authority to establish truth.** A Platform Authority may establish truth without being the eventual Accountable steward.
7. **Custody preserves; it does not establish.** A Custodian may preserve information only within an approved purpose and does not gain source authority.
8. **Administration is scoped.** An Administrator acts only through explicitly assigned Administrative Authority. The role vocabulary and scope remain pending where stated.
9. **Operation supports continuity.** An Operator acts only through explicitly assigned Operational Authority and does not establish product truth.
10. **Presentation remains subordinate.** A Presenter communicates approved information through Presentation Authority; the underlying authority remains unchanged.
11. **Downstream use does not transfer authority.** A Downstream user may use approved information only for its declared purpose.
12. **No shorthand allocation.** The terms Governed subject, Information subject, Attributable originator, Authority to establish truth, Accountable steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter and Downstream user retain their normalized constitutional meanings.

## 5. Register Columns and Definitions

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Register ID | Stable identifier for this register row; it is not a runtime identifier. |
| Governed subject | The distinct subject whose constitutional accountability relationships are recorded. |
| Domain | The approved foundational domain or cross-platform context governing the subject. |
| Primary constitutional information category | The single primary category from the Platform Data and Information Standard. |
| Information subject | The person, relationship, undertaking, event, Knowledge item or control record that the information concerns. |
| Attributable originator | The attributable source of an expression or source material; origin does not itself establish authoritative truth. |
| Authority to establish truth | The approved Platform Authority for the fact, or an explicit pending allocation. |
| Accountable steward | The answerable steward, or the specific decision or standard still required to allocate stewardship. |
| Custodian | The party permitted to preserve the information, or the later standard required to allocate custody. |
| Administrator | The scoped administrative relationship; it never implies unrestricted authority. |
| Operator | The scoped operational relationship supporting continuity. |
| Presenter | The relationship permitted to communicate approved information without redefining truth. |
| Downstream user | Capabilities or governed subjects permitted to use the information for a declared purpose. |
| Visibility dependency | The visibility or privacy governance required before audience access is final. |
| Lifecycle dependency | The founder decision or later standard needed for state, retention, correction or history rules. |
| Decision status | Whether the constitutional boundary is approved and which allocations remain pending. |
| Governing source | Source keys from section 3. |
| Notes and boundaries | Critical distinction or non-authority rule for the row. |

## 8. Authority Separation Checks

1. Participant Authority ends at Submission Intent and participant-authored expressions.
2. Acceptance Authority alone establishes Accepted Activity Events.
3. Policy Authority establishes Evidence Eligibility for each declared calculation.
4. Calculation Authority establishes Progress, Completion, Ranking and approved Derived Truth.
5. Knowledge Authority establishes canonical Knowledge meaning.
6. Runtime Catalogue authority establishes availability only and is not a twelfth Platform Authority type.
7. Identity Authority establishes governed Identity and Profile context, not participation or activity evidence.
8. Participation Authority establishes governed relationship context under later lifecycle rules, not Accepted Activity Events or Derived Truth.
9. Administrative Authority receives no implicit Acceptance, Calculation, Knowledge or unrestricted Identity Authority.
10. Operational Authority supports continuity and reconciliation without establishing product truth.
11. Presentation Authority communicates without redefining source truth.
12. Governance Authority approves constitutional boundaries and does not establish individual Group or Challenge purpose.

## 9. Stewardship and Custody Gaps

The baseline intentionally defines authority boundaries before allocating accountable actors. Consequently:

- all 63 subjects retain an explicit pending Accountable steward allocation;
- all 63 subjects retain an explicit pending Custodian allocation or purpose-specific custody standard;
- eight subjects retain a pending Authority to establish truth allocation: Group, Group purpose, Challenge, Challenge purpose, Goal, Verification, Correction reference and Recognition;
- the remaining subjects either have an approved Authority to establish truth or are explicitly non-authoritative representations whose source authority is named;
- role names, organizational assignments and technical custodians are not inferred from current implementation.

These gaps do not invalidate the constitutional boundaries recorded here, but they prevent the register from being treated as a fully allocated final accountability instrument.

This register does not define lifecycle states or transitions. ## 12. Permission, Privacy and Security Dependencies
