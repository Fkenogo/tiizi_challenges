## 5. Founder Notes Preserved

The issued records preserve verbatim the founder's notes concerning:

- Administrative Authority as a constitutional Authority rather than a particular role or individual;
- voluntary, lightweight and self-regulating Group governance; and
- permanent Council recognition without requiring every Group to establish or continuously operate a Council.

## 6. Closure Effect

GRP Foundation Closure is complete as a founder-decision programme. Its completion:

- closes GFC-01 through GFC-05 as approved decisions;
- preserves every attached constitutional condition; and
- leaves all listed deferred governance unresolved.

## 7. Deferred Governance

The following remain deferred:

- the actor, role or mechanism exercising creation authentication;
- Authority and governance for exceptional Stewardship Reassignment;
- stewardship procedures, disputes, failure and review;
- Charter content, interpretation, amendment, history, versioning and lifecycle;
- Council composition, appointment, decisions, responsibilities and relationship allocations;
- relationship allocations generally;
- membership, moderation, roles and permissions;
- the EOG-05 amendment required to change C-GRP-08 classification;
- CG-08 resolution;
- the Entity Relationship Allocation Register; and
- implementation, privacy and security governance.

## 8. Recommended Next Governance Stream

The recommended next governance stream is a separately authorized EOG-05 amendment review for CG-08. The EOG-05 amendment and CG-08 resolution remain deferred and are not begun in this task.

## 9. Completion Statement

GRP Foundation Closure is constitutionally complete on 2026-07-21. EOG-05 itself remains unchanged, C-GRP-08 remains Provisional, CG-08 remains unresolved and no relationship has been allocated.
