# Entity Ownership Resolution Plan

## 1. Purpose

This plan identifies the controlled governance work that would follow explicit founder approval of EOG-01 through EOG-06. It does not implement a recommendation, amend the [Entity Ownership Register](01-TIIZI-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER.md), or change any constitutional document.

All references to amendments are conditional: **if the corresponding founder decision is approved**, the listed documents should be updated in a later controlled reconciliation phase.

## 2. Resolution Principles

1. Record the founder decision before amending governance.
2. Preserve the original option analysis and decision trace.
3. Amend higher-order terminology and authority boundaries before dependent Domain Standards.
4. Update register rows only after the governing amendment is internally consistent.
5. Re-run domain, cross-domain and register validation after each decision group.
6. Do not introduce lifecycle, permission, privacy, security or implementation detail while resolving constitutional allocations.
7. Maintain one Authority to establish each fact and one Accountable steward per Governed subject.
8. Treat technical systems only as later execution mechanisms or technical custodians where separately governed.

## 4. EOG-01 Resolution Detail

### 4.1 Register Amendments After Approval

For each of the 63 rows:

- replace the pending Accountable steward with one durable organizational role or explicitly governed stewardship capability;
- replace the pending Custodian with one or more purpose-limited custodial roles where preservation genuinely differs;
- preserve the existing Authority to establish truth unless another approved EOG decision changes it;
- preserve Administrator, Operator, Presenter and Downstream user as separate columns;
- record any exception to the approved default framework explicitly.

### 4.2 Validation Amendments

- recalculate fully allocated subjects;
- recalculate pending stewardship and custody totals;
- verify one steward per row;
- verify no Custodian is presented as source Authority;
- verify capability-level custody does not create cross-domain stewardship;
- verify all exceptions identify their governing rationale.

### 4.3 Governance Work Unlocked

- Entity Accountability and Custody Standard;
- domain lifecycle stewardship sections;
- operational custody and audit-custody mapping;
- role responsibility design without technical permission assignment.

## 5. EOG-02 Resolution Detail

### 5.1 Register Amendments After Approval

- GRP-01: replace pending Group truth Authority and stewardship;
- GRP-04: replace pending Group-purpose truth Authority and stewardship;
- GRP-02 and GRP-03: align relationship stewardship without making Member status stewardship;
- GRP-06: align Group administration with the approved steward boundary.

### 5.2 Domain Reconciliation

The Group Domain Standard would require a refinement identifying:

- the approved Authority establishing individual Group identity;
- the approved Authority establishing Group purpose;
- the one-steward constitutional pattern;
- creator origin as distinct from stewardship;
- administrators as delegated and scope-limited;
- transfer and departure as deferred lifecycle matters.

No invitation, joining, leaving, transfer or archive transition should be added in this reconciliation.

### 5.3 Governance Work Unlocked

- Group Lifecycle Standard drafting;
- GRP-01/02/03/04 decision reconciliation;
- Group Roles and Permissions analysis;
- Challenge stewardship relationship decision.

## 11. Controlled Amendment Order

1. Record founder approval or modification for EOG-01 through EOG-06.
2. Update the Consolidated Founder Decision Register and session report.
3. Refine canonical terminology and glossary.
4. Refine the Platform Authority Model where an approved decision clarifies scope.
5. Refine the Entity Ownership Foundation with the approved allocation framework.
6. Reconcile Group, Challenge and Activity Event Domain Standards.
7. Reconcile Knowledge and Profile stewardship references only where EOG-01 requires it.
8. Update the Entity Ownership Register rows and counts.
9. Update validation and decision-gap reports.
10. Run full constitutional cross-validation before beginning lifecycle or role standards.

## 12. Reconciliation Validation Gate

After founder approval and later amendment, validation must confirm:

- every approved decision is recorded once;
- unapproved recommendations remain pending;
- every affected register row matches the approved decision;
- every row has one Accountable steward;
- every authoritative fact has one approved Authority;
- custody does not create stewardship or Authority;
- no new Authority type appears;
- Group and Challenge stewardship remain distinct unless explicitly approved otherwise;
- Verification remains distinct from acceptance and Evidence Eligibility;
- correction preserves source authority and historical attribution;
- Recognition remains distinct from Reward and presentation;
- Runtime Catalogue and Runtime Projection boundaries remain unchanged;
- all relative links resolve;
- no lifecycle, permission, privacy, security or implementation rule is introduced during reconciliation.
