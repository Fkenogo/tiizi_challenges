# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 1 — Constitutional Risk Assessment

**Status:** Risk analysis; no option ranked or recommended

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This assessment identifies constitutional risks across the GFC-01 through GFC-05 alternatives. Severity evaluates potential constitutional harm, not option preference.

## 3. Cross-cutting Risk Themes

### Authority leakage

Creation, Charter applicability and stewardship reassignment each require one declared Authority-to-establish-truth boundary. No option may infer that boundary from origin, Policy, Administration, Council or a mechanism.

### Accountability dilution

Council support and Charter participation must not distribute the singular Accountable Steward relationship. Collaboration can expand; ultimate answerability cannot become implicit or plural.

### Inventory-driven doctrine

CG-08 treatment must follow Council meaning. A desire for a convenient Entity ID is not constitutional evidence for independent entity admission.

### Historical ambiguity

Founding, adopted and layered Charter models all require historically intelligible applicability. None authorizes snapshots, versions or retention mechanisms in this phase.
