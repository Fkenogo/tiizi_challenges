# EOG-05 Phase 2 — Inventory Register Drafting Authorization Report

**Status:** Draft 1 authorized; drafting not begun

**Authorization date:** 2026-07-20

## 1. Purpose

This report defines the constitutional boundary for drafting the Entity Ownership Register following founder approval of Option B in the [Architecture Approval Record](23-EOG-05-ARCHITECTURE-APPROVAL-RECORD.md).

It authorizes a future Draft 1. It does not contain that draft, approve a candidate classification, or authorize relationship allocation.

## 2. Authorized Drafting Objective

Draft 1 may create a constitutional inventory that identifies and classifies Tiizi entities without determining who or what holds accountability relationships to them.

The draft may reconcile the [72-candidate audit universe](21-EOG-05-CANDIDATE-ENTITY-INVENTORY.md) against approved governance. Candidate inclusion in the audit universe is evidence for review, not automatic approval as an inventory entity.

## 3. Authorized Scope

Draft 1 is authorized to include:

1. purpose, status and constitutional precedence;
2. interpretation rules distinguishing entity identity from relationship allocation;
3. stable or explicitly provisional Entity IDs;
4. canonical entity names and approved aliases;
5. domain classification;
6. Governed Subject classification;
7. Information Subject treatment distinct from Governed Subject treatment;
8. constitutional definition and principal boundary;
9. one primary constitutional information category per entity;
10. governing-source and decision traceability;
11. explicit distinction among approved, provisional, consolidated, excluded and deferred classification treatment;
12. the eight unresolved classification gates as visible scope controls;
13. a record of concepts consolidated into another entity or excluded from independent entity treatment;
14. cross-domain entity-boundary checks;
15. inventory integrity rules derived from the approved cross-register contract;
16. classification dependencies on GRP, CHL, ACT, KNW and RSK governance; and
17. validation of completeness, uniqueness, links, terminology and source traceability.

## 4. Prohibited Scope

Draft 1 must not:

- contain an allocation register or relationship-allocation matrix;
- allocate or infer any EOG-01 relationship;
- resolve CG-01 through CG-08;
- convert all 72 audit candidates into approved entity rows by default;
- treat a constitutional fact, function, capability, mechanism, condition, relationship or representation as an entity merely to make the inventory complete;
- create or redefine a Platform Authority;
- infer accountability from a role, access, possession, storage, implementation mechanism or presentation capability;
- resolve GRP, CHL, ACT, KNW or RSK decisions;
- define lifecycle, role, permission, privacy, security, formula, scoring or implementation governance;
- reconcile historical EOG identifiers;
- amend EOG-01 through EOG-04; or
- modify the existing Phase E1 draft register as though it were an approved source.

## 5. Drafting Controls

Every proposed entity entry must pass the following controls:

| Control | Drafting requirement |
|---|---|
| Source control | Cite approved constitutional governance or mark the proposed classification as provisional. |
| Subject control | State separately whether the entry is a Governed Subject and how Information Subject treatment applies. |
| Identity control | Define the entity without naming a relationship holder or implying accountability. |
| Category control | Assign exactly one primary constitutional information category where an entity is admitted. |
| Boundary control | Distinguish the entity from adjacent facts, functions, mechanisms, relationships and representations. |
| Authority control | Do not describe a Governed Subject as exercising Authority. Negative Authority boundaries may be stated where approved. |
| Allocation control | Do not include Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Contributor, Participant, Delegate, Presenter, Downstream User or Attributable Originator allocations. |
| Classification-gate control | Preserve CG-01 through CG-08 as unresolved unless later founder governance expressly resolves them. |
| Duplication control | Prevent aliases, subtypes and representations from becoming competing entities or sources of truth. |
| Traceability control | Identify the constitutional source and any blocking decision for every provisional treatment. |
| Cross-register control | Use Entity IDs suitable for later reference without drafting the allocation instrument. |
| Status control | Distinguish structural completeness from constitutional approval and classification completeness. |

## 6. Classification-Gate Handling

Draft 1 must preserve the following gates without resolving them:

| Gate | Unresolved classification area | Required Draft 1 treatment |
|---|---|---|
| CG-01 | Group purpose, Challenge purpose, Goal and Target | Retain as provisional candidates or facts pending controlled classification. |
| CG-02 | Group Configuration and Community Context | Preserve as provisional composition inputs without implying Authority. |
| CG-03 | Runtime Catalogue | Preserve the distinction among governed function, availability fact and potential entity treatment. |
| CG-04 | Exercise Asset and Wellness Activity Asset | Preserve approved primary types without duplicating Activity Knowledge Asset treatment. |
| CG-05 | Historical Knowledge reference and Historical Representation | Preserve relationship-versus-representation distinction. |
| CG-06 | Template | Preserve as a governed composition guide candidate, not a Knowledge Asset or Challenge. |
| CG-07 | Creation Mechanism | Preserve the classification question without treating the mechanism as Authority. |
| CG-08 | Stewardship Council | Preserve its approved supporting Governed Subject status without inferring accountability relationships. |

Use of a provisional Entity ID is permitted for drafting traceability. It does not approve permanent row treatment.

## 7. Dependency Handling

| Dependency family | Drafting treatment |
|---|---|
| GRP | Use approved EOG-02 Group concepts; mark unresolved Group classification detail and later governance dependencies explicitly. |
| CHL | Use approved EOG-04 Challenge identity and boundaries; do not resolve Challenge purpose, Goal, Target or other CHL allocations. |
| ACT | Preserve Submission Intent, Acceptance Decision, Accepted Activity Event and Evidence Eligibility distinctions; leave Verification and correction classification or allocation dependencies explicit. |
| KNW | Use approved EOG-03 Knowledge, runtime and representation boundaries; do not resolve classification, lifecycle, versioning or contributor decisions. |
| RSK | Inventory only constitutionally grounded derived or presentation subjects; do not define formulas, Ranking, Streak or Recognition policy. |

A pending dependency must be named. It must not be represented by a blank, an unstated assumption or an inferred classification.

## 8. Cross-Register Contract Compliance

Draft 1 must implement the inventory side of the [approved cross-register constitutional contract](23-EOG-05-ARCHITECTURE-APPROVAL-RECORD.md#5-approved-cross-register-constitutional-contract):

- the inventory is authoritative only for entity identity and classification once approved;
- inventory wording cannot imply or allocate a relationship;
- Entity IDs must support unambiguous future allocation references;
- classification changes require future impact assessment of linked allocations;
- allocations cannot be imported from the Phase 0 matrix into the inventory; and
- separate approval and amendment traces must remain possible.

## 9. Draft 1 Completion Gates

Draft 1 may be presented for constitutional review only when:

1. every audit candidate has an explicit proposed disposition;
2. every admitted entity has one unique Entity ID;
3. duplicate canonical names are absent;
4. every admitted entity has one primary information category;
5. every entry has source traceability;
6. all provisional classifications name their gate or dependency;
7. no allocation column or relationship holder appears;
8. all EOG-01 terminology is used without redefinition;
9. EOG-01 through EOG-04 remain unchanged;
10. relative links, Markdown structure and whitespace validate; and
11. the draft states that classification recommendations remain pending founder approval.

## 10. Relationship-Register Deferral

The Entity Relationship Allocation Register is architecturally approved but not authorized for operational drafting in this phase.

Its drafting remains deferred until:

- the inventory supplies controlled Entity IDs;
- the inventory’s provisional classifications are sufficiently stable to avoid orphan allocation declarations;
- applicable dependencies have matured enough to make an allocation draft constitutionally useful; and
- its permanent identifier has been reconciled or an approved full-title-only convention remains in force.

This deferral does not reject the allocation instrument. It preserves the sequence approved by the founder.

## 11. Authorization Statement

The Entity Ownership Register is authorized to proceed to Founder Review Draft 1 within this report’s scope and controls. Drafting has not begun in Phase 2.

The Entity Relationship Allocation Register remains architecturally approved and operationally deferred.
