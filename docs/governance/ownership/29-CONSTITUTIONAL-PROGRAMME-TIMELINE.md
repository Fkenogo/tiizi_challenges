This timeline is dependency-based rather than calendar-based. A phase advances only when its entry conditions are met and its decisions are explicitly approved. Durations below describe recommended founder-review units, not delivery commitments.

## 4. Timeline Checkpoints

Every phase should use the same controlled pattern:

1. readiness review;
2. scope and decision blueprint;
3. founder decision material;
4. independent constitutional review where material;
5. founder approval record;
6. affected dependency and inventory impact report; and
7. validation without implementation.

## 5. Timeline Result

The earliest allocation Draft 1 readiness checkpoint occurs after MOT1, not after RSK1. This ordering preserves Truth Before Recognition while allowing RSK detail to mature during the interval between allocation Draft 1 authorization and final approval.
