## 4. Identifier Collision

A pre-existing historical document, [Founder Accountability Decision Sequence](03-FOUNDER-DECISION-SEQUENCE.md), uses the abbreviated identifier “EOG-03” for Challenge identity, purpose, Goal and stewardship governance.

The approved current phase uses “EOG-03” for Constitutional Governance of Platform Knowledge. These are not the same subject.

The historical sequence was not modified because:

- it is a prior planning artefact;
- changing its subject would not be a status-only update;
- the current task prohibits redesign and invention of new decision identifiers;
- Challenge accountability decisions remain pending.

Until controlled identifier reconciliation occurs, current governance citations shall use the full title **EOG-03 — Constitutional Governance of Platform Knowledge**. Historical Challenge-governance references remain historical evidence and must not be interpreted as approval of Challenge governance.

This collision does not change the approved constitutional content, but it is a documentation-governance issue requiring later controlled reconciliation.

## 5. Reconciliation Result

One supplemental phase-register row was added. No original decision status changed, no pending decision was silently approved, and no new decision was invented.
