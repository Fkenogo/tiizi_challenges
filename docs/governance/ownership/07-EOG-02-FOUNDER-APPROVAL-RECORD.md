# EOG-02 — Founder Approval Record

## 1. Overview

**Governance phase:** EOG-02 — Constitutional Accountability for Groups

**Approval date:** 2026-07-20

**Status:** Approved and complete

This record closes EOG-02 following founder review. It records the constitutional principles approved through the phase, preserves the matters intentionally deferred to later governance, and establishes the approval trace required before EOG-03 begins.

## 2. Purpose and Completed Scope

EOG-02 established the approved constitutional understanding of Group creation, initial membership, initial and continuing Accountable Stewardship, the distinction between Group existence and participation, and the boundaries among stewardship, custody, administration, membership, Charter governance and moderation.

The phase completed constitutional analysis through the following documents:

- [Constitutional Accountability Framework for Groups](06-EOG-02-CONSTITUTIONAL-ACCOUNTABILITY-FRAMEWORK-FOR-GROUPS.md)
- [Group Accountability Options Paper](06-EOG-02-GROUP-ACCOUNTABILITY-OPTIONS-PAPER.md)
- [Consistency Review](06-EOG-02-CONSISTENCY-REVIEW.md)
- [Founder Decision Pack](06-EOG-02-FOUNDER-DECISION-PACK.md)

Approval of EOG-02 does not approve the deferred allocation, lifecycle, permission or operational questions identified in those documents.

## 3. Relationship to EOG-01

EOG-02 applies the mandatory semantic foundation approved in [EOG-01](05-EOG-01-FOUNDER-APPROVAL-RECORD.md). It preserves the distinctions among Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Custody, Administration, Membership and Participation. It does not redefine any EOG-01 relationship or create a new Platform Authority.

## 4. Founder-Approved Principles

The founder approved the following principles:

1. Groups are created, not proposed.
2. Creation establishes existence, identity and founding purpose.
3. The creator becomes the first Member.
4. The creator becomes the first Accountable Steward.
5. Exactly one Accountable Steward relationship exists per Group.
6. Stewardship may later be reassigned.
7. Stewardship may later be voluntarily relinquished.
8. A Stewardship Council supports but never replaces Accountable Stewardship.
9. Membership is distinct from governance.
10. Participation is distinct from Group existence.
11. Groups may exist without participation.
12. Groups may be inactive without being constitutionally unhealthy.
13. Group Governance Health is recognised as future governance.
14. Governance Maturity is recognised as a future conceptual distinction.
15. A Group Charter is treated as a Governed Subject.
16. Administration remains separate from Accountable Stewardship.
17. Custody remains separate from Accountable Stewardship.
18. Moderation derives from existing constitutional relationships.

These principles are approved constitutional direction. They do not allocate the deferred authorities, stewardship processes, custody, administration, moderation powers or lifecycle responsibilities described below.

## 5. Founder-Deferred Decisions

The following matters remain intentionally deferred:

- the Platform Authority that authenticates Group creation;
- stewardship reassignment governance, including its approved basis, safeguards and attribution;
- voluntary stewardship relinquishment governance;
- stewardship succession and stewardship-failure governance;
- Stewardship Council composition, appointment, decision-making and responsibilities;
- the form and required content of a Group Charter;
- Charter amendment governance;
- whether and how the Charter becomes binding on Members;
- Charter historical governance, including whether amendments require versioning;
- Group information custody allocation and any shared-custody model;
- Group administration allocation and the scope of administrative action;
- moderation governance, including the constitutional basis and accountability for particular moderation actions;
- membership states, transitions and lifecycle governance;
- inactivity governance, including any Charter-based response to dormant membership;
- Group discoverability, visibility and related privacy detail;
- canonical Group roles and the Roles and Permissions Standard;
- security controls and implementation mapping.

The recognition of Group Governance Health and Governance Maturity does not approve a health standard, maturity model, metric, score, workflow or implementation.

## 6. Founder Observations

- Community exists independently of participation. Voluntary participation may animate a Group, but it is not the constitutional basis of Group existence.
- Governance integrity differs from engagement. Inactivity alone does not establish constitutional ill health.
- A Stewardship Council permits future collaborative support without weakening the singular answerability of the Accountable Steward relationship.
- Persistent Groups require governance capable of continuity beyond the original creator.
- Constitutional governance should evolve through controlled refinement that preserves approved distinctions and attribution.

## 7. Governance Effect

This approval:

- closes EOG-02 as constitutional analysis;
- establishes the eighteen principles in section 4 as approved;
- retains every matter in section 5 as deferred;
- preserves all EOG-01 accountability semantics;
- makes no downstream allocation beyond the founder-established initial Member and initial Accountable Steward consequences of Group creation;
- does not amend the Group Domain Standard, Entity Ownership Register, Platform Authority Model or any lifecycle, permission, privacy, security or implementation standard.

## 8. Completion Statement

EOG-02 is complete and approved as of 2026-07-20. Its approved principles form the Group-accountability baseline for later reconciliation. Its deferred matters remain outside approved governance until resolved through the stated future governance processes.
