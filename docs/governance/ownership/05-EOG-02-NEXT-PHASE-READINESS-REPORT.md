# EOG-02 — Next Phase Readiness Report

## 1. Purpose

This report determines whether EOG-02 — Group Accountability may begin following founder approval and closure of EOG-01.

It does not begin EOG-02, allocate a Group relationship or resolve any Group governance decision.

## 2. Readiness Verdict

**Verdict:** EOG-02 may begin.

The universal accountability relationship vocabulary has been approved, its distinctions are explicit, and EOG-01 is formally closed. EOG-02 may therefore evaluate Group accountability using the approved vocabulary without reopening relationship definitions.

## 4. Remaining Prerequisites and Constraints

No additional prerequisite prevents EOG-02 analysis from beginning.

EOG-02 must nevertheless preserve these constraints:

1. apply the approved EOG-01 vocabulary and relationship boundaries;
2. distinguish Authority, Accountable Stewardship, custody, administration and operation;
3. declare separately every proposed relationship, purpose and scope;
4. avoid inferring accountability from a role title, creation action, membership, access, storage or possession;
5. preserve all existing Platform Authority boundaries;
6. treat pending Group roles, permissions and lifecycle matters as pending rather than resolving them indirectly;
7. keep EOG-03 through EOG-06 outside the EOG-02 decision scope;
8. distinguish recommendations from founder-approved allocations.

Pending Group role, lifecycle, permission, privacy and security decisions may constrain later allocation approval. They do not prevent preparation of the EOG-02 founder decision material, provided every dependency remains explicit.

## 5. EOG-02 Permitted Scope

EOG-02 may prepare founder decisions concerning Group accountability relationships under the approved EOG-01 semantics.

This readiness determination does not itself approve:

- an Authority for Group identity or purpose;
- an Accountable Steward for a Group;
- a Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Contributor, Delegate or Presenter for Group information;
- stewardship transfer or reassignment rules;
- Group roles, lifecycle states, permissions or implementation.

## 6. Decision Dependencies to Carry Forward

EOG-02 should identify, without silently resolving:

- any dependency on canonical Group role decisions;
- any dependency on Group membership or lifecycle standards;
- any dependency on permission, privacy or security standards;
- any relationship between Group accountability and later Challenge accountability;
- any stewardship-reassignment matter requiring separate governance.
