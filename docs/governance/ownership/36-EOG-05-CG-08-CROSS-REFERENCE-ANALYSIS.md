## 5. Naming Reconciliation

“Group Governance Body” is now the canonical C-GRP-08 name. “Stewardship Council” remains its approved alias and the historical term used in EOG-02 and GFC records.

This treatment:

- preserves continuity with approved governance;
- avoids requiring a particular operational title;
- permits a Group to establish one or more governance bodies;
- does not require any Group to establish a governance body; and
- does not require retrospective terminology changes to historical records or EOG-02.

## 6. Information Subject Separation

The amended register distinguishes:

1. **Group Information**, which concerns the Group;
2. **Governance Body Information**, which concerns a particular body where established; and
3. **Relationship Information**, which concerns the separately governed relationship and its relevant subjects.

Relationship Information does not become Governance Body Information merely because a body participates in the relationship. Governance Body Information does not absorb Group Information merely because the body acts in Group context.

## 7. Constitutional Dependency Effect

CG-08 is no longer an inventory-classification blocker. The amendment does not remove dependencies concerning:

- governance-body composition;
- appointment or election;
- procedures or decision-making;
- Authority or relationship allocation;
- responsibility;
- tenure or lifecycle;
- roles or permissions;
- Group Governance Health;
- governance incentives; or
- implementation.

The future Entity Relationship Allocation Register may reference C-GRP-08 but cannot infer or create an allocation from its inventory status.

## 8. Cross-reference Verdict

All live references directly affected by CG-08 resolution have been reconciled. Historical artefacts remain attributable and unchanged. The cross-reference set preserves inventory-versus-allocation separation, progressive Group governance, the singular Accountable Steward relationship and all deferred operating governance.
