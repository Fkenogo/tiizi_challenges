# EOG-03 Phase 0 — Knowledge Governance Reconciliation and Constitutional Readiness Review

## 1. Executive Summary

### Verdict

**Constitutional readiness:** Ready to begin EOG-03 drafting after bounded terminology and scope reconciliation.

The existing Knowledge Catalogue corpus is substantial and remains valuable. Its strongest ideas already align with the approved constitutional baseline:

- authoritative activity meaning is distinct from activity occurrence;
- Fitness and Wellness require rich, governed Knowledge;
- one runtime authority must make approved Knowledge available;
- runtime representations remain subordinate to rich Knowledge;
- templates and Challenges may reference or compose Knowledge without redefining it;
- historical participation must remain intelligible;
- interests and executable Activities are different concepts;
- authorship, review, publication, runtime use and calculation are different responsibilities.

The corpus is not itself an approved constitutional system. Many documents predate EOG-01 and EOG-02, describe themselves as “constitutional,” “canonical,” “frozen,” “approved” or “authoritative,” and use “owner” to mean several different relationships. Those status and ownership claims must not be carried into EOG-03 without reconciliation.

The founder clarifications add four material boundaries that are not yet expressed fully and consistently across the corpus:

1. **Platform Knowledge boundary:** authoritative Exercise Assets and Wellness Activity Assets are Platform Knowledge Assets. Groups may use them but may not modify, fork or redefine them.
2. **Constitutional Composition:** a Challenge is formed by composing authoritative Platform Knowledge and applicable Policy with Group-governed configuration and context. It is neither a modified Knowledge Asset nor a free-standing copy of Knowledge.
3. **Template and wizard boundary:** a template is a governed composition guide; the Challenge Wizard is a governed creation mechanism. Neither is a Knowledge Asset or source of Knowledge truth.
4. **Discovery and Personalisation separation:** Interests, Goals, Preferences, Tags and Categories are not Platform Knowledge Assets. Their governance belongs in a separate future constitutional programme, with only boundary references in EOG-03.

The future trusted-contributor direction is partially supported by existing authoring, review, provenance and audit concepts. It is not constitutionally ready for allocation. The corpus generally assumes centrally named authors and reviewers and does not yet define the boundary between contribution, governed review and establishment of canonical meaning using EOG-01 terminology.

### Readiness conclusion

EOG-03 should consolidate constitutional Knowledge boundaries, composition and future contribution direction. It should not adopt the legacy data models, record schemas, lifecycle states, role tables, taxonomy dictionaries, formulas, snapshot contracts, authoring workflows or implementation plans found in the corpus.

This review does not create EOG-03, approve an accountability allocation, resolve KNW-02 through KNW-04, or amend any existing governance document.

### 4.3 Needs constitutional reconciliation

#### A. Platform Knowledge versus Group-governed experience

The Knowledge Asset Domain Standard already states that a Group cannot acquire stewardship of or redefine Knowledge. The new founder clarification makes the practical boundary explicit: Groups compose Platform Knowledge into their own governed experiences.

EOG-03 must state this boundary without using “Group ownership” as ambiguous shorthand. The founder direction does not, by itself, allocate an Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator or Authority for every Challenge configuration element.

#### B. Knowledge, Policy and logic

The founder clarification says Platform Knowledge provides validation, scoring, progress and recognition logic. Existing constitutional governance states that Knowledge defines meaning, Policy governs permitted behavior and Calculation Authority establishes Derived Truth.

These statements are compatible only if “provides logic” is treated as access to governed Knowledge and applicable Policy/behavior definitions, not as authority for a Knowledge Asset to calculate Progress, Completion, Ranking or Recognition. EOG-03 should make that boundary explicit and defer formulas and calculation authority application to their approved domains and later standards.

#### C. Constitutional Composition

The corpus contains strong precursors: templates “compose knowledge,” runtime behavior uses composition, and launched Challenges preserve selected meaning. It does not yet contain one stable constitutional definition that distinguishes:

- source Knowledge meaning;
- applicable Policy;
- Group configuration and community context;
- a template as a guide;
- the creation mechanism;
- the resulting Challenge;
- a historical snapshot or reference.

This is the primary genuine constitutional gap for EOG-03.

#### D. Template and wizard classification

The lifecycle proposal lists Challenge Templates among Knowledge Objects, while the unified model gives templates their own versioned data model. The founder clarification supersedes the Knowledge classification claim: templates are governed composition guides. The wizard is a mechanism, not a Governed Subject of Knowledge or a source of canonical meaning.

#### E. Discovery and Personalisation

The current Knowledge standard excludes Interests and free-form preferences from canonical Knowledge but still recognises taxonomy or controlled-vocabulary Knowledge Assets. Older taxonomy documents include Interests, Goals, Tags and Categories in the Knowledge platform.

The founder clarification requires a sharper boundary: Interests, Goals, Preferences, Tags and Categories belong to a separate constitutional area for discovery, recommendation, filtering, matching, classification and personalisation. Later reconciliation must determine how those concepts reference Platform Knowledge without becoming it.

#### F. Trusted contribution

The Knowledge Asset Domain Standard recognises that a contributor may originate source material without establishing canonical meaning. Older documents provide author, reviewer and publisher concepts. They do not yet establish a constitutional contribution boundary suitable for future decentralisation.

EOG-03 should recognise the future direction and preserve the chain “Contributor input → governed review → Knowledge Authority establishes canonical meaning,” without defining contributors, roles, workflows or permissions.

## 5. New Constitutional Principles Identified

The following are supported by the founder clarifications and corpus evidence. This review identifies them as EOG-03 drafting requirements; it does not enact EOG-03.

### 5.1 Platform Knowledge Authority Boundary

Authoritative Exercise Assets and Wellness Activity Assets form the primary Platform Knowledge corpus. Group use, reference, display or composition does not create authority to modify, fork or redefine their canonical meaning.

### 5.2 Platform Knowledge and Group Experience Separation

Platform Knowledge establishes reusable meaning. A Group contributes governed context and configuration to its own community experience. Neither side silently acquires the constitutional relationship assigned to the other.

### 5.3 Constitutional Composition

A Group Challenge is a governed composition of authoritative Platform Knowledge, applicable Policy and Group-governed configuration and context. Composition does not create a local fork, modification or competing copy of Platform Knowledge.

### 5.4 Template Subordination

A template is a governed composition guide. It may arrange or propose Knowledge and Policy references with configurable Challenge elements, but it does not establish canonical Knowledge meaning or become a primary Knowledge Asset.

### 5.5 Creation-Mechanism Subordination

A Challenge Wizard or other creation mechanism consumes governed inputs and supports formation of a Challenge. Interface presence, prefill or validation behavior does not create Knowledge Authority.

### 5.6 Historical Composition Integrity

A historical representation may preserve the meaning and configuration used by a Challenge without becoming an independent Knowledge fork. The precise snapshot and version contract remains deferred under KNW-02.

### 5.7 Discovery Is Not Knowledge

Interests, Goals, Preferences, Tags and Categories support discovery, recommendation, filtering, matching, classification or personalisation. They are not Platform Knowledge Assets merely because they refer to or help locate Knowledge.

### 5.8 Contribution Does Not Establish Truth

A trusted contributor may originate proposed Knowledge material in a future governed model. Contribution remains attributable and does not establish canonical meaning until governed review and the existing Knowledge Authority boundary are satisfied.

### 5.9 Truth Remains Governed as Contribution Broadens

Future decentralisation of contribution does not decentralise canonical truth by implication. Governance must remain capable of distinguishing origin, review, approval, stewardship, custody, administration and authoritative meaning.

## 6. Recommended EOG-03 Structure

This is an outline only. It does not draft or approve EOG-03.

1. **Purpose and Constitutional Scope**
   - Define what EOG-03 governs and explicitly exclude lifecycle, roles, permissions, schemas and implementation.
2. **Constitutional Knowledge**
   - Define Platform Knowledge and its relationship to the constitutional ontology, Policy, evidence and Derived Truth.
3. **Platform Knowledge Assets**
   - Establish the Exercise Asset and Wellness Activity Asset boundary and supporting governed meaning without approving content or record structures.
4. **Knowledge Authority and EOG-01 Accountability Relationships**
   - Apply existing Authority, Attributable Originator, Contributor, Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter and Downstream User meanings without allocating unresolved actors.
5. **Platform Knowledge and Group Experience Boundary**
   - State what Groups may compose and what they may not redefine; preserve EOG-02 boundaries.
6. **Constitutional Composition**
   - Define Knowledge, Policy, Group configuration and community context as distinct inputs to a governed Challenge composition.
7. **Templates as Governed Composition Guides**
   - Establish non-asset, non-authority status and defer template lifecycle and implementation.
8. **Creation Mechanisms**
   - Establish the wizard/mechanism boundary without describing screens, workflows or technical validation.
9. **Runtime Knowledge**
   - Preserve one Runtime Catalogue authority and the subordinate Runtime Projection boundary.
10. **Historical Knowledge and Composition References**
    - Preserve intelligibility without deciding versions, snapshots, deletion, retention or lifecycle.
11. **Knowledge Contribution Direction**
    - Recognise current Platform creation and future trusted contribution under governed review; defer contributor eligibility, roles and process.
12. **Discovery and Personalisation Boundary**
    - Exclude Interests, Goals, Preferences, Tags and Categories from Platform Knowledge Assets and identify the future programme dependency.
13. **Knowledge, Policy and Calculation Boundary**
    - Clarify that Knowledge may support governed logic and references without assuming Policy or Calculation Authority.
14. **Corpus Disposition and Precedence**
    - Classify older corpus work as evidence, proposal, reference or lower-order guidance.
15. **Deferred Areas and Decision Dependencies**
    - Preserve KNW-02, KNW-03, KNW-04 and all contributor, lifecycle, privacy, permission, security and implementation decisions.
16. **Governance Status and Decision Trace**
    - Trace founder clarifications, EOG-01, EOG-02 and existing constitutional standards.

## 7. Discovery and Personalisation Recommendation

### Recommendation

**Create a separate future Discovery and Personalisation Governance Programme.** EOG-03 should define only the constitutional boundary and cross-domain relationship.

### Reasons

1. Interests, Goals and Preferences concern a Person or Profile and may be personal expressions rather than canonical shared meaning.
2. Tags and Categories are used for several purposes: discovery, filtering, classification, recommendation, reporting and presentation. Treating them uniformly as Knowledge Assets would obscure their purpose and authority.
3. Recommendation and matching introduce privacy, consent, relevance, explainability and analytical concerns beyond Knowledge meaning.
4. The legacy corpus contains duplicated Interest vocabularies and mixed labels, demonstrating the need for governance but not proving that Knowledge Authority should govern all of them.
5. A separate programme can define reference relationships to Platform Knowledge without allowing preferences or discovery labels to redefine Knowledge.

### EOG-03 boundary

EOG-03 should state:

- these concepts are not Platform Knowledge Assets;
- they may reference Platform Knowledge under later governance;
- similarity of wording does not establish identity;
- discovery or recommendation use cannot change canonical Knowledge meaning;
- their Accountable Stewardship, Custody, lifecycle, privacy and authority details remain outside EOG-03.

This recommendation leaves KNW-04 pending. It does not create the separate programme or decide its internal authority model.

## 8. Knowledge Contribution Readiness

### Assessment

**Readiness:** Conceptually prepared, constitutionally incomplete.

### Existing support

The corpus already supports several necessary ideas:

- identifiable authors and reviewers;
- domain and safety review;
- evidence and provenance;
- attributable change history;
- human review of AI-proposed content;
- publication quality gates;
- separation of engineering/runtime use from approved Knowledge meaning.

The approved EOG-01 framework adds the essential distinction that a Contributor is an attributable non-Authority relationship. The Knowledge Asset Domain Standard further states that a contributor may originate source material without establishing approval or canonical meaning.

### Constitutional gaps

The existing corpus does not yet govern:

- what qualifies a party as a trusted contributor;
- whether contribution concerns content, evidence, relationships, translations or proposed new Assets;
- the constitutional status of a contribution before review;
- how contribution remains distinct from Accountable Stewardship, Custody, Administration and Knowledge Authority;
- how governed review establishes or rejects canonical meaning;
- how provenance, source rights, qualifications and conflicts of interest are treated;
- how a contributor relationship may be suspended or ended;
- how disputed, withdrawn or unsafe contributions affect historical attribution;
- how decentralised contribution preserves one authoritative Knowledge meaning and one Runtime Catalogue authority.

### Future-direction boundary

The later constitutional model may recognise:

> Platform governance
>
> ↓ permits contribution under declared scope
>
> Trusted Contributor
>
> ↓ supplies attributable proposed material
>
> Governed Review
>
> ↓ supports an authoritative decision
>
> Knowledge Authority
>
> ↓ establishes canonical Knowledge Asset meaning

This is a readiness illustration, not an approved workflow, lifecycle or allocation. EOG-03 should recognise the direction and defer its implementation to later Knowledge Contribution, Accountability, Role, Permission and Lifecycle standards.

## 12. Final Readiness Statement

The existing Knowledge Catalogue work is constitutionally valuable but not constitutionally self-executing. Its research, reference models and implementation thinking provide enough evidence to draft EOG-03 without starting over.

EOG-03 may proceed if it:

- uses EOG-01 accountability terminology;
- preserves EOG-02 Group boundaries;
- treats Exercise Assets and Wellness Activity Assets as authoritative Platform Knowledge Assets;
- defines Constitutional Composition without turning a Challenge into a Knowledge copy or fork;
- treats templates as composition guides and the wizard as a creation mechanism;
- separates Knowledge, Policy and Calculation Authority;
- places Interests, Goals, Preferences, Tags and Categories outside Platform Knowledge Assets;
- recognises future trusted contribution without allocating roles or designing a workflow;
- leaves KNW-02, KNW-03, KNW-04 and all implementation concerns pending.

**Final verdict:** The Knowledge governance evidence base is ready for EOG-03 constitutional drafting. It is not yet ready for EOG-03 approval, Knowledge accountability allocation, lifecycle implementation or catalogue production without the later decisions and standards identified in this review.
