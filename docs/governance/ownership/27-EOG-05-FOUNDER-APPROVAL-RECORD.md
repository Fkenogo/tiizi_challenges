## 2. Constitutional Purpose

EOG-05 establishes Tiizi's approved constitutional entity inventory. It records stable and provisional Entity IDs, canonical names, domain placement, Governed Subject classification, Information Subject treatment, constitutional definitions, boundaries, primary information categories, source traceability, classification status and dependencies.

EOG-05 does not allocate any EOG-01 accountability relationship. The Entity Relationship Allocation Register remains a separate instrument and is not approved or drafted by this approval.

## 3. Approved Baseline

The founder approves the complete Draft 2 inventory treatment:

- 72 candidate rows with unchanged Entity IDs;
- 57 Proposed inclusion classifications;
- 14 Provisional classifications governed by CG-01 through CG-08;
- Recognition as one additional Provisional classification governed by MOT-01;
- ten primary constitutional information categories;
- 14 consolidated or excluded concepts;
- the cross-domain entity boundaries;
- the dependency and cross-register treatment; and
- the explicit absence of accountability allocations.

Approval makes the register and its provisional restraints authoritative within their stated scope. It does not convert a Provisional classification into final classification and does not resolve any named dependency.
