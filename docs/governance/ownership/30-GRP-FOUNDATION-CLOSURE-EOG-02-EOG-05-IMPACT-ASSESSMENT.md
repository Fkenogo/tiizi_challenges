# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 0 — EOG-02 and EOG-05 Impact Assessment

**Status:** Prospective impact analysis; no amendment made

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This assessment identifies how later founder decisions could affect EOG-02 and EOG-05. It does not amend either approved instrument and does not presume any decision outcome.

## 2. Current Governance Preservation

| Approved instrument | Current treatment | Phase 0 effect |
|---|---|---|
| EOG-02 | Approved Group principles and explicit deferrals remain authoritative. | No change. Questions are prepared only within approved boundaries. |
| EOG-05 | Approved inventory includes stable Group rows and C-GRP-08 as Provisional under CG-08. | No row, ID, category, definition, status or gate changes. |

## 3. EOG-02 Impact Areas

| GFC question | Approved EOG-02 principle preserved | Potential later governance effect if founder approves an outcome |
|---|---|---|
| GFC-01 | Groups are created; participation does not establish existence. | Supplemental approval may identify the existing Platform Authority authenticating creation. |
| GFC-02 | Creator is first Accountable Steward; exactly one relationship exists; continuity beyond creator is required. | Supplemental approval may refine reassignment, relinquishment and continuity principles without replacing EOG-02. |
| GFC-03 | Charter is a Governed Subject. | New Charter governance may define truth and historical boundaries. |
| GFC-04 | Council supports and never replaces the Accountable Steward. | New Council governance may clarify purpose and deferred responsibility boundaries. |
| GFC-05 | Council treatment remains a classification dependency. | No EOG-02 change is inherently required; an attributable inventory decision may follow. |

Any later conflict with an approved EOG-02 principle would require founder review rather than silent reconciliation.

## 6. Cross-Register Effect

Later approval may make Group allocation drafting more useful, but inventory and allocation remain separate:

- an EOG-05 amendment may change classification only through its own approval trace;
- an allocation register may not resolve CG-08;
- a permanent C-GRP-08 row would not allocate any Council relationship; and
- removal or consolidation of the provisional row would require impact analysis of future references.

## 7. Impact Verdict

Phase 0 causes no EOG-02 or EOG-05 amendment. The questions are compatible with both instruments. Future founder outcomes are likely to require supplemental governance and may require an attributable EOG-05 classification amendment, but the amendment type cannot be selected before those outcomes exist.
