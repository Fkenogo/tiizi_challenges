## 3. Founder-approved Decision

Approve the EOG-05 CG-08 amendment with these inseparable constitutional conditions and refinements:

1. inventory is not allocation;
2. no EOG-01 relationship is granted;
3. Governance Body Information, Group Information and separately governed Relationship Information remain distinct;
4. Governance Body responsibility is not Accountable Stewardship;
5. the singular Accountable Steward relationship remains unchanged;
6. Groups choose whether and when to establish governance bodies and may establish one or more;
7. operational titles remain Group-determined, with Stewardship Council retained as an approved constitutional alias rather than a mandatory name;
8. permanent recognition does not require every Group to establish, maintain or continuously operate a governance body;
9. no governance-body composition, appointment, election, procedure, Authority, tenure, lifecycle, permission, role or implementation rule is created; and
10. no EOG-05 row other than C-GRP-08 is amended, except the necessary counts, gate range, dependency statement and attribution metadata.

The absence of a governance body shall not, by itself, prevent a Group from being created, operating, accepting members or participating in Platform activities unless expressly provided by later approved governance.

## 4. Constitutional Effect Approved

Approval authorizes the attributable application that:

- changes C-GRP-08 from Provisional — CG-08 to permanent Proposed inclusion treatment;
- adopts Group Governance Body as the canonical name and Stewardship Council as the approved non-mandatory alias;
- resolves the Information Subject boundary without merging Governance Body Information, Group Information or Relationship Information;
- changes the register totals from 57 proposed / 14 gate-provisional / 1 later-governance provisional to 58 / 13 / 1;
- closes CG-08 while preserving CG-01 through CG-07 and MOT-01; and
- records the founder approval and amendment trace.

Approval does not allocate an EOG-01 relationship. The amended register continues to state that inventory and allocation are separate constitutional instruments.
