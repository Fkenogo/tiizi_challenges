# EOG-02 — Founder Decision Pack for Group Accountability

## 1. Purpose

This pack prepares founder decisions needed to establish the constitutional accountability model for Groups.

It applies the founder-established creation consequences and the approved EOG-01 vocabulary. Every recommendation remains **Recommended — pending founder approval**. No option or recommendation changes governance until explicit approval is recorded.

## 2. Established Context — Not Reopened

The following decisions are already established:

- a Group is created, not proposed;
- creation establishes existence, initial identity and founding purpose;
- the creator becomes the first Accountable Steward and first Member;
- membership and stewardship remain distinct;
- every Group has exactly one singular Accountable Steward relationship at any given time;
- stewardship is not permanent and may later be reassigned or voluntarily relinquished through approved governance;
- a Stewardship Council may support but never replace the Accountable Steward;
- Group existence is constitutionally independent of participation;
- the Group must be capable of outliving its creator;
- Group identity may evolve without identity evolution becoming lifecycle state.

This pack does not ask the founder to approve those facts again.

## 5. Founder Decisions

### EOG-02-GRP-01 — Authentication of Group Creation

**Decision question:** Which existing Platform Authority authenticates the creation event and founding facts after the creator establishes the Group?

| Option | Model | Principal strength | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Administrative Authority authenticates the creation event and founding facts for a declared, limited purpose. | Separates creator-established existence from authoritative authentication. | Must not be interpreted as proposal approval or administrative creation. |
| B | Identity Authority authenticates the event as collective identity. | Keeps existence separate from participation. | Expands Identity Authority beyond Person and Profile identity. |
| C | Participation Authority authenticates the event because the Group may later provide participation context. | Uses an Authority already associated with Group relationships. | Makes potential participation the basis for an already existing Community identity. |

**Recommended — pending founder approval:** Option A.

**Reason:** The creator creates the Group. Administrative Authority can authenticate the attributable creation event and founding facts without causing, proposing or approving the Group's existence. Participation Authority remains concerned with governed participation relationships and context.

**Consequences if approved:** Administrative Authority authenticates the creation event and founding facts within a declared scope. It does not become the creator, Accountable Steward, general Authority over Group truth or source of participation.

**Why alternatives are not recommended:** Option B blurs personal and collective identity authority. Option C contradicts the principle that existence does not depend on participation.

**Difficulty:** High. **Decision risk:** High. **Reversibility:** Constitutionally difficult. **Long-term impact:** High.

**Founder decision:**

### Founder-Established Refinement — Singular Accountable Stewardship and Stewardship Council

This matter is no longer an EOG-02 recommendation.

Every Group has exactly one Accountable Steward relationship at any given time. That singular relationship remains ultimately answerable, may be reassigned through approved governance, may be voluntarily relinquished under later governance and supports continuity beyond the creator.

A Stewardship Council is a governed body that may support the Accountable Steward. Council membership does not create Accountable Stewardship. The Council is not a Platform Authority, Administration or Custody, and it never replaces the Accountable Steward.

Council composition, appointment, decision-making and responsibilities remain future governance. No Council operation or allocation is approved here.

Group Governance Health and Governance Maturity are also non-governing future concepts. Activity level does not determine governance health. No health metric, maturity model, score, assessment or implementation is approved in EOG-02.

### EOG-02-GRP-03 — Stewardship Reassignment, Succession and Failure

**Decision question:** What constitutional model should preserve continuity when stewardship transfers, changes or fails?

| Option | Model | Principal strength | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | The incumbent Accountable Steward alone controls reassignment. | Clear source while available. | Cannot govern absence, conflict or failure. |
| B | Policy defines valid bases; Participation Authority establishes Stewardship Reassignment; an Administrator may execute only where separately assigned. | Preserves Policy, Authority, administration and stewardship distinctions. | Requires later standards for safeguards and process. |
| C | A role or membership category inherits stewardship automatically. | Simple succession. | Treats roles or membership as implied accountability. |

**Recommended — pending founder approval:** Option B.

**Reason:** It allows the Group to outlive its creator without treating reassignment as Delegation of Authority or Assignment of Responsibility.

**Consequences if approved:** Participation Authority would establish the stewardship relationship under approved Policy. No reassignment trigger, process or lifecycle state is approved here.

**Why alternatives are not recommended:** Option A cannot handle failure. Option C conflicts with EOG-01's prohibition on role-title inference.

**Difficulty:** High. **Decision risk:** High. **Reversibility:** Constitutionally difficult. **Long-term impact:** High.

### EOG-02-GCH-02 — Authority for Charter Content and Amendment Boundary

| Option | Model | Principal strength | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Accountable Stewardship establishes and amends all Charter content. | Simple answerability. | Conflates stewardship with Authority. |

### EOG-02-MOD-02 — Dormant Membership Treatment

**Decision question:** What constitutional meaning should inactivity have?

| Option | Model | Principal strength | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Inactivity is misconduct and automatically justifies removal. | Simple active-membership expectation. | Makes an unsupported moral and conduct judgment. |
