# EOG-03 — Governance Adoption Summary

## 1. Purpose

This summary explains how future governance must use [EOG-03 — Constitutional Governance of Platform Knowledge](14-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-APPROVED.md).

It is an adoption guide, not a new constitutional standard.

## 2. What EOG-03 Governs

EOG-03 governs:

- Platform Knowledge as authoritative reusable meaning;
- Knowledge Authority as the Authority to Establish Authoritative Meaning;
- Exercise Assets and Wellness Activity Assets as the primary current Platform Knowledge Assets;
- supporting governed Knowledge meaning without defining a final classification;
- the rule that meaning precedes representation;
- the boundary between Platform Knowledge and Group Experience;
- Constitutional Composition and its current application to Version 2 Group Challenges;
- non-transfer of Authority through composition;
- Templates as governed composition guides;
- Creation Mechanisms as subordinate mechanisms;
- the Runtime Catalogue function and Runtime Projection subordination;
- Layered Meaning and Historical Representation integrity;
- the separation of Discovery classifications from governed Knowledge vocabulary;
- current Platform-generated Knowledge and future governed contribution direction.

## 3. What EOG-03 Does Not Govern

EOG-03 deliberately does not govern:

- individual catalogue content, identifiers or counts;
- final Knowledge classifications, taxonomy contents or dictionaries;
- lifecycle states, publication, revision, deprecation, retirement or deletion;
- version formats, snapshot contents, retention or historical storage;
- contributor eligibility, reviewer roles, approval workflows or disputes;
- Template, Challenge, Group or participation lifecycle;
- calculation, scoring, Progress, Completion, Ranking, Streak or Recognition rules;
- accountability allocations;
- roles, permissions, privacy or security;
- schemas, APIs, storage, interfaces or implementation.

These exclusions are intentional. A downstream programme may refine its own subject without contradicting the approved EOG-03 boundaries.

## 4. Downstream Governance Dependencies

### 4.1 Discovery and Personalisation

This programme depends on EOG-03 to preserve the distinction between Interests, Goals, Preferences, Discovery Tags and Discovery Categories on one side, and governed Taxonomy or Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge meaning on the other. It must not convert discovery convenience into Knowledge Authority.

### 4.2 Knowledge Lifecycle

Lifecycle governance depends on EOG-03's definition of Platform Knowledge, Authoritative Meaning, provenance, runtime subordination and historical integrity. It must define change without permitting silent reinterpretation. KNW-02 and KNW-03 remain prerequisites.

### 4.3 Knowledge Versioning

Versioning governance depends on “Meaning precedes representation” and the distinction among a Knowledge Asset, Runtime Projection and Historical Representation. EOG-03 does not select a version model or snapshot contract.

### 4.4 Historical Preservation

Historical-preservation governance depends on Historical Meaning as preservation of applicable Platform, Policy and Community Meaning and on the broader Historical Representation boundary. It must not create a competing Knowledge fork.

### 4.5 Knowledge Classification

Classification governance depends on the approved distinction among primary Platform Knowledge Assets, supporting Knowledge Assets, discovery classifications and governed semantic vocabulary. It may define later classifications without returning Discovery and Personalisation to Platform Knowledge.

### 4.6 Contributor Governance

Contributor governance depends on the sequence from attributable contribution through Governed Review to Knowledge Authority. It may decentralise contribution but may not decentralise canonical truth by implication.

### 4.7 Challenge Governance

Challenge governance depends on Constitutional Composition, the four distinct composition inputs, and the boundary between Group Experience and Platform Knowledge. It must not treat a Challenge, Template or Creation Mechanism as a Knowledge fork or source Authority.

### 4.8 Future Knowledge Domains

Future domains depend on EOG-03's domain-neutral Authoritative Meaning and composition doctrines. A new domain requires separate approval before becoming active; extensibility alone creates no launch capability.

## 5. Adoption Rules

Future governance using EOG-03 shall:

1. cite the approved instrument rather than a review draft when stating current governance;
2. preserve Knowledge, Policy, Acceptance, Calculation, Participation, Runtime and Presentation separation;
3. preserve EOG-01 accountability semantics and EOG-02 Group boundaries;
4. identify KNW-02, KNW-03 or KNW-04 where a deferred question is encountered;
5. avoid treating a representation, mechanism, local context or repeated use as source Authority;
6. use historical drafts and reviews only as decision trace, not current constitutional authority.

## 6. Adoption Status

EOG-03 is adopted as Approved Constitutional Governance. This summary introduces no additional doctrine and allocates no accountability.
