**Status:** Ready for decision-analysis preparation; no founder decision resolved

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This review determines whether the GRP Foundation Closure programme has a sufficient constitutional basis to prepare founder decisions concerning Group creation authentication, stewardship continuity, Charter foundations, Stewardship Council treatment and CG-08 readiness.

It does not select an option, allocate an EOG-01 relationship, resolve CG-08 or amend approved governance.

## 3. Approved Constitutional Evidence

The following may be treated as established:

1. Groups are created, not proposed.
2. Creation establishes Group existence, initial identity and founding purpose.
3. The creator becomes the first Member and first Accountable Steward.
4. Exactly one Accountable Steward relationship exists for a Group at any given time.
5. Stewardship may later be reassigned or voluntarily relinquished through approved governance.
6. A Group must be capable of continuity beyond its creator.
7. Membership, participation, Administration, Custody and Accountable Stewardship remain distinct.
8. A Stewardship Council may support but never replace the Accountable Steward relationship.
9. The Council is not a Platform Authority, Administration or Custody by its existence.
10. The Group Charter is a Governed Subject.
11. Group inactivity does not invalidate Group existence or prove governance failure.
12. Role titles do not imply EOG-01 relationships, and relationship stacking requires separate declarations.
13. EOG-05 identifies Group, Membership relationship, Member, Group purpose, Group administrative action, Group Charter and Stewardship Council as distinct or provisional inventory treatments within their recorded boundaries.

## 4. Constitutional Questions Still Open

| Question area | What remains constitutionally undecided |
|---|---|
| Creation authentication | Which existing Platform Authority authenticates the creation event and establishes authoritative Group existence, identity and founding-purpose truth; how creator expression remains distinct from authoritative authentication |
| Stewardship continuity | Constitutional basis for reassignment, voluntary relinquishment, succession and failure treatment without treating delegation, Administration or Council membership as stewardship |
| Charter foundations | Initial Charter establishment, binding constitutional effect, amendment authority boundary, historical intelligibility and relationship to the Accountable Steward |
| Stewardship Council | Permanent constitutional scope, relationship to the singular Accountable Steward, and limits of supporting responsibility without allocation or role design |
| CG-08 | Whether C-GRP-08 receives permanent independent inventory treatment and what Information Subject boundary applies |

## 5. Decision-Preparation Evidence Still Required

The later options paper should prepare neutral constitutional scenarios for founder review:

- a creator establishing a Group that initially has no further participation;
- creator departure with a willing successor;
- voluntary relinquishment without an immediately confirmed successor;
- attempted use of delegation or an Administrator title as a substitute for stewardship reassignment;
- Charter disagreement between the Accountable Steward and Members;
- Charter amendment after prior Group participation;
- a Stewardship Council offering collective support while one Accountable Steward remains singularly answerable; and
- inventory treatment of the Council as a Governed Subject versus its Information Subject and relationship boundaries.

These scenarios are constitutional evidence aids. They must not become workflows, states, permissions or implementation requirements.

## 6. Readiness Risks

| Risk | Required control |
|---|---|
| Treating creator expression as authoritative authentication by implication | Separate Attributable origin from Authority to Establish Truth. |
| Treating Stewardship Council as shared Accountable Stewardship | Preserve exactly one Accountable Steward relationship and declare the Council non-Authority. |
| Treating reassignment as ordinary delegation | Apply the EOG-01 separation among Delegation of Authority, Assignment of Responsibility and Stewardship Reassignment. |
| Treating Charter administration as Charter truth Authority | Separate Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Custody and Administration. |
| Using role labels to answer constitutional questions | Evaluate relationships before any later role vocabulary. |
| Resolving CG-08 before Council boundaries are understood | Review Council treatment before inventory disposition. |
| Introducing vacancy, suspension or archival states | Defer lifecycle treatment and use constitutional questions only. |

## 7. Readiness Verdict

**Ready for Phase 1 decision-options analysis with controlled prerequisites.**

The approved evidence is sufficient to define the founder questions and viable constitutional criteria. Founder preference and constitutional trade-off analysis are still required before recommendations can be prepared. No founder approval session should begin until the decision-options paper demonstrates that every option preserves EOG-01, EOG-02 and EOG-05 boundaries.
