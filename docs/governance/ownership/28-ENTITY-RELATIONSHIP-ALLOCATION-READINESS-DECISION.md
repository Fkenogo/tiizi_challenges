# Post-EOG-05 — Entity Relationship Allocation Readiness Decision

**Status:** Recommendation pending Governance Authority approval

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Decision Question

At what earliest constitutional milestone should drafting of the Entity Relationship Allocation Register begin?

## 2. Current Decision

**Do not begin operational drafting yet.**

The inventory baseline is approved, but the current dependency state would require Draft 1 to rely on unstable entity treatment or leave core Authority and accountability relationships unresolved across Group, Challenge, activity integrity, Knowledge and Recognition.

This record is a readiness recommendation only. It does not change the architecturally approved but operationally deferred status of the allocation register.

## 3. Earliest Safe Milestone

Drafting should begin at the **Classification Closure and Core Allocation Authority Milestone**.

That milestone is reached only when all of the following are recorded through attributable governance:

1. CG-01 through CG-07 are resolved, or Governance Authority expressly approves a restricted Draft 1 that excludes every still-Provisional entity from allocation. CG-08 is already resolved by the attributable EOG-05 amendment.
2. Group creation authentication and continuing stewardship have sufficient constitutional treatment for Group allocation.
3. CHL-01 resolves the Challenge identity, purpose, Goal and stewardship allocation basis.
4. ACT-03 and ACT-04 resolve correction and Verification Authority boundaries.
5. MOT-01 resolves Recognition qualification and final inventory treatment.
6. KNW and CHL decisions required by the classification gates are complete enough to preserve Knowledge, representation and history boundaries.
7. Governance Authority issues a dedicated Draft 1 authorization under the approved cross-register contract.

The recommended default is full classification-gate closure. The restricted-draft alternative is constitutionally viable only if Provisional entities receive no allocation rows and the authorization explicitly preserves that omission.

## 4. What May Remain Open at Draft 1

Draft 1 may begin while Allocation influences remain open, provided each affected cell names its dependency and no core Authority is inferred. These may include:

- lifecycle-sensitive custody and administration;
- role mapping;
- privacy and security constraints;
- RSK calculation-method detail that does not change Calculation Authority;
- social and Notification lifecycle detail;
- contributor eligibility and workflow; and
- implementation and technical operation.

These matters may still block final approval even when they do not block the start of drafting.

## 5. Final Approval Milestone

Final approval should require:

- no allocation against an unresolved or non-existent Entity ID;
- no unresolved Authority-to-establish-truth allocation;
- declared and bounded treatment for every applicable EOG-01 relationship or an explicitly approved non-applicability rule;
- completion of row-relevant Group, Challenge, activity, Knowledge, Ranking, Recognition, Notification, analytics and operations dependencies;
- reconciliation with later lifecycle, roles and permissions, privacy and security standards where they affect relationship scope; and
- independent constitutional review followed by founder approval.

## 6. Dependency Status Summary

| Dependency class | Readiness effect |
|---|---|
| Allocation blocker — start-critical | Must be resolved before Draft 1 or expressly excluded under a restricted-draft authorization. |
| Allocation blocker — affected-row or completion | May remain visible only if Draft 1 does not purport to complete the affected allocation; must be resolved before final approval. |
| Allocation influence | May remain explicit during Draft 1; reconcile before final approval where it changes scope. |
| No allocation impact | Does not delay constitutional allocation unless it later changes the inventory or relationship scope. |

## 7. Decision Result

**Ready with prerequisites.** The Entity Relationship Allocation Register remains operationally deferred. The [Recommended Governance Sequence](28-ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP-ALLOCATION-GOVERNANCE-SEQUENCE.md) identifies the path to the earliest safe drafting milestone without resolving any pending decision or amending approved governance.
