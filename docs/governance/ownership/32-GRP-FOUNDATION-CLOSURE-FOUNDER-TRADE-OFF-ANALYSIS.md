# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 2 — Founder Trade-off Analysis

**Status:** Founder review aid; recommendations remain unapproved

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This paper isolates the choices the founder would make by accepting or rejecting the preferred models in the [Preferred Constitutional Models Report](32-GRP-FOUNDATION-CLOSURE-PREFERRED-CONSTITUTIONAL-MODELS.md). It does not approve a model or add governance.

## 4. Alternative-model Trade-offs

### 4.1 GFC-01

- Selecting GFC-01-A would reduce administrative precedent but make the constitutional vocabulary work harder: Participation Authority would establish existence while participation itself remains irrelevant to existence.
- Selecting GFC-01-B makes the authentication event explicit but requires permanent resistance to administrative scope expansion.

### 4.2 GFC-02

- Selecting GFC-02-A minimizes doctrine now but leaves stewardship failure unresolved.
- Selecting GFC-02-B increases Charter dependency in exchange for Community-grounded continuity.
- Selecting GFC-02-C maximizes resilience in exchange for central governance burden and intervention risk.

### 4.3 GFC-03

- Selecting GFC-03-A privileges immediate coherence over delayed deliberation.
- Selecting GFC-03-B privileges later adoption over founding completeness.
- Selecting GFC-03-C privileges flexibility over simplicity and one-layer intelligibility.
- Selecting GFC-03-D privileges low Charter burden over constitutional usefulness.

### 4.4 GFC-04

- Selecting GFC-04-A minimizes leakage risk but may make the Council constitutionally ornamental.
- Selecting GFC-04-B gives the Council a support function but requires precise Assignment of Responsibility governance.
- Selecting GFC-04-C maximizes separate attribution but displaces substance from the Council body to its members or other actors.

### 4.5 GFC-05

- Selecting GFC-05-A privileges traceability and later body-level allocation readiness over inventory minimalism.
- Selecting GFC-05-B privileges a lean register but may require future Council readmission.
- Selecting GFC-05-C privileges present restraint but leaves the closure gate open.

## 5. Package-level Trade-off

The preferred package chooses explicit constitutional structure over minimal documentation:

- narrow Administrative Authority rather than semantically stretched Participation Authority;
- Charter-grounded continuity rather than permanent external intervention;
- immediate binding Charter meaning rather than delayed or layered applicability;
- a substantively useful Council rather than an advisory label; and
- permanent Council inventory identity rather than consolidation or deferral.

The resulting burden is governance precision. Future standards must preserve separate declarations for Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Assignment of Responsibility, Membership and Administration. That burden is justified because it converts ambiguity into attributable constitutional boundaries without dictating implementation.

## 6. Founder Decision Discipline

The founder may approve, reject or request refinement of each recommendation independently, subject to the compatibility constraints in the [Cross-model Consistency Assessment](32-GRP-FOUNDATION-CLOSURE-CROSS-MODEL-CONSISTENCY-ASSESSMENT.md). No option becomes constitutional governance through this analysis alone.
