# EOG-01 — Founder Approval Record

## 1. Record Purpose

This record establishes the founder approval trace for EOG-01 — Platform Accountability Framework.

It records the approved recommendations from the [Founder Decision Pack](04-PLATFORM-ACCOUNTABILITY-FRAMEWORK-FOUNDER-DECISION-PACK.md). It does not amend the [Platform Accountability Framework](04-PLATFORM-ACCOUNTABILITY-FRAMEWORK.md), allocate an accountability relationship or approve any decision outside EOG-01-FW-01 through EOG-01-FW-08.

## 2. Approval Details

**Approval date:** 2026-07-19

**Approving authority:** Founder

**Decision group:** EOG-01 — Platform Accountability Framework

**Decision status:** Approved and closed

## 3. Approved Decisions

### EOG-01-FW-01 — Canonical Relationship Set

**Approved option:** Option B — Complete bounded accountability relationship set.

**Approved decision:** The complete bounded accountability relationship set is approved.

**Constitutional consequence:** Future Entity Ownership Governance work shall distinguish the complete bounded set defined by the Platform Accountability Framework. The set refines accountability semantics and does not create a new Platform Authority.

**Superseded terminology:** No approved term is removed. A minimal relationship set or domain-specific substitute is not sufficient for formal Entity Ownership Governance work.

**Implementation effect:** None. This decision defines governance terminology only.

**Governance effect:** The canonical relationship vocabulary may be used as the common semantic foundation for later accountability allocation decisions.

### EOG-01-FW-02 — Formal Stewardship Terminology

**Approved option:** Option A — Accountable Steward is the formal term.

**Approved decision:** “Accountable Steward” is the formal constitutional term. “Steward” may be used only as explanatory shorthand where unambiguous.

**Constitutional consequence:** Formal governance shall express the relationship as Accountable Steward so that answerability remains explicit.

**Superseded terminology:** “Steward” used as an independent formal allocation term is superseded by “Accountable Steward.” Its use as unambiguous explanatory shorthand remains permitted.

**Implementation effect:** None. No steward is assigned by this decision.

**Governance effect:** Later ownership and accountability documents must use Accountable Steward in formal allocations.

### EOG-01-FW-03 — Subject Terminology

**Approved option:** Option B — Preserve two canonical Subject terms.

**Approved decision:** Formal governance shall distinguish Governed Subject from Information Subject. Unqualified “Subject” shall not be used in formal allocations.

**Constitutional consequence:** Governance must distinguish what governance concerns from the Person, entity or matter to which information relates.

**Superseded terminology:** Unqualified “Subject” is superseded in formal allocations by “Governed Subject” or “Information Subject,” as applicable.

**Implementation effect:** None. No subject classification or allocation is changed by this decision.

**Governance effect:** Future Entity Ownership Governance work must identify the applicable Subject relationship explicitly.

### EOG-01-FW-04 — Delegation and Assignment Model

**Approved option:** Option B — Differentiated model.

**Approved decision:** Delegation of Authority, Assignment of Responsibility and Stewardship Reassignment are separate constitutional concepts and shall never be treated as equivalent.

**Constitutional consequence:** A transfer or assignment must preserve the specific constitutional relationship involved. One concept cannot silently substitute for another.

**Superseded terminology:** Undifferentiated use of “delegation” for Authority, responsibility and stewardship change is superseded in formal governance.

**Implementation effect:** None. No delegation, assignment or reassignment procedure is created.

**Governance effect:** Later standards must declare which of the three concepts applies and preserve its distinct boundary.

### EOG-01-FW-05 — Relationship Composition

**Approved option:** Option B — Explicit relationship stacking.

**Approved decision:** One actor may hold multiple accountability relationships provided each relationship is declared separately with its own purpose and scope. Role titles alone shall never imply accountability relationships.

**Constitutional consequence:** Separation of relationships does not require separation of actors, but no relationship may be inferred from another relationship or from a role title.

**Superseded terminology:** Any role-bundle inference that treats a role name as an accountability declaration is superseded.

**Implementation effect:** None. No actor, role or relationship combination is approved.

**Governance effect:** Future allocations must declare each relationship independently, even where one actor later holds several relationships.

### EOG-01-FW-06 — Contributor and Participant Treatment

**Approved option:** Option B — Include both as non-Authority contextual accountability relationships.

**Approved decision:** Contributor and Participant are canonical non-Authority contextual accountability relationships. Neither relationship implies Authority, Stewardship, Custody or Approval.

**Constitutional consequence:** Contribution and participation may preserve attribution and context without becoming a source of authoritative truth or accountable governance.

**Superseded terminology:** Any use of Contributor or Participant status as shorthand for Authority, Stewardship, Custody or Approval is superseded.

**Implementation effect:** None. No participation, contribution, approval or evidence rule is created.

**Governance effect:** Later governance may use Contributor and Participant as contextual relationships only within the approved boundaries.

### EOG-01-FW-07 — Custody and Possession

**Approved option:** Option B — Purpose-limited custody with no effect from storage alone.

**Approved decision:** Custody is purpose-limited. Storage or possession alone creates no constitutional custody.

**Constitutional consequence:** Custody exists only through an explicit, purpose-limited custodial relationship. Possession, access or storage cannot silently establish custody.

**Superseded terminology:** Any treatment of storage or possession as sufficient proof of constitutional custody is superseded.

**Implementation effect:** None. No Custodian or storage arrangement is approved.

**Governance effect:** Future custody allocations must declare their purpose and scope independently of physical or technical possession.

### EOG-01-FW-08 — Framework Adoption Effect

**Approved option:** Option A — Mandatory semantic guidance without allocation.

**Approved decision:** The approved accountability vocabulary is mandatory semantic guidance for all future Entity Ownership Governance work. No accountability allocation is approved by this decision.

**Constitutional consequence:** Future Entity Ownership Governance documents must use the approved meanings consistently unless later founder-approved governance supersedes them.

**Superseded terminology:** Local or domain-specific terminology that contradicts the approved relationship meanings may not substitute for the canonical vocabulary in formal Entity Ownership Governance work.

**Implementation effect:** None. This approval does not authorize product, operational or technical change.

**Governance effect:** EOG-01 becomes the semantic prerequisite for future accountability allocation work.

## 4. Aggregate Constitutional Consequences

The eight approvals establish:

- a complete bounded accountability relationship vocabulary;
- Accountable Steward as the formal stewardship term;
- a formal distinction between Governed Subject and Information Subject;
- separate concepts for Delegation of Authority, Assignment of Responsibility and Stewardship Reassignment;
- explicit, separately scoped relationship stacking;
- Contributor and Participant as non-Authority contextual relationships;
- purpose-limited custody that cannot arise from storage or possession alone;
- mandatory semantic guidance for future Entity Ownership Governance work.

These consequences concern relationship meaning only. They allocate no relationship and change no Platform Authority.

## 5. Scope Exclusions

This approval does not:

- allocate accountability for any Governed Subject;
- resolve EOG-02 through EOG-06;
- establish lifecycle, permission, privacy, security or implementation rules;
- amend a Domain Standard;
- change an Entity Ownership Register row;
- authorize a product, operational or technical change.
