# Entity Ownership Decision Gaps

## 1. Purpose

This report records allocations that the [Tiizi Entity Ownership Register](01-TIIZI-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER.md) cannot complete without a founder decision or a later governance standard. It does not resolve any gap.

“Prevents approving the register” distinguishes approval of a final, fully allocated accountability register from review of the complete Phase E1 draft. The draft remains reviewable even when a gap blocks final approval.

## 2. Gap Classification

| Classification | Meaning in this report |
|---|---|
| Register-blocking | Prevents final approval of one or more core truth, stewardship or custody allocations. |
| Lifecycle-blocking | Prevents definition of states, transitions, amendment, correction, retention or historical treatment. |
| Permission-blocking | Prevents allocation of actor rights or role-scoped administrative action. |
| Privacy-blocking | Prevents final audience, minimum-necessary-use or retention treatment. |
| Security-blocking | Prevents final trusted-action, control, audit or protection requirements. |
| Implementation-blocking | Prevents later technical mapping or operational allocation without changing constitutional authority. |
| Non-blocking | Requires later governance but does not prevent approval of the constitutional register boundary. |

## 5. Register Approval Effect

The Phase E1 register is complete as a constitutional inventory and is ready for founder review. EOG-01 through EOG-06 prevent approval as a final fully allocated accountability register. They do not require invented authority and do not invalidate the approved authority separations.

The remaining gaps constrain later lifecycle, permission, privacy, security, operational, analytical and implementation work. They must remain explicit until their named decisions or standards are approved.
