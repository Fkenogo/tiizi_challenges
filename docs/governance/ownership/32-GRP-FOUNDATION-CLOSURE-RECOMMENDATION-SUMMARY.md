# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 2 — Recommendation Summary

**Status:** Approval-ready recommendation summary; no decision approved

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Founder Review Summary

| Decision | Recommended — pending founder approval | Core reason | Principal trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| GFC-01 | **GFC-01-B — Administrative Authority authenticates Group creation within a narrow declared scope.** | Separates Community existence from participation and follows the EOG-02 creation boundary. | Requires durable protection against Administration becoming general Group Authority. |
| GFC-02 | **GFC-02-B — successor-continuous reassignment plus Charter-governed exceptional succession.** | Preserves one steward while grounding continuity within the Group. | Depends on a binding Charter and later Authority treatment for exceptional reassignment. |
| GFC-03 | **GFC-03-A — founding binding Charter.** | Supplies immediate, single and historically intelligible constitutional meaning. | Increases the significance of founding terms and later amendment governance. |
| GFC-04 | **GFC-04-B — Council capable of bounded Assignments of Responsibility.** | Makes collaborative support constitutionally useful without creating shared stewardship. | Requires strict separation of responsibility, Authority and stewardship. |
| GFC-05 | **GFC-05-A — permanent independent Council inventory entity.** | Preserves Council identity and supports later allocation traceability without allocating anything. | Requires an attributable EOG-05 amendment and a clear Information Subject boundary. |

## 2. Package Logic

The recommendations form one coherent constitutional model:

1. a creator originates Group creation;
2. a narrowly bounded existing Authority authenticates the creation event;
3. exactly one Accountable Steward relationship continues through successor-based governance;
4. a binding founding Charter supplies internal constitutional continuity rules;
5. a Council may receive bounded responsibility supporting, but never replacing, the steward; and
6. a permanent Council Entity ID preserves future traceability while allocating no relationship.
