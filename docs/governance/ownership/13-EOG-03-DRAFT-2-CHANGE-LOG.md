# EOG-03 Founder Review Draft 2 — Change Log

## 1. Purpose

This log records the constitutional precision refinements applied between [Founder Review Draft 1](11-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-DRAFT-1.md) and [Founder Review Draft 2](13-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-DRAFT-2.md).

The implementation contract is the [Phase 3 Independent Constitutional Review](12-EOG-03-PHASE-3-INDEPENDENT-CONSTITUTIONAL-REVIEW-AND-CHALLENGE.md). No change in this log approves EOG-03 or changes its architecture.

## 2. Critical Refinements

| Review Finding ID | Location updated | Constitutional reason | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| CR-01 | Sections 4, 6 and 7; Founder Checklist | A Governed Subject must not appear to exercise Authority. | Knowledge Authority consistently establishes Authoritative Meaning. Platform Knowledge and Knowledge Assets bear, preserve and communicate that meaning. Exercise and Wellness Activity Assets no longer appear as truth-establishing actors. |
| CR-02 | Sections 4 and 16; DN-08; Founder Checklist | Governed Review must not appear to establish canonical truth or replace Knowledge Authority. | Knowledge Authority is explicit between Governed Review and Authoritative Meaning. Governed Review supplies an approved basis only. |
| CR-03 | Sections 7 and 17; Drafting Notes; Founder Checklist | Discovery classifications and governed semantic vocabulary must not be collapsed. | Interests, Goals, Preferences, Discovery Tags and Discovery Categories remain outside Platform Knowledge. Taxonomy or Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge Assets remain distinct governed semantic concepts without defining taxonomy contents. |
