# EOG-05 Phase 2 — Architecture Approval Record

**Status:** Approved architectural baseline

**Approval date:** 2026-07-20

## 1. Record Purpose

This record closes the architecture decision prepared in the [EOG-05 Phase 1 Constitutional Register Architecture and Separation Blueprint](22-EOG-05-PHASE-1-CONSTITUTIONAL-REGISTER-ARCHITECTURE-AND-SEPARATION-BLUEPRINT.md). It approves the separation of constitutional entity inventory from constitutional relationship allocation and authorizes the inventory instrument to proceed to Draft 1 under the accompanying [Drafting Authorization Report](23-EOG-05-INVENTORY-REGISTER-DRAFTING-AUTHORIZATION-REPORT.md).

This approval establishes document architecture only. It does not classify a candidate, allocate a relationship, resolve a dependency, or amend approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 governance.

## 3. Approved Rationale

The separated architecture is approved because it:

- protects stable entity identity and classification from higher-frequency allocation amendments;
- distinguishes what Tiizi governs from who or what holds a declared accountability relationship;
- allows inventory drafting and eventual classification approval to progress without implying completeness of deferred allocations;
- preserves clearer approval, amendment and audit histories for each constitutional layer;
- reduces the risk that Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Custody, Administration or other EOG-01 relationships become shorthand for entity identity;
- supports future domains without requiring premature relationship allocation; and
- provides stronger traceability through stable Entity IDs and governing-source references.

The founder accepts the additional coordination obligations created by separation, subject to the approved cross-register constitutional contract.

## 4. Superseded Architectural Options

**Option A — Single Register** is not selected for the Entity Ownership programme. Its proposal to combine entity inventory, entity classification and relationship allocation in one evolving instrument is superseded by this approval.

The Phase 1 recommendation status is also superseded by this approval record. The Phase 1 document remains historical decision evidence and is not amended.

No constitutional content or historical record is retired by rejecting Option A.

## 5. Approved Cross-Register Constitutional Contract

The following contract is the approved architectural baseline for both instruments:

1. The Entity Ownership Register is authoritative for entity identity and classification.
2. The Entity Relationship Allocation Register is authoritative only for approved relationship declarations within its scope.
3. Every allocation declaration references exactly one current Entity ID.
4. The allocation register cannot create or redefine an entity.
5. The inventory cannot imply or allocate a relationship through classification wording.
6. Every relationship uses the approved EOG-01 term without redefinition.
7. Every approved allocation identifies its governing source, purpose and scope.
8. Deferred and Undefined treatment remains explicit and cannot be converted through inference.
9. A proposed classification change requires an impact assessment of all linked allocations before approval.
10. An allocation change does not alter entity identity unless separate constitutional governance expressly changes the inventory.
11. The instruments maintain separate status, approval and amendment traces.
12. No conflict may coexist silently; unresolved conflicts escalate under existing Governance Authority rules.

This contract governs architecture and separation. It does not create an allocation or approve the status of any candidate entity.

## 6. Instrument Boundaries

### Entity Ownership Register

The inventory instrument governs:

- canonical entity identification;
- Governed Subject classification;
- Information Subject treatment;
- constitutional definition and boundary;
- primary constitutional information category;
- governing-source traceability;
- approved consolidation or alias treatment; and
- explicit provisional classification where an approved source does not yet determine final treatment.

It does not allocate EOG-01 relationships.

### Entity Relationship Allocation Register

The allocation instrument governs:

- relationship declarations for inventory Entity IDs;
- declared purpose or authoritative fact;
- relationship status;
- approved holder where later governance establishes one;
- scope and boundary;
- governing decision or standard; and
- approval and amendment traceability.

It does not define or reclassify entities.

## 7. Identifier Status

The instruments shall be cited by their full titles until controlled identifier reconciliation occurs.

The short identifier **EOG-06** is not assigned to the Entity Relationship Allocation Register by this approval. Historical founder-planning material already uses EOG-06 for Recognition Authority. This record preserves that collision as unresolved and does not amend historical artefacts.

The full title **Entity Relationship Allocation Register** is the approved provisional reference.

## 8. Drafting Authorization Effect

This approval authorizes the Entity Ownership Register to proceed to Draft 1 under the controls in the [Drafting Authorization Report](23-EOG-05-INVENTORY-REGISTER-DRAFTING-AUTHORIZATION-REPORT.md).

The Entity Relationship Allocation Register is architecturally approved as the second instrument. Its operational drafting remains deferred until the inventory provides controlled Entity IDs and the applicable dependencies are sufficiently mature to support a useful allocation draft without inference.

## 10. Completion Statement

The Option B separated-register architecture and its cross-register constitutional contract are approved. Phase 2 architecture review is closed. Inventory Draft 1 is authorized; relationship-register drafting remains operationally deferred.
