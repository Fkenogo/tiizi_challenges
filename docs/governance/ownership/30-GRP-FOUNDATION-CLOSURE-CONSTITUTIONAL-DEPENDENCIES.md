## 6. Dependency Risks

- Deciding GFC-05 before GFC-04 could make inventory shape determine constitutional meaning.
- Deciding GFC-04 before GFC-02 could weaken singular accountability through collective-language drift.
- Deciding GFC-03 before the creation and stewardship boundaries could confuse Charter authorship with Charter Authority.
- Treating GRP-01 role vocabulary as an input could reverse the EOG-01 precedence of relationships over roles.

## 7. Dependency Result

The five questions form one coherent founder programme, but they should be prepared and reviewed in order. No external implementation or lifecycle decision is a prerequisite for constitutional options analysis.
