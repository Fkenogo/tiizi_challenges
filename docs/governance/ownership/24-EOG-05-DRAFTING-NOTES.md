# EOG-05 Entity Ownership Register Draft 1 — Drafting Notes

**Status:** Companion review artefact

**Date:** 2026-07-20

## 1. Purpose

These notes identify architectural observations, unresolved classification questions and assumptions deliberately avoided while preparing the [Entity Ownership Register Founder Review Draft 1](24-EOG-05-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER-DRAFT-1.md).

They do not extend the draft, approve a classification or allocate a relationship.

## 2. Drafting Method

The draft reconciles the [72-candidate Phase 0 inventory](21-EOG-05-CANDIDATE-ENTITY-INVENTORY.md) against approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 governance. The [Relationship Coverage Matrix](21-EOG-05-RELATIONSHIP-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md) was consulted only to distinguish established Governed Subject and Information Subject treatment from unresolved classification. No allocation columns or relationship holders were imported.

The [Phase 2 Drafting Authorization Report](23-EOG-05-INVENTORY-REGISTER-DRAFTING-AUTHORIZATION-REPORT.md) controls scope.

## 3. Classification Observations

### DN-01 — Proposed inclusion is not approval

Fifty-eight candidates are marked Proposed inclusion because approved sources support distinct treatment. Founder approval of the register remains required before those recommendations become approved inventory governance.

### DN-02 — Provisional identifiers are traceability devices

Fourteen candidates retain provisional Entity IDs. Their identifiers allow stable discussion during review; they do not establish permanent entity or row treatment.

### DN-03 — Entity and fact boundaries remain difficult at CG-01

Group purpose, Challenge purpose, Goal and Target are constitutionally meaningful. Approved governance does not yet determine whether each is an independent Governed Subject or an authoritative fact concerning Group or Challenge. The draft makes no choice.

### DN-04 — Composition inputs remain distinct without presumed entity status

Group Configuration and Community Context are recognized inputs to Constitutional Composition. Their use does not establish independent entity status, Authority or accountability. Both remain provisional under CG-02.

### DN-05 — Runtime Catalogue remains a function-classification question

Approved EOG-03 governance establishes the Runtime Catalogue boundary but does not require an independent entity. Draft 1 therefore preserves function, availability-fact and possible entity treatments without selecting among them.

### DN-06 — Knowledge subtype duplication remains open

Exercise Asset and Wellness Activity Asset are approved primary Platform Knowledge types. The draft does not decide whether they require separate Entity IDs or controlled subtype treatment beneath Activity Knowledge Asset.

### DN-07 — Historical reference and representation remain separate

Historical Knowledge reference describes a reference relationship. Historical Representation describes a subordinate representation. CG-05 must prevent their consolidation from losing that distinction while also preventing duplicate entity treatment.

### DN-08 — Template is governed but not yet permanently classified

Template is a governed composition guide and is not a Knowledge Asset. Approved governance does not yet settle whether it requires an independent inventory row or a controlled composition-guide classification.

### DN-09 — Creation Mechanism is not presumed to be an entity

Creation Mechanism is constitutionally recognized as a governed means. Draft 1 assigns a provisional Presentation Information category for review because it represents and arranges composition inputs, but CG-07 remains fully open on entity-versus-capability treatment.

### DN-10 — Stewardship Council remains an approved subject with open inventory treatment

EOG-02 recognizes Stewardship Council as a supporting Governed Subject. CG-08 remains open because the separated inventory must determine permanent entity treatment without importing unresolved Council relationships.

## 4. Primary-Category Observations

Every candidate has one proposed primary category so the draft can be structurally reviewed. A primary category describes the candidate's governed information purpose; it does not allocate Authority, visibility, access or accountability.

The following judgments merit founder attention:

- Group Charter and Stewardship Council use Participation Information because their primary purpose concerns the governed Group relationship.
- Challenge Policy uses Knowledge Information because it is a governed rule, while remaining distinct from Platform Knowledge.
- Recognition uses Derived Information because it acknowledges qualifying governed participation or outcome; its qualification governance remains deferred.
- Template uses Knowledge Information provisionally as governed composition guidance without becoming a Knowledge Asset.
- Creation Mechanism uses Presentation Information provisionally and remains subject to CG-07.
- Runtime Projection and Historical Representation use Presentation Information because each is subordinate representation, not source meaning.

Changing a provisional category during review does not resolve the associated classification gate unless founder governance expressly does so.

## 5. Approved-Alias Observation

No candidate is given an approved alias in Draft 1. Terms such as Community, Undertaking, Charter, activity definition and Applicable Policy express ontology, shorthand or contextual use; the approved corpus does not establish them as interchangeable canonical entity names.

## 6. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

Draft 1 does not assume:

- that all 72 candidates are independent entities;
- that an entity exercises a Platform Authority;
- that a Governed Subject classification establishes Accountable Stewardship;
- that Information Subject treatment establishes ownership, access or control;
- that a role title implies an EOG-01 relationship;
- that a current product representation defines constitutional identity;
- that a classification gate can be resolved through table design;
- that an approved Knowledge type requires a separate inventory row;
- that a governed function or mechanism is necessarily an entity;
- that a derived or presentation subject establishes its source truth;
- that historical intelligibility requires snapshots or versioning; or
- that a dependency authorizes lifecycle, permission, privacy, security or implementation design.
