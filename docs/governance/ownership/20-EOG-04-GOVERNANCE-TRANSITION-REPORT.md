## 5. Independent Review and Precision Refinements

The independent review found no critical constitutional defect and concluded that Draft 1 was ready after minor refinements. Draft 2 applied:

- RR-01: removal of unsupported adjacent Information Subject assertions;
- RR-02: restoration of the complete conceptual Authority chain;
- RR-03: alignment of Runtime Catalogue wording with EOG-03;
- RR-04: explicit Governance Authority escalation for unresolved authority conflicts; and
- RR-05: explicit attribution for representations claiming Challenge continuity.

No additional refinement was introduced during approval.
