# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 1 — Cross-option Consistency Review

**Status:** Comparative review; no option ranked or recommended

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This review tests the alternatives in the [Constitutional Options Paper](31-GRP-FOUNDATION-CLOSURE-CONSTITUTIONAL-OPTIONS-PAPER.md) as combinations rather than isolated decisions.

## 2. Cross-question Dependency Order

| Order | Question | Dependency reason |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | GFC-01 | Creation authentication defines initial Group truth. |
| 2 | GFC-02 | Continuing stewardship follows the initial creator consequence. |
| 3 | GFC-03 | Charter Authority and continuity depend on Group truth and stewardship separation. |
| 4 | GFC-04 | Council treatment must preserve the singular stewardship model. |
| 5 | GFC-05 | Inventory treatment follows Council constitutional meaning. |

## 4. Authority Consistency

### GFC-01-A

Uses Participation Authority for Group truth. Later documents must state that Group existence follows authenticated creation, not later participation.

### GFC-01-B

Uses Administrative Authority for Group truth. Later documents must prevent this from becoming general authority over Group identity, purpose, membership or stewardship.

Neither option changes the first Member or first Accountable Steward consequences. Neither permits Policy, Identity, creator origin or an interface to become the source Authority.

## 5. Stewardship Consistency

All GFC-02 options preserve one Accountable Steward relationship. Their differences concern continuity paths, not the number or meaning of the relationship.

No Council option may fill a continuity gap automatically. Any attempt to make Council membership a default successor would contradict EOG-02 and is excluded from the viable set.

## 6. Charter Consistency

The Charter models differ across two constitutional dimensions:

- when Charter meaning becomes applicable; and
- whether that meaning is binding, layered or declarative.

Whichever model is later selected must preserve one attributable Charter truth at each relevant historical context. Layered or adopted meaning cannot become competing Charter Authority.

## 7. Inventory Consistency

GFC-05 cannot determine GFC-04. A permanent row, consolidation or continued provisional status must follow the constitutional need established by the Council option.

GFC-05-A is required if later governance assigns relationships to the Council body itself. GFC-05-B remains possible where all substantive relationships concern the Group or separately identified members. GFC-05-C postpones rather than resolves this choice.

## 8. Option Conflicts

| Conflict | Reason |
|---|---|
| Shared or vacant stewardship with any GFC-02 option | Both contradict the approved singular relationship principle and are not viable options. |
| GFC-02-B plus GFC-03-D | A declarative non-binding Charter cannot establish Charter-governed succession. |
| GFC-04-B plus GFC-05-B where Council-body responsibility must be allocated | Consolidation removes the distinct allocation target required by the Council-body model. |
| GFC-05 decision before GFC-04 | Inventory form would bias or determine constitutional meaning. |
| GFC-01-B plus unrestricted Group Administration | Administrative creation Authority would expand beyond its declared truth scope. |

## 9. Review Result

The option set is internally coherent when the stated conditions are preserved. Two substantive incompatibilities require founder attention in a later decision pack: Charter-governed succession cannot rely on a non-binding Charter, and collective Council-body responsibility cannot be allocated without an independent inventory target.
