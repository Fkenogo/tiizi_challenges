# EOG-03 Founder Review Checklist — Draft 1

## 1. Purpose

This checklist identifies the constitutional statements requiring founder confirmation before [EOG-03 Draft 1](11-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-DRAFT-1.md) may proceed toward approval.

Checking an item during review records confirmation intent only. A separate founder approval record is required before EOG-03 becomes approved governance.

## 2. Constitutional Identity and Scope

- [ ] Confirm that EOG-03 governs Platform Knowledge meaning and boundaries rather than catalogue implementation.
- [ ] Confirm that the included scope is complete.
- [ ] Confirm that lifecycle, roles, permissions, privacy, security, calculations and implementation remain excluded.
- [ ] Confirm that EOG-03 creates no new Platform Authority and allocates no EOG-01 accountability relationship.

## 3. Platform Knowledge and Authoritative Meaning

- [ ] Confirm the definition of Platform Knowledge.
- [ ] Confirm Authoritative Meaning as the foundational doctrine immediately following Platform Knowledge.
- [ ] Confirm that Platform Knowledge establishes meaning rather than merely containing information.
- [ ] Confirm that downstream use depends on Authoritative Meaning without acquiring Authority over it.
- [ ] Confirm the prohibition on competing equal authorities for one canonical Knowledge meaning.

## 4. Platform Knowledge Assets

- [ ] Confirm Exercise Assets as primary Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm Wellness Activity Assets as primary Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that “primary” permits supporting governed Metric, Unit, Instructional, Safety and Relationship Knowledge under later standards.
- [ ] Confirm that individual catalogue records, content and classifications remain unapproved by EOG-03.

## 5. Authority Boundaries

- [ ] Confirm that Knowledge Authority establishes Authoritative Meaning.
- [ ] Confirm that Policy Authority remains responsible for governed rules and permitted use.
- [ ] Confirm that Calculation Authority remains responsible for approved Derived Truth.
- [ ] Confirm that Runtime Catalogue authority is an availability function, not a twelfth Platform Authority.
- [ ] Confirm that Administration, Operation and Presentation cannot redefine Platform Knowledge.

## 6. Group Experience

- [ ] Confirm the founder statement: “Groups own their governed experiences.”
- [ ] Confirm that this statement is a constitutional domain boundary rather than an accountability allocation.
- [ ] Confirm that Groups may compose but may not modify, fork or redefine Platform Knowledge.
- [ ] Confirm that Group configuration, Community context, participation and local objectives remain distinct from Platform Knowledge.

## 7. Constitutional Composition

- [ ] Confirm Constitutional Composition as the central EOG-03 doctrine.
- [ ] Confirm the four composition inputs: Platform Knowledge, applicable Policy, Group configuration and Community context.
- [ ] Confirm that composition is not copying.
- [ ] Confirm that composition is not inheritance.
- [ ] Confirm that composition is not modification.
- [ ] Confirm that composition is not forking.
- [ ] Confirm that no input transfers Authority through composition.
- [ ] Confirm that the resulting Group Challenge is constitutionally distinct from every input.

## 8. Layered Meaning

- [ ] Confirm the Layered Meaning doctrine.
- [ ] Confirm Platform Meaning as authoritative source meaning.
- [ ] Confirm Policy Meaning as governed permitted-use meaning.
- [ ] Confirm Community Meaning as local Group context and framing.
- [ ] Confirm Historical Meaning as the meaning applicable to prior governed participation.
- [ ] Confirm that no lower layer silently overrides or redefines a source layer.

## 9. Templates and Creation Mechanisms

- [ ] Confirm that Templates are governed composition guides.
- [ ] Confirm that Templates are not Knowledge Assets, Policy, Challenges or source Authorities.
- [ ] Confirm that the Challenge Wizard is an example of a governed Creation Mechanism.
- [ ] Confirm that a Creation Mechanism consumes governed inputs but establishes no constitutional truth through interface behavior.

## 10. Runtime and Historical Integrity

- [ ] Confirm the progression Runtime Catalogue → Runtime Projection → Historical Representation.
- [ ] Confirm that a Runtime Projection remains subordinate to its source Knowledge Asset and Runtime Catalogue authority.
- [ ] Confirm that Historical Representation preserves meaning without becoming a Knowledge fork.
- [ ] Confirm that the draft does not resolve snapshot, versioning, retention or lifecycle questions.

## 11. Future Knowledge Contribution

- [ ] Confirm the current Platform-generated Knowledge direction.
- [ ] Confirm future Trusted Contributor participation as a possible governed capability.
- [ ] Confirm the sequence Platform governance → Trusted Contributors → Governed Review → Authoritative Meaning → Platform Knowledge.
- [ ] Confirm that contribution may decentralise while truth remains governed.
- [ ] Confirm that “trusted” creates no current role, eligibility or Authority.
- [ ] Confirm that contributor workflows, permissions and lifecycle remain deferred.

## 12. Discovery and Personalisation

- [ ] Confirm that Interests are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that Goals are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that Preferences are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that Tags are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that Categories are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that these concepts belong to a future Discovery and Personalisation Governance Programme.
- [ ] Confirm that future discovery references may not redefine Platform Knowledge.

## 13. Pending Decisions and Status

- [ ] Confirm that KNW-02 remains pending.
- [ ] Confirm that KNW-03 remains pending.
- [ ] Confirm that KNW-04 remains pending.
- [ ] Confirm all ten [Drafting Notes](11-EOG-03-DRAFTING-NOTES.md), amending any wording that does not reflect founder intent.
- [ ] Confirm that Draft 1 remains non-governing until a separate founder approval record is created.
