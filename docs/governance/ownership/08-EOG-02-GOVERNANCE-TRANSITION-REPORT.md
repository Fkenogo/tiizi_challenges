# EOG-02 — Governance Transition Report

## 1. Purpose

This report transitions the approved EOG-02 constitutional principles into the remaining Entity Ownership Governance programme. It identifies later reconciliation work and preserves deferred questions without amending downstream governance.

## 2. Transition Status

EOG-02 is approved and complete as recorded in the [Founder Approval Record](07-EOG-02-FOUNDER-APPROVAL-RECORD.md). EOG-01 remains its mandatory semantic foundation. The approved EOG-02 principles may inform later governance; deferred matters may not be treated as approved allocations, lifecycle rules, permissions or implementation requirements.

## 3. Approved Baseline Entering Later Governance

Later work must preserve these boundaries:

- a Group is created and may exist independently of participation;
- creation establishes initial identity and founding purpose;
- the creator becomes the first Member and first Accountable Steward;
- exactly one Accountable Steward relationship exists for a Group at a time;
- stewardship may continue beyond the creator and may later be reassigned or voluntarily relinquished under approved governance;
- a Stewardship Council may support but never replace Accountable Stewardship;
- Membership, Participation, Administration, Custody and Accountable Stewardship remain distinct;
- Group inactivity does not by itself establish poor governance;
- a Group Charter is a Governed Subject;
- moderation must derive from existing constitutional relationships.
