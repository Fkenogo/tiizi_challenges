## 8. Additional Founder Notes

### GFC-01

**Founder Note:**

Administrative Authority refers to the Platform’s constitutional Administrative Authority rather than a specific operational role or individual. The Authority may be exercised through whatever administrative actor or governed mechanism the Platform later authorizes. Its constitutional scope remains limited to authenticating the Group-creation event and its founding facts.

### GFC-04

**Founder Rationale:**

Tiizi Groups are intended to remain voluntary, self-regulating communities built on mutual respect, shared purpose and accountability to one another. Governance should remain intentionally lightweight. The Stewardship Council exists only to support the singular Accountable Steward where appropriate and must never introduce unnecessary governance layers or reduce the voluntary nature of Group participation.

### GFC-05

**Founder Rationale:**

Permanent constitutional recognition of the Stewardship Council exists solely to preserve constitutional consistency whenever a Council is established. It does not imply that every Group must establish, maintain or continuously operate a Stewardship Council throughout its lifecycle.
