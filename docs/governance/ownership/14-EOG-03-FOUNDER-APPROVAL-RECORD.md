## 2. Constitutional Purpose

EOG-03 governs the constitutional meaning and boundary of Platform Knowledge. It preserves one authoritative meaning boundary, distinguishes Platform Knowledge from adjacent domains, establishes Constitutional Composition, and ensures that Templates, Creation Mechanisms, runtime representations, historical representations and future contributions remain subordinate to governed truth.

EOG-03 applies the approved accountability semantics of EOG-01 and the approved Group boundaries of EOG-02. It creates no new Platform Authority and allocates no Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Delegate or role.

## 3. Founder-Approved Doctrines

The founder approves the following doctrines as expressed in the approved standard:

1. **Platform Knowledge** is the body of authoritative, reusable meaning governed by Tiizi for declared platform purposes.
2. **Authoritative Meaning** is established by Knowledge Authority for a declared Knowledge Asset and purpose. Platform Knowledge and Knowledge Assets bear, preserve and communicate that meaning; they do not exercise Authority.
3. **Meaning precedes representation.** Templates, Runtime Projections, Historical Representations, Presentation, documentation, interfaces and records do not establish meaning merely by representing it.
4. **Constitutional Composition** is a general constitutional doctrine currently applied to Version 2 Group Challenges. It combines Platform Knowledge, applicable Policy, Group configuration and Community context without copying, inheritance, modification, forking or Authority transfer.
5. **Layered Meaning** distinguishes Platform Meaning, Policy Meaning and Community Meaning. Historical Meaning preserves their historically applicable meaning and is not an independent Authority or source input.
6. **Knowledge Authority boundary** remains distinct from Governance, Policy, Participation, Participant, Acceptance, Calculation, Administrative, Operational and Presentation Authority.
7. **Runtime boundary** preserves one Runtime Catalogue function for governed availability within the existing Knowledge Authority boundary. Runtime Projection remains a subordinate representation.
8. **Historical Representation boundary** permits preservation of the broader constitutional composition without creating a Knowledge fork or approving snapshot, versioning, retention or lifecycle rules.
9. **Discovery boundary** keeps Interests, Goals, Preferences, Discovery Tags and Discovery Categories outside Platform Knowledge while distinguishing them from governed Taxonomy or Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge Assets.
10. **Future governed contribution direction** permits future attributable contribution and Governed Review while preserving Knowledge Authority as the Authority to Establish Authoritative Meaning.

The founder also approves the supporting integrity, Template, Creation Mechanism, Group Experience, contribution and prohibited-inference boundaries contained in the approved standard.
