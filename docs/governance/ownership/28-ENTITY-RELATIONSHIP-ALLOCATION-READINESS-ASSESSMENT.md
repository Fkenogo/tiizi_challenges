# Post-EOG-05 — Entity Relationship Allocation Readiness Assessment

**Status:** Planning assessment; CG-08 classification resolved; no drafting authorized

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This assessment determines when the Entity Relationship Allocation Register can safely enter drafting after approval of the [EOG-05 Entity Ownership Register](27-EOG-05-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER-APPROVED.md).

It applies the [cross-register constitutional contract](23-EOG-05-ARCHITECTURE-APPROVAL-RECORD.md#5-approved-cross-register-constitutional-contract) and the EOG-01 accountability vocabulary. It does not allocate a relationship, resolve a pending decision, amend EOG-05 or authorize drafting.

## 2. Readiness Question

The allocation register must eventually identify declared holders or explicit pending treatment for EOG-01 relationships without creating or redefining an entity. Safe drafting therefore requires enough stability to answer two questions separately:

1. **What entity or approved information subject does the allocation concern?**
2. **What constitutionally approved relationship may be declared, for what scope and purpose?**

EOG-05 answers the first question for 58 Proposed inclusion rows and preserves 14 Provisional rows: 13 under CG-01 through CG-07 and Recognition under MOT-01. It does not yet make every proposed allocation constitutionally supportable.

## 3. Classification Method

| Classification | Meaning for this assessment |
|---|---|
| Allocation blocker | Prevents a reliable allocation for one or more required rows. A blocker is **start-critical** only where it threatens Entity ID stability, a core Authority-to-establish-truth boundary or the minimum constitutional usefulness of Draft 1. |
| Allocation influence | May change scope, timing, lifecycle applicability, role mapping, custody, administration, operation, presentation or downstream use, but can be recorded as an explicit dependency during initial drafting. |
| No allocation impact | Does not change an allocation relationship within the current EOG-05 inventory. It may govern behavior, terminology, calculation method, implementation or a future inventory amendment. |

“Dependency owner” in the accompanying matrix means the governance decision family or future standard responsible for resolving the dependency. It does not mean an EOG-01 Accountable Steward or legal owner.

## 5. Start-Critical Blockers

The following must mature before operational drafting begins:

1. **Classification closure:** CG-01 through CG-07 must be resolved or an explicit founder decision must approve a restricted Draft 1 that omits allocation rows for every Provisional candidate. CG-08 no longer contributes to this blocker.
2. **Group continuity minimum:** Group creation authentication and the constitutional basis for continuing Group stewardship must be decided without converting role titles into relationships.
3. **Challenge core:** CHL-01 must resolve the constitutional establishment of Challenge identity, purpose and Goal and the accountable-stewardship model.
4. **Activity integrity:** ACT-03 and ACT-04 must resolve correction and Verification authority boundaries sufficiently for the affected rows.
5. **Recognition:** MOT-01 must resolve whether and how Recognition becomes qualifying truth before its allocation row can be drafted.
6. **Knowledge-boundary minimum:** KNW-04 and the knowledge decisions needed to close CG-03 through CG-06 must preserve Knowledge, Discovery and representation boundaries.
7. **Drafting authorization:** Governance Authority must expressly authorize allocation-register Draft 1 under the existing full-title identifier discipline or a later approved identifier treatment.

These conditions define the recommended safe-start milestone. They do not require every lifecycle, permission or implementation decision to be complete.

## 6. Completion Blockers and Influences

After safe drafting begins, the register may retain explicit dependency markers for lifecycle- and role-sensitive relationships. Final approval of a complete allocation baseline should wait until:

- GRP-01 through GRP-04 support durable Group administration, delegation and lifecycle-sensitive custody;
- CHL-02 through CHL-04 support Challenge continuity, amendment and participation-sensitive allocations;
- KNW-02 and KNW-03 support historical, custody, administration and operational Knowledge allocations;
- RSK-01, RSK-03 and RSK-04 support method-sensitive derived, finalization and correction allocations;
- NTF-01, ANL-01 and OPS-01 support Notification, analytical and operational rows;
- applicable social, identity, roles and permissions, privacy and security standards define their own bounded effects; and
- no allocation remains dependent on an unresolved Authority-to-establish-truth decision.

RSK-02 and implementation-only questions need not block constitutional allocation where they do not change a relationship holder or scope.

## 7. Constitutional Restraints

The roadmap must preserve that:

- inventory inclusion is not allocation;
- a Provisional Entity ID cannot silently become permanent through an allocation;
- Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Custody, Administration, Operation and Presentation remain distinct;
- role titles do not imply relationships;
- lifecycle behavior does not establish Authority;
- Calculation Authority remains distinct from Ranking method and presentation;
- Presentation Authority does not establish Recognition, Notification or analytical truth;
- Platform Authorities are not organizational roles; and
- the allocation register may reference approved entities but may not amend their definitions or classifications.

## 8. Readiness Verdict

**Ready with prerequisites; operational drafting should not begin yet.**

EOG-05 supplies the necessary inventory baseline, but classification gates and core Group, Challenge, activity-integrity, Knowledge and Recognition decisions remain insufficiently mature. The earliest safe start is the **Classification Closure and Core Allocation Authority Milestone** defined in the [Allocation Readiness Decision](28-ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP-ALLOCATION-READINESS-DECISION.md).
