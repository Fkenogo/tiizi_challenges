# Post-EOG-05 — Recommended Governance Sequence for Entity Relationship Allocation

**Status:** Recommended sequence; pending Governance Authority approval

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Objective

This sequence identifies the minimum safe order for maturing dependencies before drafting the Entity Relationship Allocation Register. It does not decide any dependency or authorize drafting.

## 2. Sequencing Principles

1. Entity classification precedes allocation.
2. Authority-to-establish-truth boundaries precede stewardship, custody and administration allocations concerning that truth.
3. Foundational decisions precede lifecycle-sensitive refinements.
4. Role vocabulary follows constitutional relationships and never substitutes for them.
5. Cross-family decisions are resolved together where separate treatment would produce contradictory allocations.
6. Drafting may begin before every influence is resolved only when unresolved cells remain explicit and cannot alter Entity identity or core Authority.

## 3. Ordered Roadmap

### Stage 0 — Preserve the approved baseline

Status: Complete.

- EOG-01 supplies mandatory relationship semantics.
- EOG-02 supplies approved Group constitutional boundaries.
- EOG-03 supplies approved Platform Knowledge boundaries.
- EOG-04 supplies approved Challenge boundaries.
- EOG-05 supplies the approved entity inventory and cross-register Entity IDs.

No approved standard is reopened.

### Stage 1 — Close core Group and Challenge decisions

Resolve, without designing lifecycle or implementation:

1. Group creation authentication and continuing stewardship basis;
2. GRP-02 stewardship continuity sufficient for allocation;
3. CHL-01 identity, purpose, Goal, Authority and stewardship; and
4. the Charter allocation questions needed to distinguish Group truth from Charter truth.

Outcome required: core Group and Challenge truth and Accountable Stewardship can be declared without relying on role names.

### Stage 2 — Close classification gates

Resolve CG-01 through CG-07 in dependency-aware clusters. CG-08 has already been resolved by the attributable EOG-05 amendment:

| Cluster | Gates | Preceding decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Group and Challenge facts/context | CG-01, CG-02, CG-07 | Stage 1; EOG-02 and EOG-04 boundaries |
| Knowledge and runtime | CG-03, CG-04, CG-06 | KNW-04 and EOG-03 boundaries |
| Historical reference and representation | CG-05 | CHL-03, KNW-02 and KNW-03 boundary decisions |

Outcome required: every permanent allocation target is an approved inventory entity, and every consolidated concept is excluded from independent allocation.

### Stage 3 — Close activity-integrity and Recognition blockers

Resolve:

1. ACT-03 correction and deletion authority boundaries;
2. ACT-04 Verification authority and downstream-effect boundaries; and
3. MOT-01 Recognition qualification and final classification boundary.

Outcome required: Submission Intent, acceptance, Verification, correction, Evidence Eligibility, Derived Truth and Recognition can receive separate allocation treatment without Authority leakage.

### Stage 4 — Record explicit drafting authorization

Governance Authority should approve a dedicated Draft 1 authorization that:

- names the approved EOG-05 register as the only entity-identity source;
- retains the existing full-title identifier discipline unless separately reconciled;
- defines required allocation columns using EOG-01 vocabulary;
- prohibits allocation through role, access, possession or implementation shorthand;
- requires explicit dependency markers for unresolved influences;
- forbids the allocation register from changing entity classification; and
- defines a completion gate distinct from the safe-start gate.

**The Entity Relationship Allocation Register may begin Draft 1 only after Stage 4.**

### Stage 5 — Draft the stable constitutional core

Draft only after authorization. Begin with approved entities and relationships whose Authority and accountability basis is sufficiently mature.

Retain explicit dependency treatment for unresolved lifecycle, role, privacy, security and operational influences. Do not create blank or inferred allocations.

### Stage 6 — Mature completion dependencies

Before final approval, reconcile the draft against:

- GRP-01, GRP-03 and GRP-04;
- CHL-02 through CHL-04;
- KNW-02 and KNW-03;
- RSK-01, RSK-03 and RSK-04;
- IDP, SOC and NTF decisions affecting inventory rows;
- ANL-01 and OPS-01; and
- later roles and permissions, privacy and security standards where they constrain allocation scope.

RSK-02 and implementation-only questions may remain outside the constitutional allocation register where they do not alter a relationship.

### Stage 7 — Independent review and founder approval

Validate:

- one unambiguous Entity ID per allocation row;
- every allocation uses an EOG-01 relationship without redefinition;
- no role title or system acts as constitutional shorthand;
- every Authority allocation uses only the eleven approved Platform Authorities;
- unresolved dependencies remain visible;
- stewardship, custody, administration, operation and presentation remain separated; and
- the allocation register and EOG-05 remain independently amendable and traceable.

Only then should founder approval be requested.

## 4. Parallel Work

The following may proceed in parallel where constitutional boundaries are preserved:

- GRP and CHL lifecycle analysis after their foundational decisions;
- KNW lifecycle and historical work after Knowledge classification decisions;
- RSK analysis after the Activity authority chain remains fixed;
- social, Notification, analytics and operations governance for their affected rows; and
- preparation of review criteria that allocates nothing.

Parallel work must not silently satisfy another programme's decision.

## 5. Sequence Result

The recommended order is:

```text
Approved EOG-05 inventory
        ↓
Core Group and Challenge decisions
        ↓
CG-01 through CG-07 closure
        ↓
ACT-03, ACT-04 and MOT-01 closure
        ↓
Explicit allocation Draft 1 authorization
        ↓
Stable-core allocation draft
        ↓
Lifecycle, role and operational reconciliation
        ↓
Independent review and founder approval
```

This is a readiness sequence, not an approval of any outcome within the listed decisions.
