# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 0 — Founder Decision Blueprint

**Status:** Drafting blueprint; no options or recommendations approved

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Blueprint Objective

This blueprint defines how a later founder decision pack should analyze GFC-01 through GFC-05. It does not draft GRP-02, select options or request approval.

## 2. Required Decision-Pack Structure

For each GFC question, the future pack should contain:

1. constitutional question;
2. approved baseline;
3. constitutional boundary and non-goals;
4. every viable constitutional option;
5. option-specific strengths and weaknesses;
6. EOG-01 relationship analysis;
7. EOG-02 consistency analysis;
8. EOG-05 entity and classification impact;
9. dependency consequences;
10. reversibility and amendment burden;
11. founder oversight implications;
12. recommended option labelled **Recommended — pending founder approval**;
13. reasons alternatives were not recommended; and
14. blank founder decision, notes and approval-date fields.

## 3. Decision Criteria

| Criterion | Question for evaluation |
|---|---|
| Constitutional legitimacy | Does the option use only approved concepts and an existing Platform Authority? |
| Singular accountability | Does it preserve exactly one Accountable Steward relationship? |
| Relationship separation | Does it keep Authority, stewardship, Custody, Administration, Delegation and Membership distinct? |
| Continuity | Can the Group outlive its creator without silent Authority or stewardship transfer? |
| Attribution | Are creation, reassignment, Charter and Council actions attributable without designing workflow? |
| Historical integrity | Can prior Group and Charter meaning remain historically intelligible? |
| Group-first coherence | Does the option preserve Group as persistent Community rather than equating existence with activity? |
| Role neutrality | Does it avoid using a role name as an allocation answer? |
| Implementation neutrality | Would the outcome remain valid across technologies and organizational models? |
| Inventory stability | Does it clarify rather than distort EOG-05 Entity boundaries? |
| Future extensibility | Can later membership, Challenge and Council governance build upon it without reopening the foundation? |

## 4. Recommended Analysis Order

### Decision block A — Existence and continuity

1. GFC-01 creation authentication.
2. GFC-02 stewardship continuity.

These questions should be analyzed together because creation establishes the initial relationship and continuity governs its later preservation.

### Decision block B — Internal constitutional instruments

3. GFC-03 Charter foundations.
4. GFC-04 Stewardship Council treatment.

These questions use the continuity model without replacing it.

### Decision block C — Inventory disposition

5. GFC-05 CG-08 readiness.

Inventory disposition follows constitutional meaning. It must not determine that meaning.

## 5. Recommended Founder Sessions

| Session | Questions | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Session 1 | GFC-01 and GFC-02 | Decide existence authentication and continuing stewardship foundation. |
| Session 2 | GFC-03, GFC-04 and GFC-05 | Decide Charter and Council foundations, then determine CG-08 disposition. |

Two sessions are recommended to prevent inventory classification pressure from biasing the foundational accountability decisions.

## 6. Required Scenario Set

The future options paper should test every viable option against:

- Group creation without later participation;
- creator continuing as steward;
- reassignment to a willing successor;
- voluntary relinquishment without immediate successor;
- attempted delegation of stewardship;
- Administrator or Council claim to stewardship by role;
- initial Charter and later amendment;
- Member disagreement with Charter meaning;
- Council support and Council disagreement; and
- CG-08 retention or consolidation consequences.

Scenarios test doctrine. They do not approve states, transitions, procedures or permissions.

## 7. Drafting Gates

Phase 1 decision-options drafting may begin only if it confirms:

- EOG-01 vocabulary will not be redefined;
- EOG-02 approved principles will not be reopened;
- no new Platform Authority will be introduced;
- no relationship will be allocated;
- no lifecycle or role decision will be implied;
- GFC-04 precedes GFC-05 analysis; and
- every recommendation remains explicitly pending founder approval.

## 8. Blueprint Result

The decision programme is architecturally ready for Phase 1 options analysis. It is not ready for founder approval because viable options, trade-offs and recommendations have not yet been produced.
