# EOG-01 — Platform Accountability Framework Consistency Review

## 1. Purpose

This review validates the [Platform Accountability Framework](04-PLATFORM-ACCOUNTABILITY-FRAMEWORK.md) and [Accountability Relationship Matrix](04-ACCOUNTABILITY-RELATIONSHIP-MATRIX.md) against Tiizi's conceptual and constitutional baseline.

It reports terminology conflicts, overlaps and approval dependencies. It does not amend existing governance or allocate an accountability relationship.

## 3. Overall Verdict

**Verdict:** Coherent as a universal, non-allocative accountability vocabulary, with founder approval required for the new Contributor, Delegate and Delegating party terms and for the formal treatment of “Steward” and “Subject.”

The framework preserves the constitutional separations among Authority, Accountable stewardship, custody, administration, operation, presentation, origin and downstream use. It adds no Platform Authority type and makes no allocation.

## 7. Domain Standard Alignment

### Profile

The Profile Domain already uses Information subject, Attributable originator, Accountable steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter and Downstream user. The framework preserves those meanings and assigns none of them.

### Group

The Group Domain distinguishes Member, Participant, Administrator, Custodian, Operator and Presenter. The framework does not choose Group Authority, stewardship, administration or custody. EOG-02 remains pending.

### Challenge

The Challenge Domain distinguishes Participant, Attributable originator, Authority, Custodian, Administrator, Operator and Presenter. The framework preserves those distinctions and does not decide Challenge identity, purpose, Goal or stewardship. EOG-03 remains pending.

### Activity Event

The Activity Event Domain distinguishes Participant origin, Acceptance Authority, Evidence Eligibility and Calculation Authority. The framework's Contributor and Delegate concepts do not alter that chain. Verification and correction remain pending under EOG-04 and EOG-05.

### Knowledge Asset

The Knowledge Asset Domain already uses “contributor” descriptively for source material. The framework formalizes Contributor as a non-approval relationship consistent with that usage. It does not allocate Knowledge stewardship or Runtime Catalogue custody.

## 9. Relationship Completeness Review

The required relationships—Authority, Steward, Custodian, Operator, Contributor, Participant, Subject and Delegate—are all defined.

The following additional relationships are demonstrably necessary because the existing constitutional baseline already uses or requires them:

- Authority to establish truth;
- Administrator;
- Governed subject;
- Information subject;
- Attributable originator;
- Delegating party;
- Presenter;
- Downstream user.

No separate Owner, Reviewer, Approver, Controller, Processor, Auditor or technical-system relationship is added. Those terms may later describe a role, task, legal concept or specialized responsibility, but the current baseline does not require them as universal constitutional accountability relationships.

## 11. Prior EOG-01 Decision Relationship

The prior Founder Accountability Decision Pack asks how Accountable stewardship and custody should later be allocated across 63 register rows. Its recommended hybrid remains pending founder approval.

This framework precedes that allocation choice. It defines what the relationships mean before a method assigns them. Approval of the framework would not approve the prior Option D recommendation, any register allocation or any EOG-02 through EOG-06 outcome.

For traceability, later governance should distinguish:

- **EOG-01 framework approval:** universal relationship semantics;
- **EOG-01 allocation-pattern approval:** method for applying stewardship and custody to register rows;
- **entity allocation decisions:** actual parties assigned to specific Governed subjects.

## 12. Opportunities for Later Alignment

1. Add approved framework terms to the Platform Domain and Terminology Standard and Constitutional Glossary.
2. Use “Accountable steward” consistently in formal registers.
3. Use “Governed subject” and “Information subject” instead of unqualified Subject.
4. Add Contributor, Delegate and Delegating party only after founder approval.
5. Reference the framework from the Entity Ownership Register before allocation begins.
6. Preserve the distinction between delegation of Authority, assignment of responsibility and reassignment of stewardship.
7. Ensure future Roles and Permissions governance treats a role as a bundle of separately declared relationships rather than an automatic source of Authority.
8. Ensure future custody standards do not treat technical storage as constitutional custody.
