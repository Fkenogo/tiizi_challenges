# EOG-05 Phase 0 — Recommended Drafting Scope and Exclusions

**Status:** Proposed drafting contract; not approved Entity Ownership governance

**Date:** 2026-07-20

## 1. Drafting Objective

A future EOG-05 Entity Ownership Register should reconcile candidate subjects and constitutional relationship treatment across approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 without allocating any relationship that remains pending, undefined or out of scope.

The first draft should be a constitutional reconciliation register. It should not present itself as a fully allocated operational accountability register.

## 2. Recommended Included Scope

The future draft should include:

1. constitutional purpose and precedence;
2. mandatory EOG-01 relationship definitions by reference, without redefinition;
3. an explicit distinction among Approved, Deferred, Undefined and Out of Scope;
4. a normalized candidate-subject inventory grounded in approved governance;
5. one row for every approved or provisionally retained Governed Subject candidate;
6. Information Subject treatment separate from Governed Subject treatment;
7. Authority to Establish Truth by named authoritative fact, including approved negative Authority boundaries;
8. Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Contributor, Participant, Delegate, Presenter, Downstream User and Attributable Originator treatment;
9. separate declarations where one candidate contains more than one authoritative fact;
10. traceability to EOG-01, EOG-02, EOG-03, EOG-04 and the controlling founder decision;
11. explicit dependency cells for GRP, CHL, ACT, KNW and RSK decisions;
12. a classification and consolidation record for concepts that should not become duplicate rows;
13. cross-domain relationship checks; and
14. a readiness status distinguishing structural completeness from allocation completeness.

## 3. Recommended Exclusions

The future draft must not:

- allocate a new Platform Authority or create a twelfth Authority;
- infer Authority from a Governed Subject, Accountable Steward, role, access, presentation, possession or implementation mechanism;
- allocate Accountable Stewardship beyond already approved treatment;
- name a Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Delegate, Presenter or organizational role where governance remains pending;
- resolve CHL-01 through CHL-04;
- resolve ACT-03 or ACT-04;
- resolve GRP-01 through GRP-04;
- resolve KNW-02 through KNW-04;
- resolve RSK-01 through RSK-04;
- define lifecycle states, transitions, correction, retention, deletion, archival or versioning;
- define roles, permissions, privacy or security controls;
- define formulas, scoring, Ranking, Streak, Recognition or Reward rules;
- define schemas, APIs, storage, services, interfaces or implementation mapping;
- treat the existing 63-row draft register as approved source governance;
- convert a doctrine, aggregate, condition or representation label into an entity merely for completeness; or
- alter approved EOG-01 through EOG-04 wording.

## 4. Candidate Classification Gates

Before permanent row identifiers are frozen, the drafting programme should record an explicit disposition for:

| Gate | Question | Permitted Phase 0 outcome | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| CG-01 | Are Group purpose, Challenge purpose, Goal and Target independent Governed Subjects or authoritative facts concerning Group/Challenge? | Preserve as provisional candidates pending classification. | Assume each fact needs its own Accountable Steward. |
| CG-02 | Are Group Configuration and Community Context independent information objects or grouped Challenge/Group information? | Preserve as provisional composition-input candidates. | Treat Group contribution as Authority over Challenge or Knowledge. |
| CG-03 | Does Runtime Catalogue require a subject row, function row or only runtime-availability fact rows? | Preserve the function and availability distinction. | Treat Runtime Catalogue as an Authority, actor or role. |
| CG-04 | Are Exercise Asset and Wellness Activity Asset separate rows or controlled subtypes of Activity Knowledge Asset? | Preserve both approved primary types and avoid duplicate allocations. | Create three equal rows for one authoritative asset fact without a classification rule. |
| CG-05 | How do Historical Knowledge reference and Historical Representation differ in the register? | Preserve reference relationship versus representation-object distinction. | Merge them in a way that makes representation an independent Authority. |
| CG-06 | Is Template an independent Governed Subject or a governed composition-guide subtype? | Retain a candidate row until Template governance decides. | Treat Template as a Knowledge Asset or Challenge. |

| CG-08 | Does Stewardship Council require its own complete relationship row? | Preserve its approved status as supporting Governed Subject with unresolved relationships. | Treat Council membership as Accountable Stewardship. |

These gates classify register scope. They do not allocate accountability or resolve the underlying domain decisions.

1. Purpose
2. Status and approval boundary
3. Constitutional precedence
4. Interpretation and status rules
5. Relationship-column definitions
6. Candidate classification record
7. Human and Identity subjects
8. Group and Community subjects
9. Challenge and composition-context subjects
10. Activity, evidence and event subjects
11. Derived, analytical and presentation subjects
12. Knowledge subjects
13. Platform-control and mechanism subjects

15. Authority separation checks
16. Accountability-allocation gaps
17. Lifecycle, permission, privacy and security dependencies
18. Decision traceability
19. Validation checklist
20. Governance status and amendment boundary

This architecture is a recommendation only. It creates no constitutional clause and no register row.

## 6. Drafting Controls

Every future row should pass these controls:

| Control | Required test |
|---|---|
| Subject test | Does an approved source establish or support this Governed Subject, or is the row explicitly provisional? |
| Information test | Is the Information Subject named without implying ownership, access or control? |
| Truth test | Is one approved Platform Authority named per authoritative fact, or is the gap explicit? |
| Stewardship test | Is Accountable Stewardship separate from Authority, Custody and Administration? |
| Custody test | Is custody purpose-limited and explicitly allocated or deferred? |
| Context test | Are Contributor and Participant used only as non-Authority contextual relationships? |
| Delegation test | Is Delegation distinct from responsibility assignment and Stewardship Reassignment? |
| Presentation test | Does Presenter remain subordinate to source truth? |
| Use test | Does downstream use preserve source Authority and purpose? |
| Deferral test | Does every unresolved cell name its blocking decision or later standard? |
| Duplication test | Do subtype, representation and relationship rows avoid competing truth or duplicate stewardship? |

## 7. Identifier Discipline

Historical planning material used **EOG-05** for correction governance. The present programme uses the full title **EOG-05 — Entity Ownership Register Readiness and Scope Review**.

This Phase 0 review must not resolve or rewrite the historical identifier collision. Future citations should use the full title until controlled decision-register reconciliation occurs.

## 9. Recommended Scope Verdict

**Ready with prerequisites.** A reconciled Draft 1 may be prepared using provisional classifications and explicit Deferred or Undefined cells. Permanent row structure should not be frozen until CG-01 through CG-08 receive controlled classification treatment.
