## 5. RR-03 — Entity-versus-Context Test

Draft 2 retains four separate rows after applying this test:

1. **Distinct governed matter:** Does the row concern a matter that can be referenced independently without substituting for the adjacent row?
2. **Different Information Subject:** Does one row concern the relationship while the other concerns the Person in that context?
3. **Non-substitution:** Would merging the rows make relationship truth and Person-in-context designation indistinguishable?
4. **No allocation effect:** Can the distinction remain valid without granting Authority, Stewardship, participation rights or another relationship?

### Membership relationship and Member

GRP-02 concerns the governed relation connecting a Person and Group. GRP-03 concerns the Person as designated through that particular relation. Neither can replace the other without collapsing relationship truth into a contextual designation.

### Challenge participation relationship and Participant

CHL-05 concerns the governed relation connecting a Person and Challenge. CHL-06 concerns the Person as designated through that particular relation. Neither can replace the other, and Participant remains distinct from the EOG-01 Participant relationship allocation that the future allocation register may record.

This test justifies proposed separate inventory treatment only. It allocates no relationship and creates no lifecycle.

## 6. RR-04 — Concept-versus-Asset Boundaries

Draft 2 applies one reciprocal rule to three pairs:

| Governed concept | Meaning-bearing asset | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Activity | Activity Knowledge Asset | Activity is the canonical concept; the Asset is the identifiable governed item that bears, preserves and communicates its meaning. |
| Metric | Metric Knowledge Asset | Metric is the canonical measurement concept; the Asset bears, preserves and communicates its meaning. |
| Unit | Unit Knowledge Asset | Unit is the canonical unit concept; the Asset bears, preserves and communicates its meaning. |

The distinction does not define cardinality, representation, lifecycle, storage or implementation. CG-04 remains open for Exercise Asset and Wellness Activity Asset subtype treatment.

## 8. OI-01 — Prospective Entity Admission Test

The following test is introduced as a drafting aid for later review. It is not applied retroactively in Phase 5 and changes no Draft 2 classification.

| Candidate kind | Admission question | Non-entity warning |
|---|---|---|
| Entity | Is it governed as a distinct subject with identity and boundaries that remain meaningful independently? | Importance alone does not create entity identity. |
| Relationship | Is the relationship itself governed distinctly from each party and contextual designation? | A relationship label must not become an actor or role. |
| Fact | Does governance require the fact to be a distinct information object rather than information concerning another entity? | An authoritative fact does not automatically need its own entity row. |
| Function | Is the governed function itself an Information Subject, or is only its result governed? | A function is not an Authority, actor or entity by operation alone. |
| Mechanism | Does approved governance establish independent constitutional identity beyond being a means? | A mechanism or capability is not an entity merely because it produces one. |
| Representation | Does the representation require distinct identity while remaining subordinate to source truth? | A copy, projection or display cannot become a competing source entity. |

Later use of this test requires attributable review. It cannot silently change Draft 2.

Draft 2 does not assume:

- a final Information Subject for any RR-01 row;
- Recognition qualification, calculation or presentation treatment;
- that a Person-in-context designation allocates the Participant relationship;
- Knowledge Asset cardinality for Activity, Metric or Unit;
- Policy applicability, lifecycle or amendment rules;
- that the prospective Entity Admission Test approves or removes a row;
- an Entity Relationship Allocation Register entry; or
- any implementation structure.
