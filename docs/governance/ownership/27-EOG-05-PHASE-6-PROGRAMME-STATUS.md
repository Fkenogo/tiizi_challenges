## 2. Approved Inventory Baseline

The amended approved register contains 72 inventory candidates: 58 Proposed inclusion classifications, 13 Provisional classifications under CG-01 through CG-07 and one additional Provisional Recognition classification under MOT-01.

Approval governs the inventory treatment and its restraints. It does not declare every candidate finally classified and does not allocate accountability relationships.

## 3. Current Governance Effect

EOG-05 supplies the current constitutional entity inventory for later governance. Future standards must preserve its Entity IDs, definitions, categories and boundaries unless an attributable amendment follows resolution of a named dependency.

The inventory may inform later governance. It cannot be used as evidence that a relationship holder, permission, lifecycle, technical structure or implementation mechanism has been approved.

## 4. Dependencies Preserved

CG-01 through CG-07, MOT-01 and the GRP, CHL, ACT, KNW, RSK and related dependencies recorded in the approved register remain unresolved. CG-08 alone is resolved by the attributable amendment. Group Governance Body composition, appointment, procedure, responsibility, relationship allocation, tenure and implementation remain deferred.

## 5. Next Programme Boundary

No relationship-register drafting is authorized by this status record. Any future authorization must respect the cross-register constitutional contract and confirm that required allocation dependencies have matured.

Phase 2 Programme Status remains historical architecture evidence. This Phase 6 record is the current statement of EOG-05 programme status.
