# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 0 — Constitutional Scope Definition

**Status:** Preparatory scope; no governance approved

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Scope Objective

GRP Foundation Closure prepares founder decisions needed to complete the constitutional foundation of Group existence and continuity before later Group role, membership, Challenge and allocation governance.

## 2. Included Scope

### Group creation authentication

- creator expression and attribution;
- authoritative authentication of Group creation;
- authoritative establishment of Group existence, initial identity and founding purpose;
- separation of existence from participation; and
- use of only an existing Platform Authority.

### Stewardship continuity

- the singular Accountable Steward relationship;
- constitutional reassignment;
- voluntary relinquishment;
- continuity beyond the creator;
- succession and stewardship-failure principles; and
- separation from Delegation, Assignment of Responsibility, Administration, Custody and Council membership.

### Charter foundations

- Charter as an approved Governed Subject;
- initial Charter truth;
- binding constitutional meaning;
- amendment-authority boundary;
- historical intelligibility; and
- relationship to Group identity, purpose, Members and Accountable Stewardship.

### Stewardship Council treatment

- Council as an approved supporting Governed Subject;
- constitutional purpose and boundary;
- relationship to the singular Accountable Steward;
- non-Authority, non-Administration and non-Custody limits; and
- future-governance dependencies.

### CG-08 readiness

- permanent inventory classification question for C-GRP-08;
- Governed Subject versus Information Subject treatment;
- independence from the Accountable Steward relationship; and
- effects on later allocation work.

## 3. Excluded Scope

This phase excludes:

- approval of any founder decision;
- GRP-02 decision drafting or resolution;
- resolution of CG-08;
- relationship allocation;
- membership states or transitions;
- invitations, removal, suspension, appeal or inactivity rules;
- final role vocabulary;
- permissions or access;
- moderation workflow;
- Charter versioning mechanism;
- Council composition, appointment, voting or procedure;
- Group deletion, archival or retention;
- privacy or security controls;
- technical actors, storage, interfaces or implementation; and
- amendment of EOG-01, EOG-02 or EOG-05.

## 4. Mandatory Interpretation Rules

1. Creation and participation remain constitutionally separate.
2. Attributable origin does not establish authoritative truth.
3. Authority to Establish Truth must use one of the eleven approved Platform Authorities.
4. Exactly one Accountable Steward relationship exists for each Group at a time.
5. Multiple people may support stewardship without becoming multiple Accountable Stewards by implication.
6. Delegation of Authority, Assignment of Responsibility and Stewardship Reassignment are not equivalent.
7. Council membership, Member status and Administrator status do not imply stewardship.
8. Charter truth, Group identity and Group purpose must not collapse into one another without an approved classification decision.
9. Inventory classification does not allocate accountability.
10. A constitutional scenario must not become a lifecycle state or workflow.

Phase 0 ends with readiness, scope, questions, dependencies, impact analysis and a drafting blueprint. Options, recommendations and approval language belong to later phases and remain pending.
