# EOG-05 CG-08 — Proposed Constitutional Amendment

**Status:** Proposed — pending founder approval

**Date prepared:** 2026-07-21

**Amendment target:** [EOG-05 — Entity Ownership Register](27-EOG-05-ENTITY-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER-APPROVED.md)

## 1. Purpose

This proposed amendment implements the founder-approved direction GFC-05-A by preparing permanent constitutional inventory treatment for C-GRP-08 Stewardship Council. It is attributable to the [issued Founder Decision Record](34-GRP-FOUNDATION-CLOSURE-FOUNDER-DECISION-RECORD.md) and remains subject to a separate founder approval before it may amend EOG-05 or resolve CG-08.

This proposal changes inventory classification only. It does not allocate any EOG-01 relationship.

## 2. Constitutional Basis

The proposal preserves the following approved conditions:

1. inventory is not allocation;
2. no EOG-01 relationship is granted;
3. Information Subject boundaries remain explicit;
4. no composition, lifecycle or responsibility is decided;
5. Council responsibility is not shared stewardship; and
6. permanent constitutional recognition does not require every Group to establish, maintain or continuously operate a Stewardship Council.

The proposal also preserves the singular Accountable Steward relationship established by EOG-02. The Stewardship Council supports that relationship where a Council is established; it does not replace it.

## 3. Amendment Scope

Only the following parts of EOG-05 may change if this proposal is approved:

- the C-GRP-08 row in section 6.2;
- the classification totals in section 7;
- the CG-08 entry in section 8;
- the Group dependency statement in section 11;
- references to CG-01 through CG-08 in sections 13 and 14; and
- approval metadata necessary to attribute the amendment.

No other entity row, Entity ID, primary information category, classification gate or constitutional boundary is within scope.

### 4.1 Effect of the Row Amendment

The amendment:

- makes the Stewardship Council a permanent independent inventory entity;
- retains C-GRP-08 without changing its Entity ID;
- preserves Participation Information as its one primary constitutional category;
- distinguishes Council information from Group information and separately governed relationship information;
- records no Council composition, appointment, lifecycle or operational rule; and
- allocates no Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Custody, Administration, Operation, Assignment of Responsibility or other EOG-01 relationship.

“Proposed inclusion” is the existing permanent-treatment label used by EOG-05. In an approved amended register it records inventory treatment; it does not make an accountability allocation.

## 6. Explicit Non-Effects

This proposed amendment does not:

- allocate an EOG-01 relationship;
- make the Council an Accountable Steward or Platform Authority;
- assign responsibility to a Council;
- require a Group to establish a Council;
- create Council membership, composition, appointment, reassignment or lifecycle governance;
- change the Group, Group Charter or Accountable Steward rows;
- amend EOG-01, EOG-02 or any other approved EOG standard;
- resolve any gate other than CG-08; or
- authorize implementation.
