# EOG-03 Founder Review Checklist — Draft 2

## 1. Purpose

This checklist identifies the remaining constitutional confirmations required before [EOG-03 Founder Review Draft 2](13-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-DRAFT-2.md) may proceed to approval.

The checklist reflects the refinements recorded in the [Draft 2 Change Log](13-EOG-03-DRAFT-2-CHANGE-LOG.md). Checking an item records review intent only. A separate founder approval record is required before EOG-03 becomes approved governance.

## 2. Constitutional Scope

- [ ] Confirm that EOG-03 governs Platform Knowledge meaning and constitutional boundaries rather than catalogue implementation.
- [ ] Confirm that Exercise and Wellness Activity Assets remain the primary current Platform Knowledge Assets without becoming the permanent platform boundary.
- [ ] Confirm that lifecycle, accountability allocation, roles, permissions, privacy, security, calculations and implementation remain excluded.
- [ ] Confirm that EOG-03 creates no new Platform Authority or EOG-01 accountability allocation.

## 3. Authoritative Meaning and Authority

- [ ] Confirm that Knowledge Authority alone establishes Authoritative Meaning for a declared Knowledge Asset and purpose.
- [ ] Confirm that Platform Knowledge bears, preserves and communicates Authoritative Meaning but does not exercise Authority.
- [ ] Confirm that a Knowledge Asset is a Governed Subject bearing governed meaning, not an actor that establishes truth.
- [ ] Confirm the principle: Meaning precedes representation.
- [ ] Confirm that Templates, Runtime Projections, Historical Representations, Presentation, documentation, interfaces and records do not establish meaning by representing it.
- [ ] Confirm that Knowledge, Policy, Acceptance, Calculation, Participation and Presentation remain constitutionally separate.
- [ ] Confirm the prohibition on competing equal authorities for one canonical Knowledge meaning.

## 4. Knowledge Assets and Discovery Boundary

- [ ] Confirm Exercise Assets as primary Fitness-domain Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm Wellness Activity Assets as primary Wellness-domain Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that supporting Metric, Unit, Instructional, Safety, Relationship, Taxonomy and Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge may be governed under later standards.
- [ ] Confirm that Interests, Goals and Preferences are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that Discovery Tags and Discovery Categories are not Platform Knowledge Assets.
- [ ] Confirm that a Taxonomy or Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge Asset is constitutionally distinct from a Discovery Tag or Discovery Category.
- [ ] Confirm that this distinction neither defines a Knowledge taxonomy nor returns Discovery and Personalisation to Platform Knowledge.

## 5. Group Experience and Constitutional Composition

- [ ] Confirm “Groups own their governed experiences” as a domain boundary rather than an accountability allocation.
- [ ] Confirm that Group action occurs only through later-governed actors and relationships.
- [ ] Confirm that Groups may compose but may not modify, fork or redefine Platform Knowledge.
- [ ] Confirm Constitutional Composition as a general doctrine currently applied to Version 2 Group Challenges.
- [ ] Confirm that this generality creates no new undertaking type or active capability.
- [ ] Confirm the four composition inputs: Platform Knowledge, applicable Policy, Group configuration and Community context.
- [ ] Confirm that composition is not copying, inheritance, modification or forking.
- [ ] Confirm that composition transfers no Authority and that the resulting Group Challenge is distinct from every input.

## 6. Layered Meaning, Runtime and History

- [ ] Confirm Platform Meaning, Policy Meaning and Community Meaning as distinct, non-overriding source layers.
- [ ] Confirm Historical Meaning as the historically applicable preservation of those meanings, not an independent Authority or source input.
- [ ] Confirm that no lower meaning layer silently overrides a source layer.
- [ ] Confirm the Runtime Catalogue as one bounded runtime-availability function within the existing Knowledge Authority boundary.
- [ ] Confirm that the Runtime Catalogue is not a twelfth Platform Authority, role, actor or accountability allocation.
- [ ] Confirm that Runtime Projection remains subordinate to its source Knowledge Asset and governed availability determination.
- [ ] Confirm that Historical Representation may preserve the broader constitutional composition and is not limited to a Runtime Projection.
- [ ] Confirm that Draft 2 does not resolve snapshots, versioning, retention, deletion or lifecycle.

## 7. Templates and Creation Mechanisms

- [ ] Confirm Templates as governed composition guides rather than Knowledge Assets, Policy, Challenges or source Authorities.
- [ ] Confirm that a Template may consume or refer to applicable Policy but cannot establish Policy or determine its applicability.
- [ ] Confirm the Challenge Wizard as one example of a governed Creation Mechanism.
- [ ] Confirm that a Creation Mechanism may consume a Policy-applicability determination but cannot make that determination authoritative.
- [ ] Confirm that mechanism behavior cannot establish Knowledge, Policy, accountability or Derived Truth.

## 8. Future Knowledge Contribution

- [ ] Confirm the current Platform-generated Knowledge origin model without treating “the Platform” as an Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator or Operator.
- [ ] Confirm future Trusted Contributor participation as a possible governed capability only.
- [ ] Confirm the chain Platform governance → Trusted Contributors → Governed Review → Knowledge Authority → Authoritative Meaning → Platform Knowledge.
- [ ] Confirm that Governed Review supplies an approved basis but does not establish truth.
- [ ] Confirm that contribution may decentralise while Knowledge Authority remains the source of Authoritative Meaning.
- [ ] Confirm that contributor eligibility, roles, workflows, permissions and lifecycle remain deferred.

## 9. Remaining Drafting Notes and Pending Decisions

- [ ] Confirm that KNW-02 remains pending.
- [ ] Confirm that KNW-03 remains pending.
- [ ] Confirm that KNW-04 remains pending.
- [ ] Confirm that Draft 2 remains non-governing until a separate founder approval record is created.
