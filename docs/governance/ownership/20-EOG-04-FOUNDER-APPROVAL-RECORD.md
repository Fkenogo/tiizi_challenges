## 2. Constitutional Purpose

EOG-04 governs the constitutional identity, relationships, integrity and historical boundaries of a Challenge. It establishes the Challenge as Tiizi's governed product expression of a time-bounded collective Undertaking within a persistent Group while preserving the constitutional distinctions among Challenge, Group, Platform Knowledge, Policy, participation, Activity Information, Derived Truth, runtime representation and presentation.

EOG-04 applies the approved accountability semantics of EOG-01, the Group boundaries of EOG-02 and the Platform Knowledge and Constitutional Composition boundaries of EOG-03. It creates no Platform Authority and allocates no Authority, Accountable Steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Delegate or Presenter.

## 3. Approval Scope

The founder approves Founder Review Draft 2 as expressed in the approved standard, including its treatment of:

1. Challenge as the constitutional product expression of a governed collective Undertaking.
2. Challenge as one distinct Governed Subject with one distinct constitutional identity.
3. Challenge as the Information Subject of Challenge Information without absorbing adjacent Information Subject relationships.
4. Constitutional Composition as an inherited EOG-03 dependency whose resulting Challenge remains distinct from every composition input.
5. Group as the persistent Community context and Challenge as the distinct time-bounded Undertaking within that context.
6. Member, Participant and Challenge participation as distinct constitutional relationships.
7. Knowledge, Policy, Community Meaning, Activity Information, Evidence Eligibility and Derived Truth as separate constitutional concerns.
8. The complete constitutional Authority chain governing meaning, rules, participation context, Submission Intent, acceptance, Evidence Eligibility and Derived Truth.
9. Challenge integrity across identity, meaning, Authority, history and presentation, including no competing truth, silent substitution or silent reinterpretation.
10. Challenge Identity Continuity as a bounded interpretive principle within Challenge Identity and History, including attributable continuity representations.
11. Presentation, Runtime Catalogue, Runtime Projection, Historical Representation, Feed, Notification, leaderboard and analytical boundaries.
12. Domain neutrality while Fitness and Wellness remain the only approved Version 2 launch domains and individual challenges remain excluded.

Approval confirms the constitutional boundaries in the approved instrument. It does not approve behavior, allocation, lifecycle, formula, role, permission or implementation choices that the instrument expressly defers.
