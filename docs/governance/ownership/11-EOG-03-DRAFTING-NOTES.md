# EOG-03 Drafting Notes — Founder Review Draft 1

## 1. Purpose

These notes record unresolved constitutional observations arising during preparation of the [EOG-03 Founder Review Draft 1](11-EOG-03-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-OF-PLATFORM-KNOWLEDGE-DRAFT-1.md).

They are not constitutional provisions. They do not resolve founder questions, amend approved governance or allocate accountability.

## 2. Drafting Note Register

### DN-01 — Authoritative Meaning section order

**Observation:** The Phase 1 blueprint placed Primary Platform Knowledge Assets before Authoritative Meaning. The founder’s Phase 2 instruction requires Authoritative Meaning immediately after Platform Knowledge.

**Draft treatment:** Sections 6 and 7 were reversed. The substantive blueprint principles remain unchanged.

**Reason:** A Knowledge Asset can be understood constitutionally only after the doctrine explaining what Knowledge establishes.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that this is the only approved structural adjustment to the Phase 1 architecture.

### DN-02 — Layered Meaning

**Observation:** The founder invited a Layered Meaning doctrine where constitutionally supportable.

**Basis:** Existing governance already distinguishes Platform Knowledge, Policy, Group context and historical meaning. It also prohibits one from silently redefining another.

**Draft treatment:** Section 10.4 recognises Platform Meaning, Policy Meaning, Community Meaning and Historical Meaning as interpretation layers. It states that they are not competing truths and creates no implementation or lifecycle model.

**Unresolved point:** Founder confirmation is required that these four layer names and their stated ordering express the intended philosophy. If the founder considers the term “Policy Meaning” or “Community Meaning” too broad, the doctrine should be refined before approval.

### DN-03 — “Groups own their governed experiences”

**Observation:** The founder wording uses “own,” while EOG-01 prohibits ambiguous ownership shorthand in formal accountability allocations.

**Draft treatment:** Section 9 preserves the wording verbatim and immediately constrains it as a constitutional domain boundary. It explicitly does not allocate Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Custody, Administration, Operation or permission.

**Unresolved point:** Later Entity Ownership and Challenge governance must allocate the relevant EOG-01 relationships. This draft does not determine them.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that the boundary interpretation faithfully captures the intended use of “own.”

### DN-04 — Knowledge-provided logic

**Observation:** Founder clarification states that Platform Knowledge provides validation, scoring, Progress and Recognition logic. Existing approved governance assigns rule-making to Policy Authority and Derived Truth to Calculation Authority.

**Draft treatment:** Sections 6, 8 and 18 treat Knowledge as supplying Authoritative Meaning used by applicable Policy and Calculation. They do not assign calculations or Recognition qualification to Knowledge Authority.

**Unresolved point:** If “provides logic” is intended to give Knowledge Authority power to establish behavioral Policy or calculations, that would conflict with the current Platform Authority Model and require a separate founder constitutional decision. The draft assumes no such change.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that “provides logic” means governed Knowledge and applicable Policy inputs rather than calculation by Knowledge Authority.

### DN-05 — Supporting Knowledge Asset types

**Observation:** Founder direction identifies Exercise Assets and Wellness Activity Assets as the primary Platform Knowledge Assets. The Knowledge Asset Domain Standard also recognises Metric, Unit, Instructional, Safety and Relationship Knowledge Assets.

**Draft treatment:** Section 7 identifies Exercise and Wellness Activity Assets as primary and acknowledges supporting Knowledge Assets already recognised by constitutional governance. It does not approve a final classification system.

**Unresolved point:** A later Knowledge classification standard may determine whether each supporting meaning is represented as a distinct Knowledge Asset or as governed meaning within another Asset.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that “primary” is non-exclusive and permits supporting governed Knowledge under later standards.

### DN-06 — Runtime-to-history progression

**Observation:** The Phase 2 instruction requires the progression Runtime Catalogue → Runtime Projection → Historical Representation.

**Draft treatment:** Section 14 preserves this progression while section 15 clarifies that Historical Representation may preserve the wider composition context, not only the Runtime Projection.

**Unresolved point:** KNW-02 must later determine the exact relationship among a Runtime Projection, a Challenge’s historical representation and any version or snapshot mechanism.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that the constitutional chain expresses subordination and historical intelligibility rather than a mandatory technical pipeline.

### DN-07 — “Trusted Contributor” status

**Observation:** The future-direction phrase uses Trusted Contributors, but no contributor eligibility, role or trust decision is approved.

**Draft treatment:** Section 16 states that “trusted” is a possible future governed condition and does not name a role or create Authority.

**Unresolved point:** Eligibility, qualifications, appointment, review and removal require later governance.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that the phrase should remain as future constitutional direction with no present contributor capability implied.

### DN-08 — Governed Review

**Observation:** Future contribution requires a boundary between attributable input and Authoritative Meaning, but no review workflow is approved.

**Draft treatment:** Governed Review is defined only as a constitutional requirement that proposed contribution undergo approved review before it may support Authoritative Meaning. No actors, stages or workflow are defined.

**Unresolved point:** Later governance must determine the applicable Authority, accountability relationships, evidence and process. Knowledge Authority remains the existing Authority to Establish Knowledge truth.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that this minimal doctrine is sufficient for EOG-03.

### DN-09 — Historical Representation

**Observation:** Existing materials use “snapshot,” “version,” “copy” and “historical record” inconsistently. KNW-02 and KNW-03 remain pending.

**Draft treatment:** Historical Representation is used as a constitutional integrity concept. It does not prescribe a snapshot, version, immutability, retention or deletion model.

**Unresolved point:** The future historical and lifecycle standards must determine the representation contract and preservation obligations.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm the term Historical Representation as the preferred constitutional term.

### DN-10 — Discovery classifications

**Observation:** Founder direction excludes Interests, Goals, Preferences, Tags and Categories from Platform Knowledge. Existing Knowledge governance recognises some controlled terminology and classification meaning.

**Draft treatment:** Section 17 excludes the five named concepts from Platform Knowledge and places their governance in the future Discovery and Personalisation Programme. Section 7 preserves supporting Knowledge meaning without adopting Tags or Categories as Platform Knowledge Assets.

**Unresolved point:** Later governance must distinguish intrinsic Knowledge relationships from discovery-oriented classifications without reopening the exclusion.

**Founder confirmation required:** Confirm that all Tags and Categories intended by the founder are discovery/personalisation concepts rather than Platform Knowledge Assets.
