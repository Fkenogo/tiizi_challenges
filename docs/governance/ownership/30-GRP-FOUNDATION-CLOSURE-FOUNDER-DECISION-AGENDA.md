# GRP Foundation Closure Phase 0 — Founder Decision Agenda

**Status:** Agenda for future decision preparation; no approval recorded

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Agenda Purpose

This agenda identifies the constitutional questions a later founder decision pack must make approval-ready. GFC labels are pack-local traceability labels only.

## 2. GFC-01 — Group Creation Authentication

**Approved baseline**

A Group is created, not proposed. Creation establishes existence, initial identity and founding purpose. The creator becomes the first Member and first Accountable Steward. Participation is not the constitutional basis of existence.

**Founder question to prepare**

Which existing Platform Authority authenticates the attributable creation event and establishes authoritative Group existence, initial identity and founding-purpose truth?

**Required distinctions**

- creator expression versus authoritative authentication;
- Group existence versus later participation;
- initial truth establishment versus later Administration; and
- Platform Authority versus role, interface or mechanism.

**Information needed**

Neutral constitutional options, consequences for GRP-01 Group and GRP-04 Group purpose, compatibility with the first Accountable Steward consequence, and treatment of a Group with no later participation.

## 3. GFC-02 — Stewardship Continuity

**Approved baseline**

Every Group has exactly one Accountable Steward relationship at a time. The creator initially holds it. The relationship may later be reassigned or voluntarily relinquished, and the Group may outlive the creator.

**Founder questions to prepare**

- What constitutional act establishes a stewardship reassignment?
- Which existing Authority establishes authoritative stewardship truth?
- What continuing answerability is required during relinquishment or succession?
- What principle governs stewardship failure without creating lifecycle states?

**Required distinctions**

- reassignment versus delegation;
- Accountable Steward versus Council member;
- stewardship versus Administration and Custody; and
- continuity principle versus workflow.

**Information needed**

Successor-present, no-successor, disputed-reassignment and creator-departure scenarios; consequences for Group continuity; and later GRP-02/GRP-03 dependencies.

## 4. GFC-03 — Charter Foundations

**Approved baseline**

The Group Charter is a Governed Subject. It is distinct from Platform governance, a role bundle and implementation configuration.

**Founder questions to prepare**

- What establishes the initial Charter truth?
- What constitutional effect may the Charter have for Members?
- What Authority boundary governs amendment?
- What historical intelligibility must be preserved?
- How does the Accountable Steward relate to Charter coherence without automatically establishing every Charter truth?

**Required distinctions**

- Charter versus Group identity and Group purpose;
- binding meaning versus permission enforcement;
- amendment Authority versus Administration; and
- historical intelligibility versus versioning implementation.

**Information needed**

Initial-Charter, Member-disagreement, amendment and prior-participation scenarios; affected EOG-05 rows; and explicit later lifecycle dependencies.

## 5. GFC-04 — Stewardship Council Constitutional Treatment

**Approved baseline**

A Stewardship Council is a governed body supporting the Accountable Steward. It is not a Platform Authority, Accountable Stewardship, Administration or Custody and does not replace the singular Accountable Steward relationship.

**Founder questions to prepare**

- What enduring constitutional purpose justifies the Council as a distinct supporting Governed Subject?
- What boundaries prevent support from becoming shared stewardship or undeclared Authority?
- Which responsibilities must remain deferred to future Council governance?

**Required distinctions**

- body membership versus accountability relationship;
- collective advice or support versus Authority;
- Council versus Administrator; and
- constitutional treatment versus composition or procedure.

**Information needed**

Support, disagreement, inactive-Council and attempted-authority scenarios; consequences for later Council governance; and inputs needed by GFC-05.

## 6. GFC-05 — CG-08 Inventory Readiness

**Approved baseline**

C-GRP-08 is an approved supporting Governed Subject with permanent inventory treatment unresolved. Its Information Subject treatment and relationship boundaries remain open under CG-08.

**Founder questions to prepare**

- Should C-GRP-08 receive permanent independent inventory treatment?
- What information primarily concerns the Council, the Group or a later Council relationship?
- What boundary prevents the row from allocating Accountable Stewardship, Authority, Administration or Custody?

**Required distinctions**

- Governed Subject status versus permanent inventory row;
- entity information versus relationship information; and
- inventory classification versus allocation.

**Information needed**

Entity-admission analysis grounded only in EOG-02 and EOG-05, effects of retaining or consolidating the row, and cross-register consequences.

2. GFC-02 — Stewardship continuity.
3. GFC-03 — Charter foundations.

5. GFC-05 — CG-08 inventory readiness.
