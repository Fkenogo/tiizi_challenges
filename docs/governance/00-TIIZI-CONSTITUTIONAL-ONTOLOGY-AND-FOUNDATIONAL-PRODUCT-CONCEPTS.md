# Document 00 — Tiizi Constitutional Ontology and Foundational Product Concepts

## Preface

### Purpose

This document describes the concepts that make Tiizi intelligible as a product. It answers the foundational question:

> What exists in the Tiizi world, and why?

The ontology exists so that every later standard can govern the same conceptual world. Without a stable account of what Tiizi believes exists, features may use familiar words while expressing incompatible ideas.

This document has conceptual precedence: it explains the world that Tiizi governance governs. It does not replace the Platform Constitution, which establishes binding platform identity and governing boundaries. It supplies the conceptual foundation from which the Constitution, principles, standards and later governance should be interpreted.

### Concepts, Governance and Implementation

- **Concepts** describe what exists in the Tiizi world and why it matters.
- **Governance** declares the rules, boundaries and accountability by which those concepts remain coherent and trustworthy.
- **Implementation** expresses governed concepts through changing products, processes and technologies.

Concepts precede governance. Governance precedes implementation. An implementation may express a concept in many forms, but it may not redefine the concept merely for convenience.

### Constitutional Mission

> Tiizi is a community-first motivation platform that helps people build healthier lives through truthful collective participation.

This is Tiizi's constitutional mission statement.

### Interpretive Boundary

This ontology does not assign Authority, stewardship, custody, roles or permissions. It does not define lifecycle states, workflows, moderation, calculation, storage, interfaces or technical design. When it names a relationship, it describes conceptual dependence rather than an operational process.

## Part I — The Tiizi Philosophy

### Why Communities

Human growth is strengthened when people belong to a continuing context of shared purpose. A community gives participation meaning beyond a single act. It makes encouragement relational, progress shareable and accountability mutual.

Tiizi therefore begins with community rather than isolated performance. The community is not an audience assembled around a temporary event. It is the enduring context in which collective undertakings become possible and meaningful.

### Why Participation

People do not become part of a shared pursuit merely by observing it or belonging near it. Participation is the voluntary expression of commitment within a collective undertaking.

Participation matters because motivation is stronger when people can contribute in ways connected to a shared purpose. It is not reducible to visibility, membership, popularity or passive presence.

### Why Truth

Collective motivation depends on a trustworthy account of what occurred. Without truth, shared progress becomes performance, Recognition becomes arbitrary and community confidence erodes.

Truth in Tiizi is attributable, governed and historically intelligible. It distinguishes expression from evidence, evidence from conclusion and presentation from the reality presented.

### Why Motivation

Motivation is the capacity to sustain meaningful participation. It grows through belonging, encouragement, visible contribution, truthful progress and appropriate Recognition.

Motivation is not manufactured by spectacle or reward alone. It is durable when people understand why their participation matters and can trust the collective account of it.

### Why Growth

The purpose of motivation is not engagement for its own sake. Tiizi exists to support healthier lives through sustained, meaningful change.

Growth may be personal and collective. It may be gradual, uneven or difficult to measure. Tiizi supports the conditions for growth; it does not claim to guarantee a particular human outcome.

### Platform

The Platform is the shared governed foundation in which Tiizi's concepts can exist together coherently. It supplies a common world for communities, undertakings, participation, knowledge, truth and motivation.

Without the Platform, each feature could define its own meaning. The Platform makes conceptual continuity possible.

### Community

Community is the enduring collective context in which people belong, organise, collaborate and pursue shared purposes. In Tiizi, the Group is the governed product concept that expresses Community.

Community depends on the Platform because collective identity and shared purpose require a common governed context.

### Undertaking

An Undertaking is a defined collective pursuit through which a Community acts toward a shared objective. In Tiizi, the Challenge is the governed product concept that expresses an Undertaking.

An Undertaking depends on Community because a collective pursuit must belong to a continuing human context rather than exist as an isolated demand for activity.

### Commitment

Commitment is the voluntary relationship by which a person elects to participate in an Undertaking and accept its shared purpose as relevant to their participation.

Commitment depends on an Undertaking because commitment must be directed toward something intelligible. Commitment is not proof that an Activity occurred, and it does not itself establish an outcome.

### Evidence

Evidence is authoritative, attributable information approved for use in a governed determination. Commitments give rise to expressions and actions from which Evidence may be established under applicable governance.

Evidence depends on Commitment because Tiizi interprets participation within a voluntarily entered collective context. Not every expression is Evidence for every purpose, and Evidence is not identical to a conclusion drawn from it.

### Truth

Truth is Tiizi's attributable and historically intelligible account of what has been accepted, determined or derived under governance. It is the reliable basis for shared understanding.

Truth depends on Evidence because claims about participation and outcomes must not be invented. Truth also depends on governed meaning and rules; Evidence without meaning cannot support a coherent account.

### Motivation

Motivation is the capacity of the Platform and Community to encourage sustained, meaningful participation through belonging, shared purpose, truthful progress, mutual encouragement and appropriate Recognition.

Motivation depends on Truth because encouragement and Recognition lose legitimacy when they contradict or precede the reality they claim to express.

### Growth

Growth is the healthier personal or collective change that meaningful participation may support over time. It is the human purpose toward which Tiizi's motivational environment is directed.

Growth depends on Motivation because sustained change is supported by continued, meaningful participation. Growth remains a human outcome, not a claim Tiizi may make without an appropriate basis.

## Part III — Foundational Product Concepts

### Platform

**Definition:** The Platform is Tiizi's shared governed conceptual environment through which Communities pursue Undertakings, participation gains meaning, truth remains coherent and motivation supports growth.

The Platform is broader than any domain, feature or technology. It is the common foundation that keeps them conceptually consistent.

### Group

**Definition:** A Group is Tiizi’s persistent governed community through which people organise, participate, collaborate and pursue shared fitness, wellness and future governed purposes. It is the enduring social foundation of collective participation within the platform.

Persistence means that the Group is conceptually distinct from any one Challenge. It does not define permanence, retention or any lifecycle state.

### Challenge

**Definition:** A Challenge is Tiizi’s governed, time-bounded collective undertaking through which members of a Group voluntarily pursue a defined objective using agreed participation rules, measurement criteria and completion conditions.

Time-boundedness distinguishes the Undertaking from the persistent Community. This definition does not establish lifecycle states, time rules, formulas or amendment processes.

### Participation

**Definition:** Participation is the voluntary relationship through which a person commits to a collective Undertaking and contributes in ways relevant to its governed purpose.

Participation is distinct from membership, observation, administration and evidence. It creates the human context in which meaningful contribution may occur; it does not itself prove contribution or establish an outcome.

### Evidence

**Definition:** Evidence is authoritative information approved for use in a governed determination about participation or outcome for a declared purpose.

Evidence is not mere assertion, visibility, Knowledge, communication or presentation. Its use remains dependent on the meaning and rules applicable to the determination it supports.

### Truth

**Definition:** Truth is the attributable, governed and historically intelligible account Tiizi may rely upon when describing participation, progress, outcomes and other platform facts.

Truth is not convenience, popularity, interface appearance or unexamined assertion. Conceptual Truth includes accepted facts and conclusions validly derived from them while preserving the distinctions among those forms.

### Recognition

**Definition:** Recognition is a truthful acknowledgement of governed participation or outcome.

Recognition follows Truth. It does not create the participation or outcome it acknowledges, and it remains distinct from encouragement, status display and Reward.

### Growth

**Definition:** Growth is healthier personal or collective change supported through meaningful participation, motivation and time.

Growth is Tiizi's purpose, not a guaranteed result. A future capability may measure an aspect of growth only under governance appropriate to that meaning.

## Part IV — Constitutional Relationships

The foundational relationships are:

1. **The Platform hosts Communities.** It supplies the coherent governed world in which collective contexts exist.
2. **Communities host Undertakings.** An enduring Community gives each temporary collective pursuit belonging and meaning.
3. **Undertakings invite Commitments.** A shared objective becomes participatory when people voluntarily relate themselves to it.
4. **Commitments generate Evidence.** Voluntary participation gives rise to attributable expressions and actions from which governed Evidence may be established.
5. **Evidence supports Truth.** Evidence provides the basis for a trustworthy account without becoming the conclusion itself.
6. **Truth enables Motivation.** People can be encouraged and recognised without deception when shared understanding is reliable.
7. **Motivation supports Growth.** Sustained, meaningful participation creates conditions in which healthier change can occur.

These relationships are conceptual dependencies. They do not define who exercises Authority, how information is accepted, how conclusions are calculated or how any state changes.

The product concepts relate to the ontology as follows:

| Ontological layer | Tiizi product expression | Constitutional boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | Tiizi Platform | Not a single feature or domain |
| Community | Group | Persistent context, not a Challenge |
| Undertaking | Challenge | Time-bounded pursuit, not the Group |
| Commitment | Participation | Voluntary relationship, not proof of activity |
| Evidence | Governed Evidence | Basis for truth, not Truth by itself |
| Truth | Accepted and derived platform truth | Not presentation or popularity |
| Motivation | Encouragement and truthful Recognition | Not Reward by default |
| Growth | Healthier personal or collective change | Not a guaranteed platform result |

## Part V — Constitutional Principles

### Community Before Competition

Competition may serve a Community, but it must not displace belonging, shared purpose or mutual respect. Competitive expression remains subordinate to the health and integrity of collective participation.

### Truth Before Recognition

Recognition must follow the truth it acknowledges. Tiizi shall not create motivational appearance by overstating participation, progress or outcomes.

### Participation Before Reward

Reward, where later governed, must never substitute for meaningful participation. The reason to participate begins with shared purpose and commitment, not the promise of value.

### Persistent Communities

A Community is conceptually more enduring than any one Undertaking. A Group may provide continuity across Undertakings without this principle determining its lifecycle, retention or archival treatment.

### One Community, Many Undertakings

A Community may pursue more than one Undertaking across its history. Each Undertaking remains distinct and governed. This principle does not determine whether Undertakings may run concurrently.

### Voluntary Commitment

Participation in an Undertaking is voluntary. Belonging to a Community does not by itself establish commitment to every Undertaking. The conditions for entering, changing or ending participation remain for later governance.

### Transparent Historical Integrity

Truth must remain attributable and historically intelligible. Material change must not silently erase or reinterpret the account on which people relied. This principle does not define correction, deletion, retention or archival processes.

### Truthful Collective Participation

Tiizi shall represent collective participation according to what can truthfully be supported. Shared purpose does not justify invented evidence, exaggerated progress or misleading Recognition.

### Growth Through Community

Tiizi supports growth by creating conditions in which people can participate meaningfully with others. Community strengthens motivation by connecting personal effort to belonging, encouragement and shared purpose.

### Principle of Conceptual Stability

Features may evolve.

Processes may evolve.

Technologies may evolve.

Constitutional concepts change only when the product itself changes.

This principle protects Tiizi from allowing temporary product expression to redefine its enduring meaning.

## Part VI — What Tiizi Is Not

Tiizi is not merely any one of the following:

### A Fitness Tracker

Fitness is a launch domain, but Tiizi's foundation is collective participation and motivation. Measurement serves a shared purpose; it is not the whole product.

### A Challenge App

Challenges are important Undertakings, but they exist within persistent Communities. Tiizi is not a succession of disconnected temporary contests.

### A Reward Platform

Motivation is not reducible to rewards. Tiizi begins with belonging, voluntary commitment, truthful participation and shared progress. Reward remains a separately governed possibility.

### A Social Network

Community in Tiizi is purpose-oriented and governed. Connection, communication and visibility exist to support meaningful collective participation, not unrestricted social reach.

### An Activity Logger

Recording activity may provide a basis for truth, but the act of logging is not the product's purpose. Tiizi connects attributable participation to Community, Undertaking, motivation and growth.

### A Leaderboard

Competition and Ranking may be governed expressions of some Undertakings. They are not the foundation of Community, participation or human worth.

### An Authority Over Human Worth

Tiizi may describe governed participation and outcomes within declared boundaries. It does not define a person's value, character or potential.

## Part VII — Future Evolution

The ontology defines concepts rather than a fixed list of features. Future capabilities should extend these concepts without replacing or weakening them.

A future domain may introduce new forms of Knowledge, Undertaking, Evidence or motivation while remaining coherent with the same foundational relationships. Conceptual possibilities include:

- Learning;
- Volunteering;
- Coaching;
- Events;
- Nutrition;
- Corporate Wellness;
- Insurance;
- other future governed domains.

These examples are possibilities, not active capabilities, launch commitments or approved domain designs. Each would require its own governance before becoming part of Tiizi.

Future evolution should ask:

1. What Community does the capability serve?
2. What Undertaking does it make possible?
3. What voluntary Commitment does it invite?
4. What could count as Evidence, without assuming that it already does?
5. What Truth may responsibly be established?
6. How does motivation remain truthful?
7. What form of Growth is being supported without being guaranteed?

## Part VIII — Constitutional Consequences

### Platform Constitution

This ontology explains the conceptual world governed by the Platform Constitution. The Constitution remains the governing source for approved platform boundaries. A later amendment should align its mission and foundational terms with Document 00 through explicit founder governance.

### Platform Principles

Platform Principles translate the ontology into durable tests for product and governance decisions. They should preserve Community before competition, Truth before Recognition and conceptual stability.

### Platform Authority Model

The Authority Model determines who may establish or communicate governed truth. Document 00 does not allocate Authority. Its Evidence and Truth concepts must be interpreted through, and must not collapse, the approved distinctions among participant expression, acceptance, evidential use, calculation and presentation.

### Domain Standards

Domain Standards define bounded meanings for Profile, Group, Challenge, Activity Event and Knowledge Asset. They should express the ontology without treating Community, Undertaking, Commitment, Evidence, Truth, Motivation or Growth as automatic new entities or Authorities.

### Entity Ownership

Entity ownership governance assigns accountability for Governed subjects. A conceptual layer is not automatically a register row, information category, steward or custody allocation.

### Lifecycle Standards

Lifecycle Standards will govern permitted states and changes for particular subjects. They must preserve the ontology but are not created by it. Persistence, time-boundedness, commitment and historical integrity require later lifecycle precision.

### Future Governance

Future governance should cite Document 00 when defining a capability's conceptual purpose. It may refine expression and domain meaning, but it should not redefine foundational concepts without an explicit constitutional decision.
