## 3. Overall Verdict

**Verdict:** Coherent as non-constitutional founder doctrine, subject to the same mission-alignment dependency already recorded for Document 00.

Document 00A faithfully reflects the Ontology's Community, Commitment, Evidence, Truth, Motivation and Growth relationships. It reinforces existing principles of meaningful participation, evidence before Recognition, truthful presentation, historical integrity, domain neutrality and platform-before-feature judgement.

It does not allocate Authority, assign stewardship, define lifecycle, create permissions, approve Recognition or Reward, or expand Version 2 launch scope.

## 6. Founder Design Principle Review

### Belonging Before Performance

Consistent with Group First and the Group Domain's purpose of belonging, accountability and mutual encouragement. It does not diminish truthful measurement or approve participation without evidence.

### Encouragement Before Comparison

Consistent with Motivation and Recognition boundaries. It does not prohibit Ranking; it places comparison in service of Community and truth.

### Clarity Before Cleverness

Consistent with Truthful User Experience. The phrase is a design preference, not a rule allowing simplification of material meaning.

### Truthful Progress Before Visual Excitement

Consistent with Presentation Authority and the prohibition on presentation becoming truth.

### Long-Term Habits Before Short-Term Engagement

Consistent with healthier-life purpose and Growth. It does not define habit, Streak or engagement calculations.

### Continuity Before Novelty

Consistent with Persistent Communities and Conceptual Stability. It does not preclude innovation.

### Transparency Before Automation

Consistent with attribution and reviewability. It does not ban automation or assign decision Authority to any actor.

### Future Evolution Without Conceptual Drift

Consistent with Domain Neutrality and Future Extensibility. It does not activate a future domain.

2. No existing Authority receives additional constitutional scope.

5. Use Simplicity Through Governance in future UX, content and accessibility standards.
