**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

## 1. Purpose

This breakdown separates the approved-in-principle [CGP-02C.2 Planning Package](06-CGP-02C-2-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) into three sequential work packages. It defines planning boundaries only. It creates no constitutional proposition, approves no decision and does not authorize drafting.

## 2. Sequence

```text
CGP-02C.1 Founder Approval Candidate
                ↓
CGP-02C.2A — Governance Lifecycle
                ↓
CGP-02C.2B — Amendment Classification
                ↓
CGP-02C.2C — Review Triggers and Proportionality
```

Each arrow indicates a planning dependency, not automatic authorization or constitutional approval.

## 3. CGP-02C.2A — Governance Lifecycle

Prepare one future Founder Review package concerning Governance Lifecycle and no other constitutional subject.

### 3.2 Scope

- conceptual distinctions among lifecycle terms identified by the frozen blueprint;
- boundary between document status and constitutional effect; and
- explicit deferral of approval procedure, adoption effect, effective dates, supersession and retirement.

- [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md), especially GAP-01 and the lifecycle questions in section 9;

- lifecycle evidence classified as observed practice rather than approved doctrine.

## 5. CGP-02C.2C — Review Triggers and Proportionality

### 5.1 Purpose

Prepare one future Founder Review package concerning Review Triggers and Proportionality and no other constitutional subject.

### 5.2 Scope

- proportionality based on constitutional consequence;
- relationship between objective reviewability and independent review; and

### 5.3 Dependencies

## 6. Common Programme Boundaries

- Each work package requires separate Founder authorization before drafting.
- One work package may address only its named constitutional subject.
- Completion, validation or Founder review does not by itself approve constitutional text.
- A later package may depend on an earlier review outcome but may not silently approve or amend it.
- Approval governance, adoption effect, traceability, supersession, retirement, validation doctrine and implementation remain outside all three packages.
- No Platform Authority or accountability relationship may be created, extended or allocated.
- The Master Programme and approved governance instruments remain unchanged.

## 7. Breakdown Verdict

The three-package sequence preserves the constitutional intent of the original CGP-02C.2 planning scope while reducing each future drafting unit to one subject. This breakdown is ready for review as planning material only; it authorizes no constitutional drafting.
