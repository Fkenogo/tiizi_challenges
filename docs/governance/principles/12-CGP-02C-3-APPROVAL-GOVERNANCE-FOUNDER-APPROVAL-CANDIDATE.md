## 1. Constitutional Meaning of Approval

**APV-01.** Constitutional approval is an explicit and attributable decision of Governance Authority concerning identified constitutional content and a declared approval scope. Approval records that Governance Authority has approved that content within that scope; it does not arise from implication, custom or administrative treatment.

**APV-02.** Approval requires an affirmative approval decision. Drafting, recommendation, review, validation, completion, publication, reliance, implementation, repository placement, silence or passage of time does not substitute for that decision.

**APV-03.** Approval is bounded by the content and scope identified in the decision. Approval of one proposition, section, instrument, exception or amendment does not approve another matter by analogy, association or shared documentation.

## 2. Governance Authority Exercised in Approval

**APV-04.** Governance Authority is the approved Platform Authority exercised in constitutional approval. Approval Governance creates no separate “Approval Authority” and does not extend, transfer or redistribute Governance Authority.

**APV-05.** Where the Founder exercises Governance Authority in an approval record, the canonical constitutional-capacity wording is `Founder, exercising Governance Authority`. Constitutional standards continue to name the Authority category as `Governance Authority`.

**APV-06.** An approval record must distinguish the attributable person or governed actor making the decision from the Governance Authority exercised. A personal title, authorship, administrative function, repository capability, custody or possession does not independently establish approval authority.

## 3. Minimum Approval Evidence

**APV-07.** Constitutional approval must be supported by governed evidence sufficient to identify: the approval decision; the constitutional content under decision; the bounded scope; the attributable approving actor; the Governance Authority capacity exercised; the decision date; and the governing basis or decision source.

**APV-08.** Approval evidence must be objectively reviewable as a coherent evidence set. Its constitutional sufficiency does not depend on one document form, repository location, technical mechanism or record format.

**APV-09.** Approval evidence must distinguish approved content from recommendations, rejected alternatives, amendments requested, pending questions, deferred matters and matters outside the decision scope. Proximity within one package does not give those matters approved status.

## 4. Minimum Approval-Record Content

**APV-10.** An approval record must identify at minimum: its approval reference; the decision outcome; the identified constitutional content; the approved scope; the approving actor and constitutional capacity; the decision date; and the source evidence on which the record relies.

**APV-11.** Where the approval concerns constitutional amendment, the approval record must preserve every additional minimum already required by the Platform Constitution, including the decision, rationale, affected documents and identified version treatment, without this package defining effective-version semantics.

**APV-12.** An approval record must state any partial approval, amendment condition, rejection, deferral or express non-effect necessary to prevent the record from communicating broader approval than the decision establishes.

## 5. Exercising Approval and Recording Approval

**APV-13.** Exercising constitutional approval and recording constitutional approval are distinct. The approval decision is an exercise of Governance Authority; recording preserves attributable evidence of that decision.

**APV-14.** Creating, transcribing, maintaining, presenting or preserving an approval record does not confer Governance Authority or authority to approve. A recorder, author, repository administrator, document custodian, operator or presenter remains within the separately approved scope of that function.

**APV-15.** A supporting actor may record only what the attributable approval decision establishes. Recording cannot expand, narrow, reinterpret, condition, replace or otherwise alter the approved content or scope.

**APV-16.** This package allocates no recorder, custodian, administrator, operator or other accountability relationship. The ability or responsibility to maintain evidence remains a later allocation question.

## 6. Approval-Reference Convention

**APV-17.** Every constitutional approval decision must have one stable and unique approval reference within Tiizi governance. The reference identifies the decision for attribution and later review; it does not itself establish Authority, approval, adoption or constitutional effect.

**APV-18.** An approval reference must remain associated with the same approval decision and must not be reused to represent a different decision. Its technical format, generation mechanism and storage remain outside constitutional scope.

## 7. Approval-Time Representation and Metadata

**APV-19.** Approval-time representation or metadata changes are permissible only where they accurately record the attributable approval decision and leave the approved constitutional meaning, content, scope and decision conditions unchanged.

**APV-20.** A change that adds, removes, expands, narrows, substitutes, reprioritizes or reinterprets constitutional meaning is not authorized merely because it occurs while approval is recorded or is described as metadata, representation, clerical treatment or status maintenance.

**APV-21.** Historical approval records are not retrospectively rewritten solely to conform to later canonical capacity wording, approval-reference conventions or record presentation. Any later representation must preserve the historically attributable decision and wording.

## 8. Approval-Record Integrity

**APV-22.** An approval record must remain attributable to its source decision and must preserve a clear distinction among the approved constitutional content, the approval decision and the record that communicates that decision.

**APV-23.** A status label, filename, location, index entry, publication action or later presentation that conflicts with the attributable approval decision must not be treated as an equal source of approval truth.

**APV-24.** Approval-record integrity requires the record to communicate no broader decision, Authority, scope or status than its attributable source supports. Absence, ambiguity or inconsistency in supporting representation cannot be cured by silently inferring approval.

## 9. Scope Boundary and Deferred Governance

This Founder Approval Candidate does not define adoption, constitutional effect, effective date, application date, lifecycle, amendment classification, review triggers, proportionality, amendment traceability, dependent-governance impact review, conflict review, supersession, retirement, withdrawal, rejection, validation doctrine, governance-index synchronization, temporary or emergency governance, implementation, repository workflow or accountability allocation.

APV-01 through APV-24 were approved for inclusion in this Founder Approval Candidate through the attributable Founder Decision Record dated 2026-07-23. This candidate does not adopt, apply or make itself, CGP-02 or any Approval Governance rule constitutionally effective.
