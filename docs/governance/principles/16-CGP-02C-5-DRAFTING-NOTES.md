# CGP-02C.5 — Drafting Notes

**Subject:** Amendment Traceability Requirements

**Status:** Founder Review drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Drafting Boundary Applied

The draft treats one constitutional subject: the constitutional requirements of an amendment trace.

It does not perform an amendment, determine approval or adoption, establish constitutional effect, conduct dependent-governance impact review, prescribe historical-preservation mechanisms or define an amendment workflow.

## 2. Architectural Observations

### DN-01 — The trace communicates; it does not govern

The draft treats the amendment trace as an attributable constitutional record. Governance Authority and the relevant attributable decisions establish constitutional change and status. The record does not exercise Authority.

### DN-02 — Amendment and approval references remain distinct

The proposed stable amendment-trace reference identifies the amendment trace. It does not replace an approval reference, and neither reference establishes Authority or constitutional status. Technical formats remain excluded.

### DN-03 — Minimum information is consequence-bounded

The proposed minimum is intended to preserve constitutional intelligibility, not to require every possible field in every circumstance. Matter-status categories and constitutional dates are included only where necessary or relevant to prevent overstatement.

### DN-04 — Status terms are preserved, not defined

Affected, unaffected, pending, deferred, rejected and withdrawn are treated only as statuses that a trace may need to communicate. The draft does not define withdrawal, rejection, retirement or any lifecycle transition.

### DN-05 — Historical intelligibility is not a preservation mechanism

The draft requires enough constitutional context for later understanding while explicitly excluding versioning, storage, retention, archival and repository processes.

## 3. Reserved Founder Questions

The Founder Review Decision Pack preserves nine unresolved questions:

1. purpose and meaning of an amendment trace;
2. minimum amendment-trace information;
3. separation from approval, adoption and status records;
4. the relationship among prior meaning, changed meaning, scope and decision;
5. treatment of affected, unaffected, pending, deferred, rejected and withdrawn matters;
6. the amendment-trace reference and its relationship to an approval reference;
7. relevant constitutional dates;
8. the trace-specific anti-inference boundary; and
9. deferred governance.

No answer is presumed.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The package does not assume:

- every proposed amendment becomes approved or effective;
- an amendment trace creates an amendment;
- a trace and approval record are the same record;
- every constitutional date must appear in every trace;
- an unrecorded status can be supplied by inference;
- listing an unaffected matter reapproves it;
- rejected or withdrawn material has constitutional effect;
- historical intelligibility requires a technical mechanism;
- an author, recorder, custodian, administrator or repository actor holds Governance Authority; or
- a successor CGP-02 subject is authorized.

## 5. Drafting Status

The five authorized Founder Review deliverables are prepared for technical and Founder review. The draft remains unapproved, unadopted and without constitutional effect.
