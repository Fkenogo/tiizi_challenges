## 3. Constitutional Scope

CGP-01 provides platform-wide constitutional governance principles. It governs the philosophy by which future governance programmes and standards should be justified, interpreted and developed, subject to the precedence boundaries expressed in the approved instrument.

Its approved scope includes governance serving Tiizi's mission; participation-enabling governance; Mission Fidelity; Participation Before Administrative Convenience; truth and attribution; declared purpose and scope; relationship separation; Community-first governance; Minimum Sufficient Governance; Progressive Governance; no speculative governance; Governance Proportionality; constitutional-purpose restraint for governance bodies; conceptual Governance Maturity; constitutional and operational governance separation; and the non-authorizing treatment of potential future governance domains.

Approval does not allocate an EOG-01 relationship, create a Platform Authority, approve an operational procedure, create a governance programme, or introduce an implementation requirement.
