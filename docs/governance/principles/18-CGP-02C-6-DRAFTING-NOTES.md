# CGP-02C.6 — Drafting Notes

**Subject:** Dependent-Governance Impact Review

**Status:** Founder Review drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Drafting Boundary Applied

The draft treats one constitutional subject: review of how a proposed constitutional amendment may affect governance that depends upon or references the meaning within the amendment scope.

It does not conduct a specific impact review, change dependent governance, resolve conflict, prescribe cross-reference updates or define later Blueprint subjects.

## 2. Architectural Observations

### DN-01 — Impact review remains analytical

The draft separates a review finding from constitutional action. Governance Authority retains approval, and the impact record cannot amend, interpret, correct, supersede, adopt or make governance effective.

### DN-02 — Dependency follows constitutional reliance

The proposed boundary follows reliance on constitutional meaning rather than filenames, shared terminology or documentary proximity. A cross-reference may evidence reliance but does not automatically establish impact.

### DN-03 — Four categories preserve uncertainty

Directly affected, potentially affected, unaffected and unresolved are proposed as evidence-bounded findings. They are not lifecycle states and change no constitutional status.

### DN-04 — Review timing is constitutional, not procedural

DGI-06 proposes that impact be examined before amendment approval because affected governance must be visible to the decision. It defines no workflow, reviewer, review sequence or approval procedure.

### DN-05 — Cross-reference impact is not synchronization

The draft permits identification of cross-reference impact while excluding changes to references, indexes, manifests or repository structures.

### DN-06 — Conflict remains deferred

An apparent conflict may make an impact unresolved. The package does not identify a conflict process, allocate escalation or decide disposition.

## 3. Reserved Founder Questions

The Founder Review Decision Pack preserves nine unresolved questions:

1. purpose and meaning;
2. constitutional review point;
3. dependency and review boundary;
4. impact categories;
5. minimum impact-review information;
6. no automatic governance change;
7. amendment-trace and approval-record relationships;
8. cross-reference impact boundary; and
9. deferred governance.

No answer is presumed.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The package does not assume:

- every cross-reference creates constitutional dependency;
- every dependency is directly affected;
- an unaffected finding is permanent;
- an unresolved finding decides approval;
- an impact review resolves conflict;
- an impact finding requires amendment of dependent governance;
- impact review and amendment trace are the same record;
- a reviewer holds Governance Authority;
- review attribution allocates responsibility;
- identifying cross-reference impact authorizes synchronization; or
- any successor package is authorized.
