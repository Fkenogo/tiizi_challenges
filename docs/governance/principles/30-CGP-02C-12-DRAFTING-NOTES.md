## 3. Architectural Observations

### DN-01 — GAP-16 is the controlling gap

Discovery recorded recurring validation practice but no canonical constitutional minimum. The draft therefore defines a bounded constitutional evidence and sufficiency boundary without constitutionalizing existing checklists or reports.

### DN-02 — Validation is not review

Governance Review examines constitutional content, reasoning, boundaries and consequence. Validation examines whether evidence supports a bounded conclusion concerning applicable requirements. VLR-04 through VLR-06 preserve interaction without merger.

### DN-03 — Validation is not a constitutional act

VLR-07 through VLR-09 prevent validation from becoming approval, adoption, constitutional effect or status creation. This preserves the Governance Authority and anti-inference doctrine already established in C.3 and C.4.

### DN-04 — Minimum means constitutional information, not a format

VLR-10 identifies the minimum constitutional information needed to understand a validation conclusion. VLR-11 requires applicable requirements to be examined according to their attributable status and effect. Neither proposition prescribes a report, schema, checklist, tool, signature or repository location.

### DN-05 — Insufficiency is evidentiary, not a silent status treatment

Insufficient, incomplete, contradictory or unavailable evidence limits what validation may conclude. It does not independently reject, invalidate or change the status of the subject examined.

### DN-06 — Existing integrity doctrines remain dependencies

Attribution, dependency, traceability, conflict, history and status integrity are preserved as constitutional dependencies. VLR-19 through VLR-22 apply them to validation evidence without restating their underlying doctrines.

### DN-07 — FEF and generic mechanics remain external

The developing Founder Engineering Framework is not used as a normative source. Generic governance and engineering controls remain future external-framework or implementation matters and receive no Tiizi constitutional status through this draft.

### DN-08 — Final substantive subject does not authorize consolidation

Bounded completion of C.12 would conclude Blueprint deliverable 16 only. Whole-instrument consolidation, complete Founder Review, approval, adoption, Stage E0 completion and transition to CGP-03 require separately governed work.

## 4. Founder Review Amendments

The Founder directed two proposition amendments on 2026-07-25.

### VLR-11

VLR-11 was replaced with:

> **VLR-11.** Validation must examine every constitutional requirement applicable within its declared scope according to that requirement’s attributable status and effect. Review proportionality, administrative simplicity, urgency, implementation readiness or apparent low consequence cannot waive an applicable constitutional requirement.

This amendment avoids silently normalizing every constitutional dependency as approved, preserves each applicable requirement’s recorded status and effect, and aligns VLR-11 with VLR-19 and VLR-20.

### VLR-23

VLR-23 was replaced with:

> **VLR-23.** Documents, reports, declarations, checklists, repositories, files, links, tests, tools, automation, hashes, signatures, interfaces and other mechanisms may support or communicate validation evidence. No particular mechanism is required by these Validation Rules merely by virtue of its form or use, and no mechanism independently establishes conformity, Authority, approval, adoption or constitutional effect. Any mechanism separately required by applicable constitutional governance retains that requirement and effect.

This amendment preserves technology neutrality, prevents C.12 from prescribing a validation mechanism and avoids negating mechanisms separately required by applicable constitutional governance.

No other proposition was substantively altered.

## 6. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The draft does not assume:

- that every constitutional matter requires the same validation breadth;
- that validation requires a particular document, report or checklist;
- that an existing validation report defines the constitutional minimum;
- that a successful mechanical check proves constitutional conformity;
- that a validation conclusion approves, adopts or makes content effective;
- that failed or insufficient validation automatically rejects or invalidates governance;
- that missing evidence proves either conformity or non-conformity;
- that contradictory evidence may be resolved by chronology or implementation;
- that one validated matter validates associated content;
- that validation assigns an actor, role, Authority or accountability;
- that a repository, tool, test, hash or signature is constitutionally required;
- that FEF supplies Tiizi constitutional doctrine;
- that generic validation mechanics belong in CGP-02;
- that this draft validates any earlier CGP package; or
- that C.12 completion authorizes consolidation or CGP-03.

## 7. Deferred Governance

The draft deliberately leaves unresolved:

- operational validation procedure and remediation workflow;
- engineering and repository validation;
- assignment and qualification of validators or reviewers;
- evidence templates, schemas, checklists and technical formats;
- thresholds, scores, tiers and assurance levels;
- scheduling, frequency, automation and service levels;
- implementation and publication controls;
- FEF and cross-project governance controls;
- validation of named instruments and historical claims;
- whole-instrument CGP-02 consolidation;
- complete Founder Review, approval, adoption and constitutional effect;
- Stage E0 completion; and
- authorization of CGP-03 or another package.
