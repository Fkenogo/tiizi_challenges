# CGP-02C.2B — Amendment Classification Founder Review Decision Pack

**Draft under review:** [Amendment Classification Founder Review Draft](08-CGP-02C-2B-AMENDMENT-CLASSIFICATION-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DRAFT.md)

**Status:** Founder decision material; recommendations accepted in principle for the Founder Approval Candidate; constitutional approval pending

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Decision Boundary

This pack presents only Amendment Classification questions. It does not ask the Founder to approve a lifecycle, approval procedure, approval evidence, adoption effect, review trigger, review threshold, proportionality rule, validation doctrine, traceability doctrine, implementation or repository workflow.

The questions are organized by constitutional function. Section A concerns the architecture and extent of the proposed classification. Section B concerns the boundaries that prevent classification from changing or bypassing constitutional meaning. This organization changes no question, option or recommendation.

## 2. Section A — Classification Architecture

### 2.1 ACQ-01 — Minimum Constitutional Classification

#### 2.1.1 Issue

Whether CGP-02 should establish a minimum two-part distinction between Meaning-Preserving Maintenance and Meaning-Changing Amendment.

#### 2.1.2 Current Approved Doctrine

Approved governance requires attributable amendment, historical intelligibility and minimum-sufficient governance. FQ-02 deferred classification design while preserving CHB-02 and CHB-04. No canonical amendment classification is approved.

#### 2.1.3 Proposed Treatment

AMC-01 through AMC-05 and AMC-09 propose two consequence-based classifications without creating subclasses, procedures or review treatment.

#### 2.1.4 Options and Consequences

| Option | Treatment | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Approve the minimum distinction between Meaning-Preserving Maintenance and Meaning-Changing Amendment. | Establishes a compact constitutional boundary while leaving subclasses and treatment to later governance. |
| B | Approve only the consequence-over-label boundary without naming classifications. | Preserves CHB-02 but leaves maintenance terminology and GAP-02 unresolved. |
| C | Establish a broader classification now, including clerical, status-only, interpretive, material and foundational classes. | Addresses more observed terminology but creates distinctions whose consequences have not been constitutionally reviewed. |

**Recommendation — pending Founder approval:** Option A.

**Propositions affected:** AMC-01 through AMC-05 and AMC-09.

**Decision required now:** Yes, for inclusion of the proposed minimum classification.

### 2.2 ACQ-05 — Subclasses and Classification Consequences

#### 2.2.1 Issue

Whether this package should determine subclasses or procedural consequences for either proposed classification.

#### 2.2.2 Current Approved Doctrine

Minimum-sufficient governance favors the least complexity capable of preserving constitutional purpose. Approval, review, validation and traceability governance remain separately authorized subjects.

#### 2.2.3 Proposed Treatment

AMC-02 limits classification complexity. AMC-11 denies independent Authority or effect to a classification label, and AMC-12 preserves historical intelligibility without defining mechanisms.

#### 2.2.4 Options and Consequences

| Option | Treatment | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Defer subclasses and every class-specific procedural consequence. | Keeps this package within classification scope and preserves later governance boundaries. |
| B | Define clerical, status-only, interpretive, material and foundational subclasses now. | Increases descriptive precision but requires unresolved consequence and boundary decisions. |
| C | Attach approval and review requirements directly to the two proposed classes. | Produces immediate treatment rules but improperly combines this package with later approval and review governance. |

**Recommendation — pending Founder approval:** Option A.

**Propositions affected:** AMC-02, AMC-11 and AMC-12.

**Decision required now:** Yes, to preserve the bounded scope of the proposed classification.

## 3. Section B — Classification Boundaries

### 3.1 ACQ-02 — Meaning-Preserving Maintenance Boundary

#### 3.1.1 Issue

How narrowly Meaning-Preserving Maintenance must be defined so that it cannot conceal constitutional change.

#### 3.1.2 Current Approved Doctrine

CHB-04 distinguishes meaning-preserving clarification from meaning-changing action but leaves classification unresolved. Approved governance prohibits silent amendment and interpretation beyond approved text, purpose, scope and precedence.

#### 3.1.3 Proposed Treatment

AMC-05 through AMC-07 limit maintenance to preservation of already-approved meaning and enumerate the constitutional matters it cannot change.

#### 3.1.4 Options and Consequences

| Option | Treatment | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Define maintenance solely by demonstrable preservation of approved meaning and effect, with bounded representation examples. | Provides a usable constitutional distinction while preserving the anti-evasion boundary. |
| B | Limit maintenance to typography and formatting only. | Minimizes ambiguity but excludes objectively demonstrable transcription, reference and status-representation defects. |
| C | Create no maintenance classification and treat every alteration as proposed amendment. | Maximizes formal uniformity but does not distinguish constitutionally unchanged meaning. |

**Recommendation — pending Founder approval:** Option A.

**Propositions affected:** AMC-05 through AMC-07.

**Decision required now:** Yes, if Meaning-Preserving Maintenance is included.

### 3.2 ACQ-03 — Uncertain Constitutional Consequence

#### 3.2.1 Issue

How classification should be bounded when preservation of constitutional meaning or effect cannot be established.

#### 3.2.2 Current Approved Doctrine

Uncertainty must remain explicit. Drafting and practice cannot silently resolve constitutional ambiguity or bypass attributable amendment.

#### 3.2.3 Proposed Treatment

AMC-08 prevents use of maintenance as an evasion label and leaves the classification unresolved without creating a default procedure.

#### 3.2.4 Options and Consequences

| Option | Treatment | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Keep classification unresolved and prohibit maintenance treatment where preservation is not established. | Preserves uncertainty and amendment protection without creating a default class or procedure. |
| B | Classify every uncertain change as Meaning-Changing Amendment. | Creates a clear conservative default but resolves uncertainty before a later treatment standard is designed. |
| C | Permit provisional maintenance classification pending later confirmation. | Reduces immediate burden but creates a pathway by which uncertain change may evade amendment requirements. |

**Recommendation — pending Founder approval:** Option A.

**Proposition affected:** AMC-08.

**Decision required now:** Yes, to prevent the proposed maintenance class from becoming an evasion path.

### 3.3 ACQ-04 — Representation Defects

#### 3.3.1 Issue

Whether objectively demonstrable transcription, formatting, cross-reference and status-representation defects may fall within Meaning-Preserving Maintenance.

#### 3.3.2 Current Approved Doctrine

Historical meaning and approval status must remain attributable. Existing housekeeping practice is not general doctrine, and no general approval-time metadata rule is approved.

#### 3.3.3 Proposed Treatment

AMC-06 permits bounded representation defects only where an attributable governing source makes unchanged meaning and effect objectively determinable. AMC-07 prohibits changing decision status or constitutional content.

#### 3.3.4 Options and Consequences

| Option | Treatment | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Include the bounded representation examples within maintenance without creating separate subclasses. | Addresses clear defects while keeping constitutional meaning and effect controlling. |
| B | Exclude all cross-reference and status-representation defects from maintenance. | Reduces classification risk but treats demonstrably non-substantive defects as unresolved constitutional changes. |
| C | Create a separate Status and Reference Maintenance class. | Provides specificity but expands the classification before class-specific consequences are governed. |

**Recommendation — pending Founder approval:** Option A.

**Propositions affected:** AMC-06 and AMC-07.

**Decision required now:** Yes, for inclusion of these bounded examples; approval-time treatment remains deferred.
