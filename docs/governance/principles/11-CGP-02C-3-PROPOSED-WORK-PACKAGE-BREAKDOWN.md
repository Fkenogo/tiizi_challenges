Every option must preserve the scope and exclusions in the [Approval Governance Planning Package](11-CGP-02C-3-APPROVAL-GOVERNANCE-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md). ## 3. Decomposition Options

### 3.1 Option A — One Bounded Drafting Package

**Proposed package:** `CGP-02C.3` — Approval Governance

**Purpose:** Treat approval meaning, Governance Authority boundaries, evidence and approval-record integrity as one constitutional subject.

**Scope:** The complete bounded scope in the Planning Package.

**Dependencies:** All preserved dependencies in the Planning Package.

**Founder decision points:** The seven substantive Approval Governance questions prepared for later Founder review.

**Validation gates:** One-subject boundary; proposition traceability; Authority discipline; recorder/approver separation; exclusion completeness; earlier-package preservation.

**Expected outputs:** Founder Review Draft, Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation, Founder Review Decision Pack, Drafting Notes, and Bounded Change and Scope Report.

**Planning assessment:** Minimizes fragmentation and keeps closely related approval-record questions with the constitutional meaning of approval.

### 3.2 Option B — Two Sequential Drafting Packages

**Proposed package A:** identifier pending — Approval Meaning and Authority Boundary

**Proposed package B:** identifier pending — Approval Evidence and Record Integrity

**Purpose:** Separate approval meaning and Authority boundaries from evidence and record questions.

**Dependencies:** Package B would depend on the reviewed outcome of Package A; both would retain all common dependencies.

**Founder decision points:** Package A would prepare questions 1, 2 and 5. Package B would prepare questions 3, 4, 6 and 7.

**Validation gates:** Each package must remain one subject, avoid effect or adoption doctrine and preserve the boundary between exercising approval and recording it.

**Expected outputs:** A separate five-document Founder Review set for each authorized package.

**Planning assessment:** Provides a narrower review unit but increases coordination, cross-package terminology risk and documentary burden.

### 3.3 Option C — Defer Decomposition

**Treatment:** Approve neither a single package nor a split package until further planning evidence is authorized.

**Consequence:** No drafting may begin. The proposed identifier and title may be considered separately, but package treatment remains unresolved.

## 4. Planning Recommendation

**Recommended — pending Founder approval:** Option A, one bounded Approval Governance drafting package.

The recommendation is planning advice only. It does not approve the identifier, title, scope, decomposition or drafting.

## 5. Common Entry Gate

Whichever option the Founder selects, drafting remains prohibited until the Founder separately records:

- identifier approval;
- title approval;
- scope approval;
- decomposition approval; and
- drafting authorization.

## 6. Common Exclusions

No option may introduce adoption, constitutional effect, dates of effect or application, earlier CGP-02C subjects, traceability doctrine, impact review, conflict governance, supersession, retirement, validation rules, index synchronization, temporary governance, implementation or accountability allocation.
