# CGP-02C.7 — Drafting Notes

**Subject:** Conflict Review and Escalation

**Status:** Founder Review drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Drafting Boundary Applied

The draft treats one constitutional subject: identifying, reviewing, escalating, evidencing and preserving governance conflict without deciding an actual conflict.

It does not determine precedence, supersede governance, allocate a reviewer or recorder, establish validation or define implementation.

## 2. Architectural Observations

### DN-01 — Conflict requires constitutional incompatibility

The proposed definition follows constitutional treatment within a shared scope rather than wording difference, document location or preference.

### DN-02 — Identification, review and escalation remain distinct

Identification states the asserted incompatibility. Review examines its basis. Escalation communicates an unresolved constitutional choice to Governance Authority. None independently disposes of the conflict.

### DN-03 — Governance Authority remains unchanged

The draft applies the existing Governance Authority boundary. It does not create a Conflict Authority, delegate Authority or allocate an actor.

### DN-04 — Interpretation remains bounded

CRE-11 permits a review to record an unambiguous conforming treatment already supplied by approved governance. It does not permit review to create a new treatment or resolve genuine conflict through preference.

### DN-05 — Review and disposition evidence remain distinct

Conflict-review evidence records analysis. Conflict-disposition evidence records an attributable Governance Authority decision where one exists. Neither replaces approval, amendment, adoption or effect records.

### DN-06 — Supersession remains deferred

A conflict disposition may identify constitutional treatment, but this package does not define whether or how another instrument is superseded. Supersession is the next distinct Blueprint subject.

## 3. Reserved Founder Questions

The Founder Review Decision Pack preserves nine unresolved questions:

1. meaning and purpose;
2. conflict-identification basis;
3. identification, review and escalation boundary;
4. Governance Authority interaction;
5. minimum conflict-review evidence;
6. minimum conflict-disposition evidence;
7. unresolved conflict treatment;
8. anti-inference boundary; and
9. deferred governance.

No answer is presumed.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The package does not assume:

- wording difference establishes conflict;
- conflict identification suspends governance;
- a reviewer may choose a precedence outcome;
- escalation directs Governance Authority;
- a conflict record creates Authority;
- disposition evidence is an approval or amendment record;
- an unresolved conflict creates a temporary constitutional rule;
- absence of an identified conflict proves consistency;
- a disposition silently establishes supersession;
- an actor is allocated to review or record conflict; or
- any successor package is authorized.
