## 1. Constitutional Purpose of Amendment Classification

**AMC-01.** Amendment Classification provides a constitutional vocabulary for distinguishing proposed changes by their consequence for approved constitutional meaning and effect. Classification supports consistent constitutional treatment but does not itself approve a change or determine a procedure.

**AMC-02.** Amendment Classification must remain no more elaborate than its constitutional purpose requires. A classification cannot be justified merely by document type, document label, organisational size, administrative convenience or repeated practice.

## 2. Constitutional Consequence Controls Classification

This package preserves CHB-02 and CHB-04 in the [CGP-02C.1 Founder Approval Candidate](05-CGP-02C-1-CONSTITUTIONAL-FRAMEWORK-AND-AMENDMENT-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT.md). It does not amend, replace or complete those propositions.

**AMC-03.** A proposed change is classified by what it would do to constitutional meaning or effect, not by the label, form, location or description applied to it. Calling a change clarification, correction, maintenance, housekeeping or operational action cannot remove an amendment requirement that follows from its constitutional consequence.

**AMC-04.** Classification must be assessed against the approved text, purpose, scope, precedence and historically applicable meaning of the constitutional instrument concerned. Intended convenience, asserted harmlessness or local acceptance cannot substitute for that constitutional basis.

## 3. Meaning-Preserving Maintenance

**AMC-05.** Meaning-Preserving Maintenance is a proposed classification for a change limited to preserving the already-approved constitutional meaning and effect of an instrument. It does not create, remove or alter constitutional meaning.

**AMC-06.** Correction of an objectively demonstrable transcription, formatting, cross-reference or status-representation defect may qualify as Meaning-Preserving Maintenance only where the attributable governing source makes the unchanged constitutional meaning and effect determinable.

**AMC-07.** Meaning-Preserving Maintenance cannot add, remove, expand, narrow, substitute, reprioritize or reinterpret an approved concept, principle, boundary, Authority type, accountability relationship, precedence position, decision status or amendment requirement. It cannot convert a pending, deferred, rejected or unapproved matter into approved governance.

**AMC-08.** Where preservation of constitutional meaning or effect cannot be established from the attributable governing source, the proposed change cannot be classified as Meaning-Preserving Maintenance merely to avoid an amendment requirement. The classification remains unresolved until later approved governance determines its treatment.

## 4. Meaning-Changing Amendment

**AMC-09.** Meaning-Changing Amendment is a proposed classification for a change that would establish different constitutional meaning or effect, including a change to what exists or why it exists, or to an approved concept, principle, boundary, Authority type, accountability relationship, precedence position or amendment requirement.

**AMC-10.** A Meaning-Changing Amendment remains constitutional amendment regardless of whether it is described as minor, beneficial, corrective, editorial, interpretive, operational, administrative or programme-related.

## 5. Classification Boundary and Explicit Deferrals

**AMC-11.** A classification label does not exercise Governance Authority, approve a proposed change, establish constitutional effect or determine lifecycle, review, approval, adoption, validation, traceability or implementation treatment.

**AMC-12.** No classification permits silent alteration or concealment of constitutional history. The distinction between preserved meaning and changed meaning must remain historically intelligible, while preservation mechanisms remain outside this package.

This draft does not determine:

- whether the two proposed classifications are sufficient or whether later subclasses are necessary;
- approval procedure or approval evidence;
- adoption or constitutional effect;
- effective dates;
- review triggers, thresholds or proportionality;
- who performs or records classification;
- how a disputed or unresolved classification is decided;
- class-specific validation or traceability requirements;
- treatment of approval-time changes;
- supersession, retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- implementation, workflow or repository process; or
- technical preservation mechanisms.

These matters require attributable Founder decision or their separately authorized CGP-02 packages. No observed practice or classification label answers them through repetition or convenience.
