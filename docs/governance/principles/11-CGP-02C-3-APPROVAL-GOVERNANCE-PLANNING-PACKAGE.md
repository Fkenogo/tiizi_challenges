## 4. Preserved Canonical Wording

The CGP-02C.1 Founder Decision Record preserves the following wording for future approval records:

> Founder, exercising Governance Authority

This planning package carries that wording forward as a dependency. It does not create, reapprove, reinterpret or apply the wording, and it does not alter historical approval records.

“Approval Authority” is not introduced as a Platform Authority name. Within this planning package, authority to establish constitutional approval remains a question bounded by the already approved Governance Authority category.

## 5. Proposed Bounded Scope

Subject to Founder approval, Approval Governance would be limited to examining:

- the constitutional meaning of approval;
- Governance Authority exercised in approval;
- canonical constitutional-capacity wording;
- minimum approval evidence;
- minimum approval-record content;
- the distinction between exercising approval and recording approval;
- boundaries preventing a recorder, author, repository administrator or document custodian from acquiring authority to approve or Governance Authority merely by performing a supporting function;
- an approval-reference convention; and
- permissible approval-time representation or metadata questions only where necessary to define approval-record integrity.

These are proposed drafting questions, not constitutional rules. No actor, procedure, record format, technical mechanism or relationship allocation is approved by listing them.

## 6. Founder Questions for Later Constitutional Review

If drafting is separately authorized, the resulting Founder Review package must prepare without presuming answers:

1. What constitutes constitutional approval?
2. What approval capacity wording must identify the Founder’s exercise of Governance Authority?
3. What is the minimum constitutional approval evidence?
4. What information must an approval record contain?
5. Who may create or maintain an approval record without gaining authority to approve or Governance Authority?
6. What approval-reference convention is required?
7. Which approval-time metadata or representation changes may be recorded without altering approved constitutional meaning?

The present Founder Planning Decision Pack separately asks:

8. Whether the identifier `CGP-02C.3` and title **Approval Governance** are approved.
9. Whether drafting is authorized after planning review.
