# CGP-02C.9 — Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules

**Instrument:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Work package:** CGP-02C.9 — Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules

**Status:** Founder Review Draft; unapproved, unadopted and without constitutional effect

**Blueprint subject:** Deliverable 13 — Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules

**Drafting specification:** [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md)

**Authorized planning boundary:** [CGP-02C.9 Planning Package](../../programme/CGP-02C-9-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md)

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Founder Review Draft proposes one bounded constitutional subject: the constitutional treatment and separation of retirement, withdrawal and rejection.

It does not apply any treatment to an actual governance instrument. It does not redefine supersession, amendment, approval, adoption or constitutional effect; define Historical Preservation, Governance Index or Validation Rules; create a Platform Authority; allocate accountability; or define implementation.

## 2. Retirement

**RWR-01.** Retirement is the constitutional treatment by which an explicit and attributable act of Governance Authority establishes that identified governance which held a current governing position no longer holds that position within a declared scope, without the retirement act itself identifying replacement governance.

**RWR-02.** The purpose of retirement is to make the attributable ending of a current governing position, its bounded scope, constitutional basis and non-replacement effect explicit and objectively reviewable.

## 3. Withdrawal

**RWR-03.** Withdrawal is the constitutional treatment by which an explicit and attributable act of Governance Authority removes identified proposed or pending governance from further constitutional consideration within a declared scope, without deciding its constitutional merits through that act.

**RWR-04.** The purpose of withdrawal is to make the attributable removal of an identified pending matter, its scope and its resulting non-pending treatment explicit without communicating approval, rejection, adoption or constitutional effect.

## 4. Rejection

**RWR-05.** Rejection is the constitutional treatment by which an explicit and attributable act of Governance Authority determines that identified proposed or pending governance is not approved within the declared decision scope.

**RWR-06.** The purpose of rejection is to preserve an attributable negative constitutional decision, its basis and its bounded scope without treating rejected content as approved, adopted, effective, retired or superseded.

## 5. Mutual and External Boundaries

**RWR-07.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection are distinct constitutional treatments. Retirement ends a current governing position; withdrawal removes a proposed or pending matter from further consideration without deciding its merits; rejection records a decision not to approve identified proposed or pending governance.

**RWR-08.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection are distinct from supersession. Supersession establishes an attributable replacement relationship; none of the three treatments establishes replacement governance merely by ending, removing or rejecting identified governance.

**RWR-09.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection are distinct from amendment. None changes constitutional content or meaning merely by changing the bounded treatment of identified governance, and none may be used to conceal a meaning-changing amendment.

**RWR-10.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection are distinct from approval. An approval decision does not establish any of the three treatments unless the same or a separate attributable Governance Authority decision expressly establishes the additional treatment and its scope.

**RWR-11.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection are distinct from adoption and constitutional effect. None by itself incorporates content into the authoritative constitutional governance of Tiizi, makes content authoritatively govern or alters an adoption, effective or application date.

**RWR-12.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection are distinct from Historical Preservation. These treatments establish bounded constitutional status; they do not define how governance and its historical context must be retained, stored, archived, represented or made available.

## 6. Governance Authority, Attribution and Scope

**RWR-13.** Retirement, withdrawal and rejection may be established only through an explicit and attributable act within existing Governance Authority. No separate Retirement Authority, Withdrawal Authority or Rejection Authority is created.

**RWR-14.** Drafting, proposing, reviewing, recording, indexing, publishing, storing, presenting, administering or implementing governance does not confer Governance Authority and cannot establish retirement, withdrawal or rejection.

**RWR-15.** One attributable Governance Authority decision may record more than one constitutional treatment, but retirement, withdrawal, rejection and every accompanying treatment must remain independently identifiable by meaning, subject and scope.

**RWR-16.** Each treatment must identify its exact constitutional subject and declared scope. Treatment of one instrument, provision, proposal, decision request or bounded matter does not extend to another matter by association, chronology, proximity or shared documentation.

## 7. Minimum Constitutional Evidence

**RWR-17.** Evidence of retirement, withdrawal or rejection must identify the attributable decision, the Governance Authority exercised, the decision date, the identified governance or matter under treatment, the treatment established and its bounded scope.

**RWR-18.** The evidence must preserve the stated constitutional basis and rationale, any partial or conditional boundary, and any unresolved limitation necessary to prevent the record from overstating the attributable decision.

**RWR-19.** The evidence must distinguish the treatment established from any matter that remains approved, adopted, effective, pending, deferred, unaffected, superseded or subject to another separately established constitutional status.

**RWR-20.** Evidence may reference approval, adoption, amendment-trace, dependent-impact, conflict-disposition or supersession records where relevant. It does not replace those records, create a missing constitutional status or enlarge their scope.

## 8. Integrity and Anti-Inference

**RWR-21.** A label, status field, index entry, cross-reference, repository action, publication, implementation, operational behaviour or repeated practice cannot establish, enlarge, narrow or substitute retirement, withdrawal or rejection except to the extent already established by an explicit attributable act of Governance Authority.

**RWR-22.** Silence, inactivity, non-use, omission from a current representation, passage of time, abandonment in practice or absence of implementation does not by itself retire, withdraw or reject governance.

**RWR-23.** Governance subject to retirement, withdrawal or rejection must remain historically identifiable and intelligible together with the attributable treatment and its declared context. This requirement does not define Historical Preservation doctrine or any preservation mechanism.

**RWR-24.** This proposed treatment does not retrospectively classify an existing instrument or matter as retired, withdrawn or rejected; validate a historical status claim; or attribute a treatment to a decision that did not expressly establish it.

## 9. Deferred Governance

The following remain outside this work package:

- application of retirement, withdrawal or rejection to an actual governance instrument or matter;
- Historical Preservation doctrine;
- governance-index, status, manifest or cross-reference synchronization;
- validation doctrine and validation procedure;
- storage, retention, archival, deletion, destruction, restoration or versioning;
- temporary or emergency governance;
- implementation and repository procedure;
- allocation of who proposes, reviews, decides, records, maintains, publishes or implements a treatment;
- roles, permissions and accountability relationships;
- technical schema, identifiers, automation or publication; and
- authorization of any successor work package.

## 10. Draft Status and Non-Effects

RWR-01 through RWR-24 are proposed constitutional treatments for Founder review. They are not approved, adopted or constitutionally effective.

This draft:

- retires, withdraws and rejects no governance;
- changes no existing governance status;
- amends, approves, adopts and supersedes no governance;
- creates no constitutional effect;
- creates no Platform Authority;
- allocates no accountability, role or permission;
- defines no Historical Preservation, Governance Index or Validation Rules;
- changes no CGP-02 dependency or programme state; and
- authorizes no successor work package.
