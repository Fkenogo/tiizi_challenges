# CGP-02C.2 — Planning Package

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Proposed programme:** CGP-02C.2 — Three sequential bounded work packages

**Status:** Planning proposal for review; not constitutional governance and not drafting authorization

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Purpose

This planning package proposes the scope, dependencies, deliverables, decision boundaries and validation objectives for CGP-02C.2. It creates no constitutional text, resolves no Founder decision and does not authorize drafting merely by existing.

CGP-02C.2 should build on the completed CGP-02C.1 Founder Approval Candidate without reopening its constitutional propositions or extending its three-section scope.

## 2. Proposed Scope

### 2.1 Sequential Planning Order

| Sequence | Work package | Sole drafting subject | Entry dependency |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | CGP-02C.2A | Governance Lifecycle | CGP-02C.1 Founder Approval Candidate and recorded decisions. |
| 2 | CGP-02C.2B | Amendment Classification | CGP-02C.2A review outcome and preserved CGP-02C.1 boundaries. |
| 3 | CGP-02C.2C | Review Triggers and Proportionality | CGP-02C.2A and CGP-02C.2B review outcomes and preserved CGP-02C.1 review principles. |

Each work package requires separate authorization. Completion or review of one package does not approve it or automatically authorize the next.

### 2.2 Bounded Drafting Subjects

#### CGP-02C.2A — Governance Lifecycle

The sole proposed drafting subject is Governance Lifecycle, limited to:

- constitutional purpose of a governance-document lifecycle;
- minimum conceptual distinctions among draft, review, approval, adoption, application, transition and completion;
- the boundary between a document’s status and its constitutional effect; and
- explicit identification of lifecycle questions that must remain deferred.

#### CGP-02C.2B — Amendment Classification

The sole proposed drafting subject is Amendment Classification, limited to:

- classification need and constitutional purpose;
- the relationship between meaning-preserving maintenance and meaning-changing amendment;
- classification by constitutional consequence rather than document label; and
- boundaries preventing a class from bypassing approved amendment requirements.

#### CGP-02C.2C — Review Triggers and Proportionality

The sole proposed drafting subject is Review Triggers and Proportionality, limited to:

- constitutional bases that may trigger review;
- consequence-based review proportionality;
- the relationship between objective reviewability and independent review; and
- boundaries preventing review depth from removing approved constitutional minima.

These subjects remain proposed drafting coverage only. This planning package does not state the eventual constitutional rules.

### 2.3 Common Excluded Subjects

CGP-02C.2A, CGP-02C.2B and CGP-02C.2C must not draft:

- approval governance or approval-record procedure;
- approval evidence requirements;
- adoption or constitutional-effect rules beyond identifying their lifecycle dependency;
- approval, effective, adoption or application date rules;
- amendment traceability requirements;
- dependent-governance impact review;
- conflict-review procedure or escalation mechanics;
- supersession rules;
- retirement, withdrawal or rejection rules;
- historical-preservation mechanisms;
- governance-index or status-synchronization rules;
- validation rules;
- temporary or emergency constitutional governance;
- roles, permissions or accountability allocations;
- technical workflow, repository tooling, schema or implementation; or
- any CGP-02C.1 proposition amendment.

## 7. Validation Objectives

### 7.1 Common Validation Objectives

Every later work package should be validated to confirm:

- its draft contains exactly one authorized constitutional subject;
- every constitutional proposition traces to an approved normative source or explicit Founder question;
- relevant discovery gaps and conflicts receive proposed treatment, a Founder question or explicit deferral without source-document correction;
- observed practice is not converted into doctrine through repetition;
- no Platform Authority or accountability relationship is created, extended or allocated;
- no approval procedure, supersession, retirement, validation, traceability, technical workflow or implementation is introduced;
- CGP-02C.1, earlier CGP-02C.2 packages and every approved governance instrument remain unchanged;
- relative links, Markdown structure, whitespace and EOF checks pass; and
- `git diff --check` passes.

### 7.2 Package-specific Validation Objectives

| Work package | Additional validation gate |
|---|---|
| CGP-02C.2A | Lifecycle terminology does not silently determine approval procedure, adoption effect, effective dates, supersession or retirement. |
| CGP-02C.2B | CHB-02 and CHB-04 remain preserved; no class bypasses constitutional effect or becomes approved without attributable Founder decision. |
| CGP-02C.2C | GVR-03 through GVR-05 remain preserved; no trigger, threshold, tier or review procedure becomes approved without attributable Founder decision; review remains distinct from approval. |

## 9. Planning Verdict

CGP-02C.2 is suitable for Founder consideration as three sequential bounded work packages: CGP-02C.2A for Governance Lifecycle, CGP-02C.2B for Amendment Classification, and CGP-02C.2C for Review Triggers and Proportionality. Each package requires separate authorization and review. This planning verdict is not drafting authorization, creates no constitutional governance and leaves all proposed decisions unapproved.
