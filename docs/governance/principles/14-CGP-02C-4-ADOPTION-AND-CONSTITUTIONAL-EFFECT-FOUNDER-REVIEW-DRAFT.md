## 1. Constitutional Adoption

**ACE-01.** Constitutional adoption is an explicit and attributable governance decision that incorporates identified, approved constitutional content into the authoritative constitutional governance of Tiizi within a declared scope.

**ACE-02.** Governance Authority is the approved Platform Authority exercised in constitutional adoption. This package creates no separate Adoption Authority and does not extend, transfer or redistribute Governance Authority.

**ACE-03.** Adoption is bounded by the approved content and scope identified in the adoption decision. Adoption of one proposition, section, instrument or amendment does not adopt another matter by association, proximity or shared documentation.

**ACE-04.** Adoption must be expressly established. Approval, drafting, review, validation, completion, publication, implementation, repository placement, reliance, silence or passage of time does not by itself establish adoption.

## 2. Constitutional Effect

**ACE-05.** Constitutional effect is the condition in which adopted constitutional content authoritatively governs within its declared constitutional scope.

**ACE-06.** Governance Authority must expressly establish when adopted constitutional content is to have constitutional effect. This package creates no separate Effect Authority.

**ACE-07.** Constitutional effect is bounded by the adopted content, declared scope and attributable effect decision. It does not by itself implement governance, allocate accountability, create operational procedure or authorize conduct outside that scope.

**ACE-08.** Constitutional effect must not be inferred from use, implementation, repository status, document presentation, administrative treatment, operational reliance, silence or passage of time.

## 3. Approval and Adoption

**ACE-09.** Constitutional approval and constitutional adoption are distinct. Approval establishes an attributable decision concerning identified constitutional content; adoption establishes that approved content as incorporated into the authoritative constitutional governance of Tiizi within a declared scope.

**ACE-10.** Approval does not by itself establish adoption, and adoption does not approve content beyond the attributable approval on which it depends.

**ACE-11.** Approval and adoption may be expressed in one attributable Governance Authority decision only where the decision identifies approval and adoption separately and leaves neither to implication. Recording approval and adoption within one attributable Governance Authority decision does not merge their constitutional meaning, constitutional sequence or constitutional effect. Each constitutional act remains independently attributable.

## 4. Constitutional Dates

**ACE-12.** The approval date is the date on which Governance Authority approves the identified constitutional content.

**ACE-13.** The adoption date is the date on which Governance Authority adopts the identified approved content into the authoritative constitutional governance of Tiizi.

**ACE-14.** The effective date is the date from which adopted constitutional content has constitutional effect within its declared scope.

**ACE-15.** An application date is optional. It exists only where Governance Authority expressly declares phased or scope-specific constitutional application. It supplements and never replaces the approval date, adoption date or effective date.

**ACE-16.** Approval, adoption, effective and application dates may coincide, but coincidence does not make their constitutional meanings interchangeable. Every date that is constitutionally relevant must remain attributable to the decision that establishes it.

## 5. Anti-Inference Boundaries

**Constitutional Anti-Inference Principle.** No constitutional status arises except through an explicit attributable act of Governance Authority. Records, labels, repository actions, implementation, operational behaviour and administrative activity communicate constitutional status only to the extent that such status has already been established by attributable Governance Authority.

**ACE-17.** A record, constitutional status designation or representation may communicate adoption or constitutional effect only to the extent supported by the attributable Governance Authority decision. Recording or presenting a constitutional status does not create that status.

**ACE-18.** A title, filename, version label, index entry, publication action, repository location, document movement or administrative designation cannot by itself establish constitutional adoption or create constitutional effect.

**ACE-19.** Implementation, operational use, compliance, reliance or technical enforcement cannot substitute for adoption or create constitutional effect.

**ACE-20.** Where adoption, constitutional effect or a relevant date is absent, ambiguous or inconsistent, the missing constitutional status must remain unresolved rather than be supplied by inference.

**ACE-21.** This proposed treatment does not retrospectively assign adoption, constitutional effect or date meanings to historical governance records that did not expressly establish them.

## 6. Scope Boundary and Deferred Governance

This Founder Review Draft defines proposed constitutional treatment only for adoption, constitutional effect, approval/adoption distinction, approval date, adoption date, effective date, application date and the minimum anti-inference boundaries necessary to keep those concepts attributable.

It does not adopt any governance, create constitutional effect, establish an effective or application date for any instrument, or approve any proposition in this draft.

It does not define governance lifecycle, amendment classification, review triggers, proportionality, approval procedure, approval evidence, amendment traceability, dependent-governance impact review, conflict review or escalation, supersession, retirement, withdrawal, rejection, validation doctrine, governance-index synchronization, temporary or emergency governance, implementation, repository workflow, technical date or identifier formats, a new Platform Authority or an accountability allocation.

No deferred constitutional subject may be introduced into this work package except through a separately authorized Planning Package and attributable Founder Authorization. No additional constitutional subjects are to be introduced.

The attributable Founder decisions are recorded in the [Founder Decision Record](14-CGP-02C-4-FOUNDER-DECISION-RECORD.md). This Founder Review Draft has not been adopted and is not constitutionally effective.
