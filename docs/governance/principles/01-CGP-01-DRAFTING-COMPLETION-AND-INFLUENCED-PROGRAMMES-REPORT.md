## 3. Governance Programmes Influenced by CGP-01

Now that CGP-01 is approved, the following programmes should apply its principles within their own approved scope:

| Programme | CGP-01 influence | Matters CGP-01 does not decide |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutional amendment and review | Proportionality, traceability and historical intelligibility | Approval actors, review procedure and amendment workflow |
| Entity Relationship Allocation Register | Relationship separation and minimum sufficient allocation | Any relationship holder or allocation |
| Group Governance Body governance | Progressive, optional and proportionate governance bodies | Composition, appointment, election, procedure, tenure and relationships |
| Group Charter governance | Community purpose, attributable evolution and proportionality | Charter content, binding effect, amendment and history rules |
| Stewardship continuity | Singular answerability and attributable change | Reassignment procedure, succession and temporary protection |
| Membership and Group lifecycle | Community-first and voluntary participation | States, transitions, eligibility, removal and retention |
| Challenge governance and lifecycle | Proportionate governance for collective Undertakings | Creation, amendment, states, closure and stewardship allocation |
| Moderation and Community conduct | Community-first restraint subject to safety and accountability | Conduct rules, enforcement, appeal and permissions |
| Roles and permissions | Titles do not imply constitutional relationships | Role vocabulary, permissions and access controls |
| Privacy governance | Proportionality remains subordinate to privacy protections | Visibility rules, consent, retention and lawful use |
| Security governance | Minimum sufficient governance cannot reduce security obligations | Security controls, threat models and technical enforcement |
| Administrative governance | Purpose, scope, reviewability and Community restraint | Administrative roles, actions and procedures |
| Operational governance | Constitutional/operational separation and attributable practice | Operating procedures, service ownership and controls |
| Audit and historical preservation | Historical intelligibility and reviewable change | Audit structures, retention periods and storage mechanisms |
| Knowledge contributor governance | Progressive participation while truth remains governed | Eligibility, review workflow, permissions and lifecycle |
| Domain admission governance | Conceptual stability and attributable extension | Which domain is admitted or how it operates |
| Governance documentation standards | Traceability, precedence and explicit status | Document tooling, templates and publication process |

Influence does not create dependency until a later programme expressly cites approved CGP-01.

## 4. Identified Tensions for Founder Review

The Cross-reference Analysis records eight bounded matters:

1. interpretive influence versus approved-instrument precedence;
2. Community preference versus Platform obligations;
3. minimum governance versus high-impact assurance;
4. Stewardship Council terminology versus Group Governance Body canonical naming;
5. conceptual maturity versus prohibited Governance Health assessment;
6. progressive governance versus existing obligations; and
7. constitutional versus operational governance.
8. potential future domains versus implied programme approval.

No material contradiction or required amendment to existing governance was identified.

## 5. Completion Boundary

This drafting phase introduced no approval, entity, Authority, relationship allocation, ownership assignment, role, permission, lifecycle, procedure, metric, incentive, product behaviour or implementation requirement.

Founder review and adoption are complete. Derivative governance remains a separate future action requiring its own justified scope and attributable approval.
