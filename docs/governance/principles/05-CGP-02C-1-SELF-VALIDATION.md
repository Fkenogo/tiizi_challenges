## 4. Summary of Proposed Constitutional Propositions

The draft introduces 26 proposed propositions for Founder review:

- **9 Amendment Principles:** governed evolution; amendment Authority; declared purpose and scope; precedence; no silent amendment; Authority discipline; historical intelligibility; conceptual stability; minimum necessary amendment.
- **9 Governance Review Principles:** objective reviewability; review dimensions; proportionality; preservation of constitutional minima; review/approval separation; evidence classification; conflict discipline; Governance Authority escalation; binding protections.
- **8 Constitutional Change Boundaries:** constitutional effect; substance over label; interpretation; clarification restraint; operational separation; technical and programme separation; draft and practice restraint; Authority restraint.

All 26 propositions remain proposed governance. None becomes constitutional governance through this draft or validation report. The draft’s final scope paragraph is not a constitutional proposition and only preserves the CGP-02C.1 boundary established by the frozen blueprint.
