## 11. VLQ-09 — Technology-Neutral, Authority and Deferred-Governance Boundary

**Selected option:** Option B

**Founder decision:** Approved with amendment to VLR-23

**Decision date:** 2026-07-25

**Affected propositions:** VLR-23–VLR-24; Founder Review Draft section 9

**Exact amendment to VLR-23:**

> **VLR-23.** Documents, reports, declarations, checklists, repositories, files, links, tests, tools, automation, hashes, signatures, interfaces and other mechanisms may support or communicate validation evidence. No particular mechanism is required by these Validation Rules merely by virtue of its form or use, and no mechanism independently establishes conformity, Authority, approval, adoption or constitutional effect. Any mechanism separately required by applicable constitutional governance retains that requirement and effect.

**Founder-directed purpose:** Preserve technology neutrality; prevent C.12 from prescribing a validation mechanism; and avoid negating mechanisms separately required by existing constitutional governance.

**Condition:** VLR-23 is approved only with the exact replacement above.
