# CGP-02C.10 — Drafting Notes

**Draft supported:** [Historical Preservation Founder Review Draft](26-CGP-02C-10-HISTORICAL-PRESERVATION-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DRAFT.md)

**Status:** Founder Review supporting record; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

These notes record drafting observations, preserved dependencies, questions reserved for Founder review and assumptions deliberately avoided. They create no constitutional proposition or decision.

## 2. Architectural Observations

### DN-01 — Dedicated subject

The frozen Blueprint identifies Historical Preservation as deliverable 14. The draft therefore treats preservation as one constitutional subject rather than as a technical appendix to amendment traceability or status governance.

### DN-02 — Historical intelligibility is the normative anchor

CGP-01 already requires a later reader to understand which constitutional meaning applied, why a decision was valid and how change affected the governing position. HPR-01 through HPR-09 consolidate that approved principle into proposed subject-specific treatment.

### DN-03 — Preservation is broader than amendment traceability

Amendment traceability concerns a bounded constitutional change. Historical Preservation also concerns decisions and status context that may arise through approval, adoption, effect, supersession, retirement, withdrawal, rejection, deferral or unresolved treatment without being an amendment.

### DN-04 — Current and historical governance remain distinct

The draft protects historically applicable governance from retrospective erasure while preventing historical presence from establishing current status. HPR-04 through HPR-06 and HPR-14 through HPR-16 must be reviewed as one reciprocal boundary.

### DN-05 — Status treatment is not preservation

Supersession, retirement, withdrawal and rejection establish bounded constitutional treatments. Historical Preservation communicates their attributable context but does not establish, change or reverse them.

### DN-06 — Meaning precedes mechanism

Storage, archival, retention, repository and versioning mechanisms may support preservation but cannot establish constitutional meaning or status. No mechanism is selected or required.

### DN-07 — No preservation actor is allocated

The draft defines what must remain constitutionally intelligible, not who records, stores, maintains or presents it. HPR-23 prevents those activities from conferring Governance Authority or creating a Historical Preservation Authority.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The draft does not assume:

- that continued file availability proves current constitutional status;
- that omission or deletion proves non-current status;
- that a repository, index, archive or storage system is constitutionally authoritative;
- that all historical records contain complete attribution, rationale, dates or status;
- that missing evidence may be reconstructed through drafting;
- that preservation requires indefinite retention or any particular retention period;
- that preservation requires immutable storage, a version-control system or a specific format;
- that one record must contain every relevant constitutional treatment;
- that Historical Preservation changes precedence;
- that governance is invalid merely because later governance changes it;
- that any named historical instrument requires correction;
- that recording or maintaining history allocates Authority or accountability; or
- that a later Blueprint subject is authorized.

## 5. Deferred Governance

The draft deliberately leaves unresolved:

- Governance Index and Status Integrity;
- Validation Rules;
- storage, archival, backup, restoration and retrieval mechanisms;
- retention, deletion and destruction policy;
- repository structure, administration and access;
- document-versioning implementation;
- technical formats, schemas, identifiers and integrity mechanisms;
- historical correction and reconciliation procedures;
- allocation of preservation responsibilities, roles and permissions; and
- authorization of later work packages.

## 6. Drafting Status

The drafting milestone is complete for technical Founder Review only when the companion validation and scope reports pass. These notes approve no proposition and do not create a Founder Decision Record, Founder Approval Candidate or completion report.
