# CGP-02C.2B — Amendment Classification Drafting Notes

**Status:** Drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Drafting Contract Applied

The draft follows the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md), the approved [CGP-02C.2 Planning Package](06-CGP-02C-2-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) and the [CGP-02C.2 Work Package Breakdown](06-CGP-02C-2-WORK-PACKAGE-BREAKDOWN.md). Amendment Classification is the only constitutional subject addressed.

## 2. Preserved Dependencies

- CHB-02 remains unchanged: constitutional effect rather than label determines whether amendment requirements apply.
- CHB-04 remains unchanged: meaning-preserving clarification is distinct from meaning-changing action, while classification awaited this package.
- FQ-02 authorized preparation of classification options; it did not approve a classification.
- CGP-02C.2A supplies reviewed lifecycle vocabulary and a status/effect boundary but remains constitutionally unapproved and is not used as normative Authority.

## 3. Architectural Observations

- The smallest classification capable of expressing CHB-04 is a distinction between meaning preservation and meaning change.
- The blueprint identifies clerical, status-only, interpretive, material and foundational terminology, but repeated terminology does not establish constitutional classes.
- A classification is unsafe if its label can determine its own consequence. The draft therefore keeps attributable governing meaning controlling.
- Bounded defect examples are proposed through ACQ-04 rather than inferred from earlier housekeeping practice.
- Class-specific procedures and review consequences would cross into later authorized CGP-02 subjects and are expressly deferred.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The draft does not assume:

- that a two-part classification is approved;
- that every representation correction is maintenance;
- that “minor,” “editorial,” “clerical” or “status-only” determines constitutional consequence;
- that uncertainty defaults automatically to maintenance or amendment;
- that classification supplies approval or Authority;
- that classification determines review depth or proportionality;
- that existing housekeeping practice is constitutional doctrine;
- that approval-time changes are permitted; or
- that a repository action has constitutional effect.

## 5. Deferred Constitutional Questions

- whether the minimum two-part classification is approved;
- whether additional subclasses are constitutionally necessary;
- how preservation of meaning is evidenced;
- how unresolved or disputed classification is decided;
- who performs or records classification;
- approval-time change treatment;
- class-specific review, approval, validation and traceability requirements;
- lifecycle and constitutional-effect consequences; and
- implementation or repository process.

## 6. Drafting Notes Verdict

No observed classification practice was converted into doctrine. Five classification choices are presented for Founder decision, CHB-02 and CHB-04 remain preserved, and every excluded subject remains deferred.
