# CGP-02C.2A — Governance Lifecycle Drafting Notes

**Status:** Drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Drafting Contract Applied

The draft follows the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md), the approved-in-principle [CGP-02C.2 Planning Package](06-CGP-02C-2-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) and the [CGP-02C.2 Work Package Breakdown](06-CGP-02C-2-WORK-PACKAGE-BREAKDOWN.md). Governance Lifecycle is the only constitutional subject addressed.

## 2. Architectural Observations

- Existing documents use Draft, Review, Approved, Adopted, Applied, Transitioned and Complete with generally understandable but non-canonical meanings.
- Repetition was treated as evidence of a question, not evidence of approved doctrine.
- The draft therefore traces the uncertain vocabulary choices to LFQ-01 through LFQ-05.
- Status/effect separation is necessary to discuss lifecycle vocabulary without allowing metadata to exercise Authority.
- Historical intelligibility is stated conceptually without prescribing versioning, retention, storage or archival mechanisms.

## 3. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The draft does not assume:

- one mandatory lifecycle sequence;
- that Approval and Adoption are identical or always separate events;
- that Adoption is universally required;
- that Application or Transition has a universal procedure or effect;
- that Completion approves a document;
- that existing status metadata is correct or should be normalized;
- that a lifecycle label determines an effective date;
- that observed EOG or CGP document practice is constitutional doctrine; or
- that repository state is constitutional status.

## 4. Deferred Constitutional Questions

- approval procedure and evidence;
- adoption necessity and effect;
- effective-date relationships;
- application and transition procedure;
- supersession and retirement;
- withdrawal and rejection;
- validation and traceability doctrine;
- status-index synchronization;
- amendment classification; and
- review triggers, thresholds and proportionality.

## 5. Drafting Notes Verdict

No ambiguity was silently resolved. Five vocabulary and boundary choices are presented for Founder decision, and every excluded subject remains deferred to its authorized later package.
