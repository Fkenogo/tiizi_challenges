# CGP-02C.3 — Drafting Notes

**Subject:** Approval Governance

**Related draft:** [Approval Governance Founder Review Draft](12-CGP-02C-3-APPROVAL-GOVERNANCE-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DRAFT.md)

**Status:** Founder Review drafting notes; non-governing

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Purpose

These notes record drafting observations, unresolved Founder questions and boundaries deliberately preserved while preparing the bounded Approval Governance draft. They create no constitutional proposition and do not interpret a Founder decision.

## 2. Architectural Observations

1. The package contains one constitutional subject: Approval Governance.
2. Governance Authority is the existing Authority category exercised in constitutional approval. No “Approval Authority” is introduced.
3. The canonical wording `Founder, exercising Governance Authority` is carried forward from the CGP-02C.1 Founder Decision Record rather than created by this package.
4. Approval evidence may be a coherent evidence set. The draft does not require one document, medium, repository location or technical form.
5. Exercising approval and recording approval are constitutionally distinct. The draft does not allocate either activity to a new role or relationship.
6. The approval-reference proposal establishes a constitutional identity requirement only. Its syntax, creation method, storage and administration remain outside scope.
7. The approval-time representation boundary preserves meaning and history without reopening amendment classification.

## 3. Unresolved Founder Questions

The [Founder Review Decision Pack](12-CGP-02C-3-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DECISION-PACK.md) presents seven unresolved questions:

- AQ-01 — Constitutional Meaning of Approval;
- AQ-02 — Canonical Constitutional-Capacity Wording;
- AQ-03 — Minimum Approval Evidence;
- AQ-04 — Minimum Approval-Record Content;
- AQ-05 — Recorder and Approver Boundary;
- AQ-06 — Approval-Reference Convention; and
- AQ-07 — Approval-Time Representation and Metadata.

No answer is presumed by the draft.

## 4. Ambiguities Preserved Rather Than Resolved

- The package does not decide who records, maintains, preserves or presents approval evidence.
- The package does not prescribe an approval procedure, sequence, workflow or technical mechanism.
- The package does not decide when approval is adopted, applied or constitutionally effective.
- The package preserves the Platform Constitution’s existing amendment-record minima without defining the meaning or operation of an effective version.
- The package does not define approval-reference syntax, issuing logic, registry, repository or uniqueness mechanism.
- The package does not define validation criteria for governance instruments or approval evidence.
- The package does not determine amendment traceability, dependent-governance impact, conflict handling, supersession, retirement, withdrawal or rejection governance.

## 5. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

Drafting did not assume that:

- repeated historical practice is approved doctrine;
- a Founder title alone constitutes an exercise of Governance Authority;
- authorship, recording, custody, administration, operation or presentation confers Authority;
- document status creates constitutional effect;
- a repository event constitutes approval;
- publication, implementation or reliance proves approval;
- all historical approval records use, or should be rewritten to use, one later convention;
- the same evidence form is necessary for every approval; or
- an approved decision automatically establishes adoption, application or effectiveness.

## 6. Drafting Boundary

These notes do not extend the draft, answer the Founder questions or authorize a Founder Approval Candidate. All excluded subjects remain reserved for separately authorized CGP-02 work.
