# CGP-02C.4 — Drafting Notes

**Subject:** Adoption and Constitutional Effect

**Status:** Historical drafting evidence supporting the Founder Approved work package; not adopted or constitutionally effective

**Date:** 2026-07-23

## 1. Drafting Boundary Applied

The draft treats one constitutional subject: the adoption and constitutional effect of approved constitutional content. It includes only the approval/adoption distinction, four authorized date concepts, anti-inference boundaries and reserved Founder questions necessary to evaluate that subject.

No completed CGP-02C package was amended. Approval Governance is treated only as an upstream conceptual dependency; no approval procedure or evidence rule is redrafted.

## 2. Architectural Observations

### DN-01 — Adoption is not assumed from historical practice

The frozen Blueprint records inconsistent historical uses of approval, adoption, promotion and transition. The draft does not normalize those records. It proposes an explicit adoption meaning for prospective Founder decision.

### DN-02 — Constitutional effect is not implementation

The draft separates the constitutional condition of governing from implementation, operational use and technical enforcement. It defines no implementation step or workflow.

### DN-03 — Dates describe different constitutional events

The proposed date vocabulary distinguishes meanings without requiring four different calendar dates. The Founder may approve, narrow or reject this treatment through ACFQ-04.

### DN-04 — Combined decision remains explicit

The proposed ability to express approval and adoption in one decision is not automatic consolidation. ACE-11 requires both effects to be separately explicit and remains subject to ACFQ-05.

### DN-05 — Historical treatment is minimal

ACE-21 prevents retrospective inference only. It does not create historical-preservation, versioning, supersession, retirement or repository rules.

## 3. Founder Amendments Recorded

The Founder approved Option A with amendment for ACFQ-01 through ACFQ-07 on 2026-07-23. The amendments were applied as follows:

1. **ACFQ-01:** ACE-01 now identifies incorporation into `the authoritative constitutional governance of Tiizi`.
2. **ACFQ-02:** ACE-09 applies the same authoritative-governance wording while preserving the approval/adoption distinction.
3. **ACFQ-03:** ACE-05 now states that adopted content `authoritatively governs`.
4. **ACFQ-04:** ACE-15 now makes Application Date optional, limits it to expressly declared phased or scope-specific application and confirms that it supplements rather than replaces the other dates.
5. **ACFQ-05:** ACE-11 now confirms that one attributable decision merges neither constitutional meaning, sequence nor effect and that each act remains independently attributable.
6. **ACFQ-06:** the Constitutional Anti-Inference Principle now precedes the retained detailed propositions.
7. **ACFQ-07:** section 6 now requires separate planning and Founder authorization before any deferred subject may enter the work package.

No amendment extends beyond the Founder’s direction.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The package does not assume:

- approval automatically adopts governance;
- adoption automatically creates effect;
- constitutional effect requires technical implementation;
- application date is always required;
- relevant dates must differ;
- a status label or repository action establishes status;
- historical instruments acquire retrospective status under proposed terminology;
- any proposition has Founder approval;
- any new Platform Authority or accountability relationship is necessary.

## 5. Drafting Status

Founder constitutional review is complete, the seven amended treatments are recorded and the editorially harmonized Founder Approval Candidate has been generated. The candidate does not adopt governance and creates no constitutional effect.
