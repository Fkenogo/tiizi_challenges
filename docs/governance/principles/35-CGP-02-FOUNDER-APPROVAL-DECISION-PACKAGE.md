## 2. Purpose

This document is the bounded Founder Approval Decision Package for CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard. It places the validated 302-proposition Whole-Standard Founder Approval Candidate (D-03) before the Founder for a separate, attributable approval decision at the Founder Approval Decision Gate.

D-07 is the final pre-decision preparation deliverable of CGP-02D. It does not make the Founder approval decision. It does not approve, adopt, apply or create constitutional effect for CGP-02. It prepares the decision materials so that the Founder can make an attributable decision with full awareness of:

- what exactly is being placed before them;
- what assurance evidence supports the decision;
- what remains deferred;
- what legal/governance effects approval would NOT automatically create;
- what decision options are available;
- what explicit Founder decision must be recorded at the Decision Gate.

D-07 is decision-neutral. It does not recommend an option. It does not pre-select an outcome.

## 12. DQ-06 / DQ-07 Explicit Decision-Gate Treatment

**DQ-06** — Is adoption required for the consolidated corpus, and what procedure governs adoption of the whole instrument?

- C.4 ACFQ-01 through ACFQ-03 resolved the definition of adoption, the approval/adoption distinction, and the effect boundary.
- Whether adoption is required for the whole instrument and its procedure remain deferred.
- **Control:** No adoption may be inferred or recorded until the Founder expressly determines the required treatment.
- **Effect on D-07:** DQ-06 does not prevent the Founder from deciding approval. Approval ≠ Adoption. If the Founder approves, adoption remains a separately governed later action.

**DQ-07** — What is the exact meaning, necessity, effect and record form of application?

- C.2A LFQ-03 explicitly deferred the meaning, necessity, procedure and record form of application.
- **Control:** No application may be inferred or recorded until the Founder expressly determines the required treatment.
- **Effect on D-07:** DQ-07 does not prevent the Founder from deciding approval. Application is a separately governed later action.

**Combined treatment:** Both DQ-06 and DQ-07 are decision-boundary controls. They ensure that approval does not silently create adoption, application or constitutional effect. D-07 surfaces them for Founder awareness but does not resolve them.

## 13. Approval / Adoption / Application / Effect Boundaries

This section defines what Founder approval of CGP-02 would and would not automatically create.

### What Founder Approval WOULD create:

- A recorded attributable Founder decision that the 302-proposition CGP-02 candidate is the approved constitutional standard for CGP-02.
- A Founder Decision Record documenting the decision, option selected, conditions (if any), and preserved deferred matters.

### What Founder Approval would NOT automatically create:

| Boundary                     | Treatment                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Adoption**                 | Approval ≠ Adoption. Adoption is a separately governed action requiring its own attributable Founder decision. DQ-06 controls this boundary.                                                                                   |
| **Application**              | Approval ≠ Application. Application is a separately governed action requiring its own attributable Founder decision. DQ-07 controls this boundary.                                                                             |
| **Constitutional effect**    | Approval ≠ Constitutional effect. No constitutional effect is created by approval alone.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Effective date**           | No effective date is created by approval alone.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Implementation authority** | Approval does not authorize implementation. Any later implementation authority must follow whatever post-approval lifecycle and authority treatment is subsequently established through approved governance.                   |
| **CGP-03 commencement**      | Approval of CGP-02 does not unblock or commence CGP-03. CGP-03 remains blocked pending completion and approval of CGP-02 and further governed actions.                                                                         |
| **Stage E0 completion**      | Approval does not complete Stage E0. Stage E0 remains In Progress.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **CGP-02D completion**       | Approval alone does not complete CGP-02D. CGP-02D completion requires additional steps (programme synchronization, D-08 completion evidence).                                                                                  |
| **CGP-02 completion**        | Approval alone does not complete CGP-02. CGP-02 remains In Progress; the post-approval lifecycle treatment (if any) of adoption, application and effect has not yet been determined through the reserved governance questions. |

These boundaries are established by PC-01, PAM-01, CGP-02C.4 (ACFQ-01 through ACFQ-07), the CGP-02D Planning Package §11, and the D17 deferred questions register.

## 14. Founder Decision Options

The following decision options are available to the Founder at the Founder Approval Decision Gate:

### OPTION A — APPROVE

Approve the CGP-02 Whole-Standard Founder Approval Candidate (302 propositions, 302 unique IDs) as the approved constitutional standard for CGP-02, subject to all explicit non-effects and unresolved adoption/application/effect matters.

**Effect:** Records attributable Founder approval. Does not adopt, apply, or create constitutional effect. Preserves all 9 D17 deferred matters. Preserves adoption/application/effect boundaries.

### OPTION B — APPROVE WITH EXPRESS CONDITIONS

Approve the candidate while recording explicit bounded conditions that do not rewrite propositions unless separately authorized.

**Effect:** Records attributable Founder approval with stated conditions. Conditions must be compatible with the validated corpus (no proposition-level rewriting without separate authorization). Does not adopt, apply, or create constitutional effect. Preserves all 9 D17 deferred matters.

### OPTION C — RETURN FOR CORRECTION

Do not approve. Return specified matters for correction before a new approval decision.

**Effect:** No approval recorded. Identifies bounded correction scope. Returns to controlled correction/revalidation. Preserves candidate and evidence state pending correction.

### OPTION D — DEFER DECISION

Take no approval decision at this time. Preserve candidate and evidence state unchanged.

**Effect:** No approval recorded. No status upgrade. Candidate and evidence preserved as-is. D-07 preparation remains complete; the decision is simply not yet taken.

### OPTION E — REJECT

Reject the current whole-standard candidate.

**Effect:** Records attributable Founder rejection. Preserves history and traceability. Does not destroy evidence; the candidate and assurance record remain as historical artefacts.

## 19. Post-Decision Path by Outcome

### If OPTION A (Approve):

1. Record the attributable Founder approval decision.
2. Synchronize the Master Programme to record Founder approval of CGP-02.
3. Preserve adoption/application/effect boundaries (no automatic adoption, application, or effect).
4. Preserve all 9 D17 deferred matters unresolved.
5. Address DQ-06/DQ-07 treatment as required by separate adoption/application governance.
6. Synchronize downstream governance records (FEF-ALIGNMENT, Decision Register, etc.).
7. Determine next authorized action per CGP-02D completion logic (programme synchronization, D-08 completion evidence).
8. CGP-02D, CGP-02, Stage E0 remain In Progress; CGP-03 remains blocked.

### If OPTION B (Approve with Conditions):

1. Record the attributable Founder approval decision with stated conditions.
2. Verify conditions are compatible with the validated corpus (no proposition rewriting).
3. If conditions require separate authorization, identify the bounded scope.
4. Follow Option A steps 2–8 as applicable, incorporating conditions.

### If OPTION C (Return for Correction):

1. Record the return decision and identify bounded correction scope.
2. Return to controlled correction/revalidation.
3. Preserve candidate and evidence state pending correction.
4. Determine whether correction requires a new D-03 candidate or bounded amendment.
5. No status upgrade occurs.

### If OPTION D (Defer):

1. Record that no decision has been taken.
2. Preserve candidate and evidence state unchanged.
3. No status upgrade occurs.
4. D-07 preparation remains complete; the decision may be taken later.

### If OPTION E (Reject):

1. Record the attributable Founder rejection.
2. Preserve history and traceability.
3. Candidate and assurance record remain as historical artefacts.
4. Determine whether a new candidate or alternative path is required.
5. No status upgrade occurs.
