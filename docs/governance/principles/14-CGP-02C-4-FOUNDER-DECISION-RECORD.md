## 6. ACFQ-04 — Constitutional Date Distinctions

**Decision question:** How do approval date, adoption date, effective date and application date differ, if at all?

**Decision Pack recommendation:** Option A — Approve four distinct concepts.

**Downstream propositions affected:** ACE-12–ACE-16.

**Selected option:** Option A with amendment.

**Founder disposition:** Approved with amendment.

**Founder amendment:** Clarify ACE-15. Application Date is optional. It exists only where Governance Authority expressly declares phased or scope-specific constitutional application. It supplements and never replaces Approval Date, Adoption Date or Effective Date. Do not change ACE-12 through ACE-14 or ACE-16.

**Decision date:** 2026-07-23.

**Founder rationale:** The approved treatment is subject only to the recorded clarification.

## 7. ACFQ-05 — Approval and Adoption in One Decision

**Decision question:** May approval and adoption occur in one attributable decision while remaining conceptually distinct?

**Decision Pack recommendation:** Option A — Permit one explicit combined decision.

**Downstream proposition affected:** ACE-11.

**Selected option:** Option A with amendment.

**Founder disposition:** Approved with amendment.

**Founder amendment:** Extend ACE-11. Recording approval and adoption within one attributable Governance Authority decision does not merge constitutional meaning, constitutional sequence or constitutional effect. Each constitutional act remains independently attributable.

**Decision date:** 2026-07-23.

**Founder rationale:** The approved treatment is subject only to the recorded clarification.

## 8. ACFQ-06 — Minimum Anti-Inference Boundary

**Decision question:** What minimum boundary prevents adoption, effect or dates from being supplied by implication?

**Decision Pack recommendation:** Option A — Approve the complete anti-inference boundary.

**Downstream propositions affected:** Constitutional Anti-Inference Principle; ACE-04, ACE-08 and ACE-17–ACE-21.

**Selected option:** Option A with amendment.

**Founder disposition:** Approved with amendment.

**Founder amendment:** Introduce a constitutional principle immediately preceding the detailed anti-inference propositions: no constitutional status arises except through an explicit attributable act of Governance Authority. Records, labels, repository actions, implementation, operational behaviour and administrative activity communicate constitutional status only to the extent that such status has already been established by attributable Governance Authority. Retain the existing detailed anti-inference propositions.

**Decision date:** 2026-07-23.

**Founder rationale:** The approved treatment is subject only to the recorded principle.
