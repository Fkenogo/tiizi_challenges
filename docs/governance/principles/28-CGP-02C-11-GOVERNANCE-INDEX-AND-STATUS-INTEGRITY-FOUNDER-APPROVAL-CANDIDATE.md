## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Founder Approval Candidate contains one bounded constitutional subject: Governance Index and Status Integrity.

It does not define Validation Rules, repository implementation, operational synchronization, maintenance responsibility, technical workflow or format, storage, archival mechanisms, versioning implementation, an actual governance index, a named-instrument status correction, a Platform Authority or an accountability allocation.

## 2. Constitutional Purpose of Governance Indexes

**GSI-01.** A governance index is a documentary representation that identifies governance and communicates attributable information about its constitutional position within a declared representational scope.

**GSI-02.** The constitutional purpose of a governance index is to make the current constitutional baseline, relevant status distinctions and historically intelligible references identifiable without making the index the source of constitutional status.

**GSI-03.** Governance-index treatment is functional and technology-neutral. It does not require one document form, repository location, technical system or implementation mechanism.

## 3. Constitutional Meaning of Status Representations

**GSI-04.** A status representation is documentary information that communicates a constitutional status or status context established by an attributable constitutional source.

**GSI-05.** A status representation may communicate only the status, subject, scope, attribution, date, condition and limitation supported by its attributable source.

**GSI-06.** A status label, metadata field or documentary classification describes constitutional status only to the extent supported by attributable constitutional governance; its wording or presence does not independently establish that status.

## 4. Constitutional Status and Documentary Representation

**GSI-07.** Constitutional status and documentary representation are distinct. Constitutional status arises only through the attributable constitutional treatment that establishes it.

**GSI-08.** An accurate representation communicates constitutional status but does not create it. A missing, stale, incomplete or inconsistent representation does not erase, suspend or alter status already established by attributable constitutional governance.

**GSI-09.** Creating, recording, maintaining, presenting, publishing or administering a status representation does not confer Governance Authority or authority to establish, change or resolve constitutional status.

## 5. Integrity of the Current Constitutional Baseline

**GSI-10.** A representation claiming to identify the current constitutional baseline must distinguish the governance represented, its attributable current status, its declared constitutional scope and the source that establishes that position.

**GSI-11.** The current constitutional baseline must remain distinguishable from proposed, pending, deferred, rejected, withdrawn, retired, superseded, historical, unadopted or otherwise non-current governance to the extent supported by attributable sources.

**GSI-12.** Where available evidence does not establish a complete or consistent current position, the representation must preserve that uncertainty and must not supply a current status, precedence outcome or constitutional effect by inference.

## 6. Preservation of Historical References

**GSI-13.** A historical reference may remain unchanged where it accurately represents the governance and status context attributable to the historical time or scope represented.

**GSI-14.** A current-baseline representation may be changed to communicate a later attributable constitutional position without requiring a historically accurate reference to be rewritten as though that later position existed earlier.

**GSI-15.** Historical terminology, identifiers and status wording must remain attributable to their source context. Later terminology or representation conventions do not retrospectively change the constitutional meaning or status originally established.

## 7. Stale, Incomplete and Inconsistent Representations

**GSI-16.** A representation is stale where it claims a current representational scope but does not reflect a later attributable constitutional treatment relevant to that scope; incomplete where it omits context necessary to avoid overstating or misidentifying status; and inconsistent where it communicates a status claim incompatible with another attributable status claim concerning the same subject and scope.

**GSI-17.** A stale, incomplete or inconsistent representation is a documentary integrity defect. The defect does not itself create a constitutional change, invalidate attributable governance or authorize an inferred correction.

**GSI-18.** Where a representational inconsistency cannot be resolved from attributable sources without constitutional judgment, the inconsistency must remain explicit and subject to the existing Conflict Review and Escalation boundary. This work package does not decide the conflict or prescribe a correction process.

## 8. Indexes, Registers, Manifests and Status Representations

**GSI-19.** Indexes, registers, manifests and status representations identify documentary functions. Their names do not by themselves create distinct constitutional entities, Platform Authorities or constitutional status.

**GSI-20.** One artefact may perform more than one documentary function, and more than one artefact may represent related constitutional information. Every status claim within those representations must remain independently attributable to its constitutional source.

**GSI-21.** A document title, label, location, consolidation, duplication, sequence, prominence or repeated use does not by itself make one index, register, manifest or status representation constitutionally authoritative or give it precedence over another.

## 9. Technology-Neutral Integrity and Anti-Inference

**GSI-22.** Repositories, files, databases, interfaces, indexes, registers, manifests, links, metadata and other mechanisms may support or communicate status integrity, but they do not establish constitutional status, Authority or constitutional effect.

**GSI-23.** Recording, maintaining, correcting, synchronizing, publishing or presenting documentary status does not create an Index Authority, Status Authority or maintenance accountability and does not allocate any EOG-01 relationship, role or permission.

**GSI-24.** This proposed treatment does not designate an actual governance index, correct a named instrument or representation, validate an existing status claim, resolve an inconsistency, or retrospectively assign status where an attributable constitutional source did not establish it.

## 10. Deferred Governance

The following remain outside this work package:

- Validation Rules and validation procedure;
- selection, designation or creation of an actual governance index, register, manifest or status repository;
- repository structure, administration, access, tooling and workflow;
- operational synchronization, reconciliation, correction, publication and maintenance processes;
- synchronization timing, triggers, service levels and escalation workflow;
- maintenance responsibility and accountability allocation;
- technical formats, schemas, identifiers, hashes, APIs and metadata implementation;
- storage, archival, backup, restoration, retention and versioning mechanisms;
- correction or reclassification of a named instrument, decision, index entry or status representation;
- allocation of who records, maintains, corrects, synchronizes, publishes or presents status information;
- roles, permissions and technical controls;
- consolidation of CGP-02 constitutional content; and
- authorization of any successor work package.

## 11. Candidate Status and Non-Effects

GSI-01 through GSI-24 were approved without amendment for inclusion in this Founder Approval Candidate through the attributable Founder Decision Record dated 2026-07-25.

This candidate:

- changes no constitutional status or current baseline;
- creates, modifies or designates no actual index, register, manifest or status representation;
- corrects or validates no named instrument or status claim;
- approves, adopts, amends, supersedes, retires, withdraws and rejects no governance;
- creates no Platform Authority;
- allocates no accountability, role or permission;
- defines no Validation Rules, repository implementation, operational synchronization or maintenance procedure;
- changes no CGP-02 dependency or programme state; and
- authorizes no successor work package.
