# CGP-01 — Founder Amendment Log

**Draft transition:** Founder Review Draft 1 → Founder Review Draft 2

**Status:** Founder-review consolidation record; not an approval record

**Date:** 2026-07-21

| Refinement | Section updated | Constitutional change | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | 2. Governance Philosophy | Establishes that governance enables, protects and sustains participation while preserving constitutional integrity, truth, privacy, safety and security. | Places participation at the purpose of governance without making it superior to constitutional protection. |
| FR-02 | 3.2 | Replaces Community Before Administrative Convenience with Participation Before Administrative Convenience and prefers simpler governance where protections remain intact. | Broadens the principle from institutional context to the human act governance exists to support. |
| FR-03 | 3.6 | Requires governance to remain no more complex than necessary, requires justification for complexity and states that simplicity is not immaturity. | Strengthens minimum sufficient governance without weakening approved minima. |
| FR-04 | 4.1–4.4 | Ties governance evolution to evolving constitutional need and adds No Speculative Governance. | Prevents size, fashion or available structures from becoming constitutional justification. |
| FR-05 | 5.1 | Makes constitutional consequence the basis of proportionality and excludes size, membership, popularity and workload as independent bases. | Keeps governance burden tied to constitutional effect rather than organisational appearance. |
| FR-06 | 7.1 | Requires every constitutional structure to fulfil constitutional purpose and rejects institutional familiarity as sufficient justification. | Preserves optional zero/one/many governance bodies without encouraging unnecessary structures. |
| FR-07 | 8.1 | Defines maturity as continuing ability to fulfil constitutional purpose and excludes quantity, documentation, committees and bureaucracy as determinants. | Clarifies the concept while preserving the prohibition on maturity models. |
| FR-08 | 9.1–9.3 | Defines constitutional governance as what/why and operational governance as how; states that operational governance derives authority and cannot create constitutional meaning. | Strengthens the boundary without defining procedures or allocating Operational Authority. |
| FR-09 | 10 | Retitles the section Potential Future Governance Domains and states that mention neither approves, requires nor prioritises a domain. | Prevents a planning list from implying inevitability. |
| FR-10 | 11 | States that identifying potential domains authorizes no structure, programme, committee or standard. | Preserves independent necessity review and attributable approval for all future governance. |

No other constitutional doctrine, section architecture or scope was changed.
