# CGP-02C.2C — Review Triggers and Proportionality Drafting Notes

**Status:** Drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Drafting Contract Applied

The draft follows the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md), the approved [CGP-02C.2 Planning Package](06-CGP-02C-2-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) and the [CGP-02C.2 Work Package Breakdown](06-CGP-02C-2-WORK-PACKAGE-BREAKDOWN.md). Review Triggers and Proportionality is the only constitutional subject addressed.

## 2. Preserved Dependencies

- GVR-03 remains unchanged: review burden follows constitutional consequence rather than organisational or administrative attributes.
- GVR-04 remains unchanged: proportionality cannot remove an approved constitutional minimum.
- GVR-05 remains unchanged: review remains analytical and does not establish governance.
- FQ-03 authorized preparation of independent-review and review-depth options; it did not approve thresholds, tiers or procedure.
- CGP-02C.2A and CGP-02C.2B supply reviewed vocabulary and boundaries but remain constitutionally unapproved and are not used as normative Authority.

## 3. Architectural Observations

- Objective reviewability applies to every constitutional rule, while Independent Review concerns a particular form of constitutional separation.
- A trigger should identify why examination is constitutionally relevant without predetermining a defect or outcome.
- The approved CGP-01 consequence considerations provide qualitative judgement factors and expressly reject a scoring or threshold model.
- Periodic review requires an independent constitutional purpose; elapsed time alone does not demonstrate constitutional deficiency.
- Review proportionality is bounded below by approved constitutional minima and bounded above by minimum-sufficient governance.

## 4. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The draft does not assume:

- a mandatory review event list;
- that every trigger proves a defect;
- that every matter requires Independent Review;
- that elapsed time requires re-review;
- a review tier, score or threshold;
- reviewer identity, qualification or appointment;
- that review establishes approval or constitutional effect;
- that a low-consequence matter may omit constitutional minima;
- that observed review practice is constitutional doctrine; or
- that repository or implementation events trigger constitutional review.

## 5. Deferred Constitutional Questions

- trigger thresholds and evidential sufficiency;
- review procedure and evidence package;
- independent-review thresholds and reviewer arrangements;
- periodic-review intervals and schedules;
- class-specific review treatment;
- approval procedure and evidence;
- validation and traceability doctrine;
- lifecycle or constitutional-effect consequences; and
- implementation or repository workflow.

## 6. Drafting Notes Verdict

No observed review practice was converted into doctrine. Five review choices are presented for Founder decision, GVR-03 through GVR-05 remain preserved, and every excluded subject remains deferred.
