## 3. Bounded Constitutional Content

The Founder Review Draft contains 24 propositions, ATR-01 through ATR-24, covering only:

- the constitutional purpose and meaning of an amendment trace;
- minimum amendment-trace information;
- treatment of affected, unaffected, pending, deferred, rejected and withdrawn matters;
- distinction from approval, adoption, constitutional effect and status records;
- amendment-trace and approval-reference separation;
- trace-specific anti-inference boundaries; and
- minimum historical intelligibility without preservation mechanisms.

Nine questions, ATQ-01 through ATQ-09, remain pending Founder decision.
