# CGP-02 — Discovery and Source Analysis

**Work package:** Discovery & Source Analysis

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Status:** Discovery evidence for review; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## 1. Executive Summary

The [Tiizi Version 2 Master Programme](../../programme/TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) Version 1.1 confirms Stage E0 as Ready, CGP-02 as the active phase, Stage D as Complete and CGP-02 as the next authorized work. This report performs discovery only. It does not draft CGP-02, establish an amendment procedure, resolve a conflict or create constitutional doctrine.

Repository-wide term search identified 226 potentially relevant Markdown artefacts across the approved governance corpus, programme control, founder decisions, platform discovery and Knowledge corpus. Detailed review separated operative governance and issued approval evidence from drafts, options papers, readiness reviews, implementation-oriented Knowledge material and historical drafting artefacts.

The current corpus already supplies a substantial distributed amendment foundation:

- constitutional statements trace to approved founder decisions;
- Governance Authority approves constitutional rules, standards, Authority boundaries, governed exceptions and amendments;
- Founder approval is the present source of Governance Authority, with later explicit delegation contemplated but not defined;
- constitutional amendments must be explicit and attributable;
- amendment records must identify at least the decision, rationale, affected provisions or documents, approving authority, effective version and amendment history;
- lower-order governance may refine but may not contradict higher-order governance;
- conflicts favor higher-precedence governance and unresolved conflicts escalate to Governance Authority;
- operational urgency, custom, interface access and repeated practice cannot silently amend governance;
- material changes and Authority actions must remain reviewable, attributable and historically intelligible;
- approval is evidenced through issued founder decisions or approval records, decision-register entries, validation, traceability and transition evidence; and
- historical drafts are generally preserved and explicitly prevented from extending approved governance.

These rules are not yet one complete standard. The corpus lacks a canonical governance-document lifecycle, a single amendment classification model, a defined review threshold, a consistent effective-date rule, an established approval-reference convention, a governed retirement or withdrawal treatment, a complete supersession register rule and an explicit responsibility for recording approvals. The corpus also contains stale or internally inconsistent status metadata that should be treated as discovery findings rather than silently corrected here.

**Discovery verdict:** The evidence base is sufficient to begin a later CGP-02 drafting phase after this report is reviewed and accepted. Drafting must consolidate existing rules without treating recurring practice as approved doctrine and must surface unresolved choices for attributable Founder review.

## 5. Governance Lifecycle Register

No approved instrument currently establishes one canonical governance-document lifecycle. The following states and transitions are present in practice.

| Lifecycle concept | Existing treatment | Principal sources | Status of rule | Discovery assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Evidence-gathering work that creates no governance. | Master Programme; EOG phase records; this work package | Implicit and programme-controlled | Recurrent but not constitutionally defined. |
| Readiness / authorization | Readiness finding precedes drafting; authorization may be separately approved. | EOG-05 Phase 2; EOG-03 and EOG-04 preparatory phases | Explicit in specific programmes | Useful recurring pattern, not universal. |
| Draft 1 | First complete review candidate; not approved governance. | EOG-03, EOG-04 and EOG-05 histories | Explicit in artefacts | Status meaning consistent in examples. |
| Independent review | Challenge or validation of Draft 1 before refinement. | EOG-03, EOG-04 and EOG-05 review records | Explicit in specific programmes | Not stated as universally mandatory. |
| Draft 2 / final review candidate | Refined source version submitted for Founder approval. | EOG-03, EOG-04, EOG-05, CGP-01 | Explicit in artefacts | Consistent practice, not canonical rule. |
| Founder review | Founder considers recommendations or final draft; review alone does not approve. | Founder decision packs and checklists | Explicit in specific programmes | Distinction from approval is generally preserved. |
| Approved | Founder or Founder exercising Governance Authority gives attributable approval within scope. | Approval records; decision register | Explicit | Meaning generally clear; evidence convention varies. |
| Adopted | Approved text is promoted as current constitutional baseline. | CGP-01 Adoption Record | Explicit for CGP-01 | Not consistently separated from approval elsewhere. |
| Applied amendment | Approved amendment has been reconciled into the governed instrument or register. | EOG-05 CG-08 Amendment | Explicit for one amendment | No general application rule. |
| Transitioned | Current status, dependencies, deferrals and historical artefacts are reconciled. | EOG transition reports | Implicit recurring practice | Not universally required. |
| Complete / closed | Phase scope and its transition evidence are complete; deferred work remains deferred. | EOG-01, EOG-02 and EOG-05 completion records | Explicit in phase records | No canonical distinction between Approved and Complete. |
| Deferred | Matter is intentionally excluded until later governance. | All foundational standards and registers | Explicit | Consistently used but no general reactivation rule. |
| Blocked | Master Programme status for unavailable prerequisite. | Master Programme | Explicit programme status | No governance-document lifecycle meaning. |
| Superseded | A selected option or status record is replaced while retained historically. | EOG-05 Architecture Approval; programme status records | Explicit in examples | No universal supersession control or index. |
| Retired | Document or rule ceases current force without a replacement. | No approved general rule found | Missing | Lifecycle gap. |
| Withdrawn / rejected | Proposal or decision is withdrawn or rejected while retaining history. | Option records imply rejection; no general status rule | Partial | Lifecycle gap. |
| Amended | Current approved instrument changes through attributable approval and history. | Constitution; Authority Model; CG-08 amendment | Explicit principle | No canonical end-to-end lifecycle. |

### 6.2 Who Records Approval

No approved document assigns a canonical Recorder, Custodian, Administrator or governance-secretariat responsibility for approval records. Records are created in the repository, but repository authorship, file possession and technical access do not constitute constitutional recording Authority.

**Assessment:** Undefined. CGP-02 will need to identify the governance question without silently allocating a relationship.

### 6.3 What Constitutes Approval

Existing evidence supports the following minimum factual pattern without yet defining a procedure:

- an explicit Founder or Governance Authority decision;
- an identifiable document, decision or scope approved;
- an approval status;
- an approval date or an explicitly unresolved date placeholder;
- the approving constitutional capacity;
- preservation of attached conditions and explicit non-effects; and
- separation of approved matters from deferred, rejected or unapproved matters.

Recommendation, completion of review, a draft status, interface action, repository possession, repeated practice and implementation do not constitute approval.

### 8.3 Implicit Assumptions

- Founder approval presently supplies Governance Authority for constitutional amendment.
- Repository Markdown is the durable governance record, although no constitutional Custodian or recorder is allocated.
- Draft 2 is normally the approval candidate, but no approved rule requires two drafts.
- Independent review is expected for material constitutional instruments, but no universal threshold defines when it is mandatory.
- Approval normally preserves body text and changes status metadata, but no general rule defines permissible approval-time changes.
- Validation and traceability are expected before closure, but no canonical minimum evidence set exists.
- Decision-register and current-index updates are expected after approval, but no timing or responsible relationship is assigned.

## 10. Recommendations for Drafting Phase

These recommendations define drafting coverage only. They do not prescribe an amendment procedure or resolve any gap.

1. Treat the Platform Constitution, Platform Authority Model and CGP-01 as the primary normative source hierarchy for CGP-02.
2. Use approval and amendment records as evidence of established practice, while distinguishing repeated practice from approved platform-wide doctrine.
3. Define the scope boundary between constitutional amendment, governance review, status maintenance, clerical housekeeping and programme change without assuming their final treatment.
4. Present the lifecycle-state ambiguity for explicit Founder determination rather than selecting a lifecycle from precedent.
5. Present approval Authority, future delegation and recording responsibility as separate questions.
6. Consolidate the minimum amendment-content rules already approved before identifying any additional proposed requirement.
7. Preserve the existing precedence hierarchy and prohibition on silent or operational amendment.
8. Address historical drafts, supersession, retirement, withdrawal and current-index synchronization as distinct traceability concerns.
9. Separate approval date, effective date, adoption date and version questions for Founder review.
10. Define objective review and validation questions without converting current repository tooling into constitutional doctrine.
11. Require later drafting to identify how dependent documents, decision registers, indexes and the Master Programme are affected without updating them during discovery.
12. Carry all eight conflicts and eighteen gaps into the drafting blueprint or Founder decision material with no silent resolution.
