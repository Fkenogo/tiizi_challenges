# CGP-01 — Traceability Report

**Status:** Approved-baseline traceability

**Date:** 2026-07-21

## 1. Purpose

This report traces the [approved CGP-01 Constitutional Governance Principles](02-CGP-01-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLES.md), whose constitutional doctrine derives unchanged from [Founder Review Draft 2](02-CGP-01-CONSTITUTIONAL-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT-2.md), to approved constitutional sources and the [Founder Amendment Log](02-CGP-01-FOUNDER-AMENDMENT-LOG.md). Traceability identifies the source of each doctrine; approval is recorded separately in the [Constitutional Adoption Record](02-CGP-01-CONSTITUTIONAL-ADOPTION-RECORD.md).

## 4. Decision Restraint

CGP-01 does not convert any pending GRP, CHL, ACT, KNW, MOT, RSK, privacy, security, lifecycle, role, permission or allocation decision into approved governance.

It references C-GRP-08 only because its constitutional inventory treatment is already approved. It creates no new entity or EOG-01 relationship.

## 5. Traceability Verdict

Every substantive CGP-01 doctrine has an identifiable source in approved governance. The approved baseline consolidates that philosophy without amending its sources.
