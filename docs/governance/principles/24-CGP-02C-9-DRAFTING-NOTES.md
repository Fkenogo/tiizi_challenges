**Work package:** CGP-02C.9 — Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules

**Status:** Founder Review drafting evidence; not constitutional governance

## 1. Purpose

These notes record architectural observations, deferred matters and assumptions deliberately avoided while preparing the CGP-02C.9 Founder Review package.

They do not supplement the propositions, answer a Founder question or create constitutional meaning.

## 3. Architectural Observations

### 3.1 One Subject, Three Treatments

The Blueprint groups retirement, withdrawal and rejection in one deliverable but names them separately. The draft therefore treats them as one bounded constitutional subject containing three non-interchangeable treatments.

### 3.2 Prior Constitutional Position

The proposed distinctions depend on the position of the identified governance before the act:

- retirement concerns governance holding a current governing position;
- withdrawal concerns proposed or pending governance removed from further consideration without a merits decision; and
- rejection concerns a negative decision on proposed or pending governance.

This architecture is proposed only and remains subject to RWQ-01 through RWQ-04.

### 3.3 Replacement Remains Separate

CGP-02C.8 defines supersession through an attributable replacement relationship and expressly defers the three treatments. The draft does not restate or modify Supersession Rules; it applies the external boundary needed to prevent replacement by implication.

### 3.4 Historical Intelligibility Without Historical Preservation Doctrine

RWR-12 and RWR-23 preserve the existing historical-intelligibility principle. They do not define Historical Preservation, retention, archival, deletion, restoration, storage, versioning or availability.

### 3.5 Evidence Without Allocation

RWR-17 through RWR-20 define proposed evidence categories, not a document format or operational procedure. The package does not determine who creates, records, maintains, publishes or validates the evidence.

## 5. Assumptions Deliberately Avoided

The draft does not assume:

- that any existing governance is retired, withdrawn or rejected;
- that retirement necessarily follows replacement, age, non-use or implementation change;
- that withdrawal may be inferred from silence, delay or abandonment;
- that rejection follows merely from absence of approval;
- that a proposer, author, recorder, custodian or repository administrator holds Governance Authority;
- that one treatment automatically changes dependent governance;
- that one decision cannot record multiple separately attributable treatments;
- that Historical Preservation requires any specific mechanism;
- that an index or status label creates constitutional truth;
- that technical deletion is constitutionally permitted or required;
- that validation or repository workflow belongs in this package; or
- that Blueprint sequence authorizes a successor package.
