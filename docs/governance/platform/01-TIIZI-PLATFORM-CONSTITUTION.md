# Tiizi Platform Constitution

## Mission

Tiizi enables people to pursue meaningful goals together through governed group challenges, measurable participation, mutual encouragement and shared progress.

## Purpose

This Constitution establishes the durable identity and governing boundaries of Tiizi Version 2. It directs all platform, domain, product, knowledge, data and administrative standards.

## Platform Identity

> Tiizi is a group-first challenge and motivation platform that helps people pursue meaningful goals together through measurable activities, mutual encouragement and shared progress.

This description is authoritative.

## Platform Scope

Tiizi governs:

- groups as the primary context for challenges;
- group participation and shared accountability;
- measurable activities connected to governed challenge purposes;
- Accepted Activity Events, Policy-governed Evidence Eligibility and authoritative derived Progress;
- governed knowledge used to define selectable activities;
- supporting profiles, preferences, analytics and settings that enable group participation;
- motivation through meaningful participation, encouragement and shared progress;
- administration under least privilege and trusted authority.

Fitness and Wellness are the Version 2 launch domains.

## Platform Philosophy

Tiizi treats trust, clarity and shared accountability as platform concerns. A feature is valid only when it preserves the platform identity, uses governed concepts, states truthfully what occurred and respects the correct authority for every fact.

## Group First Principle

Group challenges are the primary product model for Version 2. Personal capabilities support group participation; they do not constitute an individual-challenge mode.

Platform architecture and governance shall prioritize group participation, group accountability and shared progress.

Individual challenges are excluded from Version 2 launch. They may be considered only as a possible future governed capability through a separate decision process. Their possible future existence creates no current requirement.

## Shared Progress Principle

Progress represents participation toward an approved Challenge purpose. Shared Progress must be derived consistently from Accepted Activity Events that applicable Policy makes eligible as evidence for the declared calculation. Presentation must not redefine the underlying truth.

## Measurable Activity Principle

Tiizi governs one canonical platform activity-event contract. Fitness and Wellness may extend that contract with domain-specific information.

The Participant is the Attributable originator of Submission Intent, and Participant Authority establishes that expression. Acceptance Authority establishes Accepted Activity Events. Calculation Authority establishes Progress, Completion, Ranking and Projections from evidence made eligible under applicable Policy.

## Knowledge Governance Principle

The platform shall use one governed runtime catalogue authority. Knowledge made available for participation must have an identified authority and approved meaning.

Local assets may serve only an explicitly governed purpose. They shall not silently override or act as equal production authorities.

## Truth Before Convenience Principle

Tiizi shall describe identity, privacy, participation, progress, completion, ranking, verification and administrative action according to their actual governed meaning.

A visible control shall not promise an effect that the platform cannot enforce.

## Single Source of Truth Principle

Every authoritative concept shall have one declared source of truth. Every derived value shall have one declared calculation authority.

Copies, summaries and projections may improve access or presentation. They do not acquire independent authority.

## Least Privilege Principle

Access and action shall be limited to what an actor requires for an approved purpose.

Visibility is not authorization. Sensitive access is deny-by-default outside its approved visibility class. High-impact state changes belong to trusted backend authority. Sensitive actions require immutable audit records.

## Future Extensibility Principle

The platform foundation shall remain neutral enough to admit future governed domains without weakening the integrity of Fitness, Wellness or group challenges.

An unimplemented domain is not an active capability. Future extensibility shall not create present launch commitments, acceptance criteria or hidden runtime assumptions.

## Out of Scope

This Constitution does not authorize:

- individual challenges in Version 2 launch;
- any domain other than Fitness and Wellness as a launch domain;
- client-authored authoritative progress, completion, rankings, projections or derived analytics;
- local assets as silent or equal production catalogue authorities;
- access based solely on interface visibility;
- high-impact state changes without trusted authority;
- any future capability merely because the foundation permits later extension.

The Constitution does not settle pending lifecycle, challenge-type, correction, ranking, streak, social, notification, reward, operational or retention decisions.

## Constitution Governance

1. Constitutional statements shall trace to an approved founder decision.
2. New standards shall conform to this Constitution.
3. A conflict shall be resolved in favor of this Constitution unless an approved amendment supersedes it.
4. An amendment shall identify the decision, rationale, affected documents and effective version.
5. Terms defined by the Platform Domain and Terminology Standard shall be used consistently.
6. Every constitutional rule shall be capable of objective review.
