# Platform Domain and Terminology Standard

## Purpose

This standard assigns one canonical meaning to foundational Tiizi terms. A domain standard may add detail but shall not contradict these definitions.

## Canonical definitions

| Term | Definition | Purpose | Relationship to other concepts | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platform | The shared governed foundation through which Tiizi capabilities operate. | Provide stable identity, rules and authority across domains. | Contains domains and capabilities. | A single feature or catalogue. |
| Domain | A governed area of knowledge or platform responsibility with a defined boundary. | Organize specialized meaning without fragmenting the platform. | Contains capabilities and may define domain extensions. | An interface label or arbitrary content category. |
| Capability | A stable business ability the platform governs. | Describe what Tiizi can do independently of presentation. | Capabilities may be expressed through features. | A particular interface or implementation. |
| Feature | A specific product expression of one or more capabilities. | Deliver governed capability to an actor. | Must conform to platform and domain standards. | An independent source of platform policy. |
| Governed subject | An entity, relationship or information object governed as a distinct subject under Tiizi standards. | Identify what a domain or later register governs. | May also be an Information subject or have an Accountable steward. | Automatically the actor with Authority to establish truth. |
| Information subject | The Person, entity or governed matter to which information relates. | Distinguish what information concerns from who governs or handles it. | May be represented by information held under another authority. | The Accountable steward, Custodian or authority merely by being the subject. |
| Attributable originator | The identifiable source of an expression or submitted information. | Preserve attribution without overstating authority. | A Participant is the Attributable originator of Submission Intent. | Automatically the authority over accepted or derived truth. |
| Authority to establish truth | The approved right and responsibility to establish a particular authoritative fact, state, calculation, Knowledge item or decision for a declared purpose. | Identify who may establish governed truth. | Is assigned through a canonical Platform Authority. | Ownership, access, visibility, origin or interface capability. |
| Accountable steward | The declared party accountable for stewardship of a Governed subject within approved boundaries. | Preserve accountability distinct from truth-establishment and custody. | May coordinate with authorities, Custodians, Administrators and Operators. | Automatically an unrestricted owner or authority. |
| Custodian | A party entrusted to preserve or handle information within an approved purpose. | Protect continuity and integrity without transferring authority. | Acts within assigned custody and under the relevant authority. | The author, Information subject or Authority to establish truth merely through possession. |
| Administrator | An actor explicitly assigned Administrative Authority for a declared purpose and scope. | Perform approved administrative action under least privilege and accountability. | May act on a Governed subject only within assigned authority. | A universal role, Accountable steward or unrestricted authority. |
| Operator | An actor explicitly assigned Operational Authority for a declared purpose and scope. | Support reliable and controlled Platform Operation. | Acts within approved operational governance. | Governance Authority or permanent exception authority. |
| Presenter | An actor or authority that communicates information without acquiring authority over the underlying truth. | Preserve truthful communication and source authority. | Uses Presentation Authority for an approved audience and purpose. | A Calculation Authority or source of truth merely through display. |
| Downstream user | An approved consumer of information for a declared purpose. | Govern reuse without transferring authority or meaning. | Remains constrained by source authority, privacy and Policy. | An owner, steward or authority merely through use. |
| Person | The human subject who may hold governed identity and participation relationships. | Identify the human Information subject without conflating the person with a platform representation or role. | A Person may be recognized through Identity, represented by a Profile, and related to Groups and Challenges. | A Profile, Member, Participant or authority. |
| Identity | The governed identity context through which a Person is recognized and attributable. | Support reliable attribution, privacy and participation. | A Profile is the governed representation of Identity. | The Person, a Profile presentation or permission. |
| Group | The primary social and accountability context for Version 2 challenges. | Enable people to pursue goals together. | Has members and provides context for group challenges. | An individual-challenge wrapper or informal audience label. |
| Member | A person with a governed relationship to a group. | Establish participation context and group accountability. | A member may become a participant in a challenge. | Automatically a participant in every challenge. |
| Challenge | A governed shared undertaking through which participants pursue a defined goal using measurable activities. | Structure group motivation, contribution and progress. | Belongs to a group in Version 2 and uses knowledge, policy and activity events. | A standalone activity, catalogue item or individual launch mode. |
| Challenge Type | A governed classification of challenge behavior. | Declare the behavior family under which a challenge operates. | Constrains policy, progress, completion and any ranking. | A free-form label or an authorization to invent behavior. |
| Activity | A governed behavior or action that may be performed and measured. | Connect Knowledge to meaningful participation. | Defined by Knowledge and referenced by Submission Intent and Accepted Activity Events. | An occurrence record. |
| Activity Event | The canonical platform concept governing the representation of a measurable Activity occurrence. | Preserve the boundary between participant expression, trusted acceptance and downstream calculation. | Submission Intent is the pre-acceptance expression; only an Accepted Activity Event is authoritative Activity Information. | An Activity definition, a calculated total or a synonym for Submission Intent. |
| Submission Intent | A Participant's attributable expression that an Activity occurred in a governed context. | Preserve participant expression before trusted acceptance. | The Participant is the Attributable originator and Participant Authority establishes the expression for Acceptance Authority to evaluate. | An Accepted Activity Event, eligible evidence or Derived Truth. |
| Accepted Activity Event | The authoritative accepted record, established by Acceptance Authority, that a measurable Activity occurred in a governed context. | Provide authoritative Activity Information while preserving attribution and accepted meaning. | May be considered for Evidence Eligibility under applicable Policy and used by Calculation Authority only when eligible. | Submission Intent, automatic evidence for every calculation or Derived Truth. |
| Evidence Eligibility | The Policy-governed determination that an Accepted Activity Event may be used for a declared calculation. | Prevent acceptance from being treated as universal permission for every derived use. | Connects Accepted Activity Events to Calculation Authority for a specific governed purpose. | Acceptance itself, Verification or a calculation result. |
| Metric | The governed dimension by which an activity or goal is measured. | State what a value means. | A metric uses an approved unit and may support progress calculation. | A raw number without meaning. |
| Unit | The governed expression used to quantify a metric. | Make measurements interpretable and comparable where permitted. | Belongs to a metric contract. | A display suffix chosen independently of the metric. |
| Goal | A stated outcome toward which participation is directed. | Give activity and progress a meaningful purpose. | A challenge applies policy and measurement to a goal. | Automatically a completion rule. |
| Progress | The authoritative derived account of advancement toward a Goal. | Communicate how eligible accepted participation relates to the Goal. | Established by Calculation Authority from eligible evidence under Policy. | A participant-authored total or visual estimate. |
| Completion | An authoritative determination that approved Completion conditions have been met. | Establish a governed outcome. | Established by Calculation Authority from eligible evidence, Progress and Policy. | A visual state or participant declaration. |
| Leaderboard | An authoritative or clearly identified presentation of ranked participant results. | Communicate ordered competitive results where ranking is governed. | Presents ranking under an approved policy. | A reward balance or independent calculation authority. |
| Ranking | The governed ordering of eligible results according to an approved rule. | Support fair comparison where permitted. | May be presented by a leaderboard. | Recognition, reward or arbitrary score. |
| Streak | A governed pattern of qualifying participation across defined time periods. | Represent continuity when an approved rule exists. | Depends on activity events, time rules and policy. | Any sequence of logs or a general synonym for motivation. |
| Knowledge | Governed meaning used by Tiizi to define concepts, activities, relationships and constraints. | Ensure consistent interpretation. | Organized into Knowledge Assets and made available through the runtime catalogue. | Participant-generated evidence or an unreviewed label. |
| Knowledge Asset | A governed unit of Knowledge with distinct identity, canonical meaning, declared authority and defined relationships. | Preserve reusable, reviewable Knowledge. | May be represented for runtime use through a subordinate Runtime Projection. | Submission Intent, an Accepted Activity Event, a Runtime Projection or a launched Challenge. |
| Runtime Catalogue | The single governed authority that makes approved knowledge available for runtime use. | Provide one authoritative selection source. | Exposes governed knowledge to platform capabilities. | A collection of equal local and production sources. |
| Runtime Projection | A governed, purpose-specific representation of a Knowledge Asset made available through the Runtime Catalogue authority. | Communicate only the approved Knowledge needed for a declared runtime purpose. | Remains attributable and subordinate to its Knowledge Asset and the Runtime Catalogue authority. | A Knowledge Asset classification, independent Knowledge authority or second catalogue source. |
| Policy | A governed rule set that constrains allowed behavior and authoritative calculation. | Make decisions consistent and reviewable. | Applies to challenges, activities, privacy or administration. | Optional interface guidance. |
| Participant | A person with a governed relationship to a challenge. | Attribute submission intent and participation. | May also be a group member and profile owner. | The authority over accepted events or derived totals. |
| Motivation | The platform capability of supporting sustained, meaningful participation. | Help people pursue goals together. | May use progress, encouragement and recognition. | Necessarily a points or reward system. |
| Recognition | A truthful acknowledgement of governed participation or outcome. | Reinforce meaningful contribution. | Must follow accepted evidence and approved rules. | Ranking value, payment or unverified praise presented as fact. |
| Reward | A governed benefit or value granted under an explicitly approved reward policy. | Distinguish granted value from recognition and ranking. | May exist only when separately governed. | Progress, ranking, recognition or an implicit launch capability. |
| Notification | A governed communication about an event, state or required attention. | Inform an appropriate recipient truthfully. | May refer to another entity or capability. | Proof that an event occurred merely because a message was displayed. |
| Feed | An ordered presentation of governed group-relevant activity or communication. | Support awareness and mutual encouragement. | May present events, progress or social content under access policy. | An independent source of activity or progress truth. |
| Profile | The governed representation of Identity and participation-supporting information. | Support identity, privacy and group participation. | Represents the governed Identity context of a Person and contains information assigned to approved visibility classes. | The Person, Identity authority, a public record by default or an individual-challenge mode. |
| Interest | A governed or personal expression of a subject relevant to the person. | Support relevant participation and preference. | May relate to domains, goals or knowledge. | Automatically a Knowledge Asset or accepted activity event. |
| Wellness | A Version 2 launch domain governing approved wellbeing knowledge and activities. | Support meaningful wellbeing participation. | May include hydration, recovery, mindfulness, sleep, mobility, healthy routines and general wellbeing. | A requirement to make each governed area a top-level domain. |
| Fitness | A Version 2 launch domain governing approved physical-activity knowledge and activities. | Support meaningful fitness participation. | Extends shared platform contracts with fitness-specific meaning. | The permanent boundary of Tiizi. |
| Administration | The governed exercise of platform authority for approved operational purposes. | Protect integrity, safety and accountable decision-making. | Uses canonical roles, least privilege, trusted authority and audit records. | Interface access or unrestricted control. |
| Platform Operation | A governed activity required to keep the platform reliable, controlled and reviewable. | Sustain platform integrity. | Operates under administration, authority and later operational standards. | Product behavior or an informal engineering action. |

## Usage rules

1. Canonical terms shall not be replaced by narrower implementation language in governing documents.
2. A feature name may differ for clarity but shall map to one canonical concept.
3. A term shall not imply an unapproved launch capability.
4. Domain extensions shall preserve the shared definition.
5. Pending lifecycle and behavior details shall be defined only by later approved standards.
