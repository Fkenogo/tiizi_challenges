# Tiizi Platform Capability Map

## Purpose

This map expresses Tiizi as business capabilities. It does not prescribe features, interfaces, systems or implementation.

## 1. Platform Governance

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Platform identity governance | Preserve the approved group-first identity and purpose. |
| Scope governance | Distinguish launch commitments from future possibilities. |
| Terminology governance | Maintain one stable meaning for foundational concepts. |
| Authority governance | Assign one authority to each authoritative or derived truth. |
| Decision traceability | Connect governing statements to approved decisions. |

## 2. Identity and Privacy

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Participant identity | Represent a person sufficiently for safe group participation. |
| Profile governance | Govern participation-supporting personal information. |
| Visibility classification | Classify information as Public, Authenticated-discoverable, Shared-group, Private or Privileged-operational. |
| Consent accountability | Record the identity, source, time and applicable version of consent. |
| Privacy enforcement | Ensure controls correspond to enforceable visibility and authorization. |

Account lifecycle, retention and profile-media details remain deferred.

## 3. Groups

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Group context | Provide the primary context for Version 2 challenges. |
| Group participation | Establish governed relationships between people and groups. |
| Group accountability | Support shared responsibility for challenge participation and progress. |
| Group stewardship | Provide Accountable stewardship subject to later role and lifecycle standards; Authority to establish truth remains separately declared. |

Role vocabulary, transfer, departure, invitation, removal and archival behavior remain deferred.

## 4. Challenges

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Group challenge definition | Define a measurable shared undertaking within a group. |
| Challenge participation | Establish a governed relationship between a participant and a group challenge. |
| Goal and policy application | Connect a challenge to a meaningful goal and approved rules. |
| Progress authority | Derive progress from accepted activity events. |
| Completion authority | Determine completion through trusted authority. |
| Ranking authority | Derive any ranking through trusted authority and approved policy. |

Specific challenge types and lifecycle behavior remain deferred. Individual challenges are excluded from Version 2 launch.

## 5. Activity and Measurement

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Submission intent | Allow a participant to express that an activity occurred. |
| Activity acceptance | Establish an authoritative accepted activity event through trusted authority. |
| Canonical event governance | Maintain one shared activity-event contract with governed domain extensions. |
| Measurement governance | Attach meaningful metrics and units to measurable activity. |
| Derived truth | Calculate progress, completion, ranking and projections under one authority. |
| Provenance and auditability | Preserve the origin and authority of important facts and actions. |

Correction, verification and retention lifecycles remain deferred.

## 6. Knowledge

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Knowledge governance | Maintain approved meaning for platform and domain concepts. |
| Knowledge Asset governance | Give governed knowledge an identity and authority. |
| Runtime catalogue authority | Make approved runtime knowledge available from one governed authority. |
| Fitness knowledge | Govern Fitness definitions as a launch domain. |
| Wellness knowledge | Govern Wellness definitions as a launch domain. |
| Domain extension | Admit future domains only through explicit governance. |

Knowledge versioning, deprecation and snapshot details remain deferred.

## 7. Motivation and Shared Experience

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Meaningful participation | Connect participation to an understandable group purpose. |
| Shared progress | Communicate one authoritative account of advancement. |
| Mutual encouragement | Support truthful encouragement among people pursuing goals together. |
| Recognition integrity | Ensure recognition follows accepted evidence and approved meaning. |

The reward model, social lifecycle, feed governance and notification behavior remain deferred.

## 8. Administration and Platform Control

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Role governance | Maintain one canonical platform role model. |
| Least-privilege administration | Limit authority to approved operational need. |
| Trusted state change | Place high-impact state change under trusted backend authority. |
| Sensitive-action audit | Record sensitive actions immutably. |
| Permission-risk governance | Permit direct action only through an approved permission and risk matrix. |

Detailed roles, security controls and operational lifecycles remain deferred.

## 9. Information and Analytics

| Capability | Constitutional mandate |
|---|---|
| Authoritative information | Distinguish accepted facts from derived truth and presentation. |
| Projection | Communicate authoritative truth without becoming a competing authority. |
| Participation-supporting analytics | Support group participation without creating an individual-challenge mode. |
| Truthful reporting | Label information according to its governed authority and meaning. |

Metric definitions, freshness and operational reporting remain deferred.

## Capability boundary

A capability belongs in Version 2 launch only when the Version 2 Launch Scope includes it. Recognition in this map does not settle pending behavior or create a feature commitment.
