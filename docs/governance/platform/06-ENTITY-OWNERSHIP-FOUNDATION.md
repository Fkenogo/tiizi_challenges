# Entity Ownership Foundation

## Purpose

This document establishes constitutional rules for entity and information ownership. It does not create the full entity register, lifecycle standards or implementation model.

## Meaning of ownership

Ownership means accountable stewardship of a Governed subject within declared constitutional boundaries. It is distinct from the Authority to establish truth, the Information subject, Attributable originator, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter and Downstream user. Ownership is not mere possession, visibility or the ability to display or submit information.

## Constitutional rules

### 1. One entity, one Accountable steward

Every Governed subject shall have one declared Accountable steward. Multiple actors may interact with it as an Information subject, Attributable originator, authority, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter or Downstream user, but those relationships shall remain distinct and explicit.

### 2. One derived value, one calculation authority

Every authoritative derived value shall have one declared calculation authority. Alternative presentations may not establish competing truth.

### 3. Submission intent differs from accepted fact

A Participant is the Attributable originator of Submission Intent, and Participant Authority establishes that expression. Submission Intent is a claim for consideration; it is not an Accepted Activity Event.

Acceptance Authority establishes the Accepted Activity Event as authoritative Activity Information.

### 4. Raw evidence differs from calculated truth

An Accepted Activity Event is authoritative Activity Information. Applicable Policy establishes Evidence Eligibility for a declared calculation. Progress, Completion, Ranking and authoritative analytics are Derived Truth established by Calculation Authority from eligible evidence.

Evidence and derived truth shall remain distinguishable in meaning, authority and review.

### 5. Calculation Authority establishes Challenge truth

Calculation Authority establishes authoritative Progress, Completion, Ranking and Projections under approved Policy.

The client shall not author authoritative challenge totals, participant aggregates, completion status, leaderboard rank, recap results, verification status or derived analytics.

### 6. Projection never becomes authority

A projection is a representation of authoritative information for access, communication or analysis. It remains subordinate to its declared source and calculation authority.

A projection shall not silently become a new source of truth.

### 7. Knowledge differs from runtime participation

Knowledge defines approved meaning. An activity event records accepted participation. A challenge applies governed policy to knowledge and participation.

Knowledge, policy, challenge and activity event are distinct entities even when they reference one another.

### 8. One governed runtime catalogue authority

Runtime knowledge shall be supplied by one governed catalogue authority.

Local assets may act only as versioned development fixtures, test fixtures, controlled seed or bootstrap inputs, or explicitly approved packaged fallback assets. Their purpose shall be declared. They shall not silently override or act as equal production authorities.

### 9. One lifecycle per entity

Every entity shall have one governed lifecycle and one canonical state vocabulary. Presentation states and compatibility labels shall not create parallel lifecycles.

The actual lifecycle states and transitions are deferred to later founder decisions.

### 10. Authority follows purpose, not visibility

Visibility does not grant ownership or action authority. Interface access is not authorization.

Authority shall be explicitly assigned under least privilege.

### 11. Sensitive change requires trusted authority

High-impact state changes belong to trusted backend authority. Direct action outside trusted authority requires explicit approval in a documented permission and risk matrix.

### 12. Sensitive action requires durable accountability

A sensitive action shall identify its actor, authority, purpose and time in an immutable audit record.

The retention and correction policy for records is deferred.

### 13. Privacy classification constrains ownership access

Entity ownership does not grant unrestricted access to personal information. Access remains constrained by the approved visibility classes and deny-by-default principle.

### 14. Historical truth shall not be silently replaced

Material accepted facts and high-impact actions shall remain attributable. Later change shall not conceal the prior accepted state.

This rule does not settle pending correction, deletion, retention or archival behavior.

## Foundational stewardship and authority statements

| Concept | Constitutional allocation |
|---|---|
| Profile information | The Person is the Information subject. Identity Authority establishes authoritative identity state; visibility and operational access follow approved classes. Accountable stewardship remains deferred. |
| Submission Intent | The Participant is the Attributable originator; Participant Authority establishes the expression. |
| Accepted Activity Event | Acceptance Authority establishes authoritative Activity Information. |
| Evidence Eligibility | Policy Authority establishes eligibility for a declared calculation under approved Policy. |
| Progress | Calculation Authority establishes authoritative Progress. |
| Completion | Calculation Authority establishes authoritative Completion. |
| Ranking | Calculation Authority establishes authoritative Ranking. |
| Projection | Calculation Authority establishes the source truth where calculated; Presentation Authority may communicate a subordinate representation. A Projection is never independent truth. |
| Runtime Knowledge | Knowledge Authority governs canonical meaning; the Runtime Catalogue is the single governed runtime authority. |
| High-impact administrative change | Administrative Authority performs the declared action through trusted execution under an approved role and purpose. |
| Sensitive audit record | The applicable authority creates an attributable record of its action; accountable stewardship and retention remain deferred. |

## Deferred work

The full entity register shall be created only after later decisions establish group, challenge, activity-correction, knowledge, social, retention, analytics and operational lifecycles. This foundation does not pre-empt those decisions.
