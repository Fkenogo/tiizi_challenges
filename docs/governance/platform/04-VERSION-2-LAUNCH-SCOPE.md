# Tiizi Version 2 Launch Scope

## Purpose

This document distinguishes Version 2 launch commitments from supporting foundations, future possibilities and non-goals.

## Guiding Decisions

The scope is governed by:

- PLT-01 — platform identity;
- PLT-02 — group-first launch boundary;
- PLT-03 — Fitness and Wellness launch domains;
- PLT-04 — individual challenges excluded from launch;
- IDP-01 and IDP-02 — privacy classification and enforceable controls;
- ACT-01 and ACT-02 — canonical activity events and trusted authority;
- KNW-01 — governed runtime catalogue authority;
- ADM-01 — least privilege and trusted high-impact actions.

## Launch Domains

### Fitness

Fitness is a launch knowledge and activity domain. It may provide governed activity definitions and domain-specific extensions to the canonical activity-event contract.

### Wellness

Wellness is a launch knowledge and activity domain. It may include governed hydration, recovery, mindfulness, sleep, mobility, healthy routines and general wellbeing without making each area a separate top-level launch domain.

## Included

Version 2 includes the constitutional foundation for:

- group challenges as the primary product model;
- group participation, accountability and shared progress;
- profiles, preferences, analytics and settings that support group participation;
- measurable participant submissions;
- trusted acceptance of canonical activity events;
- authoritative progress, completion, ranking and projections under trusted authority;
- Fitness and Wellness knowledge and activity extensions;
- one governed runtime catalogue authority;
- enforceable profile visibility classes and minimal disclosure;
- least-privilege administration;
- trusted authority for high-impact state changes;
- immutable audit records for sensitive actions.

Inclusion here establishes platform scope. Detailed behavior remains subject to its approved domain and lifecycle standard.

## Launch Capabilities

The launch foundation recognizes these business capabilities:

- participant identity and privacy;
- group formation and participation;
- group challenge definition and participation;
- governed activity discovery;
- activity submission and acceptance;
- shared progress communication;
- completion and ranking authority;
- motivation through participation, encouragement and shared progress;
- governed administration and auditability.

Specific challenge types, lifecycle transitions, ranking rules, streak rules, correction rules, rewards and notification behavior remain subject to later approved decisions.

## Not Included

Version 2 launch does not include:

- individual challenges;
- an individual-challenge runtime type;
- an individual-challenge product promise;
- any top-level activity domain other than Fitness and Wellness;
- unimplemented future domains presented as active capabilities;
- local assets acting as silent or equal production catalogue authorities;
- client authority over accepted events or derived challenge truth;
- interface visibility treated as authorization.

## Future Possibilities

Future governance may consider:

- additional knowledge and activity domains;
- individual challenges;
- further domain-specific activity extensions.

A future possibility requires its own product, lifecycle, permission, analytics and implementation decisions. It creates no Version 2 launch requirement, acceptance criterion or obligation.

## Non-goals

This launch-scope document does not:

- choose specific active challenge types;
- define challenge or participant lifecycles;
- define correction, verification, ranking, streak or retention rules;
- approve a reward model;
- approve additional notification channels;
- create the full entity ownership register;
- create the security standard;
- prescribe architecture or implementation.

## Scope Test

A proposed Version 2 launch capability is in scope only when it:

1. supports the approved group-first identity;
2. belongs to the platform foundation, Fitness or Wellness;
3. uses governed knowledge and canonical terminology;
4. respects the declared Authority to establish accepted and Derived Truth;
5. respects least privilege and enforceable privacy;
6. does not rely on an unapproved future capability.
