# Tiizi Platform Authority Model

## 1. Purpose

Tiizi requires one constitutional authority model so that every exercise of platform authority preserves truth, integrity, accountability, privacy and reliable shared progress.

This model defines the kinds of authority recognized by Tiizi, the limits of each authority and the constitutional chain through which information becomes authoritative. It governs later entity, role, permission, privacy, security, lifecycle and operational standards without replacing them.

The model serves Tiizi's group-first Version 2 scope. Group Challenges are the primary product model, Fitness and Wellness are the launch domains, and individual challenges remain excluded from launch. Domain neutrality does not confer authority on an unapproved future domain or capability.

This model does not assign final roles, define lifecycle transitions, create an entity register or prescribe technical authorization.

## 2. Meaning of Authority

Authority is the approved right and responsibility to establish, accept, calculate, govern, change or communicate platform information or state for a declared purpose.

Authority is always attributable, limited and constrained by constitutional governance, approved purpose, privacy and least privilege.

| Concept | Constitutional meaning | Distinction from authority |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Accountable stewardship of a Governed subject within declared constitutional boundaries. | Ownership identifies accountability; it does not grant every form of authority or unrestricted access. |
| Responsibility | A duty to perform or oversee an approved purpose. | Responsibility does not itself confer the right to establish authoritative truth. |
| Access | Approved ability to view, use or act upon information for a purpose. | Access permits use within scope; it does not establish the truth or authority of information. |
| Visibility | The approved audience for information. | Visibility determines audience, not the right to change information or state. |
| Capability | A stable business ability governed by the platform. | A capability describes what Tiizi can do; it is not an assignment of authority. |
| Role | A governed grouping of responsibilities and authority assignments. | A role name has no authority unless an approved model explicitly assigns it. |
| Presentation | Communication of authoritative, derived or contextual information. | Presentation may express truth but cannot establish or redefine it. |
| Authority | The approved right and responsibility to establish, accept, calculate, govern, change or communicate information or state for a declared purpose. | Authority is the governing concept against which the other concepts are distinguished. |

## 3. Authority Principles

### 3.1 One Authority Per Authoritative Truth

Every authoritative fact, state, calculation, knowledge item and decision shall have one declared authority. Multiple representations shall not create equal authorities.

### 3.2 Authority Must Be Declared

Authority shall be explicitly established by approved governance. Custom, convenience, possession, visibility or repeated use shall not create authority.

### 3.3 Authority Follows Governed Purpose

Every authority shall exist for a declared purpose. Authority shall not be exercised for an unrelated purpose merely because the actor can access the information or action.

### 3.4 Authority Is Limited by Scope

Authority shall be limited by subject, action, information, context and any approved conditions. Authority over one concern does not imply authority over another.

### 3.5 Interface Access Does Not Create Authority

The ability to see or invoke an action does not authorize the action or make its result authoritative.

### 3.6 Possession of Information Does Not Create Authority

Holding, receiving, copying or presenting information does not establish ownership, truth or authority over that information.

### 3.7 Authority May Not Transfer Silently

Authority remains with its declared source unless an explicit, attributable delegation or governance amendment changes it.

### 3.8 High-Impact Authority Requires Trusted Execution

High-impact state changes shall be exercised through Trusted Platform Authority under approved controls. Direct authority outside that trust boundary requires explicit later approval under the applicable permission and risk standard.

### 3.9 Derived Authority Remains Attributable

Every authoritative derived value shall remain attributable to accepted evidence, approved policy and its declared Calculation Authority.

### 3.10 Authority Actions Must Be Reviewable

An exercise of authority shall preserve enough attribution to determine the actor or authority, purpose, basis and relevant time.

### 3.11 Least Privilege Applies to Every Authority

No authority shall receive broader scope, information or action than its approved purpose requires.

### 3.12 Privacy Constrains Authority

Authority shall operate within approved visibility classes, minimum necessary use and deny-by-default access. Authority over state or operation does not remove privacy constraints.

## 4. Canonical Authority Types

The following authority types are constitutional categories. They do not assign a final organization chart, technical mechanism or role vocabulary.

### A. Participant Authority

**Purpose:** Enable a participant to express attributable personal intent, preference, consent and permitted communication within a governed participation context.

**May establish:**

- personal Submission Intent;
- personal preferences where permitted;
- consent or withdrawal expressions where governed;
- participant-authored communication where permitted.

**Must not establish:**

- Accepted Activity Events;
- Progress, Completion or Ranking;
- Verification;
- Policy or Authoritative Knowledge;
- authoritative analytics.

**Source of authority:** The person's governed relationship to the relevant identity, group or Challenge context, as limited by approved policy.

**Accountability requirement:** Expressions shall remain attributable to the participant, purpose and relevant context. Privacy and later lifecycle standards constrain their use.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Participant Authority creates Submission Intent for Acceptance Authority to evaluate. It does not inherit the authority of Identity, Participation, Acceptance or Calculation Authority.

### B. Identity Authority

**Purpose:** Govern authoritative identity and Profile state within approved privacy and lifecycle rules.

**May establish:** Authoritative identity or Profile information and valid identity-related determinations that later approved standards assign to it.

**Must not establish:** Group or Challenge participation, Accepted Activity Events, Derived Truth, Knowledge, Policy or unrestricted access to private information.

**Source of authority:** Approved identity, privacy and account governance, including the constitutional visibility classes.

**Accountability requirement:** Identity authority shall preserve attributable change, minimum disclosure, truthful consent meaning and deny-by-default access outside approved visibility.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Identity Authority supplies trusted identity context to other authorities. It does not determine their outcomes or establish account lifecycle transitions in this model.

### C. Participation Authority

**Purpose:** Govern relationships among people, Groups, Challenges and participation contexts.

**May establish:** Authoritative participation relationships and participation context where later approved group and Challenge standards permit.

**Must not establish:** Final group roles, membership states, Challenge states, activity acceptance, Derived Truth, Knowledge or Policy through this model.

**Source of authority:** Approved group, Challenge and participation governance.

**Accountability requirement:** Participation determinations shall be attributable, scoped to the relevant Group or Challenge and constrained by privacy and least privilege.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Participation Authority determines the governed context in which Participant Authority may act. It supports Acceptance and Calculation Authority without replacing them.

### D. Knowledge Authority

**Purpose:** Govern approved meaning used across Tiizi and preserve one governed runtime catalogue authority.

**May establish:**

- Knowledge Assets;
- canonical meanings;
- activity definitions;
- Metrics and Units;
- approved knowledge relationships;
- approved runtime availability through the single governed Runtime Catalogue authority.

**Must not establish:** Submission Intent, Accepted Activity Events, evidence of participation, Progress, Completion, Ranking or participant eligibility.

**Source of authority:** Approved platform and domain knowledge governance.

**Accountability requirement:** Knowledge shall have identified provenance, meaning, approval authority and governed runtime status. Local assets shall not silently override governed knowledge.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Knowledge Authority defines meaning used by Participant, Acceptance and Calculation Authority. Policy Authority governs how that meaning may be used.

### E. Policy Authority

**Purpose:** Govern approved rules that constrain behavior and authoritative determinations.

**May establish:** Approved rules concerning permitted behavior, evidence use, calculations, access and administrative action, but only where those policies have been separately governed.

**Must not establish:** Evidence that an activity occurred, participant identity, knowledge meaning that belongs to Knowledge Authority, or an outcome unsupported by approved evidence and calculation.

**Source of authority:** Approved constitutional, platform, domain, lifecycle or policy governance within the applicable precedence hierarchy.

**Accountability requirement:** Policy shall identify its scope, authority, approval basis and relationship to higher governance.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Policy Authority constrains every operational authority. It guides Acceptance and Calculation Authority but does not perform their determinations merely by defining rules.

### F. Acceptance Authority

**Purpose:** Evaluate Submission Intent and establish Accepted Activity Events as authoritative Activity Information.

**May establish:** Acceptance or non-acceptance of Submission Intent and the resulting Accepted Activity Event within approved policy.

**Must not establish:** Policy, Knowledge, participant-authored intent, Progress, Completion, Ranking or any other Derived Truth.

**Source of authority:** Trusted Platform Authority acting under approved evidence and activity governance.

**Accountability requirement:** Acceptance shall remain attributable to the Submission Intent, participant, applicable context, governing policy and acceptance authority.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Acceptance Authority receives meaning from Knowledge Authority, constraints from Policy Authority and context from Identity and Participation Authority. It establishes authoritative Activity Information. Policy Authority separately establishes Evidence Eligibility for declared calculations.

### G. Calculation Authority

**Purpose:** Establish authoritative Derived Truth from evidence made eligible for a declared calculation under approved Policy.

**May establish:**

- Progress;
- Completion;
- Ranking;
- governed aggregates;
- Projections;
- other approved derived determinations.

**Must not establish:** Submission Intent, Accepted Activity Events, Knowledge, Policy or evidence that does not exist.

**Source of authority:** Trusted Platform Authority designated by approved calculation and domain policy.

**Accountability requirement:** Every result shall remain attributable to eligible evidence, applicable Policy, declared method and Calculation Authority.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Calculation Authority consumes eligible evidence and approved rules. It does not determine Evidence Eligibility unless Policy Authority has separately assigned that determination. Presentation Authority may communicate its results but cannot independently recalculate or redefine them.

### H. Administrative Authority

**Purpose:** Perform approved high-impact state changes necessary for governed platform administration.

**May establish:** Administrative decisions and state changes explicitly permitted by approved governance and limited by assigned scope.

**Must not establish:** Unrestricted access, self-defined policy, unaudited exceptions, arbitrary evidence, or authority outside an approved assignment.

**Source of authority:** Approved governance and later role or permission assignments exercised through Trusted Platform Authority.

**Accountability requirement:** High-impact actions shall identify authority, actor, purpose, basis and relevant time in a durable, immutable audit record.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Administrative Authority may carry out approved decisions across domains but remains subordinate to Governance and Policy Authority. This model does not define the final role hierarchy.

### I. Operational Authority

**Purpose:** Govern reliability, continuity, reconciliation and controlled platform operation.

**May establish:** Operational determinations and controls expressly assigned by later approved operational governance.

**Must not establish:** Product policy, constitutional exceptions, permanent authority changes, participant evidence or business truth outside its operational scope.

**Source of authority:** Approved platform-operations governance and explicit assignment.

**Accountability requirement:** Operational action shall be attributable, purpose-limited, reviewable and constrained by least privilege.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Operational Authority supports the reliable exercise of all other authorities. It cannot override Governance, Policy, privacy or the declared authority of platform truth.

### J. Presentation Authority

**Purpose:** Communicate authoritative, derived or contextual information to an approved audience.

**May establish:** Approved formatting, summarization, arrangement and communication of information without changing its meaning or authority.

**Must not establish:** Underlying facts, Accepted Activity Events, Progress, Completion, Ranking, Policy, Knowledge or independent authority for a Projection.

**Source of authority:** The approved purpose, audience and presentation policy governing the underlying information.

**Accountability requirement:** Presentation shall identify or preserve the source, status and limitations of the information communicated and shall not overstate certainty or authority.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Presentation Authority communicates outputs from authoritative sources. It remains subordinate to the authority and privacy classification of those sources.

### K. Governance Authority

**Purpose:** Approve the constitutional rules, standards and authority boundaries under which Tiizi operates.

**May establish:**

- constitutional rules;
- platform and domain standards;
- authority boundaries;
- governed exceptions;
- amendments.

**Must not establish:** Hidden or unattributed authority, silent amendments, operational convenience as permanent governance, or rules that disregard approved precedence and amendment history.

**Source of authority:** Founder approval and any later explicit, governed delegation of governance authority.

**Accountability requirement:** Governance decisions shall identify their authority, rationale, affected standards, approval and amendment history.

**Relationship to other authorities:** Governance Authority establishes the boundaries within which all other authorities operate. This model does not invent an organization chart or allocate future governance roles.

## 5. The Tiizi Authority Chain

The canonical Tiizi Authority Chain is:

> **Governance Authority**
>
> ↓ approves
>
> **Knowledge Authority and Policy Authority**
>
> ↓ define meaning and rules
>
> **Participation Authority**
>
> ↓ establishes Participation Context
>
> **Participant Authority**
>
> ↓ establishes Submission Intent
>
> **Acceptance Authority**
>
> ↓ makes an Acceptance Decision and establishes an Accepted Activity Event
>
> **Policy Authority**
>
> ↓ establishes Evidence Eligibility for a declared calculation
>
> **Calculation Authority**
>
> ↓ establishes Derived Truth
>
> **Presentation Authority**
>
> ↓ communicates without redefinition
>
> Approved audience

Cross-cutting constraints apply throughout the chain:

- Identity Authority establishes the identity context recognized by other authorities.
- Participation Authority establishes the governed relationship context in which group participation occurs.
- Administrative Authority exercises only approved high-impact authority within scope.
- Operational Authority supports reliable and controlled operation without overriding governance.
- Privacy limits audience, access and use at every transition.
- Auditability preserves attribution for material authority actions.
- Least Privilege limits every authority to the minimum necessary scope.

### 5.1 Governance Approval

Governance Authority approves the standards and authority boundaries that make later authority legitimate. Approval shall be explicit and attributable.

### 5.2 Meaning and Rules

Knowledge Authority establishes approved meaning. Policy Authority establishes approved rules. Neither establishes that a participant acted.

### 5.3 Participant Expression

Participation Authority establishes the governed Group and Challenge relationship context. Participant Authority establishes attributable Submission Intent within that context. The Participant is the Attributable originator. Intent is an expression for evaluation, not an accepted fact.

### 5.4 Trusted Acceptance

Acceptance Authority evaluates Submission Intent under approved Knowledge and Policy, makes an Acceptance Decision and establishes the Accepted Activity Event as authoritative Activity Information. Applicable Policy separately establishes Evidence Eligibility for a declared calculation. Acceptance does not establish universal evidential use or Derived Truth.

### 5.5 Authoritative Calculation

Calculation Authority applies approved Policy to eligible evidence and establishes authoritative Progress, Completion, Ranking, governed aggregates or Projections.

### 5.6 Truthful Presentation

Presentation Authority communicates authoritative or derived information to an approved audience without changing meaning, value, state or authority.

### 5.7 Trusted Backend and Trusted Platform Authority

The constitutional term **Trusted Backend** identifies the governed authority responsible for accepted activity events and authoritative derived platform truth. **Trusted Platform Authority** is the wider authority-model term for governed, attributable, constrained and reviewable execution of trusted authority, including high-impact administrative action. The wider term does not rename, weaken or transfer the authority already assigned to the Trusted Backend.

## 6. Authority Boundaries

1. Participant Authority ends at Submission Intent.
2. Identity Authority establishes identity context but does not decide participation, evidence or Derived Truth.
3. Participation Authority establishes relationship context but does not define the still-pending group-role or lifecycle model.
4. Knowledge Authority defines meaning but does not prove participation.
5. Policy Authority defines approved rules but does not manufacture evidence or outcomes.
6. Acceptance Authority establishes Accepted Activity Events but does not create Policy, universal Evidence Eligibility or Derived Truth.
7. Policy Authority establishes Evidence Eligibility for a declared calculation; Calculation Authority establishes Derived Truth but does not invent or rewrite evidence.
8. Administrative Authority receives only specifically approved authority; it is never unrestricted.
9. Operational Authority supports reliability but does not override constitutional governance.
10. Presentation Authority communicates truth but does not change it.
11. Governance Authority may approve rules and amendments but shall preserve attribution and amendment history.

Every boundary is enforced under least privilege. Crossing from one authority type to another requires an approved assignment or delegation; proximity within a flow does not expand authority.

## 7. Delegation of Authority

Authority may be delegated only under these constitutional conditions:

1. Delegation shall be explicit.
2. Delegation shall name its scope.
3. Delegation shall state its governed purpose.
4. Delegation shall identify the delegating authority.
5. Delegation shall identify the receiving authority.
6. Delegation shall be time-bound or reviewable where appropriate to its purpose and risk.
7. Delegation shall not exceed the delegating authority's own approved authority.
8. Delegation and its exercise shall remain attributable and auditable.
9. Delegation shall be revocable through approved governance.
10. Interface access, information possession, job title or role label alone shall not constitute delegation.

Delegation transfers only the authority explicitly stated. It does not transfer ownership, visibility, access or authority outside the declared scope.

Every delegation remains subject to least privilege for its full duration.

## 8. Authority and Trust

**Trusted Platform Authority** is authority exercised through approved controls for a governed purpose with accountable attribution.

Trusted means:

- governed;
- attributable;
- constrained;
- reviewable;
- operated under approved controls.

Trusted does not mean:

- infallible;
- unrestricted;
- hidden;
- immune from review.

Trust is a governed condition of authority, not a personal status or permanent exemption. A trusted action remains subject to privacy, least privilege, auditability and constitutional precedence.

## 9. Authority and Information Categories

This matrix maps authority categories at a constitutional level. It does not assign entities, roles or lifecycle actions.

| Information category | Primary authority | Supporting authority | Authority exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity Information | Identity Authority | Participant, Administrative and Governance Authority within approved scope | Visibility, possession or operational access does not create identity authority. |
| Participation Information | Participation Authority | Identity, Participant, Policy and Administrative Authority within approved scope | Participation context does not create activity acceptance or calculation authority. |
| Knowledge Information | Knowledge Authority; the single governed Runtime Catalogue authority governs runtime availability | Governance and Policy Authority | Local assets, participants and presentation cannot silently establish Authoritative Knowledge. |
| Activity Information | Participant Authority for Submission Intent; Acceptance Authority for Accepted Activity Events | Identity, Participation and Knowledge Authority; Policy Authority for Evidence Eligibility | Participants cannot establish accepted events; Acceptance alone does not grant universal evidential use; Knowledge cannot prove participation. |
| Derived Information | Calculation Authority | Acceptance, Knowledge and Policy Authority | Presentation, Participant and Administrative Authority cannot create competing derived truth. |
| Administrative Information | Administrative Authority | Governance and Policy Authority | Privileged access does not grant unrestricted or unaudited administrative authority. |
| Operational Information | Operational Authority | Governance, Policy and Administrative Authority within approved scope | Operational urgency cannot permanently override governance or redefine product truth. |
| Analytical Information | Calculation Authority or a separately declared analytical authority under approved policy | Policy, Acceptance and Presentation Authority | Analysis cannot alter source evidence or silently become an independent authority. |
| Presentation Information | Presentation Authority | The authority of the underlying information and applicable Policy Authority | Formatting, prominence or repetition cannot establish or change truth. |
| Temporary Information | The authority designated for its declared temporary purpose | Relevant Participant, Administrative or Operational Authority within scope | Temporary information is not authoritative unless accepted through a governed transition. |

## 10. Authority and Visibility

Visibility, access, authority and ownership are separate constitutional concepts:

- **Visibility** identifies the approved audience.
- **Access** permits approved viewing, use or action for a declared purpose.
- **Authority** establishes the right and responsibility to create or change governed truth within scope.
- **Ownership** establishes accountable stewardship of a Governed subject within declared constitutional boundaries.

Therefore:

1. Visible information is not editable by default.
2. Private information is not owner-controlled in every operational context; approved operational use may exist, but only under defined authority, privacy and minimum necessary access.
3. Privileged access does not imply unrestricted authority.
4. Ownership does not grant unrestricted access.
5. Authority remains constrained by privacy, visibility classification, deny-by-default access and minimum necessary use.
6. A display control that cannot enforce its stated privacy effect shall not imply that it can.

## 11. Authority and Auditability

Durable accountability is required for authority actions that establish, materially change, delegate or correct significant platform truth.

At minimum, durable accountability applies to:

- Acceptance Decisions that establish Accepted Activity Events;
- determinations of Evidence Eligibility where materially consequential;
- high-impact administrative decisions;
- changes to Policy Authority or Knowledge Authority;
- delegation or revocation of authority;
- correction of authoritative or derived information;
- constitutional amendments.

The accountability record shall identify the relevant authority, action, purpose, basis and time. Where an attributable person acts for an authority, that person shall also be identifiable.

This model does not define retention periods, correction procedures or technical audit structures.

## 12. Authority Conflict Rules

Conflicts shall be evaluated according to this precedence:

1. approved constitutional governance;
2. approved platform standard;
3. approved domain or lifecycle standard;
4. approved Policy;
5. authoritative operational decision within scope;
6. Presentation or local interpretation.

The following conflict rules apply:

- A lower layer may refine but shall not contradict a higher layer.
- Equal, conflicting authorities shall not coexist silently.
- An unresolved conflict shall be escalated to Governance Authority.
- No actor or authority may select whichever source is most convenient.
- Operational urgency may support a temporary, attributable response within approved authority; it does not create a permanent constitutional exception.
- Presentation shall defer to the authoritative source when local wording or arrangement conflicts with governed truth.

## 13. Prohibited Authority Patterns

The following patterns are prohibited:

1. client-authored authoritative totals, participant aggregates, Completion, leaderboard rank, recap results, Verification status, Projections or derived analytics;
2. Presentation becoming an independent authority;
3. shared credentials or anonymous high-impact actions;
4. overlapping equal Calculation Authorities for the same derived truth;
5. local knowledge silently overriding governed knowledge or acting as an equal runtime authority;
6. administrators bypassing durable accountability;
7. operational urgency being used to permanently override governance;
8. undeclared delegation of authority;
9. role names being treated as authority without an approved role and authority model;
10. information possession being treated as ownership or authority;
11. visibility or interface access being treated as authorization;
12. Acceptance Authority creating Policy or Calculation Authority inventing evidence.

## 14. Deferred Areas

This model explicitly defers:

- the Entity Ownership Register;
- canonical role vocabulary;
- group-role authority;
- Challenge lifecycle authorities;
- account lifecycle authority;
- activity correction and Verification authority;
- Ranking and Streak policy detail;
- Knowledge publication and deprecation lifecycle;
- social moderation authority;
- Notification and Reward authority;
- support and donation authority;
- operational ownership;
- technical authorization design;
- the permission matrix;
- security controls;
- the Implementation Mapping.

Nothing in this model assigns a pending authority, approves a pending lifecycle or represents a possible future capability as active Version 2 scope.

## 15. Governance and Amendment

### Status

Status: Tiizi Foundational Governance Baseline v1.0 — reconciled for founder review. Approved Session 1 constitutional facts are incorporated and are not reopened by this model.

### Precedence

This model is subordinate to the Tiizi Platform Constitution and approved founder decisions. It governs later authority, role, permission, privacy, security, lifecycle, operational and entity standards within its scope.

### Amendment Requirement

An amendment shall identify the approving Governance Authority, rationale, affected provisions, decision trace, effective version and amendment history. A pending decision shall not amend this model until formally approved.

### Decision Trace

This model traces to approved decisions PLT-01, PLT-02, PLT-03, PLT-04, IDP-01, IDP-02, ACT-01, ACT-02, KNW-01 and ADM-01.

### Review Responsibility

Founder Governance Authority approves this draft and may later delegate defined review authority through explicit governance. No organization chart or final role allocation is created here.

### Relationship to Later Standards

Later standards may assign authority within the boundaries defined here. They may refine scope, conditions and accountability but shall not create competing authority or contradict constitutional precedence.
