# Tiizi Knowledge Corpus Traceability Appendix

## 1. Purpose and Method

This appendix records how the complete repository corpus under `docs/reports/knowledge-catalogue/` informed the [Knowledge Asset Domain Standard](./05-KNOWLEDGE-ASSET-DOMAIN-STANDARD.md).

The review enumerated all 29 artefacts present on 2026-07-19: 28 Markdown documents and one CSV inventory. Each artefact was reviewed for its purpose, evidence class, principal concepts, claimed authority and constitutional disposition.

The classifications used here are:

- **Current-state:** reports observed repository sources, behavior or inventory.
- **Intended-state:** proposes future governance, models, production methods or decisions.
- **Reference:** demonstrates a candidate asset, catalogue, dictionary, matrix or content structure.
- **Mixed:** combines more than one of the above.

“Authority status” records what the artefact claims or represents. It does not grant constitutional authority. Only approved founder decisions and higher-order approved governance bind this draft.

## 7. Preservation and Non-Rationalisation Statement

This review preserves every corpus artefact and every raw inventory row as evidence. It makes no Keep, Merge, Variant, Alias, Reclassify, Retire, Rename or canonical-ID decision.

Detailed catalogue specifications, family and taxonomy proposals, authoring guidance, reference assets, graph models, lifecycle proposals, compatibility work, recommendation frameworks and production programmes remain valuable supporting standards or inputs for later governance. They are not discarded, rewritten or superseded merely because a constitutional domain standard now exists.

In particular, this review does not:

- force the catalogue to equal 113, 154, 369 or any other historical count;
- assert that the local fitness catalogue equals the current live Runtime Catalogue;
- merge overlapping Fitness and Wellness records;
- treat interests, categories, templates, tests or reference assets as canonical Activities;
- adopt a proposed taxonomy, ontology, family dictionary or compatibility matrix;
- approve any proposed content, safety statement, instruction or benefit;
- approve a Knowledge lifecycle, snapshot contract or deletion rule;
- rewrite or supersede any corpus document.

The corpus remains available for later rationalisation and governance decisions. Where corpus claims conflict, the conflict is explicit rather than resolved by convenience.
