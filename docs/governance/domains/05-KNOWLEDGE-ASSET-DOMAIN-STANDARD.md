# Tiizi Knowledge Asset Domain Standard

## 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Asset Domain governs the meaning that Tiizi uses to describe measurable activities, measurements, guidance and their approved relationships.

It exists to ensure that Fitness and Wellness participation relies on knowledge that is attributable, coherent, reviewable and fit for its declared purpose. It establishes the constitutional boundary between rich governed Knowledge, the Runtime Catalogue used for participation, Challenge Policy, evidence of participation and presentation.

Governed Knowledge preserves:

- consistent Activity meaning;
- consistent Metric and Unit meaning;
- safe and useful Fitness and Wellness content;
- reusable domain knowledge;
- Challenge integrity;
- Activity Event interpretability;
- runtime consistency;
- historical intelligibility;
- domain extensibility through later governance.

This standard defines what a Knowledge Asset is. It does not approve a catalogue taxonomy, record structure, publication lifecycle, content set, compatibility rule, safety protocol or technical design.

## 2. Constitutional Role

A Knowledge Asset is a governed unit of Knowledge with distinct identity, canonical meaning, declared authority and defined relationships. It may define or support Activities, Metrics, Units, domain concepts, relationships, compatibility meaning, instructional content, descriptive content and safety information. It may be represented through a subordinate Runtime Projection.

Knowledge Assets support:

- truthful selection and interpretation of Activities;
- consistent measurement meaning;
- governed Challenge configuration;
- safe and intelligible guidance;
- historically intelligible understanding;
- domain-neutral platform participation across the Fitness and Wellness launch domains.

A Knowledge Asset is not:

- an Activity occurrence;
- Submission Intent;
- an Accepted Activity Event;
- evidence that participation occurred;
- a Challenge, Group, Profile, Member or Participant;
- Progress, Completion, Ranking or Recognition;
- a preference merely because a person selected a label;
- an Interest;
- a Goal unless separately governed as Knowledge;
- a Group message;
- Policy;
- a Runtime Catalogue representation merely because the representation exists;
- presentation-only content;
- a local implementation constant;
- an unrestricted claim of medical, clinical or professional authority;
- an individual-challenge mode.

## 3. Existing Tiizi Knowledge Foundation

The existing Tiizi knowledge-catalogue corpus contains four distinct evidence classes:

| Evidence class | Constitutional treatment |
|---|---|
| Current-state discovery | Evidence of present and legacy catalogue sources, overlaps, runtime paths and information gaps. It does not establish future governance by itself. |
| Documented intent | Proposed models, standards, programmes and matrices that describe a possible governed future. They inform this standard but do not override approved decisions. |
| Reference models | Example assets, catalogues, families, dictionaries and production templates that demonstrate useful information needs. They are not automatically approved catalogue content or taxonomy. |
| Approved founder governance | The ten Session 1 decisions are binding inputs. They determine platform scope, privacy, event authority, catalogue authority and administrative trust boundaries. |

The corpus demonstrates the need for rich activity meaning, controlled measurement, safety context, provenance, versioning, relationships and runtime projections. It also demonstrates unresolved differences in counts, identifiers, taxonomy, naming, entity boundaries, lifecycle language, Challenge compatibility and production authority.

This standard consolidates rather than replaces that body of work. It carries forward unified Knowledge Asset thinking; Fitness and Wellness support; Activity, Metric and Unit meaning; authoring and safety meaning; relationship and compatibility concepts; provenance; historical meaning; catalogue authority; and domain extensibility.

Detailed authoring, taxonomy, lifecycle and implementation artefacts remain supporting or future standards. Current runtime behavior is not proof that the full proposed Knowledge model is implemented. This standard does not adopt a corpus document merely because that document calls itself canonical, constitutional, approved or production-ready. Detailed reconciliation is recorded in the [Knowledge Corpus Traceability Appendix](./05-KNOWLEDGE-CORPUS-TRACEABILITY-APPENDIX.md).

## 4. Single Runtime Catalogue Authority

Tiizi shall have one governed Runtime Catalogue authority.

The Runtime Catalogue is the single governed authority through which governed Knowledge becomes available for a declared platform purpose. It shall not have a competing equal authority.

Multiple representations may exist without creating multiple authorities. Runtime authority remains distinct from Knowledge authorship, approval, custody and presentation. Templates, cached copies and local selection options do not acquire authority through use or visibility.

Local assets may serve only as:

- versioned development fixtures;
- test fixtures;
- controlled seed or bootstrap inputs;
- explicitly approved packaged fallback assets.

Local assets shall never silently override, merge with or act as an equal production authority. Any production fallback shall be explicit, observable and governed.

This principle implements KNW-01. It does not decide the future catalogue structure, projection contract, publication process, availability rules or fallback mechanism.

## 5. Rich Knowledge Asset Versus Runtime Projection

A rich Knowledge Asset and its Runtime Catalogue representation serve different purposes.

| Concept | Purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Rich Knowledge Asset | Preserve governed meaning, context, provenance, relationships, guidance and approved constraints. | It is not necessarily exposed in full for every runtime purpose. |
| Runtime Projection | Provide a governed representation containing the approved subset of a Knowledge Asset required for a declared participation, selection, measurement or display purpose. | It is not a Knowledge Asset classification and does not become an independent Knowledge authority. |
| Runtime availability | Indicate that a governed projection is approved for a declared runtime purpose. | Availability does not prove publication lifecycle state, safety suitability or universal Challenge compatibility. |
| Historical reference | Preserve the meaning used by a past governed context. | It does not make superseded Knowledge available for new use. |

Every Runtime Projection shall remain attributable and subordinate to the Knowledge Asset from which it is derived and to the Runtime Catalogue authority through which it is made available. A Runtime Projection may simplify presentation; it may not silently redefine identity, measurement, safety meaning, compatibility or provenance.

The constitutional relationship is:

> **Knowledge Asset**
>
> ↓ governed availability through
>
> **Runtime Catalogue Authority**
>
> ↓ provides
>
> **Runtime Projection**
>
> ↓ communicated through
>
> **Presentation**

The detailed version and snapshot contract remains deferred under KNW-02.

## 6. Domain Boundaries

### Included

The Knowledge Asset Domain includes the constitutional governance of:

- Knowledge Asset identity and meaning;
- canonical meaning;
- Activity definitions;
- Metric and Unit meaning;
- domain and classification meaning;
- descriptive information;
- family and variant relationships as governed concepts;
- instructional and safety knowledge boundaries;
- aliases and terminology relationships;
- compatibility meaning as distinct from Policy-governed use;
- provenance and historical intelligibility;
- approval basis as an attributable concept;
- Policy relationships;
- Runtime Catalogue authority and projection boundaries;
- runtime availability meaning;
- knowledge relationships to Challenge configuration and Activity Events;
- knowledge-related presentation and administrative boundaries.

### Excluded

This domain does not define or approve:

- final Fitness or Wellness catalogues;
- final taxonomy, category, family or variant dictionaries;
- canonical identifiers for legacy records;
- merge, keep, reclassify, variant, alias or retirement decisions;
- publication, review, deprecation or deletion lifecycle states;
- approval roles;
- authoring, clinical review or editorial workflows;
- content-authoring interfaces;
- localization workflows;
- Challenge eligibility or compatibility rules;
- target, scoring, ranking, streak or completion formulas;
- acceptance, Verification or correction rules;
- Profile interest and goal vocabularies;
- runtime synchronization;
- migration;
- technical structures or interfaces.

### Future Considerations

Later approved standards may govern catalogue lifecycle, taxonomy, authoring, safety review, Challenge compatibility, projections, snapshots, aliases, translations, recommendation use and domain expansion. These possibilities create no present catalogue record, workflow, state or launch capability.

## 7. Domain Principles

### 7.1 One Governed Meaning per Canonical Concept

Each canonical concept shall have one approved meaning and declared purpose before it governs participation.

### 7.2 One Declared Runtime Catalogue Authority

No equal competing runtime source may establish the same authoritative Knowledge meaning.

### 7.3 Local Representations Remain Subordinate

Fixtures, caches, templates, constants and local copies do not become equal authorities.

### 7.4 Knowledge Defines Meaning but Does Not Prove Occurrence

An Activity definition describes meaning. It does not prove that an Activity occurred.

### 7.5 Knowledge Differs from Policy

Knowledge describes approved meaning. Policy determines permitted behavior and governed use.

### 7.6 Knowledge Differs from Presentation

Presentation may communicate governed Knowledge but cannot establish or redefine it.

### 7.7 Knowledge Asset Differs from Runtime Projection

A Runtime Projection is a subordinate purpose-specific representation, not an independent Knowledge authority.

### 7.8 Activity Differs from Activity Event

An Activity defines governed behavior meaning. An Activity Event represents an occurrence in context.

### 7.9 Metric Differs from Value and Score

A Metric defines what is measured. It is not a measured value or a score.

### 7.10 Unit Differs from Display Text

A Unit has governed meaning that cannot be replaced by a suffix or label alone.

### 7.11 Interests and Personal Text Do Not Become Canonical Knowledge Automatically

Preferences, interests, goals and free-form text require separate governance before they can become canonical Knowledge.

### 7.12 Similar Names Do Not Prove Identity or Compatibility

Naming similarity does not authorize merger, aliasing, relationship or compatible use.

### 7.13 Safety Meaning Is Governed Where Applicable

Instructional and safety meaning forms part of governed content where the Activity or context requires it.

### 7.14 Provenance Matters

Knowledge shall remain attributable to its source, authority, purpose and material transformation.

### 7.15 Historical Meaning Remains Intelligible

Knowledge used in governed participation shall remain understandable in its historical context.

### 7.16 Domain Extensibility Requires Governance

Future domains require approved meaning and governance before becoming active.

### 7.17 Fitness and Wellness Remain Launch Domains

Fitness and Wellness are the approved Version 2 launch Knowledge domains.

### 7.18 Unapproved Domains Remain Inactive

Extensibility does not make an unapproved domain a runtime or launch capability.

### 7.19 Knowledge Changes Remain Attributable

Material changes to governed meaning shall not be silent and shall preserve provenance and historical intelligibility.

## 8. Knowledge Asset Classification

The following classifications define constitutional purposes, not a final taxonomy, storage arrangement or record model.

| Classification | Purpose | Authority boundary | Relationship to runtime use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activity Knowledge Asset | Define the governed meaning of a measurable or truthfully evaluable Activity. | It is not an occurrence or Challenge prescription. | A projection may expose approved selection, measurement or presentation meaning. |
| Metric Knowledge Asset | Define the governed dimension being measured or counted. | It is not a value, Unit, target or score. | Runtime use must preserve the approved measurement meaning. |
| Unit Knowledge Asset | Define the governed expression of a Metric. | A display label alone is not sufficient authority. | Runtime use may abbreviate but may not redefine the Unit. |
| Domain Concept Knowledge Asset | Define an approved domain concept and its scope. | It does not make a future domain active or automatically identify an Activity. | Runtime use is limited to approved domains and purposes. |
| Relationship Knowledge Asset | Express a governed relationship among Knowledge Assets. | A relationship does not establish event evidence or identity equivalence. | Runtime use may expose only relationships approved for its purpose. |
| Compatibility Knowledge Asset | Express governed compatibility meaning for later Policy use. | It does not itself create Challenge eligibility or behavior. | Runtime use requires applicable approved Policy. |
| Instructional Knowledge Asset | Explain an approved method, cue, protocol or contextual guidance. | It does not prove performance or replace Policy. | Runtime use shall preserve applicable context and safety meaning. |
| Safety Knowledge Asset | Express approved cautions, constraints or review context. | It does not establish diagnosis, individual suitability or Verification. | Runtime use shall not omit or contradict required safety meaning. |
| Descriptive Knowledge Asset | Provide governed explanatory meaning. | It is not presentation-only copy or evidence. | Runtime presentation remains attributable and subordinate. |
| Taxonomy or Controlled-Vocabulary Knowledge Asset | Define an approved classification or controlled term. | It does not replace Activity identity or prove equivalence. | Runtime filters and labels must derive from governed terminology where applicable. |

Final classification dictionaries, cardinalities and content requirements remain deferred. These classifications need not be represented as separate technical objects.

### Governed representation type

Runtime Projection is a governed representation type, not a Knowledge Asset classification. It exposes an approved subset for a declared runtime purpose and remains subordinate to its Knowledge Asset and the single Runtime Catalogue authority. The projection contract remains pending under KNW-04; this classification boundary does not define versioning, storage, fields or implementation.

## 9. Fitness and Wellness Domain Boundary

Fitness and Wellness are the Version 2 launch Knowledge domains.

Both domains shall conform to the same constitutional requirements for:

- governed identity and meaning;
- Metric and Unit integrity;
- provenance;
- authority;
- runtime projection;
- historical intelligibility;
- privacy and least privilege;
- separation of Knowledge, Policy, evidence and Derived Truth.

The domains may require distinct purpose, content, safety context, measurement, relationships, instruction and review expertise. Shared names such as walking, stretching, mobility, yoga, recovery or breathing do not by themselves prove that records are identical or should be merged. Overlapping labels require governed relationship or compatibility analysis. Domain overlap shall be resolved only through later governed rationalisation.

Movement, recovery, breathing, mindfulness, sleep, nutrition and other Wellness concepts shall not be forced into Fitness structures without approved meaning.

Wellness may contain governed areas such as hydration, recovery, mindfulness, sleep, mobility, healthy routines and general wellbeing without making each a separate top-level launch domain.

## 10. Activity Family, Variant and Relationship Boundary

An Activity Family is a governed relationship that groups Activities with a shared conceptual basis. An Activity Variant is a governed Activity meaning that remains materially distinct within a related family or lineage.

The domain may later distinguish canonical Activity, variation, progression, regression, left/right or unilateral form, hold or dynamic form, related Activity, alternative and compatibility relationship. These concepts do not approve any current assignment.

Family and variant relationships may help preserve:

- shared conceptual meaning;
- meaningful execution differences;
- equipment or environment distinctions;
- bilateral or unilateral distinctions;
- progression and regression relationships;
- aliases without identity loss;
- challenge and measurement suitability differences.

No record shall be classified as a family, variant, alias or duplicate solely because of spelling, punctuation, singular or plural wording, regional terminology, equipment wording, difficulty wording or a shared target. The existing corpus contains useful proposed family and variant models, but their dictionaries and assignments remain reference material pending approval.

Mechanical expansion does not automatically prove separate canonical identity. Variants may differ materially in measurement, instruction, safety or Challenge compatibility.

## 12. Activity Knowledge Meaning

An Activity is a governed measurable or truthfully evaluable behavior. Its Knowledge meaning may address purpose, domain, classification, execution, measurement, safety, variants, relationships, Challenge suitability and historical interpretability.

An Activity definition:

- does not prove an occurrence;
- does not create a Challenge;
- does not prescribe a universal target;
- does not establish participation eligibility;
- does not establish safety for every person or context;
- does not become a score, Progress or Completion;
- shall not be identified solely by its display name.
- is not an Interest;
- may have domain-specific meaning.

The final mandatory content, identifier, naming and classification rules remain deferred.

## 13. Metric and Unit Meaning

A Metric defines the property being measured, counted or truthfully evaluated. A Unit defines the governed expression of that Metric.

Metric, Unit, value, target and score are distinct:

- a Metric is not a value;
- a Unit is not a Metric;
- a value without governed meaning is insufficient;
- a target is a Challenge evaluation reference, not an Activity definition;
- a score exists only where later approved Policy defines it;
- a display abbreviation does not create Unit authority.

Compatibility, conversions, precision, bounds, direction, cadence, aggregation and formula rules remain deferred.

Activity–Metric–Unit compatibility must be governed. Similar Units do not prove comparable meaning, and conversion does not automatically create semantic equivalence. Count, time, distance, frequency, completion and qualitative occurrence shall not be treated as interchangeable.

## 14. Instructional and Safety Meaning

Instructional Knowledge may explain setup, execution, form cues, breathing guidance, common mistakes, progressions, regressions, substitutions, recommended use, warm-up or recovery context where approved.

Safety Knowledge may express cautions, contraindication context, prerequisites, review requirements or limits for a declared purpose.

Both shall:

- remain attributable to the applicable Knowledge and review authority;
- distinguish evidence-backed statements from editorial guidance where later standards require;
- avoid implying universal suitability;
- avoid unsupported medical, clinical or body-composition claims;
- preserve the limits of their approved purpose;
- remain separate from Activity Event evidence and Verification.
- not be contradicted by descriptive presentation for convenience.

This standard does not approve any existing instruction, benefit, protocol, safety statement, review level or editorial workflow.

## 15. Knowledge and Profile Preference Boundary

Profiles may express interests, goals and preferences to support participation and personalization. Those expressions are Profile or preference information, not Knowledge authority.

- Selecting an interest does not create an Activity definition.
- A free-form preference does not become canonical Knowledge.
- A goal does not become an Activity, Metric or Challenge Policy.
- Similar labels across preference and activity vocabularies do not prove identity.
- Recommendation use shall remain constrained by privacy, minimum necessary purpose and later approved Policy.
- Knowledge may support controlled preference vocabularies where later approved.

The ownership and vocabularies of interests and goals remain deferred under KNW-04.

## 16. Knowledge and Policy Boundary

Knowledge defines approved meaning. Policy governs permitted behavior and use.

Policy may later determine:

- whether an Activity is eligible for a Challenge context;
- which Metrics and Units are permitted together;
- evidence and Verification requirements;
- target and calculation constraints;
- safety acknowledgements or review requirements;
- publication, access or operational conditions.

A Knowledge Asset may reference applicable Policy, but it does not create Policy merely by containing a recommendation or compatibility label. Policy Authority shall not silently rewrite Knowledge meaning.

Knowledge alone does not create eligibility, acceptance, scoring or Completion rules. Compatibility or safety meaning does not itself create Challenge behavior.

## 17. Knowledge and Evidence Boundary

The Knowledge Asset Domain conforms to the Tiizi Information Authority Chain:

```text
Governed Knowledge
            ↓ defines meaning
Policy
            ↓ defines rules
Participation Context
            ↓ permits attributable expression
Submission Intent
            ↓ evaluated through an Acceptance Decision
Accepted Activity Event
            ↓ assessed under applicable Policy
Evidence Eligibility
            ↓ permits declared Calculation use
Calculation
            ↓ establishes
Derived Truth
            ↓ communicated without redefinition
Presentation
```

Knowledge defines what an Activity, Metric and Unit mean. The Participant is the Attributable originator of Submission Intent, and Participant Authority establishes that expression. Acceptance Authority makes the Acceptance Decision and establishes Accepted Activity Events as authoritative Activity Information. Policy Authority establishes Evidence Eligibility for declared calculations. Calculation Authority establishes Progress, Completion, Ranking and other Derived Truth from eligible evidence. Presentation Authority communicates results without redefining them.

Knowledge never proves participation. An Activity definition is not an event, a Metric definition is not a value, and a Unit definition is not evidence. Acceptance Authority preserves the Knowledge meaning applied at acceptance. Later changes shall not silently reinterpret historical event meaning. Evidence never creates Knowledge or Policy.

## 18. Runtime Availability Boundary

Runtime availability means that an approved Knowledge projection is available for a declared platform purpose under the single governed Runtime Catalogue authority.

Runtime availability shall be:

- purpose-declared;
- attributable to governed Knowledge;
- distinguishable from drafting, testing and historical reference;
- distinguishable from Challenge compatibility;
- distinguishable from participant eligibility;
- observable where fallback or substitution is permitted;
- constrained by approved visibility and access.

Availability in one context does not imply availability in every context. This standard defines no availability state, publication state or selection rule.

Approved Knowledge may exist without being runtime-active. Inactive, draft, reference-only or fixture content shall not appear as an authoritative active choice.

## 19. Provenance and Source Boundary

Provenance preserves why governed Knowledge can be trusted and interpreted. It shall support attribution to:

- the originating source or approved derivation;
- authorship;
- evidence basis;
- review basis;
- transformation history;
- imported or inherited meaning;
- confidence or uncertainty where applicable;
- the authority responsible for the governed meaning;
- the purpose and domain for which it was approved;
- material review or amendment context;
- relationships to prior or derived meaning;
- the Runtime Catalogue projection where one exists.

Source presence and copying do not establish authority, ownership or approval. Provenance is not approval by itself. Missing provenance shall not be silently replaced with inference. Exact citation, licensing, source-review and provenance requirements remain deferred.

## 20. Historical Integrity and Change Boundary

Knowledge that has influenced participation shall remain historically intelligible.

Material changes to identity, Activity meaning, Metric, Unit, safety meaning, compatibility or applicable Policy shall not silently alter the meaning of past participation. Historical references shall remain attributable to the Knowledge meaning that applied at the relevant time.

Replacement does not prove semantic equivalence. Deprecation, where later approved, shall not erase historical references.

Authoritative historical references shall not be silently destroyed, and deletion shall not be used to conceal governed history. This is an integrity boundary, not a deletion or retention workflow.

This principle does not approve snapshot minimums, a version scheme, deprecation states, retention periods, deletion rules or an amendment workflow. Those matters remain pending under KNW-02 and KNW-03.

## 22. Ownership Model

This foundation describes ownership boundaries without creating the Entity Ownership Register.

| Constitutional relationship | Constitutional position |
|---|---|
| Governed subject | The Knowledge Asset is the Governed subject whose canonical meaning is preserved. |
| Information subject | The Knowledge Asset is the Information subject of Knowledge Information and its attributable provenance. |
| Attributable originator | A contributor may be the Attributable originator of source material without establishing approval or canonical meaning. |
| Authority to establish truth | Knowledge Authority establishes authoritative Knowledge Asset meaning. Runtime Catalogue authority establishes governed runtime availability, not canonical meaning. |
| Accountable steward | Accountable stewardship remains deferred; Fitness and Wellness expertise may support it without creating competing authority. |
| Custodian | A Custodian may preserve the Runtime Catalogue or a Runtime Projection without acquiring authorship or Knowledge Authority. |
| Administrator | An Administrator may perform explicitly assigned Knowledge actions but does not personally acquire stewardship or canonical-meaning authority. |
| Operator | An Operator may preserve catalogue continuity within assigned scope without establishing canonical meaning. |
| Presenter | A Presenter may communicate a Runtime Projection without redefining Knowledge. |
| Downstream user | Challenges and Activity Events are Downstream users of governed meaning and do not acquire authority to redefine it. |

No Group, Challenge, Participant, local fixture, Runtime Projection or presentation acquires accountable stewardship or Authority to establish Knowledge truth merely by using, copying or displaying it.

## 24. Legacy Catalogue Boundary

The existing repository demonstrates multiple sources that represent or influence fitness and wellness meaning: runtime catalogue records, local source data, seeded templates, Challenge snapshots, activity logs, hard-coded selections, taxonomies, tests, reference assets and documentation.

These sources provide evidence of current or intended behavior. They do not become equal constitutional authorities.

Accordingly:

- the local count of 154 fitness records is evidence of one local catalogue, not a confirmed live runtime total;
- historical references to 113 records are not reconciled by this standard;
- 369 legacy discovery rows are inventory evidence, not a canonical exercise count;
- 67 wellness records are current catalogue evidence, not automatically approved V2 Knowledge Assets;
- overlapping Fitness and Wellness labels are not merged;
- hard-coded interests, categories, units and templates are not promoted to canonical Knowledge;
- existing identifiers are preserved as evidence but are not approved as V2 identifiers;
- current runtime paths do not determine future constitutional structure;
- proposed taxonomies, ontologies, families and compatibility matrices remain supporting or deferred unless separately approved.

Live-environment reconciliation remains necessary before later catalogue rationalisation. This standard makes no Keep, Merge, Variant, Alias, Reclassify or Retire decision.

## 26. Visibility

Knowledge-related information shall be classified under the constitutional visibility classes according to purpose:

| Visibility class | Knowledge Asset application | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Approved Knowledge may be communicated publicly where later governance permits. | Public visibility does not establish authority, compatibility or universal suitability. |
| Authenticated-discoverable | Approved projections may support discovery by authenticated people. | Discoverability does not establish access to all content or participation eligibility. |
| Shared-group | Approved Knowledge and summaries may support a governed Group context. | Group visibility does not expose private Profile or evidence information. |
| Private | Draft, personal or purpose-limited Knowledge-related information may be restricted. | Private information is not automatically controlled without operational limits. |
| Privileged-operational | Minimum necessary access may support approved review, administration and operation. | Privileged access is purpose-limited and does not create unrestricted authority. |

Visibility, access, accountable stewardship and Authority to establish truth remain distinct. Final publication, discoverability and audience rules are deferred.

## 27. Administrative and Operational Boundary

Administrative and Operational Authority in the Knowledge Asset Domain:

- must be explicitly assigned;
- remains constrained by least privilege, privacy and declared purpose;
- does not arise from interface access or possession of source material;
- cannot silently create, approve, replace, merge, retire or delete authoritative Knowledge;
- cannot create a second Runtime Catalogue authority;
- requires durable accountability for high-impact actions;
- may support controlled catalogue continuity and reconciliation only under approved governance;
- does not establish final roles, workflows or technical privileges.

High-impact Knowledge actions shall use Trusted Platform Authority. This preserves the approved constitutional principle that high-impact state changes belong to trusted authority without defining an implementation mechanism.
