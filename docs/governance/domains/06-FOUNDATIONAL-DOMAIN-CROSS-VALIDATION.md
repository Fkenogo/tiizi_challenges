# Tiizi Foundational Domain Cross-Validation

## 1. Executive Verdict

**Verdict: Coherent and reconciled as Tiizi Foundational Governance Baseline v1.0.**

The five foundational Domain Standards form one coherent constitutional system. They preserve Tiizi's group-first scope, the separation of Profile, Group, Challenge, Activity Event and Knowledge Asset, the Participant-to-trusted-authority boundary, one Runtime Catalogue authority, least privilege and the five visibility classes.

The approved reconciliation has been implemented. Accepted Activity Event now means the authoritative accepted record established by Acceptance Authority; applicable Policy separately establishes Evidence Eligibility for a declared calculation. Ownership shorthand has been replaced by the normalized constitutional vocabulary. Runtime Projection is a governed representation rather than a Knowledge Asset classification. Every information grouping has one primary constitutional category. Undefined authority labels and stale future-domain references have been removed.

The Runtime Projection direction resolves only its constitutional classification. KNW-04 remains pending for the detailed projection contract, interests, goals and taxonomy ownership. No approved founder decision was reopened and no pending lifecycle or product decision was resolved.

## 6. Information Authority Chain

The complete cross-domain chain should read:

```text
Governance Authority
  → approves Knowledge and Policy authority boundaries
Governed Knowledge
  → supplies Activity, Metric, Unit and relationship meaning
Policy
  → constrains participation, acceptance, evidence eligibility and calculation
Participation Context
  → establishes the relevant Group, Challenge and Participant relationships
Submission Intent
  → records the Participant's attributable expression
Acceptance Decision
  → applies Knowledge and Policy through Acceptance Authority
Accepted Activity Event
  → establishes authoritative Activity Information
Evidence Eligibility
  → determines permitted calculation use under Policy
Calculation
  → applies approved method through Calculation Authority
Derived Truth
  → establishes Progress, Completion, Ranking or approved aggregate
Presentation
  → communicates without redefinition
```

### Cross-standard result

- **Missing links:** None. Higher-order and domain chains include Participation Context, Acceptance Decision and Evidence Eligibility.
- **Conflicting order:** None. Knowledge and Policy remain distinct and occur before participant expression and acceptance.
- **Duplicated authority:** No equal Acceptance or Calculation Authority is assigned.
- **Incorrect collapse:** None. Accepted Activity Event is authoritative Activity Information; applicable Policy separately establishes Evidence Eligibility.
- **Different treatment:** None. All higher-order and domain chains use the normalized sequence.

## 7. Ownership-Language Review

The five standards now use the normalized constitutional vocabulary. The following table preserves the pre-reconciliation shorthand and its implemented replacement for traceability.

| Current statement pattern | What it appears to mean | Risk | Normalized treatment for later amendment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Participant “owns” Submission Intent. | Participant is the attributable originator and Participant Authority establishes the expression. | “Owns” may imply unrestricted editing, deletion or legal property. | Preserve the approved founder quotation; elsewhere use “Participant is the attributable originator; Participant Authority establishes Submission Intent.” |
| Acceptance Authority “establishes and owns authoritative accepted-event truth.” | Acceptance Authority establishes the authoritative event. | Conflates authority with ownership and custody. | “Acceptance Authority establishes authoritative Accepted Activity Event truth.” |
| Policy Authority “owns” Challenge Policy. | Policy Authority establishes approved rules. | Suggests proprietary ownership or lifecycle allocation before the register. | “Policy Authority establishes the approved Challenge Policy.” |
| Calculation Authority or Derived Truth “ownership.” | Calculation Authority establishes authoritative calculated results. | Conflates calculation authority with entity ownership and stewardship. | “Calculation Authority establishes Derived Truth and is accountable for its declared calculation.” |
| Identity and Participation Authority “own” context. | Each authority establishes its respective contextual truth. | Implies that authority types are entity owners. | “Identity Authority establishes identity context; Participation Authority establishes relationship context.” |
| Group/Challenge “ownership.” | Accountable stewardship or lifecycle responsibility remains to be assigned. | Could pre-empt GRP-02, CHL-01 or the register. | Use “governed subject” and “accountable stewardship remains deferred.” |
| Administrator “maintains” information. | Administrative custody within assigned scope. | Maintenance may be mistaken for truth-establishing authority. | “Administrative custodian preserves information within assigned authority; custody grants no additional authority.” |
| Runtime Catalogue or projection “custody.” | Operational preservation of an authoritative representation. | Custody may be mistaken for Knowledge approval authority. | Separate Knowledge Authority, Runtime Catalogue authority, operational custodian and presenter. |

### Proposed normalized vocabulary

| Term | Reserved meaning |
|---|---|
| Governed subject | The entity or information whose identity and lifecycle are governed. |
| Information subject | The person or entity about whom information is recorded. |
| Attributable originator | The actor whose expression or contribution originated the information. |
| Authority to establish truth | The declared authority that makes a fact, state, Knowledge item or calculation authoritative. |
| Accountable steward | The party answerable for preserving the governed subject and ensuring proper authority is exercised. |
| Custodian | The party maintaining information under delegated scope without acquiring truth authority. |
| Administrator | An actor exercising explicitly assigned Administrative Authority. |
| Operator | An actor supporting continuity and control under Operational Authority. |
| Presenter | An actor or capability communicating information without redefining it. |
| Downstream user | A domain or capability using authoritative information without owning or changing its source truth. |

**Ownership-language result:** Pass. The approved ACT-02 quotation remains preserved as decision evidence. Operative standards distinguish Governed subject, Information subject, Attributable originator, Authority to establish truth, Accountable steward, Custodian, Administrator, Operator, Presenter and Downstream user.

### Runtime Projection classification

Knowledge section 5 defines Runtime Projection as a subordinate purpose-specific governed representation of a Knowledge Asset. Section 8 lists ten Knowledge Asset classifications and separately defines Runtime Projection as a governed representation type. The platform terminology and glossary contain the same treatment.

**Implemented resolution:** Runtime Projection is a governed representation type, not a Knowledge Asset classification. It remains attributable to a Knowledge Asset and is made available through the Runtime Catalogue authority; it does not acquire independent Knowledge identity or authority.

This treatment does not define the projection contract, versioning or storage. KNW-04 remains pending for those detailed matters and for interests, goals and taxonomy ownership.

**Runtime Catalogue and projection result:** Pass. The constitutional chain is Knowledge Asset → Runtime Catalogue Authority → Runtime Projection → Presentation. Runtime Projection is a governed representation, not a Knowledge Asset classification or independent authority. KNW-04 remains pending for the detailed contract.
