# Tiizi Foundational Domain Reconciliation Plan

## 1. Reconciliation Objective

The objective was to make the five foundational Domain Standards operate as one precise constitutional system before the Entity Ownership Register assigns accountable stewardship, custody or lifecycle responsibility.

**Implementation status: Complete as Tiizi Foundational Governance Baseline v1.0 on 2026-07-19.**

This document now records the approved correction plan and its implementation. The reconciliation amended constitutional wording only. It did not approve a pending decision, define a lifecycle, assign a role, allocate entity ownership or create an implementation design.

The amendments should:

- preserve all ten approved Session 1 decisions;
- keep KNW-02, KNW-03 and KNW-04 pending;
- remove wording that could create competing truth authorities;
- normalize Activity Event, evidence and ownership terminology;
- establish one primary information category per governed grouping;
- preserve reciprocal domain boundaries;
- retain the five standards as the complete foundational domain layer.

## 4. Ownership-Language Normalization

The following vocabulary governs Baseline v1.0 and shall govern the later Entity Ownership Register.

| Canonical term | Meaning | Permitted use | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governed subject | The entity or information whose identity, accepted state or lifecycle is governed. | Name the object of governance. | Actor, owner, authority or custodian. |
| Information subject | The Person or entity about whom information is recorded. | Profile, event and participation attribution. | Unrestricted control or truth authority. |
| Attributable originator | The actor whose expression or contribution originated information. | Participant and contributor attribution. | Acceptance, approval, ownership or deletion rights. |
| Authority to establish truth | The declared authority that makes information or state authoritative within scope. | Identity, Participation, Knowledge, Policy, Acceptance and Calculation determinations. | Legal ownership, custody or unrestricted access. |
| Accountable steward | The assigned party answerable for correct governance and preservation of a subject. | Later entity-register and lifecycle allocations. | Automatic authority outside assigned scope. |
| Custodian | The party maintaining information under explicit delegation. | Preservation, controlled maintenance and continuity. | Approval, authorship, ownership or authority expansion. |
| Administrator | An actor exercising explicitly assigned Administrative Authority. | High-impact actions under least privilege and auditability. | Superuser status, ownership, Acceptance or Calculation Authority. |
| Operator | An actor supporting reliability under Operational Authority. | Continuity, reconciliation and controlled operation. | Product Policy, permanent exception or business-truth authority. |
| Presenter | An actor or capability arranging and communicating approved information. | Formatting, summarization and audience-specific communication. | Source-truth authority or recalculation. |
| Downstream user | A domain or capability consuming authoritative information for an approved purpose. | Challenge use of Knowledge, calculation use of Evidence, presentation use of Derived Truth. | Source ownership, source amendment or independent authority. |

### Approved ACT-02 wording

The founder-approved statement remains part of the decision record:

> “The participant owns the submission intent. A trusted backend owns acceptance, authoritative activity events, progress, completion, rankings and projections.”

Operative domain and future register language interprets that statement constitutionally:

- Participant is the attributable originator, and Participant Authority establishes Submission Intent.
- Acceptance Authority establishes authoritative Accepted Activity Event truth.
- Policy Authority establishes Evidence Eligibility for declared calculations.
- Calculation Authority establishes authoritative Derived Truth from eligible evidence.
- A later register may assign accountable stewardship and custody without transferring those authorities.

This interpretation preserves ACT-02 rather than reopening it.

## 7. Runtime Projection Resolution — Implemented

### Approved treatment

**Runtime Projection is a governed representation type, not a Knowledge Asset classification.**

Rationale:

1. Platform governance already defines a Projection as subordinate representation.
2. Knowledge section 5 distinguishes rich Knowledge Asset from Runtime Projection.
3. Treating Projection as a Knowledge Asset classification creates unnecessary identity and authority ambiguity.
4. A representation type can still be governed, attributable, purpose-limited and available through the single Runtime Catalogue authority.
5. This treatment does not decide version identity, snapshot contents, storage, synchronization or implementation.

### Implemented conceptual treatment

```text
Knowledge Asset
  → holds approved reusable meaning
Runtime Catalogue Authority
  → approves availability for a declared runtime purpose
Runtime Projection
  → represents the approved subset without independent authority
Challenge or Activity Event
  → references the governed meaning appropriate to its context
```

### Founder direction and pending boundary

Founder direction for the constitutional classification was supplied in Phase D7 and has been implemented. It does not approve the detailed Runtime Projection contract, versioning, storage, interests, goals or taxonomy ownership. KNW-04 remains pending.
