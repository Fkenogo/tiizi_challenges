## 1. Purpose

This record assesses how the Tiizi Version 2 project aligns with the current Founder Engineering Framework (FEF) operational baseline.

It does not replace the project's own programme, architecture, decisions, or delivery controls. It does not import FEF doctrine into Tiizi constitutional governance. It does not authorise implementation of any alignment action.

This record is the project's controlled entry point to FEF. It records the FEF baseline used, the project baseline, areas already aligned, genuine evidence-supported gaps, project-specific matters, intentional deviations, unresolved conflicts, evidence gaps, proportionate proposed actions, Founder decisions required, possible Framework lessons, and the next review trigger.

## 4. Existing Strengths

1. **Single Source of Truth discipline.** The Master Programme self-declares and enforces one authoritative programme-state record, with dashboard, metrics, status and change-log synchronization requirements (Master Programme §1, §2, §3, §17).
2. **Attributable Founder decision records.** The Consolidated Decision Register and per-package Founder Decision Records preserve decision trace, options, dispositions and affected constitutional treatment.
3. **Separation of decision from implementation authority.** The Work Package Authorization Lifecycle explicitly distinguishes Founder Decision Recorded from Authorized to Begin, and evidence from approval (Master Programme §17 Rule 7).
4. **Explicit lifecycle gates and dependencies.** Stage gates (D→H), the CGP-02C.x bounded work-package sequence, and the blocked status of CGP-03 demonstrate controlled transitions without silent prerequisite skipping.
5. **Evidence-bounded validation.** Validation reports, completion reports, milestone validation reports and PTRA transition assessments accompany each governed phase.
6. **Visible exceptions and deferrals.** Pending decisions, deferred EOG-02 decision areas, the 45 unresolved CGP-02C.13 Founder dispositions, and the Master Programme's stale authorized-not-executed CGP-02C.13 record are explicitly visible and reported rather than silently resolved.
7. **Explicit external-framework boundary.** The project has documented that the developing FEF is a boundary consideration only and not a Tiizi normative source (see §7), preserving project ownership and preventing governance import by assumption.

## 6. Project-Specific Matters

1. **Constitutional governance programme.** Tiizi operates its own constitutional governance programme (CGP-01, CGP-02, EOG-01–05, PTRA assessments) that is more detailed than the FEF minimum baseline. This is project-owned by design and should remain so.
2. **Master Programme as authoritative record.** The Tiizi Master Programme is the project's single source of truth for programme state. FEF does not replace it and no FEF artefact should duplicate it.
3. **Project-specific decision vocabulary.** The project uses its own decision identifiers (PLT, IDP, GRP, CHL, ACT, RSK, KNW, SOC, NTF, MOT, SUP, ANL, ADM, OPS, EOG, GFC, CGP, FWA, PTRA) and work-package lifecycle. These are project conventions and do not require FEF standardisation.
4. **Governance folder structure.** The project's `docs/governance/` hierarchy (`platform/`, `principles/`, `ownership/`, `domains/`) and `docs/programme/` folder are established conventions. This alignment record is placed in `docs/governance/` per the FEF Adoption Guide's instruction to use the existing governance folder.
5. **Engineering change records.** `CHANGE.md` and `docs/reports/` contain legacy engineering change logs and phase reports. These are operational records distinct from the constitutional governance baseline and remain project-owned.

## 12. Current Alignment Position

The Tiizi Version 2 project is **substantially aligned** with the FEF operational baseline across Founder authority, programme authority, decision lifecycle, evidence and validation, lifecycle and gates, and exceptions handling. The Founder has approved FEF adoption as Tiizi's baseline governance reference, with Tiizi's project-specific governance, Master Programme authority and historical records preserved.

**FEF relationship (prospective):** FEF is the adopted baseline governance reference for Tiizi. Tiizi's Master Programme remains authoritative for Tiizi programme state. Tiizi's product-specific constitutional doctrine and prior Founder decisions remain project-owned. Historical records are not rewritten merely because FEF has now been adopted. This record does not claim universal or permanent "full FEF compliance."

**CGP-02C.13 programme-state synchronization gap:** Repository evidence shows CGP-02C.13 execution has begun (Phases 1, 2A, 2B, 2C and 2D/2E preparation artefacts exist, including the Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0), but the Master Programme (v1.21) still records CGP-02C.13 as "authorized to begin... not yet executed; consolidation has not begun; no whole-instrument draft exists." This is a genuine synchronization gap. Per the Founder's Step 2 instruction, the Master Programme was **not modified** because existing project governance does not clearly authorize the required synchronization. The Founder must authorize the Master Programme synchronization for CGP-02C.13 execution state.

**Remaining gaps:** (1) repository integrity — the authoritative governance baseline is not fully committed/pushed (no blanket commit/push authorized); (2) EOG-03 approval date — evidence gap retained pending Founder input.

The immediate next governed actions are: **Founder authorization of the CGP-02C.13 Master Programme synchronization**, **Founder confirmation of commit/push scope for classified change sets**, and **Founder provision of the EOG-03 approval date**.

- Stage E0 completes (CGP-02 approval and adoption) or a new major programme phase begins;
