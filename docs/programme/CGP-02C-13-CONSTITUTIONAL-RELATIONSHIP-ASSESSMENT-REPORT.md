## 1. Purpose

This report records observable constitutional relationships among the 302 propositions assembled in the Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0.

The assessment identifies relationships only. It makes no constitutional judgement, disposition, ranking, amendment recommendation, deletion recommendation, merge recommendation or wording recommendation.

## 2. Assessment Boundary

The permitted classifications are:

- exact duplicate propositions;
- partial overlaps;
- complementary propositions;
- dependency relationships;
- cross-reference relationships;
- apparent conflicts;
- structural repetition; and
- multi-document relationship chains.

An observation records only proposition identity, source identity, textual evidence and a neutral description of the relationship observed.

## 3. Assessment Method

The assessment used:

1. exact normalized-text comparison across all 302 propositions;
2. cross-source lexical-similarity screening;
3. inspection of explicit references to other constitutional treatments and records;
4. comparison of repeated constitutional structures, including scope, Authority, evidence and anti-inference boundaries;
5. tracing of proposition sequences across bounded source documents; and
6. validation of every cited identifier against the Proposition Source Crosswalk.

Similarity screening identified candidates for inspection only. Every recorded observation is supported by the proposition wording cited in the [Constitutional Observation Register](CGP-02C-13-CONSTITUTIONAL-OBSERVATION-REGISTER.md).

## 6. Exact Duplicate and Apparent Conflict Findings

The exact normalized-text comparison returned no duplicate proposition groups.

The relationship inspection returned no evidence-backed apparent conflict. This result records only that no assessed proposition pair supplied incompatible requirements on its face; it does not determine constitutional compatibility beyond the observed text.
