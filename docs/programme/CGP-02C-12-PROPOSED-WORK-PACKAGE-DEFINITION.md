# CGP-02C.12 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.12

**Proposed title:** Validation Rules

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Purpose

This document defines one proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package for Validation Rules. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only.

It creates no constitutional proposition, Founder constitutional question, doctrine, approval, adoption, constitutional effect or drafting authorization.

## 2. Sequence and Blueprint Mapping

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) identifies **Validation Rules** as deliverable 16, immediately after **Governance Index and Status Integrity** and before the final consolidation subjects in deliverables 17 through 20.

[PTRA-06](PTRA-06-FINAL-CONSTITUTIONAL-READINESS-ASSESSMENT.md) is accepted Stage E0 programme evidence. Its [Blueprint Coverage Matrix](PTRA-06-BLUEPRINT-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md), [Constitutional Coverage Matrix](PTRA-06-CONSTITUTIONAL-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md), [Dependency Integrity Report](PTRA-06-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRITY-REPORT.md) and [Founder Recommendation Report](PTRA-06-FOUNDER-RECOMMENDATION-REPORT.md) confirm:

- bounded treatment of the preceding fifteen substantive Blueprint subjects;
- Validation Rules as the only remaining substantive constitutional subject;
- completion of the C.10 and C.11 documentary prerequisite;
- consolidation as dependent on bounded completion of Validation Rules;
- no Blueprint amendment;
- no programme-state change; and
- no CGP-02C.12 or successor authorization.

Blueprint order and accepted PTRA-06 evidence permit planning only. They do not establish `CGP-02C.12` or authorize drafting.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The sole proposed constitutional subject is **Validation Rules**.

The proposed package would prepare constitutional treatment for the minimum validation integrity required when constitutional governance is reviewed or changed. It would preserve the distinction between constitutional validation requirements and operational, documentary, repository, engineering or cross-project validation mechanics.

It would remain Tiizi-specific, technology-neutral and bounded to constitutional consequence. It would not establish generic governance mechanics, engineering controls or Founder Engineering Framework requirements.

## 4. Proposed Purpose

The proposed drafting purpose is to prepare a constitutional boundary for evidence that supports an attributable conclusion that constitutional governance has received the validation required by its constitutional context.

This purpose identifies the planned inquiry. It does not define a validation rule, prescribe validation activity, determine evidence sufficiency or answer any constitutional uncertainty.

## 5. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to attributable Founder work-package authorization, later drafting would be limited to:

1. the constitutional meaning and purpose of validation;
2. the distinction between constitutional validation and governance review;
3. the distinction between validation evidence and approval, adoption or constitutional effect;
4. the constitutional minimum that validation evidence may need to establish;
5. the relationship between validation and constitutional consequence;
6. treatment of incomplete, failed, contradictory or unavailable constitutional validation evidence;
7. preservation of scope, dependency, Authority, traceability, status, history and non-effect boundaries during validation;
8. technology-neutral constitutional boundaries;
9. explicit anti-inference protections; and
10. explicit deferred governance.

These are planning topics only. They are not constitutional propositions, Founder constitutional questions or decisions.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not:

- draft constitutional propositions during planning;
- create Founder constitutional questions during planning;
- define document-validation procedures, checklists, commands or workflows;
- define engineering validation, technical quality assurance, testing, coding standards or software acceptance;
- define repository validation, link-checking, formatting, linting, hashing, file-status or version-control procedures;
- define implementation validation, operational assurance, release gates or deployment controls;
- define reusable governance mechanics or cross-project governance standards;
- define, amend or apply Founder Engineering Framework controls;
- create a generic validation framework for Founder projects;
- prescribe validators, reviewers, recorders, maintainers or decision-makers;
- allocate validation responsibility, Authority, accountability, stewardship, custody, administration or operation;
- create a Validation Authority or another Platform Authority;
- define evidence formats, schemas, templates, thresholds, scores, levels, tiers or technical identifiers;
- redefine Governance Review, review triggers, proportionality or independent review;
- redefine approval, adoption, constitutional effect or constitutional dates;
- redefine Amendment Traceability or Dependent-Governance Impact Review;
- redefine Conflict Review, Supersession, Retirement, Withdrawal, Rejection or Historical Preservation;
- redefine Governance Index and Status Integrity;
- validate any named governance instrument or determine that it is constitutionally valid;
- resolve any existing validation finding or documentary inconsistency;
- amend any dependency;
- modify the Master Programme or Consolidated Decision Register;
- create Founder Review material during planning;
- authorize drafting or a successor work package; or
- begin whole-instrument consolidation.

Generic governance or engineering controls identified during later work remain future implementation or external-framework matters outside this bounded constitutional package.

## 8. Constitutional Uncertainty Boundary

This planning package creates no Founder constitutional questions.

The included topics remain unresolved constitutional subject matter until a separately authorized Founder Review drafting package prepares explicit questions or proposed treatment. Programme-authorization decisions FWA-01 through FWA-05 concern only the proposed identifier, title, boundary, structure and permission to begin bounded drafting.

## 10. Proposed Completion Boundary

One bounded drafting package is proposed because Validation Rules is one Blueprint subject with a common constitutional-versus-operational validation boundary.

The future drafting milestone would be complete for technical Founder Review only when:

- all five authorized deliverables exist;
- every proposition is unique and fully traced;
- the package remains limited to Validation Rules;
- operational, repository, engineering, cross-project and Founder Engineering Framework controls remain excluded;
- constitutional uncertainty remains reserved for Founder Review;
- no Platform Authority or accountability relationship is created;
- relative-link, Markdown, whitespace, EOF and repository-scope checks pass; and
- no later work package or consolidation programme is authorized by completion.

Completion would not approve, adopt or make the proposed content constitutionally effective; complete CGP-02 or Stage E0; unblock CGP-03; or authorize consolidation or another Blueprint subject.

Completion of CGP-02C.12 concludes the final substantive constitutional Blueprint subject only. It does not authorize whole-instrument consolidation, Founder Approval, constitutional adoption, Stage E0 completion or transition to CGP-03. Each requires separate planning, authorization and attributable Founder decisions.
