## 1. Recommendation Summary

The next governed activity should be preparation of a planning and Founder authorization package for **Conflict Review and Escalation**, Blueprint deliverable 11.

No identifier, scope, decomposition or drafting authorization is established by this recommendation.

## 2. Why This Subject Is Next

Conflict Review and Escalation is next because:

1. it is the next ordered substantive deliverable in the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md);
2. CGP-02C.6 intentionally leaves unresolved dependent-governance impact for later conflict treatment;
3. GVR-07 and GVR-08 already supply principle-level precedence and escalation dependencies;
4. it should precede Supersession Rules so conflict treatment does not silently become replacement governance; and
5. its completion will remove a dependency for later status-integrity and final consolidation work.

Blueprint order supports the recommendation but does not itself authorize the work.

## 3. Recommended Planning Boundary

A future planning package should assess only:

- constitutional meaning of an apparent governance conflict;
- conflict identification boundaries;
- relationship between precedence analysis and escalation;
- Governance Authority interaction;
- minimum conflict-review and disposition evidence;
- unresolved-conflict treatment; and
- anti-inference boundaries preventing review or escalation from silently amending governance.

These are proposed planning topics, not constitutional propositions.

## 4. Recommended Explicit Exclusions

Planning should exclude:

- resolving a named or existing governance conflict;
- changing precedence;
- creating a Conflict Authority;
- allocating a reviewer, escalator, recorder or decision-maker;
- defining supersession;
- defining retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- amendment approval or adoption;
- cross-reference or index synchronization;
- validation doctrine;
- repository procedure or implementation; and
- authorizing any later Blueprint subject.

4. prepare a Founder Work Package Authorization Package;

This report performs none of those actions.
