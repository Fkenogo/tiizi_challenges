# CGP-02 Whole-Instrument Consolidation — Planning Package

**Programme:** Tiizi Version 2

**Stage:** Stage E0 — Governance Architecture

**Phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Planning phase:** Phase 1 — Planning Package

**Document type:** Programme-governance planning

**Status:** Planning complete for review; consolidation not authorized

**Planning date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Purpose

This package defines the programme boundary, inputs, dependencies, risks, deliverables and gates for a possible CGP-02 Whole-Instrument Consolidation Programme.

It plans integration of the completed bounded CGP-02 constitutional packages and Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20. It does not consolidate constitutional content, create a constitutional instrument, request or record Founder authorization, or change programme state.

## 2. Programme Position

The [Tiizi Version 2 Master Programme Version 1.20](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records:

- Stage E0 as In Progress;
- CGP-02 as In Progress;
- CGP-02C.12 as complete;
- Blueprint Deliverable 16 as complete;
- CGP-03 as blocked;
- whole-instrument consolidation as separately governed and unauthorized; and
- preparation of consolidation planning and Founder authorization evidence as the next programme activity.

This planning package performs only the planning portion of that activity.

## 3. Consolidation Subject

The planned subject is documentary and constitutional integration of completed CGP-02 bounded content into one reviewable whole-instrument candidate.

The planned integration layer comprises:

1. Blueprint Deliverable 17 — Deferred Constitutional Questions;
2. Blueprint Deliverable 18 — Governance Status and Decision Trace;
3. Blueprint Deliverable 19 — Founder Review Questions; and
4. Blueprint Deliverable 20 — Draft Self-Validation.

These are integration outputs. They are not four new constitutional subjects and must not be used to create doctrine absent from the completed bounded packages.

## 4. Objectives

A future authorized consolidation programme would:

- assemble the completed bounded constitutional content into one intelligible CGP-02 whole-instrument draft;
- preserve every proposition’s wording, identifier, order within its bounded subject and attributable status;
- preserve the distinction among Founder Review approval, Founder Approval Candidate status, Founder Approved status, adoption and constitutional effect;
- create an exact proposition-to-source and proposition-to-decision crosswalk;
- consolidate genuine deferred constitutional questions without reopening resolved decisions;
- identify only genuinely unresolved whole-instrument Founder questions;
- preserve historical and package-level traceability;
- verify that all 18 Blueprint discovery gaps and 8 carried conflicts have an attributable treatment or explicit deferral;
- disposition the 9 duplicate-rule clusters through explicit retention or meaning-preserving structural treatment without silently changing doctrine; and
- produce whole-draft self-validation suitable for later independent review and Founder review.

## 5. Included Scope

Planning covers only:

- the integration sequence for completed CGP-02 bounded packages;
- source-status preservation;
- proposition inventory and crosswalk design;
- exact-text parity controls;
- deferred-question consolidation;
- governance status and decision-trace assembly;
- whole-instrument Founder-review question preparation;
- whole-draft validation design;
- duplicate and overlap assessment;
- conflict and gap disposition verification;
- document architecture for a future whole-instrument draft;
- approval-stage and adoption-stage separation; and
- readiness evidence for later Founder authorization.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The planned programme must not:

- change constitutional wording;
- modify, add, delete, merge or renumber a proposition;
- resolve a deferred constitutional question;
- reopen or reinterpret an attributable Founder decision;
- normalize different package statuses;
- amend a completed bounded package;
- create constitutional doctrine;
- perform constitutional approval or adoption;
- create constitutional effect or a constitutional date;
- establish precedence among source packages by document order;
- correct historical metadata or a named source instrument;
- create a Platform Authority;
- allocate accountability, a role or permission;
- define implementation, repository, engineering or operational governance;
- import Founder Engineering Framework or cross-project governance;
- begin CGP-03;
- complete CGP-02 or Stage E0; or
- authorize a later review, approval, adoption or transition package.

Any constitutional wording change identified as necessary during future integration must be recorded as a separate issue and withheld from the consolidation output until governed amendment is separately authorized.

## 9. Planning Assumptions

This plan assumes:

- CGP-02C.12 bounded completion remains valid;
- no protected bounded package changes before consolidation authorization;
- recorded package statuses remain intentionally non-uniform;
- the whole-instrument draft will be a new candidate and not a replacement for historical bounded records;
- proposition identifiers remain the primary cross-package trace keys;
- structural organization may be proposed, but constitutional wording cannot be edited;
- repeated doctrine may be retained where exact consolidation would otherwise alter subject-specific meaning; and
- whole-instrument Founder review, approval, adoption and programme transition remain later, separately governed stages.

An assumption that fails must remain explicit and be addressed through planning amendment or a separately authorized governance action.

## 10. Risks and Controls

| Risk | Planned control |
|---|---|
| Silent wording drift | Exact proposition extraction, hash comparison and 100% text-parity validation |
| Proposition loss or duplication | Complete identifier inventory and one-to-one crosswalk |
| Status normalization | Source-status register preserved beside every included package |
| Resolved decision reopened | Decision trace distinguishes resolved, deferred and genuinely unresolved matters |
| Consolidation treated as approval | Candidate metadata and non-effects explicitly preserve review, approval, adoption and effect boundaries |
| Structural order implies precedence | Explicit anti-inference statement and source-attribution crosswalk |
| Repeated doctrine removed incorrectly | Duplicate-rule register requires explicit retain-or-refer treatment without proposition deletion |
| Deferred matter resolved by editing | Deferred-question register permits identification only |
| Historical records overwritten | New consolidation artefacts created separately; bounded packages remain unchanged |
| Integration expands into FEF or implementation | Explicit exclusion and source-scope validation |
| Premature programme advancement | Master Programme remains unchanged until attributable authorization or later completion synchronization |

- every completed bounded proposition appears exactly once in the constitutional proposition inventory;
- proposition wording, identifiers and attributable decisions achieve 100% parity with protected sources;
- every included package retains its recorded status;
- every Blueprint deliverable 17–20 output is present;
- every genuine deferral is distinguishable from a resolved decision;
- no resolved question is presented as unresolved;
- all Blueprint gaps, conflicts and duplicate clusters have an explicit traceable disposition;
- no constitutional doctrine is introduced through headings, transitions, summaries or document ordering;
- all historical bounded artefacts remain unchanged;
- all required relative-link, Markdown, whitespace, EOF and repository-scope checks pass; and
- the resulting draft is ready for later independent review and Founder review without claiming approval, adoption or effect.
