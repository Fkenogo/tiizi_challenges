## 1. Purpose

This trace shows, for every included constitutional subject in the Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0, where its constitutional status comes from, what decision evidence established it, whether adoption and effect are separately attributable, and what consolidation changed.

It preserves the distinction between source content, approval decision, adoption, constitutional effect, amendment, consolidation/assembly, review, Founder observation disposition, and whole-instrument approval. No state is inferred from another.

## 2. Immutable Boundaries

- Assembly does **not** equal approval.
- Founder disposition of CRA observations does **not** equal approval of V0.
- Approval does **not** equal adoption.
- Adoption and effect must be separately attributable.
- Repository presence does **not** create status.
- Current representation must never manufacture missing status.
