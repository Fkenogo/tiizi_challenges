## 3. Executive Assessment

CGP-02 is aligned with the frozen Blueprint through deliverable 10, Dependent-Governance Impact Review. Its completed packages preserve single-subject boundaries, Authority discipline, explicit deferrals and consistent separation among review, approval, adoption, amendment trace and dependent-impact evidence.

No contradiction or boundary drift blocks further planning. Several deliberate overlaps exist because later packages depend on earlier principles, but no completed package silently redefines another. The principal integration risk is status asymmetry: the completed materials include unapproved candidates, Founder-approved candidates and one Founder-approved instrument, while none is adopted as the complete CGP-02 standard. That distinction is consistently recorded and must remain explicit through later consolidation.

The programme is **Ready with prerequisites** to prepare a Founder Authorization Package for the next Blueprint subject. It is not authorized to draft that subject.

## 6. Governance Methodology Evidence

Execution repeatedly used:

- discovery before drafting;
- frozen bounded planning;
- attributable Founder work-package authorization;
- one constitutional subject per drafting package;
- proposition-level traceability;
- decision packs that separate recommendations from decisions;
- preservation hashes and proposition-parity checks;
- separate Founder Decision Records;
- explicit candidate, approval, adoption and effect statuses;
- completion and milestone-validation reports; and
- programme and decision-register synchronization after bounded completion.

These are recurring programme practices and evidence patterns. They do not become constitutional doctrine or reusable programme governance merely because they recur.

The practices may later be evaluated for reusable programme methodology after CGP-02 is sufficiently complete. Any standardization requires separate scope, review and Founder authorization. The previously recorded retrospective remains evidence only.

- the Blueprint places conflict treatment immediately after dependent-governance impact review; and

No identifier is established by this recommendation. No planning package, Founder Authorization Record or drafting authorization is created.
