# CGP-02C.8 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.8

**Proposed title:** Supersession Rules

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package following CGP-02C.7. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only, creates no constitutional proposition and authorizes no drafting.

## 2. Sequence and Blueprint Mapping

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) identifies **Supersession Rules** as deliverable 12, immediately after **Conflict Review and Escalation**.

[PTRA-02](PTRA-02-STAGE-E0-MID-PROGRAMME-TRANSITION-ASSESSMENT.md) has been accepted as official Stage E0 programme evidence. It confirms bounded Blueprint alignment through deliverable 11, finds no contradiction or dependency break and recommends Supersession Rules as the next planning subject. [Master Programme Version 1.13](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records that no successor work package is authorized.

Therefore:

- the next proposed constitutional subject is **Supersession Rules**;
- the exact proposed identifier is `CGP-02C.8`;
- Blueprint order and accepted PTRA-02 evidence do not authorize the package; and
- identifier, title, scope, structure and drafting authorization remain subject to attributable Founder decision.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The proposed package would prepare bounded constitutional treatment for supersession without superseding an existing governance instrument or determining the status of any historical instrument.

It would address one constitutional subject only: Supersession Rules. It would not define retirement, withdrawal or rejection, perform governance-index synchronization, allocate an actor or create a Supersession Authority.

## 4. Proposed Purpose

The proposed drafting purpose is to make the constitutional meaning, attributable basis, bounded scope, evidence and historical-integrity boundaries of supersession available for Founder review while preserving existing Governance Authority and the distinctions established by earlier CGP-02 packages.

This purpose identifies the planned inquiry. It does not answer a constitutional question.

## 5. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02C.8 would be limited to:

1. the constitutional meaning and purpose of supersession;
2. the distinction between supersession and amendment;
3. the distinction between supersession and adoption;
4. the distinction between supersession and conflict disposition;
5. the distinction between supersession and retirement, withdrawal and rejection;
6. the interaction between supersession and existing Governance Authority;
7. the requirement that supersession be attributable rather than inferred;
8. the bounded constitutional scope of supersession;
9. the historical-preservation implications of supersession;
10. the minimum categories of supersession evidence;
11. anti-inference boundaries preventing records, labels, indexes, repository acts or implementation from silently superseding governance; and
12. explicit identification of questions deferred to later Blueprint subjects.

These subjects identify questions for later constitutional drafting. They do not define constitutional answers.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not draft, determine or perform:

- supersession of an actual, named, current or historical governance instrument;
- a determination that an existing instrument is or is not superseded;
- resolution or disposition of an actual governance conflict;
- a precedence outcome for any actual conflict;
- amendment approval;
- adoption, constitutional effect, effective date or application date;
- retirement;
- withdrawal;
- rejection;
- validation doctrine or validation procedure;
- governance-index, status or cross-reference synchronization;
- designation of an authoritative repository location or current-document index;
- historical storage, retention, archival or versioning mechanisms;
- temporary or emergency governance;
- implementation workflow;
- repository procedure;
- reviewer, recorder, decision-maker, publisher or maintainer roles;
- a Supersession Authority or any other Platform Authority;
- accountability, responsibility, role or permission allocation;
- technical schema, storage, identifier format, automation, publication or deployment;
- retrospective status correction or inferred historical supersession; or
- authorization of a successor work package.

## 8. Reserved Constitutional Questions

If drafting is later authorized, the Founder Review package must present without presuming answers:

1. What is the constitutional meaning and purpose of supersession?
2. How must supersession remain distinct from amendment?
3. How must supersession remain distinct from adoption?
4. How must supersession remain distinct from conflict disposition?
5. How must supersession remain distinct from retirement, withdrawal and rejection?
6. What relationship should exist between attributable supersession and existing Governance Authority without creating another Authority?
7. How must the constitutional scope of supersession be identified and bounded?
8. What historical-preservation implications and minimum evidence must an attributable supersession preserve?
9. What anti-inference and deferred-governance boundaries prevent records, labels, indexes, repository actions or implementation from silently superseding governance?

These are future constitutional questions, not propositions or decisions made by this definition.

## 10. Proposed Completion Boundary

The recommendation is one bounded drafting package because meaning, distinctions, Authority interaction, scope, evidence, history and anti-inference form one Blueprint subject.

The package would be complete for technical Founder Review only when all five expected deliverables exist, every proposition is unique and traceable, every reserved question remains pending, all exclusions remain preserved and mechanical validation passes.

Completion would not supersede governance, approve a proposition, adopt governance, create constitutional effect, complete CGP-02 or Stage E0, unblock CGP-03 or authorize a successor package.
