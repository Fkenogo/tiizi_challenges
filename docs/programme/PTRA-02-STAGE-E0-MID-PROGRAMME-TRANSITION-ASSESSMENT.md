## 7. Architectural Cohesion

The emerging architecture remains cohesive:

1. constitutional change is identified by effect rather than label;
2. lifecycle and classification provide conceptual boundaries;
3. review examines proposed or existing governance without exercising Authority;
4. approval and adoption remain distinct attributable constitutional acts;
5. traceability and impact records preserve different evidence;
6. conflict review identifies and escalates incompatibility without deciding it; and
7. supersession remains the next separate subject for explicit replacement treatment.

This sequence prevents documentary status, conflict disposition or repository action from silently replacing approved governance.
