## 1. Purpose

This assessment determines the remaining work required to complete CGP-02 after bounded closure of CGP-02C.9. It compares the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md) with completed packages, Founder decisions, approval candidates, approved instruments, completion evidence, the [Master Programme](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) and the [Consolidated Decision Register](../reports/platform-foundation-decisions/10-CONSOLIDATED-DECISION-REGISTER.md).

It creates no constitutional proposition, Founder question, decision, work-package authorization, programme-state change, adoption or constitutional effect.

## 2. Executive Assessment

CGP-02 has bounded constitutional treatment through Blueprint deliverable 13. The completed work comprises 11 bounded drafting packages covering deliverables 1–13, 230 propositions and 79 recorded package-level Founder questions or dispositions.

CGP-02 is not complete. Three substantive Blueprint subjects remain:

1. Historical Preservation;
2. Governance Index and Status Integrity; and
3. Validation Rules.

Four cross-cutting Blueprint deliverables also remain incomplete at whole-instrument level:

- Deferred Constitutional Questions;
- Governance Status and Decision Trace;
- Founder Review Questions; and
- Draft Self-Validation.

These four are integration outputs rather than evidence of four additional standalone constitutional subjects.

## 3. Readiness Verdict

**Ready with a documentary prerequisite for planning only; not ready for further drafting authorization.**

The substantive sequence remains coherent and the next Blueprint subject is identifiable as Historical Preservation. Before any further drafting authorization, the CGP-02C.9 programme-authorization evidence must be reconciled: its Founder Authorization Record and Authorization Validation Report still state that FWA-01 through FWA-05 are unresolved and drafting is unauthorized, while the Master Programme and CGP-02C.9 completion evidence record bounded authorization and completion.

This is a documentary status inconsistency. It does not invalidate the Founder-approved RWR propositions or authorize this assessment to correct the records.

## 6. Constitutional and Architectural Coherence

No contradiction was identified among CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.9.

The following repeated controls remain coherent:

- Governance Authority is preserved without a subject-specific Authority;
- approval, adoption, constitutional effect and status representation remain distinct;
- evidence communicates but does not create constitutional status;
- attribution remains specific to the constitutional act or record concerned;
- anti-inference protections prevent labels, indexes, repository actions and implementation from creating governance;
- historical intelligibility is preserved while Historical Preservation doctrine remains deferred; and
- no recorder, reviewer, custodian, administrator or other accountable relationship is allocated.

The detailed [Governance Gap Analysis](PTRA-04-GAP-ANALYSIS.md) records overlap and consolidation risks without treating intentional dependency reuse as a defect.

## 7. Dependency Position

The dependency chain supports the remaining order:

```text
Completed status and change treatments
        ↓
Historical Preservation
        ↓
Governance Index and Status Integrity
        ↓
Validation Rules
        ↓
Whole-instrument integration
        ↓
Founder Review and approval
        ↓
Separate adoption and transition
```

Historical Preservation requires the completed lifecycle, traceability, conflict, supersession and status-ending treatments. Governance Index and Status Integrity then depends on both constitutional status treatment and preservation. Validation Rules should follow the substantive subjects so its constitutional minimum can be assessed against the complete standard.
