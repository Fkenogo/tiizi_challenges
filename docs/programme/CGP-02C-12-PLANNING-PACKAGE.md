## 4. Proposed Constitutional Scope

The future package would be bounded to the constitutional meaning, minimum integrity and anti-inference boundaries of validation for constitutional governance.

It would plan treatment for:

- constitutional validation as distinct from review, approval, adoption and constitutional effect;
- the constitutional purpose and minimum evidentiary concern of validation;
- proportionality to constitutional consequence without prescribing mechanisms;
- incomplete, failed, contradictory or unavailable constitutional validation evidence;
- preservation of scope, dependency, Authority, traceability, status, history and non-effect boundaries;
- technology neutrality;
- anti-inference; and
- explicit deferral.

This section identifies scope only. It defines no constitutional treatment or sufficiency test.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

| Validation is treated as approval, adoption or constitutional effect | Preserve C.3 and C.4 distinctions and explicit anti-inference boundaries. |
| Validation becomes a repository checklist | Exclude commands, files, formats, tools and workflows. |
| Engineering QA enters constitutional governance | Exclude testing, coding, release, deployment and technical assurance. |

| Failed validation silently changes status | Preserve attribution and separate evidence from approval, adoption, effect and status representation. |
| C.12 completion is treated as consolidation authorization | State that consolidation requires a later transition assessment, plan and attributable authorization. |
