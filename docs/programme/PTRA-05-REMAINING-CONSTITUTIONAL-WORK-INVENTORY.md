## 1. Purpose

## 2. Remaining Constitutional Subject 1 — Governance Index and Status Integrity

**Blueprint deliverable:** 15

**Classification:** Still required.

### Existing partial evidence

Completed packages establish that:

- an index, register, label, status field, cross-reference, repository action or presentation does not exercise Governance Authority;
- documentary representation cannot create approval, adoption, constitutional effect, supersession, retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- current governance must arise from attributable constitutional treatment;
- historical context must remain intelligible; and
- impact identification does not itself synchronize dependent documents.

These boundaries do not yet define Governance Index and Status Integrity.

### Remaining bounded inquiry

A later planning package must determine the appropriate bounded scope for:

- the constitutional purpose of governance indexes and status representations;
- integrity between attributable constitutional status and documentary representation;
- identification of a current constitutional baseline without making an index constitutive;
- preservation of historical references and status distinctions;
- treatment of incomplete, stale or inconsistent representations without silently changing governance;
- relationships among indexes, registers, manifests and other status representations; and
- explicit separation from repository implementation, operational synchronization and allocation of maintenance responsibility.

This inventory does not answer those questions.

## 3. Remaining Constitutional Subject 2 — Validation Rules

**Blueprint deliverable:** 16

**Classification:** Still required.

### Existing partial evidence

Every bounded package contains self-validation or milestone validation. These records demonstrate recurring programme practice and package-specific assurance. Repetition does not establish constitutional doctrine.

Completed packages also preserve:

- objective reviewability;
- proposition traceability;
- dependency and scope integrity;
- Authority and non-effect checks;
- historical intelligibility; and
- separation of review, recording, approval, adoption and constitutional effect.

### Remaining bounded inquiry

A later planning package must determine the appropriate bounded scope for:

- the constitutional purpose of validation;
- minimum constitutional assurance before later governance treatment;
- identity, scope, Authority, attribution, dependency, traceability, status and non-effect integrity;
- treatment of unresolved validation findings;
- the relationship between validation and review;
- the relationship between validation and approval;
- the boundary between constitutional validation and operational or repository checks; and
- anti-inference protections preventing validation from becoming approval, adoption or constitutional effect.

This inventory does not answer those questions.

### Dependencies

All substantive CGP-02 subjects, including completed Governance Index and Status Integrity treatment.

### Readiness

Not ready to precede deliverable 15. It becomes ready for planning only after the Governance Index and Status Integrity bounded milestone is complete and a governed transition confirms dependency integrity.

## 5. Matters That Are Not Remaining Constitutional Subjects

No additional bounded constitutional subject is required for:

- temporary or emergency constitutional governance, because FQ-05 preserved no special pathway;
- a universal periodic-review schedule, because RTP-06 requires independent constitutional justification and creates no schedule;
- independent-review thresholds, reviewer qualifications or review arrangements, which remain deferred rather than silently required;
- approval, adoption, effect, amendment traceability, dependent-impact review, conflict review, supersession, retirement, withdrawal, rejection or Historical Preservation;
- repository workflow, document formats, technical identifiers or implementation;
- creation of a Platform Authority; or
- allocation of accountability, recorder, reviewer, custodian, administrator or operator relationships.
