# PTRA-06 — Founder Recommendation Report

**Programme:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme recommendation evidence

**Status:** Recommendation only; no Founder decision or authorization

**Recommendation date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Recommendation

The recommended next sequence is:

1. perform status-only documentary synchronization of the CGP-02C.10 and CGP-02C.11 Founder Authorization Records and Authorization Validation Reports;
2. prepare, but do not presume approval of, a planning and Founder authorization package for proposed **CGP-02C.12 — Validation Rules**;
3. if separately authorized, complete Validation Rules as one bounded constitutional subject;
4. conduct a post-C.12 transition and whole-instrument integration-readiness assessment; and
5. if separately authorized, proceed to one consolidation programme covering Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20.

## 2. Why CGP-02C.12 Is Required

Validation Rules remains:

- frozen Blueprint deliverable 16;
- the constitutional subject mapped to open GAP-16;
- the only substantive deliverable without bounded treatment; and
- a prerequisite to integrated draft self-validation.

Package-level validation reports cannot be treated as doctrine merely because they recur. A separately authorized constitutional work package is required to determine the constitutional validation minimum and its operational boundary.

The identifier `CGP-02C.12` and title **Validation Rules** are recommendations only until separately approved through the Programme Authorization Framework.

## 3. Why No Other Bounded Subject Is Recommended

The assessment found no additional substantive constitutional gap beyond Validation Rules.

Governance Index and Status Integrity has bounded treatment through GSI-01 through GSI-24. Historical Preservation, status treatments, conflict, impact review, traceability, approval, adoption, lifecycle, classification and review proportionality are already bounded.

The C.10 and C.11 authorization-status inconsistencies are documentary maintenance matters. They do not justify a constitutional package.

## 4. Why Direct Consolidation Is Not Recommended

Direct consolidation would:

- omit Blueprint deliverable 16;
- leave GAP-16 open;
- require the consolidation team to infer validation doctrine from repeated practice;
- undermine the Blueprint rule that evidence precedes interpretation; and
- prevent complete Draft Self-Validation.

Consolidation should integrate completed doctrine, not create the missing constitutional minimum.

## 5. Proposed C.12 Planning Boundary

A future planning package may examine only:

- constitutional purpose of validation;
- minimum constitutional assurance;
- identity, scope, Authority, attribution, dependency, traceability, status and non-effect integrity;
- unresolved validation findings;
- validation versus review;
- validation versus approval;
- constitutional versus operational validation; and
- anti-inference protections.

It should explicitly exclude:

- technical test tools and commands;
- repository workflow;
- file formats and schemas;
- reviewer or validator allocation;
- validation scores, tiers and thresholds unless separately justified and approved;
- approval, adoption or constitutional effect;
- correction of named instruments;
- actual validation of existing governance; and
- successor authorization.

These are recommended planning boundaries only. They are not constitutional answers or authorization.
