# CGP-02 Whole-Instrument Consolidation — Executive Summary

**Document type:** Programme planning summary

**Status:** Planning evidence only; consolidation not authorized

**Date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Programme Position

CGP-02C.12 and Blueprint Deliverable 16 are complete. Stage E0 and CGP-02 remain In Progress, CGP-03 remains blocked and whole-instrument consolidation remains unauthorized under [Master Programme Version 1.20](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md).

## 2. Planning Result

The [Planning Package](CGP-02-WHOLE-INSTRUMENT-CONSOLIDATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) defines one integration programme covering Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20:

- Deferred Constitutional Questions;
- Governance Status and Decision Trace;
- Founder Review Questions; and
- Draft Self-Validation.

The programme would assemble unchanged bounded constitutional propositions into a traceable whole-instrument Founder Review Draft. It would not create a new constitutional subject.

## 3. Principal Controls

Any future consolidation must:

- preserve exact proposition wording and identifiers;
- preserve every package’s attributable status;
- preserve all Founder decisions and genuine deferrals;
- prevent document order from implying precedence;
- retain historical bounded artefacts;
- introduce no Authority or accountability allocation;
- create no approval, adoption or constitutional effect; and
- stop if a constitutional wording change is required.

## 4. Authorization Position

Planning does not authorize consolidation.

Before work may begin, the programme requires:

1. package-specific dependency verification;
2. a decision-ready Founder authorization package;
3. attributable Founder authorization;
4. a completed authorization validation checklist; and
5. Master Programme synchronization.

## 5. Recommendation

Proceed next to a bounded **CGP-02 Whole-Instrument Consolidation Dependency Verification and Founder Authorization Package**.

Do not create a consolidation draft until that package is authorized and all entry gates pass.
