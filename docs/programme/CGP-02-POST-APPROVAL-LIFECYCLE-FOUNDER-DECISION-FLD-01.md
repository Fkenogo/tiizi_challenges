## 1. Purpose

This record documents the Founder's attributable determination of the CGP-02 post-approval lifecycle, resolving DQ-06 and DQ-07, establishing constitutional effect, and determining the CGP-02 completion criterion.

This is a decision record, not a decision package. It records a decision already made. It does not create a new governance framework.

---

## 2. Authority and Context

FAD-01 (2026-08-31) approved the CGP-02 whole standard (302 propositions, 302 unique proposition IDs) as Option A — Approve. FAD-01 expressly did not adopt, apply, or create constitutional effect. DQ-06 (adoption requirement) and DQ-07 (application requirement) were preserved as unresolved.

D-08 (2026-09-01) recorded CGP-02D as COMPLETE and classified the post-approval lifecycle as requiring Founder authorization (Classification B). The Founder accepted and closed D-08.

FLD-01 is the authorized Founder determination of that lifecycle.

---

## 3. Founder Decisions

### 3.1 DQ-06 — Adoption

**No separate adoption act is required for CGP-02.**

FAD-01 remains an Approval decision and MUST NOT be retrospectively renamed or reinterpreted as an adoption decision.

**DQ-06 is RESOLVED.**

### 3.2 DQ-07 — Application

**No separate application act or application record is required for CGP-02.**

Once the approved standard has constitutional effect, it governs subsequent Tiizi governance within its defined scope. Application occurs through compliance with the standard when relevant governance work is performed; it is not a separate lifecycle ceremony.

**DQ-07 is RESOLVED.**

### 3.3 Constitutional Effect

**CGP-02 acquires constitutional effect upon the attributable recording of FLD-01.**

Constitutional effect is NOT backdated to FAD-01. FAD-01 remains dated 2026-08-31 and remains exactly the Founder Approval decision it was.

FLD-01, dated 2026-09-01, establishes the post-approval lifecycle determination and constitutional effect.

### 3.4 CGP-02 Completion

**Upon attributable recording of FLD-01, CGP-02 becomes COMPLETE.**

The required lifecycle for this instrument is:

```
Development → Founder Review → Validation → Founder Approval → Constitutional Effect / Complete
```

There is NO mandatory separate Adoption stage.
There is NO mandatory separate Application stage.

### 3.5 Adoption Record

The historical programme Adoption Record checklist item is **NOT REQUIRED — DQ-06 resolved by FLD-01.**

It is retired/reclassified. It is NOT checked as though an adoption occurred. Traceability to this determination is preserved in this record and in the Master Programme v1.40 change log.
