# CGP-02C.5 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.5

**Proposed title:** Amendment Traceability Requirements

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the next proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package remaining within CGP-02. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only. It creates no constitutional proposition and authorizes no drafting.

## 2. Sequence Determination

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) lists **Amendment Traceability Requirements** immediately after **Adoption and Constitutional Effect**.

[CGP-02C.4 — Adoption and Constitutional Effect](../governance/principles/15-CGP-02C-4-ADOPTION-AND-CONSTITUTIONAL-EFFECT-FOUNDER-APPROVED.md) has completed bounded Founder approval closure. The [Master Programme](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records that no successor drafting is authorized and that the next action is preparation of separate Founder authorization.

Therefore:

- the next planned constitutional subject is **Amendment Traceability Requirements**;
- the exact proposed identifier is `CGP-02C.5`;
- neither sequence nor this definition authorizes the package; and
- the identifier, title, boundary and drafting authorization remain subject to attributable Founder decision.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The proposed package would prepare constitutional treatment for the trace that keeps a constitutional amendment attributable, bounded and historically intelligible.

The package would address one subject only: the constitutional requirements of an amendment trace. It would not perform an amendment, approve an amendment, adopt amended content, establish constitutional effect, prescribe a preservation mechanism or conduct dependent-governance impact review.

## 4. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02C.5 would be limited to:

1. the constitutional purpose and meaning of an amendment trace;
2. the constitutional relationship among the source governance, proposed change, attributable amendment decision and resulting constitutional treatment;
3. the minimum categories of information that an amendment trace may be required to preserve;
4. the boundary between an amendment trace and an approval record;
5. the boundary between amendment traceability and adoption, constitutional effect or status representation;
6. the treatment of affected, unaffected, pending, deferred, rejected and withdrawn matters within an amendment trace;
7. the attribution and scope boundaries preventing an amendment trace from communicating a broader change than its source decisions support;
8. the relationship between an amendment trace reference and an existing approval reference, without defining a technical identifier format;
9. the minimum historical-intelligibility boundary necessary to understand prior and amended constitutional meaning, without defining historical-preservation mechanisms; and
10. explicit identification of questions that remain deferred.

These subjects identify questions for later constitutional drafting. They do not answer those questions.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not draft or determine:

- an amendment to any existing governance instrument;
- approval, adoption or constitutional effect for any existing candidate or instrument;
- lifecycle doctrine already treated by CGP-02C.2A;
- amendment classification already treated by CGP-02C.2B;
- review triggers or proportionality already treated by CGP-02C.2C;
- approval governance already treated by CGP-02C.3;
- adoption or constitutional effect already treated by CGP-02C.4;
- dependent-governance impact review;
- cross-reference review requirements;
- conflict review or escalation;
- supersession;
- retirement, withdrawal or rejection doctrine;
- historical-preservation mechanisms;
- governance-index or status synchronization;
- validation doctrine;
- complete governance status or decision-trace doctrine;
- amendment procedure or workflow;
- who authors, records, maintains, validates or approves an amendment trace;
- any Authority, accountability, role, permission or responsibility allocation;
- technical versioning, schema, storage, retention, identifier format or repository process;
- implementation, automation, publication or deployment; or
- retrospective assignment of amendment status, approval, adoption or constitutional effect to historical records.

## 6. Reserved Constitutional Questions

If drafting is later authorized, the Founder Review package must present without presuming answers:

1. What is the constitutional purpose and meaning of an amendment trace?
2. What minimum information must every constitutional amendment trace preserve?
3. How must an amendment trace remain distinct from an approval record, adoption record or status representation?
4. What relationship among prior meaning, changed meaning, amendment scope and the attributable decision must remain intelligible?
5. How should affected, unaffected, pending, deferred, rejected and withdrawn matters be represented without changing their status?
6. Is a stable unique amendment-trace reference constitutionally required, and how should it relate to an approval reference without prescribing a technical format?
7. Which approval, adoption, effective or application dates, if any, belong in an amendment trace without being created by the trace?
8. What minimum boundary prevents a trace, label, record or repository action from creating or enlarging an amendment by implication?
9. Which matters must remain deferred to Dependent-Governance Impact Review, Historical Preservation, Governance Index and Status Integrity, Validation Rules or other later CGP-02 subjects?

These are future constitutional questions, not propositions or decisions made by this definition.

## 7. Proposed Deliverables

If and only if drafting is authorized and the Master Programme is synchronized, the bounded drafting package would produce:

1. Amendment Traceability Requirements Founder Review Draft;
2. Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation;
3. Founder Review Decision Pack;
4. Drafting Notes; and
5. Bounded Change and Scope Report.

No Founder Decision Record, Founder Approval Candidate, approved instrument, amendment record or adoption record is authorized at this planning stage.

## 8. Proposed Work-Package Boundary

The recommendation is one bounded drafting package because the constitutional purpose, minimum information questions and anti-inference boundaries of an amendment trace form one subject in the frozen Blueprint.

Dependent-governance impact review is the next distinct Blueprint subject and must not be absorbed into this package.

Any decomposition, expansion or change of constitutional subject requires attributable Founder authorization before drafting begins.

## 9. Definition Status

`CGP-02C.5` and **Amendment Traceability Requirements** remain proposed until the Founder records an authorization decision, the package-specific validation checklist passes and the Master Programme is synchronized. This document supplies no authorization by sequence, title, readiness or repository presence.
