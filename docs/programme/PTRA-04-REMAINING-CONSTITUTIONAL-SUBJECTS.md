# PTRA-04 — Remaining Constitutional Subjects

**Programme:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme assessment evidence

**Status:** Subject assessment only; no planning or authorization

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Assessment Boundary

This report identifies the genuinely remaining bounded constitutional subjects in the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md). It does not define their constitutional answers, create Founder questions or authorize planning or drafting.

## 2. Remaining Subject 1 — Historical Preservation

**Blueprint deliverable:** 14

**Current coverage:** Partial.

Completed packages already require historical intelligibility, attributable context and preservation of prior meaning. They deliberately exclude storage, retention, archival, versioning and repository procedure.

**Remaining bounded subject:**

- the constitutional meaning and purpose of Historical Preservation;
- the relationship between historically applicable governance and current governance;
- the minimum constitutional integrity needed to keep prior governance, decisions, status treatments and context intelligible;
- boundaries preventing preservation records or representations from creating, changing or erasing constitutional status; and
- technology-neutral separation from storage, archival and implementation mechanisms.

**Principal dependencies:** CGP-02C.1, C.2A–B, C.3–C.9, especially amendment traceability, dependent impacts, conflict evidence, supersession and retirement/withdrawal/rejection.

**Readiness:** Next in Blueprint order, but no planning or drafting is authorized. Documentary reconciliation of the CGP-02C.9 authorization evidence is a programme prerequisite before any further drafting authorization.

## 3. Remaining Subject 2 — Governance Index and Status Integrity

**Blueprint deliverable:** 15

**Current coverage:** Not started as a dedicated subject.

Completed packages establish that labels, indexes, registers, cross-references, publication and repository actions do not exercise Governance Authority or create constitutional status. They do not define the constitutional integrity expected of current-status representations or identify the authoritative index for each governance-document class.

**Remaining bounded subject:**

- the constitutional purpose and boundary of governance indexes and status representations;
- integrity between attributable constitutional status and its documentary representation;
- current-baseline identification without treating an index as the constitutive act;
- preservation of historical references and status distinctions; and
- treatment of inconsistent, incomplete or stale status representations without silently changing governance.

**Principal dependencies:** Approval Governance, Adoption and Constitutional Effect, Amendment Traceability, Dependent-Governance Impact Review, Supersession, Retirement/Withdrawal/Rejection and Historical Preservation.

**Readiness:** Not ready for drafting before Historical Preservation.

## 4. Remaining Subject 3 — Validation Rules

**Blueprint deliverable:** 16

**Current coverage:** Not started as constitutional doctrine.

Every package contains validation evidence, but those checks remain bounded programme practice. Repetition does not establish a constitutional minimum.

**Remaining bounded subject:**

- the constitutional purpose of validation for constitutional change;
- the minimum assurance questions required before an instrument may proceed through later governance;
- validation of identity, scope, Authority, traceability, dependencies, status and explicit non-effects;
- the boundary between constitutional validation requirements and operational or repository checks; and
- anti-inference protection preventing validation from becoming approval, adoption or constitutional effect.

**Principal dependencies:** All substantive CGP-02 subjects, including Historical Preservation and Governance Index and Status Integrity.

**Readiness:** Not ready for drafting before deliverables 14 and 15.

## 5. Cross-Cutting Integration Work

The following Blueprint deliverables remain, but they should not be treated as four new standalone constitutional subjects:

| Deliverable | Required integration treatment |
|---|---|
| 17 — Deferred Constitutional Questions | Consolidate genuine deferrals; do not reopen resolved package decisions. |
| 18 — Governance Status and Decision Trace | Reconcile source statuses, decision records and whole-instrument trace without upgrading any source by inference. |
| 19 — Founder Review Questions | Present only genuinely unresolved whole-instrument decisions after consolidation. |
| 20 — Draft Self-Validation | Validate the integrated draft against all Blueprint completion criteria. |

These outputs depend on completion of deliverables 14–16 and a separately governed integration activity.

## 6. Subjects Not Remaining

The following do not require an additional bounded constitutional subject:

- temporary or emergency constitutional governance, because the Founder disposition preserved no special pathway;
- periodic-review schedules, reviewer allocation or review workflow, because these remain outside the completed principle-level treatment;
- approval, adoption, effect, supersession, retirement, withdrawal or rejection, because bounded treatment already exists;
- repository workflow, implementation, file formats or tooling;
- Platform Authority creation; and
- accountability, recorder, reviewer, custodian or administrator allocation.

## 7. Subject Verdict

Exactly three substantive constitutional subjects remain under the frozen Blueprint. Their order is:

1. Historical Preservation;
2. Governance Index and Status Integrity;
3. Validation Rules.

This finding identifies sequence only. It establishes no identifier, scope approval, Founder question or drafting authorization.
