# Work Package Authorization Lifecycle

**Document type:** Programme-governance lifecycle

**Programme:** Tiizi Version 2

**Applicability:** Every bounded Tiizi Version 2 work package

**Status:** Founder-approved programme governance; integrated through Master Programme Version 1.5

**Date:** 2026-07-23

## 1. Purpose

This lifecycle defines the programme sequence by which a proposed work package may become authorized to begin. It operationalizes the [Founder Work Package Authorization Standard](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-STANDARD.md) without authorizing any package.

The lifecycle is programme governance only. It creates no constitutional lifecycle, Platform Authority, accountability allocation, role, permission or implementation workflow.

## 2. Lifecycle Overview

```text
Proposed
   ↓
Planning
   ↓
Planning Validated
   ↓
Founder Decision Pending
   ↓
Founder Decision Recorded
   ↓
Programme Synchronization
   ↓
Authorized to Begin
   ↓
Work Package In Progress
   ↓
Work Package Completion Recorded
```

A rejected, deferred, amended or blocked decision leaves the package outside **Authorized to Begin** until the applicable condition is resolved through an attributable programme decision.

## 3. Lifecycle States

| State | Meaning | Required evidence | Work permitted? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposed | A possible package has been identified. | Proposed identifier, title and subject. | Planning only. |
| Planning | Scope, dependencies, deliverables, exclusions and gates are being prepared. | Planning artefacts in progress. | Planning only. |
| Planning Validated | The plan is decision-ready and its boundaries pass validation. | Completed planning package and planning validation. | Authorization preparation only. |
| Founder Decision Pending | An attributable Founder decision has been requested. | Decision-ready authorization template and decision pack where used. | No substantive package work. |
| Founder Decision Recorded | The Founder outcome is attributable. | Completed Founder authorization record. | No substantive work until synchronization and remaining gates pass. |
| Programme Synchronization | The Master Programme is being aligned to the recorded decision. | Proposed programme-state update and validation. | No substantive work. |
| Authorized to Begin | Founder authorization, conditions, dependencies and synchronization are complete. | Passed authorization checklist and synchronized Master Programme. | Work within the authorized boundary may begin. |
| Work Package In Progress | Work has begun within the authorized boundary. | Commencement evidence required by the package plan. | Authorized bounded work only. |
| Work Package Completion Recorded | Required outputs and completion evidence have been reviewed and programme status synchronized. | Completion package and Master Programme update. | No successor work by implication. |

These lifecycle states describe programme authorization status. They do not determine the constitutional status, approval, adoption, effectiveness, release or implementation status of package outputs.

## 4. Transition Gates

### 4.1 Proposed to Planning

Planning may begin when:

- the possible package is consistent with the Master Programme sequence;
- planning itself is permitted; and
- planning does not begin the substantive package.

### 4.2 Planning to Planning Validated

Planning becomes validated only when:

- the identity, purpose, scope and exclusions are explicit;
- dependencies and blockers are evidenced;
- deliverables and gates are reviewable;
- non-effects are preserved; and
- the planning validation passes.

### 4.3 Planning Validated to Founder Decision Pending

An authorization request may be presented only when the reusable [Authorization Template](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-TEMPLATE.md) can be completed without guessing prerequisites, dependencies or boundaries.

### 4.4 Founder Decision Pending to Founder Decision Recorded

The transition requires an attributable Founder outcome:

- Authorized;
- Authorized with explicit conditions;
- Amended;
- Deferred;
- Rejected; or
- Blocked.

Silence, review, recommendation or planning completion is not a decision.

### 4.5 Founder Decision Recorded to Programme Synchronization

Only an **Authorized** decision, or an **Authorized with explicit conditions** decision whose pre-synchronization conditions are satisfied, may proceed toward authorization.

An **Amended** decision returns to planning validation. A **Deferred**, **Rejected** or **Blocked** decision does not proceed.

### 4.6 Programme Synchronization to Authorized to Begin

The package becomes authorized to begin only when:

- the Master Programme records the package as the authorized next action;
- stage and phase status remain accurate;
- dependencies and completion gates remain preserved;
- all entry conditions are satisfied;
- the [Authorization Validation Checklist](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-CHECKLIST.md) passes; and
- no unrelated work package is advanced.

### 4.7 Authorized to Begin to Work Package In Progress

Work may start only within the recorded authorization boundary. Commencement does not approve the expected output or expand scope.

### 4.8 Work Package In Progress to Work Package Completion Recorded

Completion requires:

- all package deliverables;
- the required review and validation evidence;
- an attributable completion finding;
- preserved deferrals and non-effects; and
- Master Programme synchronization.

Completion authorizes no successor package.

## 5. Decision Outcomes Outside the Forward Path

### Amended

An amended authorization request returns to **Planning** or **Planning Validated**, depending on the change. The amended boundary must be validated before work begins.

### Deferred

The package remains unauthorised. The record must identify the deferred question or condition and the required later decision.

### Rejected

The proposed package must not begin. A materially different later proposal requires new planning and authorization.

### Blocked

The package must not begin while the stated prerequisite, dependency or conflict remains unresolved.

### Withdrawn

A proposed or authorized-but-not-started package may be withdrawn through an attributable Founder programme decision. Withdrawal does not alter completed historical evidence.

## 6. Boundary Changes After Authorization

A change is outside the existing authorization when it materially changes:

- identifier or title in a way that changes package identity;
- constitutional or programme subject;
- included scope;
- exclusions;
- deliverables;
- dependency basis;
- entry or completion gates; or
- authority to perform an external or irreversible action.

Such a change requires amended planning, revalidation and attributable Founder authorization before the changed work begins.

Minor meaning-preserving record maintenance does not expand authorization. It must remain attributable and historically intelligible.

## 7. Evidence Chain

The authorization lifecycle preserves this evidence chain:

```text
Master Programme position
        ↓
Bounded planning package
        ↓
Dependency and scope validation
        ↓
Attributable Founder authorization record
        ↓
Master Programme synchronization
        ↓
Authorization validation
        ↓
Bounded work
        ↓
Completion evidence
        ↓
Master Programme synchronization
```

No element silently substitutes for another.

## 8. Lifecycle Non-Effects

This lifecycle:

- authorizes no current or future work package;
- does not identify the next work package;
- does not amend the Master Programme;
- does not create constitutional doctrine;
- does not create or exercise a Platform Authority;
- does not allocate accountability;
- does not define product or technical lifecycle;
- does not approve package outputs;
- does not prescribe repository operations; and
- does not make any governance or implementation effective.

## 9. Lifecycle Status

This lifecycle is programme governance integrated through Founder-approved Master Programme Version 1.5. It provides no authorization by itself and changes no programme state by itself.
