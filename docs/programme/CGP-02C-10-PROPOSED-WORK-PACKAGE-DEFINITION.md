# CGP-02C.10 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.10

**Proposed title:** Historical Preservation

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines one proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package for Historical Preservation. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only.

It creates no constitutional proposition, Founder constitutional question, doctrine, approval, adoption, constitutional effect or drafting authorization.

## 2. Sequence and Blueprint Mapping

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) identifies **Historical Preservation** as deliverable 14, immediately after **Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules**.

[PTRA-04](PTRA-04-STAGE-E0-TRANSITION-ASSESSMENT.md) has been accepted as programme evidence. It records:

- bounded Blueprint treatment through deliverable 13;
- Historical Preservation as the next remaining constitutional subject;
- Governance Index and Status Integrity and Validation Rules as later subjects;
- no successor authorization; and
- a documentary prerequisite concerning CGP-02C.9 authorization evidence.

That prerequisite has been completed through the synchronized [CGP-02C.9 Founder Authorization Record](CGP-02C-9-FOUNDER-AUTHORIZATION-RECORD.md) and [Authorization Validation Report](CGP-02C-9-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md).

Blueprint order, PTRA-04 acceptance and DOC-01 completion permit planning only. They do not establish `CGP-02C.10` or authorize drafting.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The sole proposed constitutional subject is **Historical Preservation**.

The proposed package would prepare constitutional treatment for preserving the intelligibility of historically applicable governance, attributable decisions and historical status context without defining storage, archival, retention or repository mechanisms.

Historical Preservation would remain distinct from amendment traceability, supersession, retirement, withdrawal, rejection and current-status representation.

## 4. Proposed Purpose

The proposed drafting purpose is to prepare technology-neutral constitutional treatment that keeps governance history attributable and intelligible across constitutional change and status treatment.

This purpose identifies the planned inquiry. It does not define what Historical Preservation constitutionally means or answer any reserved constitutional uncertainty.

## 5. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to attributable Founder work-package authorization, later drafting would be limited to:

1. the constitutional meaning of Historical Preservation;
2. its constitutional purpose;
3. the meaning and boundary of historically applicable governance;
4. preservation of constitutional intelligibility;
5. preservation of attributable decisions;
6. preservation of historical status context;
7. separation from amendment traceability;
8. separation from supersession;
9. separation from retirement, withdrawal and rejection;
10. separation from storage;
11. separation from archival mechanisms;
12. separation from retention policy;
13. separation from repository implementation;
14. technology-neutral constitutional boundaries; and
15. explicit deferred governance.

These are planning topics only. They are not constitutional propositions, Founder questions or decisions.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not:

- draft constitutional propositions during planning;
- create Founder constitutional questions during planning;
- define or require a storage medium, location, service or architecture;
- define archival, backup, restoration, deletion, destruction, migration or retrieval mechanisms;
- define retention periods, schedules, triggers, exceptions or policy;
- define repository structure, administration, access, tooling, commands or workflow;
- define document formats, schemas, identifiers, hashes or technical evidence formats;
- define versioning or historical-storage implementation;
- define Governance Index and Status Integrity;
- define Validation Rules;
- redefine amendment traceability;
- redefine approval, adoption or constitutional effect;
- redefine conflict review or dependent-governance impact review;
- redefine supersession, retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- determine the current or historical status of a named governance instrument;
- correct an existing historical record or status representation;
- create a Historical Preservation Authority or another Platform Authority;
- allocate accountability, stewardship, custody, administration, operation, recording or maintenance;
- create roles, permissions or procedures;
- amend any dependency;
- modify the Master Programme or Consolidated Decision Register;
- create Founder Review material during planning; or
- authorize drafting or a successor work package.

One bounded drafting package is proposed because Historical Preservation is one Blueprint subject with a common technology-neutral boundary.

- all five authorized deliverables exist;
- every proposition is unique and fully traced;
- the package remains limited to Historical Preservation;
- storage, archival, retention, repository, index and validation matters remain excluded;
- constitutional uncertainty remains reserved for Founder Review;
- no Platform Authority or accountability relationship is created;
- relative-link, Markdown, whitespace, EOF and repository-scope checks pass; and
- no later work package is authorized by completion.

Completion would not approve, adopt or make the proposed content constitutionally effective; complete CGP-02 or Stage E0; unblock CGP-03; or authorize another Blueprint subject.
