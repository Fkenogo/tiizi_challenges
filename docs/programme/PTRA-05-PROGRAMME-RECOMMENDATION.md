# PTRA-05 — Programme Recommendation

**Programme:** Tiizi Version 2 — Stage E0

**Status:** Non-governing recommendation; no authorization

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Recommendation Summary

Preserve Stage E0 and CGP-02 as In Progress and CGP-03 as blocked.

The recommended next programme phase is:

**Governed planning and Founder work-package authorization preparation for Blueprint deliverable 15 — Governance Index and Status Integrity.**

This recommendation does not assign an identifier, create the planning package or authorize drafting.

## 2. Recommended Completion Sequence

### Phase 1 — Governance Index and Status Integrity

Prepare one bounded planning package after PTRA-05 is accepted as programme evidence.

The future planning package should preserve:

- status representation versus status creation;
- current-baseline versus historical-reference integrity;
- index, register and manifest non-Authority boundaries;
- unresolved inconsistency treatment;
- technology neutrality;
- no repository workflow;
- no maintenance-role or accountability allocation; and
- no correction of named historical instruments.

### Phase 2 — Validation Rules

After deliverable 15 completes its bounded milestone, perform a governed transition assessment and prepare separate planning evidence for Validation Rules.

The subject should remain bounded to constitutional validation purpose and minimum integrity requirements. It must not define tooling, repository commands, testing software, reviewer roles, approval procedure or constitutional effect.

### Phase 3 — Final CGP-02 Consolidation Programme

After deliverables 15 and 16 are complete, prepare a separately authorized consolidation programme covering Blueprint deliverables 17–20.

It should:

- assemble the complete CGP-02 instrument in coherent order;
- preserve the source and recorded status of every proposition;
- consolidate genuine duplication without changing meaning;
- produce a complete deferred-governance register;
- produce Governance Status and Decision Trace;
- isolate genuinely unresolved whole-instrument Founder questions;
- validate coverage of all gaps, conflicts and duplicate clusters; and
- prepare the whole instrument for independent review and Founder decision.

The consolidation programme must not use integration as implied approval.

### Phase 4 — Whole-Instrument Review and Founder Approval

The integrated standard should undergo:

1. technical consistency review;
2. independent constitutional review proportionate to consequence;
3. correction of genuine integration defects through attributable governance;
4. Founder constitutional review;
5. an attributable Founder Decision Record; and
6. preparation of a whole-instrument Founder Approval Candidate.

Package-level approvals remain traceable inputs but do not substitute for whole-instrument approval.

### Phase 5 — Adoption and Programme Transition

Approval must remain distinct from adoption and constitutional effect.

Only after separately attributable adoption treatment should programme governance determine:

- whether CGP-02 is Complete;
- whether CGP-03 is unblocked;
- which indexes, registers or manifests require representation updates; and
- the next Stage E0 work package.

## 3. Immediate Next Action

The immediate next action should be:

1. Founder acceptance of PTRA-05 as programme evidence; then
2. preparation of a planning and Founder Authorization package for Governance Index and Status Integrity under the existing Programme Authorization Framework.

No drafting should begin until the planning package, attributable Founder authorization, authorization validation and Master Programme synchronization are complete.
