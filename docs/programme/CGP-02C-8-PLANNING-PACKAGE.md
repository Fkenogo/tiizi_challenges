## 10. Planning Risks and Controls

| Risk | Planning control |
|---|---|
| Amendment is treated as automatic supersession | Require explicit separation and attributable supersession inquiry. |
| Adoption is treated as replacement | Preserve C.4 and examine supersession separately. |
| Conflict disposition silently supersedes governance | Preserve C.7’s express deferral and exclude actual conflict resolution. |
| Supersession is collapsed into retirement, withdrawal or rejection | Keep Blueprint deliverable 13 outside the package. |
| A current index or label is treated as constitutive | Defer index synchronization and preserve anti-inference. |
| Historical governance is erased | Require planning treatment for historical preservation implications without implementation. |
| A recorder or repository administrator gains Authority | Preserve existing Governance Authority and exclude allocation. |
| Programme readiness is mistaken for authorization | Require attributable FWA-01 through FWA-05 decisions, validation and Master Programme synchronization. |
