## 10. Planning Risks and Controls

| Risk | Planning control |
|---|---|
| Conflict identification becomes conflict resolution | Exclude actual conflict outcomes and require unresolved treatment to remain explicit. |
| Escalation implies a new Authority | Preserve Governance Authority and prohibit a Conflict Authority. |
| Conflict review becomes amendment approval | Preserve review/decision and approval boundaries from C.1 and C.3. |
| Disposition evidence becomes constitutional effect | Preserve C.4 adoption and effect boundaries. |
| Conflict record merges with amendment trace or impact review | Preserve C.5 and C.6 record distinctions. |
| Conflict treatment silently creates supersession | Defer supersession to Blueprint deliverable 12. |
| A reviewer or recorder is allocated | Exclude all relationship, role and responsibility allocation. |
| Programme readiness is mistaken for authorization | Require attributable FWA-01 through FWA-05 decisions, validation and Master Programme synchronization. |
