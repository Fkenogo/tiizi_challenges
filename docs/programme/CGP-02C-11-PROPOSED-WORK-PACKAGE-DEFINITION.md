# CGP-02C.11 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.11

**Proposed title:** Governance Index and Status Integrity

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Purpose

This document defines one proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package for Governance Index and Status Integrity. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only.

It creates no constitutional proposition, Founder constitutional question, doctrine, approval, adoption, constitutional effect or drafting authorization.

## 2. Sequence and Blueprint Mapping

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) identifies **Governance Index and Status Integrity** as deliverable 15, immediately after **Historical Preservation** and before **Validation Rules**.

[PTRA-05](PTRA-05-FINAL-BLUEPRINT-COMPLETION-ASSESSMENT.md) and its [Acceptance Record](PTRA-05-ACCEPTANCE-RECORD.md) are official Stage E0 programme evidence. They confirm:

- bounded Blueprint treatment through deliverable 14;
- Governance Index and Status Integrity as the next remaining constitutional subject;
- Validation Rules as the later remaining substantive subject;
- Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20 as one future consolidation programme;
- no Blueprint amendment;
- no programme-state change; and
- no successor work-package authorization.

Blueprint order and accepted PTRA-05 evidence permit planning only. They do not establish `CGP-02C.11` or authorize drafting.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The sole proposed constitutional subject is **Governance Index and Status Integrity**.

The proposed package would prepare constitutional treatment for the integrity of documentary representations of constitutional status, including indexes, registers, manifests and other status representations, without allowing any representation to create or alter constitutional status.

It would preserve the current constitutional baseline and historically intelligible references while remaining distinct from Validation Rules, operational synchronization, repository implementation and maintenance responsibility.

## 4. Proposed Purpose

The proposed drafting purpose is to prepare technology-neutral constitutional treatment that keeps representations of governance status attributable, distinguishable from constitutional status itself and capable of identifying the current constitutional baseline without rewriting history.

This purpose identifies the planned inquiry. It does not define the constitutional purpose of an index, establish a canonical index or answer any constitutional uncertainty.

## 5. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to attributable Founder work-package authorization, later drafting would be limited to:

1. the constitutional purpose of governance indexes;
2. the constitutional meaning of status representations;
3. the distinction between constitutional status and documentary representation;
4. integrity of the current constitutional baseline;
5. preservation of historical references without changing governance;
6. treatment of stale, incomplete or inconsistent representations;
7. the constitutional relationship among indexes, registers, manifests and status representations;
8. technology-neutral constitutional boundaries;
9. explicit anti-inference protections; and
10. explicit deferred governance.

These are planning topics only. They are not constitutional propositions, Founder constitutional questions or decisions.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not:

- draft constitutional propositions during planning;
- create Founder constitutional questions during planning;
- define Validation Rules;
- define validation procedures, tests, thresholds, evidence formats or assurance levels;
- designate, create or modify an actual governance index, register, manifest or status representation;
- determine or correct the status of a named governance instrument;
- synchronize an index, register, manifest, repository or programme tracker;
- define operational synchronization, publication, reconciliation or maintenance procedures;
- allocate responsibility for creating, recording, maintaining, correcting or publishing a representation;
- define repository structure, administration, access, tooling, commands or workflow;
- define document formats, schemas, identifiers, hashes, APIs or technical status mechanisms;
- define storage, archival mechanisms, retention policy or versioning implementation;
- redefine approval, adoption, constitutional effect or constitutional dates;
- redefine amendment traceability, dependent-governance impact review or conflict review;
- redefine supersession, retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- redefine Historical Preservation;
- create an Index Authority, Status Authority or another Platform Authority;
- allocate accountability, stewardship, custody, administration, operation, recording or maintenance;
- create roles, permissions or procedures;
- amend any dependency;
- modify the Master Programme or Consolidated Decision Register;
- create Founder Review material during planning; or
- authorize drafting or a successor work package.
