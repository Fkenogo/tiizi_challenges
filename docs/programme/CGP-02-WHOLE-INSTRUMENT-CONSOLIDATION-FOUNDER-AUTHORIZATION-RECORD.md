## 3. Proposed Work-Package Boundary

### 3.1 Purpose

Integrate the unchanged protected CGP-02 bounded proposition corpus into one traceable whole-instrument Founder Review Draft and prepare Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20.

### 3.2 Included work

- unchanged proposition assembly;
- proposition inventory and source crosswalk;
- source-status and decision trace;
- Deferred Constitutional Questions register;
- whole-instrument Founder Review Questions limited to genuinely unresolved matters;
- duplicate, overlap and structural-retention analysis;
- whole-instrument traceability;
- Draft Self-Validation;
- consolidation notes; and
- bounded change and scope evidence.

### 3.3 Explicit exclusions

The proposed package must not:

- modify, add, delete, merge or renumber a proposition;
- change constitutional wording;
- resolve a deferred question;
- reopen a Founder decision;
- normalize source status;
- approve, adopt or make any content constitutionally effective;
- establish a constitutional date;
- create Authority, accountability, a role or permission;
- modify a protected source;
- begin CGP-03;
- complete CGP-02 or Stage E0; or
- authorize Founder review, approval, adoption, transition or a successor package by implication.

### 3.4 Entry gates

Work may begin only after:

1. FWA-01 through FWA-05 receive attributable Founder dispositions;
2. the record contains the exact decision outcome, date, conditions and notes;
3. every authorization condition is satisfied;
4. the package-specific Authorization Checklist passes;
5. the Master Programme identifies the package as the authorized next action;
6. no conflicting package occupies the boundary; and
7. all protected-source hashes are reverified immediately before work begins.
