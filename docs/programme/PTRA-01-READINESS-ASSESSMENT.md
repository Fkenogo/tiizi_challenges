## 3. Dependency Readiness

Conflict Review and Escalation can be planned from:

- GVR-07 and GVR-08, which preserve conflict discipline and Governance Authority escalation;
- AMP-04 and the approved precedence hierarchy;
- DGI-08 and DGI-12, which preserve unresolved dependent-governance impact without deciding conflict;
- the Authority Model’s escalation boundary;
- Blueprint GAP-14 and conflict question set; and
- the existing prohibition on silent conflict resolution.

These dependencies are sufficient for planning. They do not answer the reserved constitutional questions or authorize propositions.

## 4. Prerequisites Before Authorization

Before a Founder authorization decision is sought:

1. define one bounded subject: Conflict Review and Escalation;
2. distinguish conflict identification, escalation and disposition evidence;
3. exclude resolution of any existing instrument-specific conflict;
4. exclude amendment, approval, adoption, supersession and implementation rules already assigned elsewhere;
5. verify dependencies against GVR-07–GVR-08, DGI-08, DGI-12 and the Authority Model;
6. preserve every unresolved Founder question;
7. define five bounded Founder Review deliverables only if supported by planning;
8. complete the Founder Authorization Validation Checklist; and
9. synchronize the Master Programme only after attributable Founder authorization.

## 5. Recommended Adjustments

No completed constitutional package requires amendment before planning.

The planning package should explicitly control three integration risks:

- **Identification versus disposition:** recognizing an apparent conflict must not decide it.
- **Escalation versus Authority creation:** escalation to Governance Authority must not create a Conflict Authority or allocate an escalator.
- **Conflict treatment versus supersession:** conflict disposition must not silently establish supersession rules assigned to the following Blueprint subject.

These are planning controls, not constitutional answers.
