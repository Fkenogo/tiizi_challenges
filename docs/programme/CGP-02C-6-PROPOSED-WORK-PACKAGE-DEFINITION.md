# CGP-02C.6 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.6

**Proposed title:** Dependent-Governance Impact Review

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the next proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package remaining within CGP-02. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only, creates no constitutional proposition and authorizes no drafting.

## 2. Sequence Determination

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) lists **Dependent-Governance Impact Review** immediately after **Amendment Traceability Requirements**.

[CGP-02C.5 — Amendment Traceability Requirements](../governance/principles/17-CGP-02C-5-COMPLETION-REPORT.md) has completed its bounded post-Founder milestone. [Master Programme Version 1.9](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records that no successor work package is authorized.

Therefore:

- the next planned constitutional subject is **Dependent-Governance Impact Review**;
- the exact proposed identifier is `CGP-02C.6`;
- sequence and planning readiness do not authorize the package; and
- identifier, title, scope, structure and drafting authorization remain subject to attributable Founder decision.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The proposed package would prepare constitutional treatment for identifying and recording how a proposed constitutional amendment may affect governance that depends upon, references or applies the amended constitutional position.

It would address one subject only: dependent-governance impact review. It would not perform an impact review for an existing amendment, change a dependent instrument, resolve a conflict, update a cross-reference or synchronize an index.

## 4. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02C.6 would be limited to:

1. the constitutional purpose and meaning of dependent-governance impact review;
2. the constitutional relationship among a proposed amendment, its declared scope and governance that depends upon or references the affected constitutional meaning;
3. the constitutional basis for determining which dependent governance requires impact consideration;
4. conceptual distinctions among directly affected, potentially affected, unaffected and unresolved dependent-governance impacts;
5. the minimum categories of information that an impact-review record may be required to preserve;
6. the boundary between identifying impact and amending, interpreting, superseding or correcting dependent governance;
7. the relationship between dependent-governance impact review, an amendment trace and an approval record without merging those records;
8. the treatment of cross-reference impact as review evidence without prescribing cross-reference updates;
9. the minimum anti-inference boundary preventing an impact finding from changing governance status or meaning; and
10. explicit identification of questions that remain deferred.

These subjects identify questions for later constitutional drafting. They do not answer those questions.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not draft or determine:

- an impact review for a specific existing or proposed amendment;
- an amendment, correction, interpretation or reconciliation of any dependent instrument;
- amendment traceability already treated by CGP-02C.5;
- approval, adoption, constitutional effect or governance lifecycle;
- amendment classification, review triggers or proportionality;
- conflict identification, escalation or disposition governance;
- supersession;
- retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- historical-preservation mechanisms;
- governance-index or status synchronization;
- validation doctrine or validation procedure;
- cross-reference update requirements, mechanics or execution;
- a complete decision-trace or change-log standard;
- amendment, approval or review workflow;
- who performs, records, maintains, validates or approves an impact review;
- any Authority, accountability, role, permission or responsibility allocation;
- technical schemas, storage, retention, identifier formats or repository processes;
- implementation, automation, publication or deployment;
- retrospective impact classification for historical governance; or
- authorization of a successor work package.

## 6. Reserved Constitutional Questions

If drafting is later authorized, the Founder Review package must present without presuming answers:

1. What is the constitutional purpose and meaning of dependent-governance impact review?
2. At what constitutional point must dependent-governance impact be considered, without defining workflow?
3. Which forms of dependency or cross-reference place governance within the review boundary?
4. How should directly affected, potentially affected, unaffected and unresolved impacts remain distinguishable?
5. What minimum information must a dependent-governance impact-review record preserve?
6. What boundary prevents an impact finding from amending, interpreting, correcting or superseding dependent governance?
7. How should impact review relate to an amendment trace and approval record without merging their constitutional meanings?
8. How should cross-reference impact be identified without prescribing or performing synchronization?
9. Which matters must remain deferred to Conflict Review and Escalation, Supersession Rules, Historical Preservation, Governance Index and Status Integrity, Validation Rules or other later CGP-02 subjects?

These are future constitutional questions, not propositions or decisions made by this definition.

## 7. Proposed Drafting Deliverables

If and only if drafting is authorized and the Master Programme is synchronized, the bounded drafting package would produce:

1. Dependent-Governance Impact Review Founder Review Draft;
2. Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation;
3. Founder Review Decision Pack;
4. Drafting Notes; and
5. Bounded Change and Scope Report.

No Founder Review Draft, Founder Decision Record, Founder Approval Candidate, impact-review record or approved instrument is authorized by this planning document.

## 8. Proposed Work-Package Boundary

The recommendation is one bounded drafting package because identifying dependent-governance impact, preserving its evidence and preventing an impact finding from changing governance form one constitutional subject in the frozen Blueprint.

Conflict Review and Escalation is the next distinct Blueprint subject and must not be absorbed into this package.

Any decomposition, expansion or change of constitutional subject requires attributable Founder authorization before drafting begins.

`CGP-02C.6` and **Dependent-Governance Impact Review** remain proposed until the Founder records an authorization decision, authorization validation passes, every condition is satisfied and the Master Programme is synchronized.

This document supplies no authorization by sequence, title, readiness, completion of CGP-02C.5 or repository presence.
