## 5. Internal Consistency Review

### 5.1 Consistent records

- The Master Programme, Consolidated Decision Register, CGP-02C.4 approval record, approved instrument and Approval Validation Report agree that CGP-02C.4 is Founder Approved but not adopted or constitutionally effective.
- Stage E0 and CGP-02 remain In Progress.
- CGP-03 remains blocked by completion and approval of CGP-02.
- No source reviewed identifies a successor package as authorized.
- The frozen Blueprint places **Amendment Traceability Requirements** immediately after Adoption and Constitutional Effect.
- The CGP-02C.4 retrospective is registered as programme evidence only and changes no governance status, dependency or authorization.

### 5.2 Documentary inconsistency requiring reconciliation

The Master Programme recognizes the Founder Work Package Authorization Framework as the reusable programme-governance mechanism and states that future bounded work packages shall use it. Its Version 1.5 change entry records Founder-approved programme-governance integration.

The following framework artefacts retain draft or non-adopted metadata:

- [Founder Work Package Authorization Standard](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-STANDARD.md);
- [Founder Work Package Authorization Template](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-TEMPLATE.md);
- [Founder Authorization Validation Checklist](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-CHECKLIST.md); and
- [Work Package Authorization Lifecycle](WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-LIFECYCLE.md).

The Standard and Lifecycle also contain concluding clauses stating that they do not govern the programme until separately adopted. Those clauses conflict with the Master Programme’s mandatory-use and Founder-approved-integration statements.

This review does not decide which status statement controls and does not amend either source. The inconsistency must be resolved through attributable, meaning-preserving programme housekeeping or a separately approved programme decision before the framework is relied upon for the next Founder Authorization Package.

## 7. Readiness Verdict

**Verdict: Not fully ready; one documentary programme-governance prerequisite remains.**

The constitutional drafting corpus is sufficiently mature to identify the next planned subject, and no unanswered package-level Founder question blocks planning. Programme position, CGP-02C.4 approval status, register treatment, manifest treatment and historical records are otherwise coherent.

The programme should not prepare the next Founder Authorization Package while the Programme Authorization Framework simultaneously appears mandatory in the Master Programme and non-adopted in its own controlling documents.

This is a documentary programme-governance blocker, not a constitutional-content defect and not authorization to amend the affected documents.
