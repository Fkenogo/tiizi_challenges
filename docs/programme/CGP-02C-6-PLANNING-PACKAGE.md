## 4. Proposed Scope

The complete proposed scope is defined in the [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-6-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md#4-proposed-included-scope).

Drafting would address only:

- constitutional purpose and meaning of dependent-governance impact review;
- the relationship among amendment scope, dependent governance and cross-references;
- conceptual impact distinctions;
- minimum impact-review information questions;
- impact identification versus governance-change boundaries;
- relationship to amendment traces and approval records; and
- anti-inference and deferred-subject boundaries.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

Every exclusion in the [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-6-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md#5-explicit-exclusions) binds any later drafting authorization.

In particular, the package must not perform a specific impact review, amend dependent governance, resolve conflicts, define supersession, prescribe cross-reference or index synchronization, define validation, allocate responsibility or prescribe implementation.

## 6. Expected Drafting Deliverables

If drafting is authorized, produce only:

1. Founder Review Draft;
2. Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation;
3. Founder Review Decision Pack;
4. Drafting Notes; and
5. Bounded Change and Scope Report.

A Founder Decision Record or Founder Approval Candidate would require later attributable constitutional decisions and separate execution authority.

## 7. Proposed Drafting Controls

Any authorized drafting must:

- contain one constitutional subject only;
- trace every proposition to approved normative governance or an explicit Founder question;
- preserve source-status distinctions;
- preserve every earlier CGP-02 package unchanged;
- distinguish impact identification from amendment, interpretation, correction, supersession and conflict disposition;
- avoid conducting an instrument-specific impact analysis;
- avoid prescribing cross-reference changes, index synchronization or implementation;
- state every exclusion and deferral explicitly;
- create no Platform Authority; and
- allocate no accountability relationship.
