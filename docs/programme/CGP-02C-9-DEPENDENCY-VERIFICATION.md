## 3. Dependency Conclusions

The dependencies establish that:

- deliverable 13 is the next ordered Blueprint subject;
- retirement, withdrawal and rejection remain constitutionally undefined;
- supersession is separately treated and cannot be inferred as any of the three proposed treatments;
- approval, adoption and constitutional effect remain distinct;
- records, labels and implementation cannot acquire Governance Authority;
- historical intelligibility must be preserved without drafting Historical Preservation in this package; and
- no existing package status supplies authority to draft.

They do not establish a constitutional answer or approve the proposed package.
