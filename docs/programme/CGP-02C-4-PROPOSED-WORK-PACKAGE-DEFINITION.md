# CGP-02C.4 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.4

**Proposed title:** Adoption and Constitutional Effect

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-23

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the next proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package remaining within CGP-02. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only. It creates no constitutional proposition and authorizes no drafting.

## 2. Sequence Determination

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) lists **Adoption and Constitutional Effect** immediately after **Approval Governance**.

[CGP-02C.3 — Approval Governance](../governance/principles/13-CGP-02C-3-COMPLETION-REPORT.md) is complete as a bounded work package. The [Master Programme](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records that no successor drafting is authorized and that a separate Founder authorization is required.

Therefore:

- the next planned constitutional subject is **Adoption and Constitutional Effect**;
- the exact proposed identifier is `CGP-02C.4`;
- neither sequence nor this definition authorizes the package; and
- the identifier, title, boundary and drafting authorization remain subject to attributable Founder decision.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The proposed package would prepare constitutional treatment for the relationship among:

- approval;
- adoption;
- constitutional effect;
- effective date;
- application date; and
- the boundary preventing status language or administrative treatment from creating constitutional effect by implication.

The package would explain only these constitutional concepts and relationships. It would not adopt any existing candidate or make any governance instrument effective.

## 4. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02C.4 would be limited to:

1. the constitutional meaning of adoption;
2. the constitutional meaning of constitutional effect;
3. the distinction between approval and adoption;
4. the distinction between approval date, adoption date, effective date and application date;
5. the conditions conceptually necessary for approved governance to become adopted or constitutionally effective;
6. the relationship between an attributable approval decision and a later adoption or effect decision;
7. the boundary between document status and constitutional effect;
8. the prohibition on inferred adoption or effect through publication, repository treatment, implementation, reliance, passage of time or status labels; and
9. explicit identification of questions that remain deferred.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not draft or determine:

- an adoption decision for CGP-02C.1, CGP-02C.2, CGP-02C.3 or any other existing candidate;
- constitutional effect for any existing instrument;
- a current-baseline index or governance-index synchronization rule;
- lifecycle doctrine already treated by CGP-02C.2A;
- amendment classification already treated by CGP-02C.2B;
- review triggers or proportionality already treated by CGP-02C.2C;
- approval governance already treated by CGP-02C.3;
- amendment traceability;
- dependent-governance impact review;
- conflict review or escalation;
- supersession;
- retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- historical-preservation rules beyond the minimum boundary needed to avoid inferred effect;
- validation doctrine;
- temporary or emergency governance;
- implementation, deployment or repository workflow;
- technical date, version or identifier formats;
- a new Platform Authority; or
- an accountability relationship, role or permission.

## 6. Reserved Constitutional Questions

If drafting is later authorized, the Founder Review package must present without presuming answers:

1. What is constitutional adoption?
2. When is adoption distinct from approval?
3. When does approved governance become constitutionally effective?
4. How do approval date, adoption date, effective date and application date differ, if at all?
5. Can approval and adoption occur through one attributable decision while remaining conceptually distinct?
6. What minimum decision boundary prevents status, publication, implementation or passage of time from creating effect by implication?
7. Which questions must remain deferred to Governance Index and Status Integrity or other later CGP-02 subjects?

These are future constitutional questions, not propositions or decisions made by this definition.

## 7. Proposed Deliverables

If and only if drafting is authorized and the Master Programme is synchronized, the bounded drafting package would produce:

1. Adoption and Constitutional Effect Founder Review Draft;
2. Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation;
3. Founder Review Decision Pack;
4. Drafting Notes; and
5. Bounded Change and Scope Report.

No Founder Approval Candidate, adoption record or effective instrument is authorized at this planning stage.

## 8. Proposed Work-Package Boundary

The recommendation is one bounded drafting package because adoption and constitutional effect are interdependent concepts identified as one drafting deliverable in the frozen Blueprint.

Any later decomposition, expansion or change of constitutional subject requires attributable Founder authorization before drafting begins.

## 9. Definition Status

`CGP-02C.4` and **Adoption and Constitutional Effect** remain proposed until the Founder records an authorization decision and the Master Programme is synchronized. This document supplies no authorization by sequence, title, readiness or repository presence.
