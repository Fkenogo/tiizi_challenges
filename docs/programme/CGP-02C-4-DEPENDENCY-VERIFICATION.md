## 4. Preserved Deferrals

The dependency basis does not resolve:

- the meaning of adoption;
- the relationship between approval and adoption;
- when approved governance becomes constitutionally effective;
- distinctions among approval, adoption, effective and application dates;
- whether approval and adoption may be recorded through one decision;
- later traceability, impact, conflict, supersession, retirement, historical-preservation, index or validation rules; or
- adoption or effect of any existing candidate.

These remain future Founder Review questions or later Blueprint subjects.

## 5. Conflict and Blocker Assessment

No dependency contradiction prevents the proposed package from being presented for Founder authorization.

Substantive drafting remains blocked until:

1. identifier, title, scope and package structure decisions are attributable;
2. drafting authorization is attributable;
3. authorization validation passes; and
4. the Master Programme is synchronized.

Sequence alone does not satisfy these blockers.
