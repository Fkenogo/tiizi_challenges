## 4. Proposed Scope

The complete proposed scope is defined in the [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-5-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md#4-proposed-included-scope).

Drafting would address only:

- constitutional purpose and meaning of an amendment trace;
- minimum amendment-trace information questions;
- relationship among source governance, changed meaning, amendment scope and attributable decision;
- amendment-trace versus approval-record and status-representation boundaries;
- preservation of affected, unaffected, pending, deferred, rejected and withdrawn matters;
- amendment-reference questions without technical identifier design; and
- minimum historical intelligibility without preservation mechanisms.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

Every exclusion in the [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-5-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md#5-explicit-exclusions) is binding on any later drafting authorization.

In particular, the package must not conduct dependent-governance impact review, define validation or historical-preservation mechanisms, reopen completed CGP-02 subjects, create an Authority, allocate accountability, prescribe implementation or update the Master Programme through drafting.

## 6. Expected Deliverables

If drafting is authorized, produce only:

1. Founder Review Draft;
2. Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation;
3. Founder Review Decision Pack;
4. Drafting Notes; and
5. Bounded Change and Scope Report.

The Founder Review Draft must remain proposed governance. A Founder Decision Record or Founder Approval Candidate would require a later attributable review outcome and separate instruction.

## 7. Proposed Drafting Controls

Any authorized drafting must:

- contain one constitutional subject only;
- trace every proposition to an approved normative source or explicit Founder question;
- identify candidate and approved dependencies by their exact recorded status;
- preserve all earlier CGP-02 packages unchanged;
- preserve the distinction among amendment, approval, adoption, constitutional effect, trace and representation;
- avoid performing or approving an amendment;
- avoid dependent-document analysis or cross-reference review;
- avoid technical identifiers, schemas, storage, retention or repository process;
- state every deferral and exclusion explicitly;
- create no new Platform Authority;
- allocate no accountability relationship; and
- remain unapproved until attributable Founder decision.

## 8. Authorization Prerequisites

Before drafting may begin:

- the Founder must approve or amend the identifier;
- the Founder must approve or amend the title;
- the Founder must approve or amend the bounded scope and exclusions;
- the Founder must approve one-package treatment or an alternative decomposition;
- the Founder must expressly authorize drafting;
- the [Dependency Verification](CGP-02C-5-DEPENDENCY-VERIFICATION.md) must remain satisfied;
- the [package-specific Founder Authorization Validation Checklist](CGP-02C-5-FOUNDER-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-CHECKLIST.md) must be completed and pass;
- every Founder-recorded condition must be satisfied; and
- the Master Programme must be synchronized to identify the authorized package as the next permitted action.

The bounded drafting package would be ready for technical Founder Review only when:

- all five expected deliverables exist;
- every proposition is unique and traceable;
- all reserved Founder questions are presented without presumed approval;
- the Founder Review Draft remains within the authorized subject;
- amendment traceability remains distinct from dependent-governance impact review and historical-preservation mechanisms;

These are drafting-package gates. They do not approve, adopt or make the resulting draft constitutionally effective.
