The dependency chain supports planning because:

- C.1 supplies constitutional-change, Authority and anti-label boundaries;
- C.2 preserves lifecycle, classification and review treatment as separate subjects;
- C.3 preserves approval and recorder separation;
- C.4 preserves approval, adoption and effect distinctions;
- C.5 preserves attributable amendment-trace meaning;
- C.6 identifies dependent impact without changing governance;
- C.7 preserves supersession as a separate later subject;
- approved normative sources preserve Governance Authority and historical intelligibility; and
- the Blueprint and PTRA evidence establish programme order without supplying authorization.

No dependency supplies an answer to the nine reserved Founder constitutional questions.
