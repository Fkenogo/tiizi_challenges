## 2. What Tiizi V1 Means Now

Tiizi V1 is not the authority for V2 product truth. It remains valuable as a **reference implementation and evidence source**.

V1 should later be assessed feature by feature against the approved V2 baseline using five treatments:

- **KEEP** — already consistent with V2 and suitable to retain.
- **ADAPT** — useful implementation requiring bounded change.
- **MIGRATE** — valid data/content/behaviour that should move into the V2 model.
- **REBUILD** — capability whose implementation conflicts materially with V2 requirements.
- **RETIRE** — obsolete, superseded or no longer required.

No blanket decision to preserve or discard V1 should be made before Stage G.

## 3. Early Knowledge Reconstruction — What It Contributed

Before the programme moved deeply into governance, Tiizi had already accumulated substantial product and knowledge work around:

- exercise catalogues and exercise identity;
- wellness activity catalogues;
- stable activity identity separate from challenge-specific targets;
- challenge templates;
- challenge creation/composition flows and wizard behaviour;
- metrics and units;
- activity logging and evidence;
- catalogue/runtime availability;
- challenge-specific configuration around reusable activity definitions.

Examples still present in the repository include the exercise catalogue, the Wellness Activity Framework, wellness challenge specifications/templates, and challenge architecture documents.

These materials are **not automatically authoritative V2 governance**. They are important **V2 input and carry-forward evidence**.

One strong pre-governance design principle already documented is:

> **Activity identity is separate from target.**

That is a useful knowledge-model insight, but its final V2 expression belongs under Stage EK and later Stage F rather than being treated as binding simply because V1 implemented it.

## 4. Why the Programme Moved Upstream into Governance

The knowledge/product work exposed questions that could not safely be answered only through schemas, Firestore collections, UI flows or implementation conventions:

- What concepts are canonical platform meaning?
- Who may change that meaning?
- What belongs to a Group versus the Platform?
- What makes a Challenge a governed undertaking rather than an arbitrary configuration?
- What counts as Evidence and Truth?
- How do approval, adoption, amendment, supersession and historical integrity work?
- What authority may product or implementation exercise?

The programme therefore moved upstream to build the constitutional and governance layer before completing the new knowledge and product model.

That was not a restart. It was a dependency correction.

## 5. Stage D — Constitutional Foundation

**Status: Complete.**

Stage D established the conceptual and constitutional world that later work must preserve, including Tiizi's constitutional ontology, Platform Constitution, Platform Principles, Authority Model, domain standards, Entity Ownership Foundation, and CGP-01 Constitutional Governance Principles.

The constitutional ontology establishes the product world around:

**Platform → Community → Undertaking → Commitment → Evidence → Truth → Motivation → Growth**

with Group as the product expression of Community and Challenge as the product expression of Undertaking.

This is the governing conceptual foundation for later knowledge and product work.

## 7. What FEF Alignment Did — and Did Not Do

FEF alignment did **not** replace Tiizi's programme, constitutional governance or project-specific decisions.

It established FEF as Tiizi's prospective baseline governance reference while preserving:

- the Tiizi Master Programme as Tiizi's programme SSOT;
- Tiizi-specific constitutional doctrine;
- Tiizi-specific decision vocabulary and structure;
- historical Tiizi records;
- Founder authority over Tiizi programme changes.

FEF should therefore be understood as a cross-project baseline/guardrail, not as a separate Tiizi destination or another product-rebuild phase.

- current Stage E0 governance outputs according to their recorded status.

### Carries forward as V2 input requiring reconciliation

- exercise catalogue/content;

- challenge architecture and existing challenge behaviours;
- template concepts;
- challenge creation/wizard concepts;
- existing metrics and units;

- useful V1 user-flow and implementation evidence.

### Does not carry forward automatically as authority

- old pre-beta plans;
- old clean-build specifications;
- old coding-agent prompts;
- V1 Firestore/schema decisions merely because they exist;
- historical wellness template documentation describing itself as "production ready";
- legacy TODO/change documents as statements of current V2 roadmap.
