## 4. Architectural Gaps

The following are architectural incompletenesses, not defects in completed packages:

1. **Status termination without replacement:** retirement, withdrawal and rejection remain undefined and must stay distinct from supersession.
2. **Dedicated historical preservation:** preservation requirements recur, but their complete constitutional treatment is not yet assembled.
3. **Current-baseline representation:** the relationship among authoritative status, indexes and status synchronization remains undefined.
4. **Validation boundary:** no constitutional minimum distinguishes required validation evidence from programme or operational checks.
5. **Cross-cutting consolidation:** deferred matters, governance status, decision trace, final Founder Review questions and draft self-validation remain unassembled.

## 5. Consolidation Opportunities

Later consolidation may:

- arrange bounded propositions in Blueprint order;
- harmonize shared vocabulary without changing approved meaning;
- consolidate repeated Authority and anti-inference statements while retaining each subject-specific application;
- create a source-to-integrated-section trace;
- preserve the distinct status of each bounded source package;
- identify apparent duplication as dependency reuse rather than deletion by default; and
- present one coherent deferred-matters and non-effects boundary.

Consolidation must not begin through this assessment and must not silently amend a proposition or normalize different approval statuses.
