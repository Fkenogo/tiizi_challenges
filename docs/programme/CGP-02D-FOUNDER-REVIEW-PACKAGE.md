## B. Purpose

This package presents the complete **CGP-02 Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard** — 302 propositions assembled in Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0 — for **attributable whole-standard Founder Constitutional Review**.

D-01 is the decision-ready submission that enables the Founder to conduct a structured, evidence-supported review of the entire standard as a unified instrument. It does not conduct that review automatically. It does not record Founder dispositions. It does not approve, adopt, or create constitutional effect. Those acts belong to the Founder, through a separate attributable interaction, recorded in D-02.

The D-01 package is designed to be usable without requiring reconstruction of the entire programme history. Evidence is referenced, not reproduced wholesale.

---

## C. Review Boundary

The following distinctions are constitutional and must be preserved throughout this review:

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Review** | The Founder reads, considers, and records dispositions on the whole standard. |
| **Approval** | A separate, later, attributable Founder decision that the standard is approved for adoption. |
| **Adoption** | A further separate governed act giving the standard constitutional effect. |
| **Constitutional effect** | The state in which propositions bind the platform. Not created by review or approval alone. |

### What D-01 is not

- D-01 preparation **≠** Founder review decision.
- D-01 preparation **≠** approval of any proposition or the whole instrument.
- D-01 preparation **≠** adoption.
- D-01 preparation **≠** creation of constitutional effect.

### What records what

- **D-01** (this document): presents the material for Founder review.
- **D-02**: will record the actual attributable Founder review dispositions. D-02 has not been started. No D-02 disposition exists.
- **D-07** (to be prepared after D-01 through D-06 pass): prepares the Founder Approval Decision Package. Preparation ≠ execution of approval.
- **Founder Approval Decision Gate**: the separate, explicit Founder decision point after D-01 through D-06 that determines whether the whole standard is Approved, Rejected, Deferred, Amended, or Returned.

## F. Whole-Standard Review Structure

The Founder is not asked to re-read all 302 propositions as a flat questionnaire. The review is structured across ten constitutional dimensions. For each dimension, the Founder's task is to assess the whole standard against that dimension and form a disposition (recorded in D-02).

### Dimension 1 — Completeness

Does the assembled standard address all constitutionally necessary subjects within its authorized scope? Are there absent or inadequately treated subjects?

*Evidence:* CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint (blueprint subject list, 18 discovery gaps); D20 (blueprint coverage); D18 (14-package trace).

### Dimension 2 — Internal Coherence

Are the 302 propositions internally coherent as a whole? Are there cross-subject tensions, term-usage inconsistencies, or asymmetric treatments not already captured by prior validation evidence?

*Evidence:* D20 (all dimensions Pass; 0 apparent conflicts); C.13 Relationship Assessment Report; C.13 Relationship Validation Report.

### Dimension 3 — Authority Boundaries

Does the standard correctly and consistently bound constitutional authority across all 14 subjects — distinguishing Founder authority, delegation conditions, platform authority types, and the standard's own limits?

*Evidence:* D18 (source-status provenance); CGP-02C.3 (Approval Governance); CGP-02C.4 (Adoption & Constitutional Effect).

### Dimension 4 — Lifecycle Coherence

Does the standard govern the complete constitutional amendment and governance-instrument lifecycle in a coherent end-to-end manner — from initiation through review, approval, adoption, effect, and supersession?

*Evidence:* CGP-02C.2A (Governance Lifecycle), CGP-02C.2B (Amendment Classification), CGP-02C.2C (Review Triggers), CGP-02C.3, CGP-02C.4, CGP-02C.5 (Traceability), CGP-02C.8 (Supersession), CGP-02C.9 (Retirement/Withdrawal/Rejection).

### Dimension 5 — Cross-Subject Relationships

Are cross-subject dependencies, references, and constraints among the 14 subjects correctly expressed and mutually consistent?

*Evidence:* D20 (cross-subject dimensions); CGP-02C.6 (Dependent-Governance Impact Review); D17 DQ-04, DQ-05.

### Dimension 6 — Amendment and Review Discipline

Does the standard establish clear, attributable, and proportionate amendment and review discipline? Is the independent review grounding adequate given the deferred details (DQ-01, DQ-02)?

*Evidence:* CGP-02C.2B (Amendment Classification — DQ-05 deferred); CGP-02C.2C (Review Triggers — DQ-01, DQ-02 deferred); CGP-02C.1 (Foundational Principles).

### Dimension 7 — Traceability Readiness

Is the standard sufficiently structured and identified that proposition-level traceability to primary normative authority (D-04) can be established?

*Evidence:* D20 (proposition uniqueness 302/302; source crosswalk); CGP-02C.5 (Amendment Traceability Requirements); C.13 Proposition Source Crosswalk.

### Dimension 8 — Adoption and Effect Boundary

Does the standard correctly and consistently distinguish approval from adoption, and adoption from constitutional effect? Is the adoption boundary (with DQ-06 still deferred) coherently bounded?

*Evidence:* CGP-02C.4 (Founder Approved); D17 DQ-06; D18 (immutable boundaries).

### Dimension 9 — Unresolved and Deferred Matters

Given the 9 D17 deferrals, does the Founder consider the standard ready for the Approval Decision Gate with those deferrals in their current state? Are any deferrals ripe for resolution before approval?

*Evidence:* D17 register; D19 §4; D18 §5.

### Dimension 10 — Whole-Standard Approval Readiness (Preliminary Orientation)

Does the Founder's initial review indicate that the standard — subject to completing D-02 through D-06 — is on a trajectory toward the Founder Approval Decision Gate? Or are there fundamental structural concerns that would prevent reaching that gate regardless of subsequent work?

> This is a preliminary orientation. The actual approval decision (Approved / Rejected / Deferred / Amended / Returned) occurs at the Founder Approval Decision Gate, after D-01 through D-06 are complete, and is recorded in a separate attributable Founder approval decision.
