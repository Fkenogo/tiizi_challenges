## 3. Findings Accepted

The Founder accepts the following PTRA-05 findings as official Stage E0 programme evidence:

- 14 of the 16 substantive frozen Blueprint deliverables have bounded treatment;
- exactly two substantive constitutional subjects remain:
  1. Governance Index and Status Integrity; and
  2. Validation Rules;
- Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20 remain whole-instrument integration outputs;
- no Blueprint deliverable is merged into approved earlier doctrine;

- CGP-02 remains incomplete until the remaining substantive subjects, integration, whole-instrument review, approval, adoption treatment and programme transition are complete; and
- no successor work package is currently authorized.

## 4. Sequencing Recommendation Accepted

The Founder accepts the PTRA-05 sequencing recommendation for future programme planning:

1. Governance Index and Status Integrity;
2. Validation Rules;
3. one final consolidation programme covering Blueprint deliverables 17 through 20;
4. whole-instrument review and attributable Founder approval;
5. separately attributable adoption and constitutional-effect treatment; and
6. CGP-02 programme transition.

Acceptance of this sequence:

- does not establish a work-package identifier;
- does not approve a bounded scope or exclusions;
- does not create a planning package;
- does not constitute Founder work-package authorization;
- does not authorize constitutional drafting; and
- does not authorize the consolidation programme.
