## 3. Sequence Integrity

The remaining Blueprint sequence is coherent:

1. Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection depend on the completed supersession distinction.
2. Historical Preservation follows once all principal status-change and status-ending treatments can be assessed together.
3. Governance Index and Status Integrity follows constitutional status treatment and preservation so representation cannot be mistaken for the constitutive act.
4. Validation Rules follow the substantive rules so constitutional validation minima can be assessed against a complete subject set.
5. Deferred questions, status and decision trace, Founder Review material and draft self-validation are final integration deliverables.

No sequence bypass is justified.

## 4. Next-Subject Readiness

Deliverable 13 has sufficient dependency evidence for a future planning exercise because:

- C.2A supplies conceptual lifecycle distinctions without defining status-ending rules;
- C.4 prevents status treatment from silently creating adoption or effect;
- C.5 requires historical and attributable trace integrity;
- C.8 expressly distinguishes supersession from retirement, withdrawal and rejection; and
- the Blueprint and GAP-12 require dedicated treatment.

These dependencies do not answer the constitutional questions and do not authorize a package.

## 5. Remaining Risks

| Risk | Dependency control |
|---|---|
| Retirement treated as supersession without replacement | Preserve C.8’s express distinction. |
| Withdrawal or rejection treated as constitutional effect | Preserve C.3 and C.4 approval, adoption and effect boundaries. |
| Historical artefacts erased by status-ending treatment | Preserve C.1, C.5 and C.8 historical intelligibility. |
| Index entry treated as the constitutional act | Defer index treatment and preserve anti-inference. |
| Validation rules designed around current repository practice | Apply technology neutrality and distinguish programme evidence from doctrine. |
| Final integration silently changes bounded propositions | Require source-level parity and attributable change treatment. |

## 6. Dependency Verdict

Dependencies are intact. The programme is ready with prerequisites for planning deliverable 13 only. Deliverables 14–20 remain dependent on later sequence completion.

## 7. Non-Effects

This review changes no dependency, sequence, status or completion gate. It creates no package, question, proposition, decision, Authority or authorization.
