## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed bounded work package for the next governed lifecycle step of the CGP-02 programme. It translates the completed whole-instrument consolidation baseline ([CGP-02C.13](CGP-02C-13-POST-CLOSURE-STATE-AND-TRANSITION-REPORT.md)) into a decision-ready package for Founder review and approval preparation.

This definition creates no constitutional proposition, resolves no deferred question, alters no proposition text, and authorizes no work without attributable Founder decision.

---

## 2. Sequence Determination

The [Tiizi Version 2 Master Programme](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) and [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md) establish the following governed lifecycle sequence for the remainder of CGP-02:

```text
1. Discovery (CGP-02A) [Complete]
   ↓
2. Drafting Blueprint (CGP-02B.1) [Complete]
   ↓
3. Constitutional Drafting & Consolidation (CGP-02C.1–CGP-02C.13) [Complete]
   ↓
4. Whole-Standard Founder Review & Approval Preparation (CGP-02D) [Proposed]
   ├── Founder Constitutional Review (D-01, D-02)
   ├── Candidate Formulation (D-03)
   ├── Proposition Traceability & Cross-Reference Impact (D-04, D-05)
   ├── Whole-Standard Validation (D-06)
   ├── Founder Approval Decision Package & Draft Approval Record Prepared (D-07)
   ├── [FOUNDER APPROVAL DECISION GATE — Separate Attributable Founder Approval Decision]
   └── Package Completion & Transition Reporting (D-08)
   ↓
5. Whole-Standard Adoption Record [Separate Adoption Milestone]
```

With all 13 bounded drafting and consolidation packages (CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.13) closed and reconciled, the constitutional drafting phase (CGP-02C) is complete. The next governed action is the whole-standard Founder Review and Approval Preparation sub-phase, designated **CGP-02D**.

---

## 3. Proposed Subject

The proposed subject is the preparation, execution, and validation of the attributable **Founder Constitutional Review of the Complete CGP-02 Standard**, operating on the assembled [Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0](../governance/principles/33-CGP-02C-13-WHOLE-INSTRUMENT-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DRAFT-V0.md) (302 propositions), its companion integration deliverables (D17–D20), and preparing the candidate materials, traceability, and validation evidence for a separate attributable Founder approval decision.

---

## 4. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02D covers:

1. **Founder Review Package Preparation:** Presenting the frozen Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0, the 9 D17 retained deferrals, and D19 review questions for attributable Founder review;
2. **Founder Review Execution & Recording:** Recording attributable Founder decisions and dispositions on the whole instrument without presumption;
3. **Founder Approval Candidate Formulation:** If amendments or refinements are directed by the Founder, incorporating them into a bounded Founder Approval Candidate (Draft 1);
4. **Whole-Standard Validation & Traceability:** Conducting comprehensive whole-standard validation and traceability across all 302 propositions against the approved foundation baseline;
5. **Founder Approval Decision Preparation:** Preparing the Founder Approval Decision Package and Draft Approval Record for the complete CGP-02 standard;
6. **Approval Decision Recording:** Recording the attributable outcome of the separate Founder approval decision (Approved, Rejected, Deferred, Amended, or Returned);
7. **Master Programme Synchronization:** Aligning programme state to reflect the actual recorded outcome of Founder review and approval.

---

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package must NOT:

- alter any proposition text, identifier, numbering, or ordering prior to attributable Founder direction;
- predetermine, deem executed, or automatically create Founder approval;
- perform whole-instrument adoption or declare constitutional effect;
- establish an effective date or application date prior to separate adoption;
- silently resolve any of the 9 D17 deferred constitutional questions;
- complete Stage E0 or unblock CGP-03;
- allocate any accountability relationship, role, or permission;
- introduce implementation, operational, or technical mechanisms into constitutional doctrine.

## 8. Completion Criteria

CGP-02D cannot become Complete merely because an approval form or draft approval record exists.

CGP-02D is complete only when:

1. Whole-standard Founder Constitutional Review (D-01) is completed;
2. Attributable Founder review dispositions (D-02) are recorded;
3. Whole-Standard Founder Approval Candidate (D-03) is prepared and verified;
4. Proposition Traceability (D-04) is completed;
5. Cross-Reference / Impact Analysis (D-05) is completed;
6. Whole-Standard Validation (D-06) is completed and gates pass;
7. Founder Approval Decision Package / Draft Approval Record (D-07) is prepared and the Founder Approval Decision Gate is reached;
8. A separate attributable Founder approval decision is recorded (Approved, Rejected, Deferred, Amended, or Returned);
9. Programme state is synchronized according to the actual Founder outcome;
10. Bounded completion evidence (D-08) is issued;
11. No whole-instrument adoption or constitutional effect is claimed prior to separate adoption governance.

An Approved outcome is not the only valid procedural result.
