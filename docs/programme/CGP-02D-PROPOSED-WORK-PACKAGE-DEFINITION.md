## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed bounded work package for the next governed lifecycle step of the CGP-02 programme. It translates the completed whole-instrument consolidation baseline ([CGP-02C.13](CGP-02C-13-POST-CLOSURE-STATE-AND-TRANSITION-REPORT.md)) into a decision-ready package for Founder review and approval preparation.

This definition creates no constitutional proposition, resolves no deferred question, alters no proposition text, and authorizes no work without attributable Founder decision.

---

## 2. Sequence Determination

The [Tiizi Version 2 Master Programme](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) and [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md) establish the following lifecycle sequence for CGP-02:

```text
1. Discovery (CGP-02A) [Complete]
   ↓
2. Drafting Blueprint (CGP-02B.1) [Complete]
   ↓
3. Constitutional Drafting & Consolidation (CGP-02C.1–CGP-02C.13) [Complete]
   ↓
4. Whole-Standard Founder Review & Approval Preparation (CGP-02D) [Proposed]
   ↓
5. Whole-Standard Validation & Traceability [Post-Review / Approval Gate]
   ↓
6. Whole-Standard Founder Approval [Approval Milestone]
   ↓
7. Whole-Standard Adoption Record [Adoption Milestone]
```

With all 13 bounded drafting and consolidation packages (CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.13) closed and reconciled, the constitutional drafting phase (CGP-02C) is complete. The next governed action is the whole-standard Founder Review and Approval Preparation sub-phase, designated **CGP-02D**.

---

## 3. Proposed Subject

The proposed subject is the preparation, execution, and validation of the attributable **Founder Constitutional Review of the Complete CGP-02 Standard**, operating on the assembled [Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0](../governance/principles/33-CGP-02C-13-WHOLE-INSTRUMENT-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DRAFT-V0.md) (302 propositions), its companion integration deliverables (D17–D20), and preparing the candidate materials for subsequent whole-standard validation, traceability, and Founder approval.

---

## 4. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02D covers:

1. **Founder Review Package Preparation:** Presenting the frozen Whole-Instrument Founder Review Draft V0, the 9 D17 retained deferrals, and D19 review questions for attributable Founder review;
2. **Founder Review Execution & Recording:** Recording attributable Founder decisions and dispositions on the whole instrument without presumption;
3. **Founder Approval Candidate Formulation:** If amendments or refinements are directed by the Founder, incorporating them into a bounded Founder Approval Candidate (Draft 1);
4. **Whole-Standard Validation & Traceability:** Conducting comprehensive whole-standard validation and traceability across all 302 propositions against the approved foundation baseline;
5. **Approval Milestone Preparation:** Preparing the formal Founder Approval Record for the complete CGP-02 standard;
6. **Master Programme Synchronization:** Aligning programme state to reflect the outcome of Founder review.

---

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package must NOT:

- alter any proposition text, identifier, numbering, or ordering prior to attributable Founder direction;
- presume or infer Founder approval of the whole standard;
- perform whole-instrument adoption or declare constitutional effect;
- establish an effective date or application date prior to separate adoption;
- silently resolve any of the 9 D17 deferred constitutional questions;
- complete Stage E0 or unblock CGP-03;
- allocate any accountability relationship, role, or permission;
- introduce implementation, operational, or technical mechanisms into constitutional doctrine.
