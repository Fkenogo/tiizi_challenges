# CGP-02C.7 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.7

**Proposed title:** Conflict Review and Escalation

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package following CGP-02C.6. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only, creates no constitutional proposition and authorizes no drafting.

## 2. Sequence and Blueprint Mapping

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) identifies **Conflict Review and Escalation** as deliverable 11, immediately after **Dependent-Governance Impact Review**.

[PTRA-01](PTRA-01-STAGE-E0-PROGRAMME-TRANSITION-ASSESSMENT.md) confirms bounded Blueprint alignment through deliverable 10, finds no contradiction or boundary drift and recommends Conflict Review and Escalation as the next planning subject. [Master Programme Version 1.11](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records that no successor work package is authorized.

Therefore:

- the next proposed constitutional subject is **Conflict Review and Escalation**;
- the exact proposed identifier is `CGP-02C.7`;
- Blueprint order and PTRA-01 readiness do not authorize the package; and
- identifier, title, scope, structure and drafting authorization remain subject to attributable Founder decision.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The proposed package would prepare bounded constitutional treatment for governance-conflict review and escalation without resolving an actual conflict or changing constitutional precedence.

It would address one constitutional subject only: Conflict Review and Escalation. It would not decide a conflict, supersede governance, approve an amendment, allocate a reviewer or recorder, or create a Conflict Authority.

## 4. Proposed Purpose

The proposed drafting purpose is to make the constitutional boundaries of identifying, reviewing, escalating, recording and preserving a governance conflict available for Founder review while maintaining Governance Authority, precedence, historical integrity and explicit uncertainty.

This purpose identifies the planned inquiry. It does not answer a constitutional question.

## 5. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02C.7 would be limited to:

1. the constitutional meaning and purpose of governance-conflict review;
2. the constitutional basis for identifying a governance conflict;
3. the boundary among conflict identification, review and escalation;
4. the interaction between conflict review, escalation and Governance Authority;
5. the minimum categories of conflict-review evidence;
6. the minimum categories of conflict-disposition evidence where an attributable disposition exists;
7. the treatment of unresolved conflict without inferred resolution;
8. anti-inference boundaries preventing identification, review, escalation or recording from changing governance; and
9. explicit identification of questions deferred to later Blueprint subjects.

These subjects identify questions for later constitutional drafting. They do not define constitutional answers.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not draft, determine or perform:

- resolution of an actual, named or historical governance conflict;
- a precedence outcome for any conflict;
- a change to the approved precedence hierarchy;
- supersession;
- retirement;
- withdrawal;
- rejection;
- amendment approval;
- amendment adoption;
- constitutional effect, effective date or application date;
- amendment classification, review proportionality, approval governance, amendment traceability or dependent-governance impact review already treated by earlier packages;
- validation doctrine or validation procedure;
- governance-index or status synchronization;
- cross-reference updates;
- implementation workflow;
- repository process;
- reviewer, recorder, escalator or decision-maker roles;
- a Conflict Authority or any other Platform Authority;
- accountability, responsibility, role or permission allocation;
- technical schema, storage, retention, identifier format, automation, publication or deployment;
- retrospective conflict classification or disposition; or
- authorization of a successor work package.

## 8. Reserved Constitutional Questions

If drafting is later authorized, the Founder Review package must present without presuming answers:

1. What is the constitutional meaning and purpose of governance-conflict review?
2. What constitutional conditions are sufficient to identify a governance conflict?
3. How must conflict identification remain distinct from conflict review and escalation?
4. What relationship should exist between escalation and Governance Authority without creating another Authority?
5. What minimum evidence must a conflict-review record preserve?
6. What minimum evidence must be preserved where Governance Authority records a conflict disposition?
7. How must an unresolved conflict remain represented without inferred resolution, precedence or constitutional change?
8. What anti-inference boundaries prevent conflict identification, review, escalation or recording from amending governance or creating Authority?
9. Which matters must remain deferred to Supersession Rules, Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules, Historical Preservation, Governance Index and Status Integrity, Validation Rules or other later CGP-02 subjects?

These are future constitutional questions, not propositions or decisions made by this definition.

## 10. Proposed Completion Boundary

The recommendation is one bounded drafting package because conflict identification, review, escalation, evidence and unresolved treatment form one Blueprint subject.

The package would be complete for technical Founder Review only when all five expected deliverables exist, every proposition is unique and traceable, every reserved question remains pending, all exclusions remain preserved and mechanical validation passes.

Completion would not approve a proposition, resolve a conflict, adopt governance, create constitutional effect, complete CGP-02 or Stage E0, or authorize a successor package.
