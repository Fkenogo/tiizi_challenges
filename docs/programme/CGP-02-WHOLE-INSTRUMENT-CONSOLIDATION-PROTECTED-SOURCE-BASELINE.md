# CGP-02 Whole-Instrument Consolidation — Protected Source Baseline

**Programme:** Tiizi Version 2

**Stage:** Stage E0 — Governance Architecture

**Phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme-governance source-protection evidence

**Status:** Baseline captured for Founder authorization review; consolidation not authorized

**Baseline date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Purpose

This baseline identifies every constitutional source that a future authorized CGP-02 Whole-Instrument Consolidation Programme would be required to protect.

It records source identity, lifecycle and constitutional status, proposition identity, decision evidence and a SHA-256 digest. It does not alter a protected source, normalize status, consolidate content, approve an instrument, perform adoption or create constitutional effect.

## 2. Baseline Rules

The following rules govern this baseline:

- a digest identifies the exact repository bytes present on the baseline date;
- a digest does not itself establish constitutional status, approval, adoption or effect;
- a source without numbered CGP-02 propositions is recorded as not proposition-structured rather than assigned artificial proposition identifiers;
- Founder approval of questions or content for a candidate does not silently convert the candidate into a Founder Approved instrument;
- adoption and constitutional effect remain separately recorded statuses;
- inclusion in this baseline creates no precedence among sources;
- every later byte change requires a new attributable baseline comparison before consolidation work proceeds; and
- a mismatch blocks use of the affected source until it is explained through governed evidence.

## 6. Status-Preservation Finding

The source corpus is intentionally non-uniform:

- CGP-02C.4 is a Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument that is not adopted and has no constitutional effect;
- CGP-02C.5 through CGP-02C.12 are recorded in the Consolidated Decision Register as Founder Approved for candidate, not as approved instruments;
- CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.3 retain the candidate-level statuses recorded in their own metadata and Founder decision evidence; and
- the normative sources retain their pre-existing status and are not absorbed into the bounded candidate corpus.

A future consolidated document must preserve these differences and may not imply that physical integration equalizes approval, adoption or constitutional effect.

## 7. Baseline Non-Effects

This baseline:

- changes no protected source;
- changes no constitutional wording or proposition;
- performs no consolidation;
- approves no package or proposition;
- creates no adoption or constitutional effect;
- creates no Authority or accountability allocation;
- modifies neither the Master Programme nor the Consolidated Decision Register; and
- authorizes no whole-instrument work.
