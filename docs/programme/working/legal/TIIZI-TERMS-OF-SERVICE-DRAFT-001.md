## 1. About Tiizi

Tiizi is a voluntary fitness and wellness community. Members create and join Groups, join Challenges, log activity and see their progress.

Tiizi is currently an **invitation-only pilot** for invited members. It may open to more people later, and these Terms will be updated when it does. These Terms describe Tiizi as it works today and do not promise features that are not yet available.

## 2. Who can use Tiizi

Tiizi is for people aged **18 or older**. If you are under 18, do not create or use an account.

Give accurate information, keep your sign-in details safe, and email us if you think someone else has used your account. Do not pretend to be another person or use someone else's account.

## 3. Groups and Challenges are voluntary

- You choose whether to create or join a Group and which Challenges to join. Nobody has to take part in any activity or Challenge.
- You can generally leave a Group or withdraw from a Challenge, with two limits: the Accountable Steward of a Group cannot leave while responsible for it, and once a Challenge has ended you can no longer join or withdraw. If you later rejoin a Challenge, Tiizi records a new period of participation. Earlier records of your participation can remain in that Challenge's and Group's history after you leave. Leaving or withdrawing is not the same as deleting your account or erasing those records.
- Read a Group's or Challenge's details before you join. Do only what is safe and suitable for you.
- Any member can create a Group. The creator becomes the Group's **Accountable Steward**, the member responsible for the Group. Today only the Accountable Steward can change the Group's settings and approve or decline requests to join. A member's role label in a Group (such as owner, admin or member) does not by itself give these powers. There are no other Group management powers on Tiizi today.
- Tiizi does not guarantee any result, rank or outcome from taking part.

## 4. Optional contributions

- Some Challenges may, in future, offer an optional way to support a cause or to support Tiizi. Contributing is **entirely optional**.
- A contribution does **not** buy participation, progress, ranking, results, recognition or any preferential treatment. Not contributing never affects any of these.
- **Payment processing is not available in Tiizi.** These Terms do not set up any payment, custody or fund-holding arrangement. Any such feature would need its own terms before it is offered.
- A cause named by a Challenge creator is that creator's choice. Tiizi does not promise to deliver, verify or guarantee what a cause does with support.

## 5. Respect and safety

Treat other members with respect. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, defraud or impersonate others. Do not try to cheat, manipulate Challenge results or interfere with the security of Tiizi. Do not collect other members' information without permission.

If you have a safety or conduct concern, email us (section 9). Tiizi does not yet have in-app reporting. Where needed to keep members safe or to follow the law, MiledgeVentures may take reasonable steps, which can include limiting access.

## 6. What you share

You are responsible for what you choose to share, including activity you log and details you put in a Group or Challenge. Other members may see what the Group or Challenge makes visible to them. Share only what you are comfortable with others seeing. Tiizi is not medical advice; check with a health professional if you have concerns about a particular activity.

You keep ownership of what you provide. You allow Tiizi to store and show it as needed to run the features you use. How personal information is handled is explained in the Tiizi Privacy Policy.

## 7. Recognition and rewards

Challenge progress and results are shown according to each Challenge's rules. Tiizi does not currently offer prizes, rewards, awards or other recognition features and does not promise them. If a Group or Challenge creator independently offers a prize or benefit, that offer is theirs alone.

## 8. The service

We aim to keep Tiizi reliable but cannot promise uninterrupted service. Features may change, and MiledgeVentures may change, pause or stop the pilot. We will give appropriate notice of material changes to these Terms where the law requires it.

You are responsible for your own choices and conduct. Tiizi is not a party to arrangements between members. Nothing in these Terms limits any right that the law does not allow to be limited.

**Drafting notes**

- **Operator wording (§9).** Uses "MiledgeVentures" exactly. States only the Founder-confirmed facts: registered in Burundi; designed mainly for Kenya; Kenyan registration planned and not complete. It claims no Kenyan company, address or jurisdictional status, and does not describe Tiizi as Burundi-based. Corporate registration is not mentioned in §1 or elsewhere in the ordinary product description.
- **18+ (§2).** The Terms state the rule only. Sign-up has no age check and no acceptance record today. An 18+ self-declaration and a recorded acknowledgement are part of the reduced MC-3 pilot gate (FD-PILOT-01 §16.5), separately authorized and not yet built. Do not add wording that says Tiizi checks age or records acceptance until it does.
- **Stewardship and exit (§3).** Matches the code: only the Accountable Steward changes Group settings and reviews join requests; the steward cannot leave while responsible (`owner_cannot_leave`); ended Challenges cannot be joined or withdrawn from.

- **Unimplemented items not promised.** No rewards, awards, in-app reporting, automated enforcement, deletion or financial services are described as existing.
