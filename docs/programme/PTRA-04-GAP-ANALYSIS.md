# PTRA-04 — Governance Gap Analysis

**Programme:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme assessment evidence

**Status:** Analysis only; no governance changed

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This analysis identifies duplication, overlap, unresolved gaps, sequencing issues and consolidation opportunities after CGP-02C.9. It does not resolve a gap or amend a completed package.

## 2. Duplication Assessment

No completed bounded package is substantively redundant. Repetition generally applies a shared constitutional control to a different constitutional subject.

| Repeated control | Finding | Integration requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Governance Authority | Consistently preserves the existing Authority across approval, adoption, conflict and status treatment. | Retain one common Authority basis and each subject-specific non-transfer boundary. |
| Attribution | Applies separately to approvals, traces, impacts, dispositions and status changes. | Do not merge distinct attributable acts or records. |
| Anti-inference | Repeatedly prevents labels, records, indexes, repositories and implementation from creating governance. | Common wording may be harmonized only if no subject-specific protection is lost. |
| Evidence minimums | Each subject requires different evidence content. | Preserve separate evidentiary purposes while removing only literal duplication that adds no boundary. |
| Historical intelligibility | Appears across classification, traceability, impacts, conflicts, supersession and status-ending treatment. | Use Historical Preservation to consolidate the common foundation without deleting local safeguards. |
| Status separation | Approval, adoption, effect, supersession, retirement, withdrawal and rejection are mutually bounded. | Preserve all non-equivalence statements in the integrated standard. |

The nine original duplicate clusters remain either substantively treated or assigned to remaining work:

- DUP-01–DUP-05 are covered by the amendment, Authority, precedence, attribution and deferred-governance controls;
- DUP-06 belongs principally to Historical Preservation;
- DUP-07 belongs to Validation Rules;
- DUP-08 belongs to Governance Index and Status Integrity and integrated decision trace; and
- DUP-09 is treated by classification, approval and adoption boundaries, with final integration still required.

## 3. Overlap Assessment

| Overlap | Finding | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Historical Preservation and Amendment Traceability | Traceability preserves change-specific history; Historical Preservation must govern broader historical intelligibility. | Collapsing them would make amendment records the sole preservation doctrine. |
| Historical Preservation and status-ending treatments | C.8 and C.9 preserve history but expressly defer preservation mechanisms and general doctrine. | Repetition could be mistaken for complete deliverable 14 coverage. |
| Governance Index and Dependent-Governance Impact Review | Impact review identifies affected references; it does not synchronize them. | Index updates could be inferred from impact identification. |
| Governance Index and constitutional status | An index represents status but cannot establish it. | Documentary placement could be mistaken for Governance Authority. |
| Validation and governance review | Review evaluates substance and consequence; validation tests required integrity evidence. | Validation could become an unauthorized approval or review tier. |
| Governance Status and Decision Trace versus Amendment Traceability | A whole-programme decision trace is broader than an amendment trace. | One record could be treated as substituting for another. |

## 4. Unresolved Constitutional Gaps

### 4.1 Historical Preservation

Distributed safeguards do not yet define a complete technology-neutral preservation doctrine. Blueprint deliverable 14 remains partially completed.

### 4.2 Governance Index and Status Integrity

GAP-17 and CON-08 remain open. No constitutional rule identifies the relationship between authoritative status, current-baseline representations, indexes, registers and inconsistent status metadata.

### 4.3 Validation Rules

GAP-16 remains open. Current validation reports differ by package and are evidence of practice, not a universal constitutional minimum.

### 4.4 Whole-Instrument Integration

No integrated CGP-02 draft exists. The programme has not yet:

- consolidated all bounded propositions in Blueprint order;
- reconciled the different source-package approval statuses;
- mapped all 18 gaps, 8 conflicts and 9 duplicate clusters at whole-instrument level;
- produced integrated deferred-governance and non-effects sections;
- produced a complete decision trace;
- isolated genuinely unresolved Founder Review matters; or
- run full-draft self-validation.

### 4.5 Source-Status Heterogeneity

The bounded sources have different attributable statuses:

- CGP-02C.1 and C.2A–C are candidates prepared from in-principle dispositions and remain unapproved;
- CGP-02C.3 and C.5–C.9 contain propositions approved for candidates but remain unadopted and ineffective; and
- CGP-02C.4 is a Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument but remains unadopted and ineffective.

Integration must preserve these distinctions until an attributable whole-instrument decision changes them.

### 4.6 CGP-02C.9 Authorization Evidence Conflict

The [CGP-02C.9 Founder Authorization Record](CGP-02C-9-FOUNDER-AUTHORIZATION-RECORD.md) states that FWA-01 through FWA-05 are unresolved and drafting is blocked. The [CGP-02C.9 Authorization Validation Report](CGP-02C-9-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md) states the same.

By contrast, the [Master Programme](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md), [CGP-02C.9 Completion Report](../governance/principles/25-CGP-02C-9-COMPLETION-REPORT.md) and attributable work instruction record authorization and bounded completion.

This documentary inconsistency must be reconciled from attributable evidence before another drafting authorization. PTRA-04 does not decide which text changes or perform the reconciliation.

### 4.7 Decision-Register Coverage

The Consolidated Decision Register records work-package rows for CGP-02C.4 through C.9, but not equivalent rows for C.1 through C.3. This may reflect their differing statuses, but the integrated Governance Status and Decision Trace deliverable must explicitly disposition the difference rather than infer equivalence.

## 6. Sequencing Issues

No Blueprint reorder is justified.

The sole pre-sequence issue is the C.9 authorization-evidence conflict. Once documentary consistency is restored through a separately attributable action:

1. Historical Preservation should precede index treatment because status representations must preserve historically applicable meaning.
2. Governance Index and Status Integrity should precede Validation Rules because validation must test the completed status-representation boundary.
3. Integration should follow all substantive subjects to avoid repeatedly restructuring the whole instrument.
4. Whole-instrument Founder Review and approval must follow integration and validation.

## 7. Consolidation Opportunities

### 7.1 Appropriate Consolidation

- Combine Blueprint deliverables 17–20 into one final integration and Founder Review preparation activity.
- Consolidate shared definitions and common Authority or anti-inference foundations with exact source trace.
- Produce one integrated deferred-governance section while preserving each source deferral.
- Produce one integrated status-and-decision trace that records source status without upgrading it.
- Use one full-draft self-validation against Blueprint gaps, conflicts, duplicate clusters and mechanical checks.

### 7.2 Inappropriate Consolidation

- Do not merge Historical Preservation with Amendment Traceability.
- Do not merge Governance Index with Dependent-Governance Impact Review.
- Do not merge Validation Rules with review triggers or approval governance.
- Do not collapse approval, adoption, effect or status-ending terms into a generic lifecycle event.
- Do not remove repeated non-effects unless exact review proves that no distinct boundary is lost.

## 8. Gap Verdict

No constitutional contradiction requires amendment of a completed package. Three substantive subjects and one integration sequence remain. The C.9 authorization-evidence conflict is a documentary prerequisite before further drafting authorization.

This analysis creates no correction, proposition, Founder question, decision or authorization.
