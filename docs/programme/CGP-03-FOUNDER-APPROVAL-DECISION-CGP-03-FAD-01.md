## 1. Decision Context

CGP-03 — Governance Documentation & Traceability Standard is the second substantive governance instrument developed under Stage E0 (Governance Architecture), following CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard.

CGP-01 §10 explicitly identified "governance documentation and traceability" as a potential future governance domain. CGP-03 is the realization of that domain.

CGP-03's dependency on CGP-02 (completion and approval) was satisfied by FAD-01 (2026-08-31) and FLD-01 (2026-09-01). CGP-03 was unblocked and ready for Founder-authorized commencement.

The Founder accepted the integrated CGP-03 v0.1 draft (40 propositions, 12 sections) for repository reconciliation and validation. The [reconciliation report](CGP-03-REPOSITORY-RECONCILIATION-AND-VALIDATION-REPORT.md) confirmed:

- All 50 validation checks PASS.
- No blocking constitutional conflicts.
- Full consistency with PC-01, PAM-01, CGP-01, CGP-02, FAD-01, FLD-01.
- Classification A — YES, READY FOR DIRECT FOUNDER APPROVAL.
