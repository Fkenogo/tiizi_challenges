## E. Founder Rationale

### WRQ-01 — Completeness

The CGP-02 whole standard is sufficiently complete for its stated constitutional purpose and for progression through the whole-standard review lifecycle. The assembled corpus preserves the authorised subject coverage and proposition baseline, and the completed consolidation, integrity, relationship and self-validation evidence does not identify a material omitted CGP-02 subject. The nine D17 matters remain expressly deferred and visible; their unresolved status does not by itself constitute incompleteness of the assembled standard or require reopening the completed bounded drafting packages.

### WRQ-02 — Internal Coherence

The CGP-02 whole standard is internally coherent for its stated constitutional purpose. Whole-instrument integrity validation, constitutional relationship assessment and subsequent self-validation provide sufficient evidence that the constituent subjects can operate together without a presently identified material contradiction. CRA-001 through CRA-045 remain closed under their attributable Founder dispositions. The nine D17 deferred matters remain visible unresolved questions but do not presently establish an internal conflict within the assembled standard.

### WRQ-03 — Authority Boundary Integrity

The CGP-02 whole standard sufficiently preserves and distinguishes constitutional authority, Founder authority, delegated authority, administrative execution, decision recording and implementation for its stated constitutional purpose. The completed constituent reviews, relationship assessment and whole-instrument validation do not identify a material authority ambiguity, overreach or under-specification requiring correction before the review continues. DQ-11 remains an expressly deferred question concerning detailed recorder/actor duty allocation; its unresolved status does not presently transfer, enlarge or obscure the underlying authority exercised in an attributable decision.

**Preserved interpretive boundary:** Neither documentation, repository state, automation nor technical capability independently creates constitutional authority.

### WRQ-04 — Lifecycle Coherence

The CGP-02 whole standard establishes a sufficiently coherent end-to-end constitutional amendment and governance-review lifecycle for the whole-standard review to continue. The assembled standard maintains material distinctions among initiation, classification, authority, review, decision, approval, adoption/effect, recording, historical preservation and subsequent review or amendment. The D17 deferred matters concerning detailed lifecycle mechanisms remain expressly unresolved and do not presently establish a sequencing contradiction or material lifecycle gap requiring correction.

DQ-06 remains particularly relevant to the eventual Founder Approval Decision Gate and must remain visible for explicit Founder treatment before any assumption is made regarding whole-instrument adoption or constitutional effect.

### WRQ-05 — Cross-Subject Relationship Integrity

The cross-subject dependencies, references and constitutional constraints within the CGP-02 whole standard are sufficiently expressed and mutually consistent for the review lifecycle to continue. The dedicated Constitutional Relationship Assessment examined the assembled subject relationships through CRA-001–CRA-045, all of which received attributable Founder acceptance without amendment follow-up, and D20 subsequently reconciled all 45 dispositions. Whole-instrument validation also found no material reference or integrity defect requiring correction. The D17 deferred matters remain visible where further procedural or operational detail may later be required, but no presently identified cross-subject relationship requires correction or clarification.

### WRQ-06 — Amendment and Review Discipline

The CGP-02 whole standard establishes sufficient constitutional amendment and review discipline for its stated purpose. It preserves attributable authority, controlled change, historical integrity and the distinction between review, decision, amendment and implementation. No completed review evidence identifies a material defect that would permit constitutional meaning to be altered informally or through implementation alone. DQ-01, DQ-02 and DQ-05 remain expressly deferred matters concerning detailed review triggers, schedules, qualifications and amendment-classification mechanisms; their unresolved status does not presently undermine the governing requirement that constitutional change occur through controlled and attributable authority.

### WRQ-07 — Traceability Readiness

The CGP-02 whole standard is sufficiently traceable to continue toward an approval candidate. Its protected source boundaries, proposition identities, consolidation history, constitutional relationship assessment, Founder dispositions and deferred matters remain reconstructable without treating programme evidence as constitutional authority.

Final approval-candidate traceability is not asserted. D-04, D-05 and D-06 remain required later CGP-02D controls. Broader governance-documentation and repository traceability remain appropriately reserved for CGP-03 rather than being silently absorbed into CGP-02.

### WRQ-08 — Adoption and Effect Boundary

The CGP-02 whole standard sufficiently preserves the constitutional distinction among preparation, review, approval, adoption, application and constitutional effect. No drafting, assembly, validation, repository action, implementation action or present Founder review disposition independently creates approval, adoption, application or constitutional effect.

DQ-06 and DQ-07 remain expressly unresolved concerning whether a separate adoption act is required and the meaning, necessity, effect and record form of application.

**Founder control:** DQ-06 and DQ-07 must remain visible through D-03–D-07 and may not be silently resolved by document preparation, technical implementation or inference. Their required treatment must be expressly addressed before the programme records any adoption, application or constitutional effect for the CGP-02 whole standard.

### WRQ-09 — D17 Deferred Matters — Review-Stage Treatment

The nine D17 deferred constitutional matters have been reconsidered at whole-standard review stage. None presently requires promotion into a mandatory pre-approval correction or clarification of the CGP-02 proposition baseline.

DQ-06 and DQ-07 also need not prevent preparation of the approval candidate or the eventual Founder Approval Decision Gate, but remain expressly controlled unresolved matters.
