## 4. Proposed Scope

The complete proposed scope is defined in the [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-4-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md#4-proposed-included-scope).

Drafting would address only:

- adoption;
- constitutional effect;
- approval/adoption/effect distinctions;
- approval, adoption, effective and application date distinctions;
- conceptual conditions for adoption and effect;
- the decision/status boundary; and
- anti-inference boundaries.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

Every exclusion in the [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-4-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md#5-explicit-exclusions) is binding on any later drafting authorization.

In particular, the package must not adopt an existing candidate, define later Blueprint subjects, create an Authority, allocate accountability, prescribe implementation or update the Master Programme through drafting.

## 6. Expected Deliverables

If drafting is authorized, produce only:

1. Founder Review Draft;
2. Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation;
3. Founder Review Decision Pack;
4. Drafting Notes; and
5. Bounded Change and Scope Report.

The Founder Review Draft must remain proposed governance. A Founder Decision Record or Founder Approval Candidate would require a later attributable review outcome and separate instruction.

## 7. Proposed Drafting Controls

Any authorized drafting must:

- contain one constitutional subject only;
- trace every proposition to an approved normative source or explicit Founder question;
- preserve all earlier CGP-02C packages unchanged;
- preserve Approval Governance without reopening APV-01 through APV-24;
- avoid adopting or making any instrument effective;
- avoid creating adoption procedure, implementation workflow or repository process;
- state every deferral and exclusion explicitly;
- create no new Platform Authority;
- allocate no accountability relationship; and
- remain unapproved until attributable Founder decision.

## 8. Authorization Prerequisites

Before drafting may begin:

- the Founder must approve or amend the identifier;
- the Founder must approve or amend the title;
- the Founder must approve or amend the bounded scope and exclusions;
- the Founder must approve one-package treatment or an alternative decomposition;
- the Founder must expressly authorize drafting;
- the [Dependency Verification](CGP-02C-4-DEPENDENCY-VERIFICATION.md) must remain satisfied;
- the [Founder Authorization Validation Checklist](FOUNDER-WORK-PACKAGE-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-CHECKLIST.md) must pass for the completed authorization record; and
- the Master Programme must be synchronized to identify the authorized package as the next permitted action.

## 10. Proposed Completion Gates

The bounded drafting package would be ready for technical Founder Review only when:

- all five expected deliverables exist;
- every proposition is unique and traceable;
- all reserved Founder questions are presented without presumed approval;
- the Founder Review Draft remains within the authorized subject;
- every exclusion and deferral is preserved;
- earlier packages and the Master Programme remain unchanged during drafting;
- relative links and Markdown validation pass; and
- `git diff --check` passes.

These are drafting-package gates. They do not adopt the resulting draft or make it effective.

## 11. Planning Non-Effects

This package:

- does not authorize CGP-02C.4;
- does not draft a constitutional proposition;
- does not approve an answer to a reserved question;
- does not adopt or make effective any governance;
- does not amend the Master Programme;
- does not authorize a later package;
- creates no Platform Authority; and
- allocates no accountability.

## 12. Planning Recommendation

Submit the proposed identifier, title, scope, one-package structure and drafting authorization as five separate Founder programme decisions. Drafting may begin only after an Authorized outcome, passed validation and Master Programme synchronization.
