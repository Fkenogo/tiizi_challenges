## 3. Executive Assessment

CGP-02 is aligned with the frozen Blueprint through deliverable 12, Supersession Rules. Twelve of twenty Blueprint deliverables now have bounded treatment. One later subject is partially treated, three substantive or dedicated subjects remain, and four cross-cutting integration and review deliverables remain.

No contradiction, circular dependency, Authority leakage, accountability allocation or material boundary drift was identified across CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.8. Repeated treatment of Governance Authority, evidence, status, attribution, historical intelligibility and anti-inference is complementary, but those repetitions will require controlled consolidation before whole-instrument Founder Review.

The programme is not ready for integrated Founder Review, final CGP-02 approval or adoption. It is **Ready with prerequisites** for a later planning and authorization activity concerning the next ordered Blueprint subject, Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules. This assessment does not create that activity or authorize drafting.
