# CGP-02C.9 — Proposed Work Package Definition

**Proposed identifier:** CGP-02C.9

**Proposed title:** Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules

**Programme stage:** E0 — Governance Architecture

**Programme phase:** CGP-02 — Constitutional Amendment & Governance Review Standard

**Document type:** Programme planning and authorization evidence

**Status:** Proposed; not Founder-authorized and not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed bounded constitutional drafting work package following CGP-02C.8. It establishes a decision-ready programme boundary only, creates no constitutional proposition or Founder constitutional question and authorizes no drafting.

## 2. Sequence and Blueprint Mapping

The frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md#10-drafting-deliverables) identifies **Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules** as deliverable 13, immediately after **Supersession Rules**.

[PTRA-03](PTRA-03-STAGE-E0-GOVERNANCE-ARCHITECTURE-ASSESSMENT.md) has been accepted as programme evidence. It confirms bounded Blueprint treatment through deliverable 12, finds no contradiction or dependency break and recommends planning only for deliverable 13. [Master Programme Version 1.15](TIIZI-V2-MASTER-PROGRAMME.md) records that no successor work package is authorized.

Therefore:

- the next proposed constitutional subject is **Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules**;
- the exact proposed identifier is `CGP-02C.9`;
- Blueprint order and accepted PTRA-03 evidence do not authorize the package; and
- identifier, title, scope, structure and drafting authorization remain subject to attributable Founder programme decision.

## 3. Proposed Constitutional Subject

The proposed package would prepare bounded constitutional treatment for retirement, withdrawal and rejection and their distinctions from supersession, amendment, approval, adoption, constitutional effect and historical preservation.

It would address one constitutional subject only: the constitutional treatment and separation of retirement, withdrawal and rejection. It would not apply those treatments to an existing instrument, define historical-preservation doctrine or change any current governance status.

## 4. Proposed Purpose

The proposed drafting purpose is to make the constitutional meanings, boundaries, attributable basis, evidence and anti-inference protections for retirement, withdrawal and rejection available for later Founder review while preserving existing Governance Authority and all distinctions established by earlier CGP-02 packages.

This purpose identifies the planned inquiry. It does not define a constitutional answer.

## 5. Proposed Included Scope

Subject to Founder authorization, CGP-02C.9 would be limited to planning for later drafting concerning:

1. the constitutional meaning and purpose of retirement;
2. the constitutional meaning and purpose of withdrawal;
3. the constitutional meaning and purpose of rejection;
4. the distinctions among retirement, withdrawal and rejection;
5. the distinction between those treatments and supersession;
6. the distinction between those treatments and amendment;
7. the distinction between those treatments and approval;
8. the distinction between those treatments and adoption;
9. the distinction between those treatments and constitutional effect;
10. the distinction between those treatments and historical preservation;
11. interaction with existing Governance Authority without creating another Authority;
12. attributable and bounded treatment;
13. minimum evidence categories at a constitutional level;
14. anti-inference boundaries preventing labels, records, indexes, repository actions, implementation or non-use from silently establishing a treatment; and
15. explicit deferral of later Blueprint subjects.

These are possible headings of inquiry for later authorized drafting. They are not constitutional propositions, questions or decisions.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The proposed package would not draft, determine or perform:

- retirement, withdrawal or rejection of an actual, named, current or historical governance instrument;
- a determination that an existing instrument is retired, withdrawn, rejected or otherwise non-current;
- amendment, approval, adoption, constitutional effect, effective date or application date;
- supersession or a determination that an instrument has been superseded;
- conflict identification, escalation, resolution or disposition;
- historical-preservation doctrine;
- historical storage, retention, archival, deletion, destruction, restoration or versioning mechanisms;
- governance-index, status, manifest or cross-reference synchronization;
- validation doctrine or validation procedure;
- temporary or emergency governance;
- implementation workflow;
- repository procedure;
- reviewer, recorder, decision-maker, publisher, custodian or maintainer roles;
- a Retirement, Withdrawal or Rejection Authority or any other Platform Authority;
- accountability, responsibility, role or permission allocation;
- technical schema, storage, identifier format, automation, publication or deployment;
- retrospective status correction or inferred historical treatment;
- constitutional propositions or Founder constitutional questions during planning;
- Founder Review material during planning; or
- authorization of a successor work package.

This planning task creates no Founder constitutional questions. Any constitutional uncertainty concerning retirement, withdrawal or rejection must remain explicit and unanswered until constitutional drafting and Founder Review are separately authorized.

Programme-authorization decisions FWA-01 through FWA-05 concern only identifier, title, planning boundary, package structure and permission to draft. They do not answer constitutional uncertainty.

## 10. Proposed Completion Boundary

The recommendation is one bounded drafting package because the three treatments and their mutual and external boundaries form one Blueprint subject.

Completion would not retire, withdraw, reject, amend, approve, adopt or supersede governance; create constitutional effect; complete CGP-02 or Stage E0; unblock CGP-03; or authorize a successor package.
