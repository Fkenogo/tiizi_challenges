### F2. Founder Authority (P28)

P28 — Direct Founder Decision: "Where the Founder already has sufficient evidence and authority to make a bounded governance decision, Tiizi governance may proceed directly to an attributable Founder decision without requiring a separate preparation package unless preparation is materially necessary."

**Assessment:** This accurately reflects existing Founder authority. PC-01 reserves governance authority to the Founder. PAM-01 §4.K sources governance authority from Founder approval. PAM-01 §5.1 requires explicit attributable approval for constitutional instruments. P28 does not bypass any constitutionally mandatory process — it recognizes that the Founder may decide directly when evidence and authority are sufficient.

FLD-01 itself exemplifies this principle: the Founder determined the post-approval lifecycle directly, without a separate preparation package, because the evidence (FAD-01, D-08, D17) was already sufficient.

The question is whether existing authority REQUIRES additional process, not whether precedent happened to contain it. No existing authority mandates a preparation package for every Founder decision.

**F2 result: PASS.**
