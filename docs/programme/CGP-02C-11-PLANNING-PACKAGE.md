## 4. Proposed Constitutional Scope

The future package would be bounded to the constitutional purpose and integrity boundaries of governance indexes and status representations.

It would plan treatment for:

- the distinction between constitutional status and its documentary representation;
- integrity of the current constitutional baseline;
- historical references that remain intelligible without changing governance;
- stale, incomplete or inconsistent status representations;
- constitutional relationships among indexes, registers, manifests and status representations;
- technology neutrality;
- anti-inference; and
- explicit deferral.

This section identifies scope only. It defines no constitutional treatment.

## 5. Explicit Exclusions

## 13. Planning Risks and Controls

| Risk | Planning control |
|---|---|
| A documentary representation is treated as creating constitutional status | Preserve attributable Governance Authority and explicit anti-inference boundaries. |
| An index is treated as constitutional Authority | Separate status representation from the constitutional act that establishes status. |
| Existing repository structure is constitutionalized | Exclude named files, locations, tooling, schemas and workflows. |
| Stale information is silently treated as corrected governance | Exclude named-instrument correction and preserve historical attribution. |
| Historical references are overwritten by the current baseline | Preserve C.10 Historical Preservation and distinguish current from historical context. |
| Index, register and manifest labels are treated as interchangeable truth | Plan their constitutional relationship without inferring equivalence or precedence. |
| Status integrity becomes operational synchronization | Exclude synchronization processes, timing, triggers and maintenance procedures. |
| Maintenance responsibility or Authority is allocated | Exclude actors, roles, responsibility and new Authority. |
| Validation practice enters deliverable 15 | Defer Validation Rules to deliverable 16. |
