# CGP-02C.4 — Governance Retrospective and Standardization Proposal

**Document type:** Programme retrospective and standardization proposal

**Status:** Proposal for Founder review; not constitutional governance

**Date:** 2026-07-23

**Reviewed work package:** CGP-02C.4 — Adoption and Constitutional Effect

## 1. Purpose and Boundary

This report captures the constitutional production methodology demonstrated by CGP-02C.4 and identifies practices that may justify programme-wide standardization.

It does not draft, approve, adopt or make effective a new standard. It does not amend CGP-02C.4, the Master Programme or any approved constitutional instrument. Any recommended standard requires its own planning, Founder authorization, drafting, review, approval, adoption and effect decisions.

## 2. Executive Retrospective

CGP-02C.4 demonstrated a controlled chain from bounded programme planning to attributable Founder approval:

```text
Programme position
      ↓
Bounded planning
      ↓
Founder drafting authorization
      ↓
Founder Review Draft
      ↓
Founder constitutional decisions
      ↓
Founder Approval Candidate
      ↓
Founder approval closure
      ↓
Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument
      ↓
Validation, register and programme synchronization
```

The strongest feature was explicit separation among authorization, drafting, constitutional decision, approval, adoption and constitutional effect. The work package closed as Founder Approved without silently becoming adopted or constitutionally effective.

The principal standardization opportunity is to make this evidence chain predictable across future constitutional work packages while retaining proportionality for simpler packages.

## 3. Lifecycle Audit

### 3.1 Planning Package

**Primary evidence:** [CGP-02C.4 Planning Package](CGP-02C-4-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md)

**Supporting evidence:**

- [Proposed Work Package Definition](CGP-02C-4-PROPOSED-WORK-PACKAGE-DEFINITION.md);
- [Dependency Verification](CGP-02C-4-DEPENDENCY-VERIFICATION.md);
- [Founder Authorization Package](CGP-02C-4-FOUNDER-AUTHORIZATION-PACKAGE.md).

**Method demonstrated:**

- one bounded constitutional subject;
- explicit included scope and exclusions;
- identified normative sources and dependencies;
- declared deliverables, entry gates and completion gates;
- explicit non-effects;
- no drafting before authorization.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory in substance. The evidence may be consolidated into fewer documents for a low-complexity package, but identity, scope, exclusions, dependencies, deliverables and gates must remain explicit.

### 3.2 Founder Authorization

**Evidence:** [Founder Authorization Record](CGP-02C-4-FOUNDER-AUTHORIZATION-RECORD.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- separate decisions for identifier, title, scope, package structure and drafting authorization;
- canonical Founder attribution;
- authorization conditions;
- post-decision entry-gate validation;
- explicit statement that authorization approved no constitutional proposition.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory. Sequence, readiness or repository presence must never substitute for attributable authorization.

### 3.3 Founder Review Draft

**Evidence:** [Founder Review Draft](../governance/principles/14-CGP-02C-4-ADOPTION-AND-CONSTITUTIONAL-EFFECT-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DRAFT.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- proposition identifiers;
- one-subject drafting boundary;
- status metadata;
- explicit exclusions and deferred subjects;
- direct links to planning and authorization evidence;
- no self-approval.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory for proposition-bearing constitutional work. The draft must remain historical after approval.

### 3.4 Founder Review Decision Pack

**Evidence:** [Founder Review Decision Pack](../governance/principles/14-CGP-02C-4-FOUNDER-REVIEW-DECISION-PACK.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- one decision per unresolved constitutional question;
- current doctrine, proposed treatment, alternatives, recommendation and consequences;
- exact proposition impact;
- blank decision fields before Founder review;
- preservation of historical recommendations after decisions were recorded.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory where the draft contains a Founder question or competing constitutional treatment. A package with no unresolved question should instead contain an explicit no-decision-required assessment.

### 3.5 Founder Decision Record

**Evidence:** [Founder Decision Record](../governance/principles/14-CGP-02C-4-FOUNDER-DECISION-RECORD.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- attributable capacity and date;
- selected option and disposition for every question;
- exact Founder amendment;
- affected propositions;
- preserved non-effects;
- no interpretation beyond the issued decision.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory whenever a Founder decision is required. It must remain historical and attributable.

### 3.6 Founder Approval Candidate

**Evidence:** [Founder Approval Candidate](../governance/principles/14-CGP-02C-4-ADOPTION-AND-CONSTITUTIONAL-EFFECT-FOUNDER-APPROVAL-CANDIDATE.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- only Founder-approved propositions;
- exact proposition identifiers and traceability;
- approval-ready status boundary;
- no adoption or constitutional effect;
- later use as the authoritative approval record.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory before approval of proposition-bearing constitutional content. The future standard should decide whether the candidate itself receives approval metadata, as in CGP-02C.4, or remains immutable while a separate Founder Approval Record carries approval metadata.

### 3.7 Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument

**Evidence:** [Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument](../governance/principles/15-CGP-02C-4-ADOPTION-AND-CONSTITUTIONAL-EFFECT-FOUNDER-APPROVED.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- approved constitutional body preserved from the candidate;
- approving authority and date;
- direct links to historical evidence;
- explicit distinction among Founder approval, adoption and constitutional effect;
- no implied effective or application date.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory after Founder approval. The instrument must communicate its exact status without implying adoption or effect.

### 3.8 Approval Validation Report

**Evidence:** [Approval Validation Report](../governance/principles/15-CGP-02C-4-APPROVAL-VALIDATION-REPORT.md)

**Method demonstrated:**

- approval evidence check;
- constitutional-body parity;
- status-transition matrix;
- boundary and non-effect validation;
- register and programme checks;
- repository validation;
- explicit isolation of pre-existing validation warnings.

**Retrospective finding:** Mandatory. Validation must distinguish package defects from pre-existing repository debt and must not repair unrelated governance silently.

## 4. Artefact Classification

### 4.1 Mandatory Artefacts or Evidence

| Artefact or evidence | Mandatory treatment | Proportionality allowance |
|---|---|---|
| Work Package Definition and Planning Package | Required | May be one document if every required field remains explicit. |
| Dependency Verification | Required | May be a planning section for a simple package. |
| Founder Authorization Record | Required | Must remain a separate attributable record. |
| Founder Review Draft | Required for constitutional propositions | Not required for a non-drafting documentary package. |
| Proposition Traceability and Self-Validation | Required for proposition-bearing drafts | A non-proposition package may use requirement-level traceability. |
| Founder Decision Pack | Required when constitutional questions exist | Replace with a no-decision-required assessment only when no question exists. |
| Founder Decision Record | Required when decisions exist | Must record every disposition separately. |
| Founder Approval Candidate | Required before constitutional approval | Candidate-body freeze must be verifiable. |
| Authoritative approval record | Required | May be the Candidate with approval metadata or a separate record, subject to a future canonical decision. |
| Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument | Required after approval | Must preserve approved body content. |
| Approval Validation Report | Required | Depth may scale with constitutional consequence. |
| Register and programme synchronization evidence | Required | Update only the registers and programme fields affected by the status change. |
| Historical artefact preservation | Required | Historical drafts and decisions remain attributable and unchanged except status metadata where governed. |

### 4.2 Optional or Conditional Artefacts

| Artefact | When useful or required |
|---|---|
| Separate Founder Authorization Package | Useful where authorization has multiple decisions; may be integrated with planning for a simple package. |
| Drafting Notes | Required where ambiguity, avoided assumptions or architectural observations need preservation. |
| Bounded Change and Scope Report | Recommended for packages with multiple companion documents or a dirty worktree. |
| Independent Constitutional Review | Required only where later approved review-trigger and proportionality governance requires it. |
| Founder Amendment Log | Required when amendments are extensive enough that the Decision Record alone does not provide efficient comparison. |
| Completion or Transition Report | Useful where closure unlocks a successor dependency or changes several programme records. |
| Manifest or index update report | Required only where the status transition affects a governed manifest or index. |
| Body-integrity hash | Recommended when metadata differs between candidate and approved instrument or when byte parity is material. |

## 5. Reusable Templates

The following templates should be prepared if a future standard is authorized:

1. **Constitutional Work Package Definition**
   - identifier, title, subject, purpose, included scope, exclusions, dependencies, deliverables and gates.
2. **Founder Authorization Record**
   - planning decisions, selected options, conditions, non-effects and entry-gate validation.
3. **Founder Review Draft**
   - canonical metadata, proposition format, scope boundary and deferred-governance statement.
4. **Proposition Traceability Register**
   - proposition, treatment, source, assessment and status.
5. **Founder Review Decision Pack**
   - question, doctrine, options, recommendation, consequences and affected propositions.
6. **Founder Decision Record**
   - selected option, outcome, date, notes, rationale, amendments and affected propositions.
7. **Founder Approval Candidate**
   - frozen approved proposition body, exclusions, decision and traceability links, and non-effect statement.
8. **Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument**
   - approval metadata, approval-record link, historical links and explicit adoption/effect status.
9. **Approval Validation Report**
   - content parity, status transition, boundary checks, register checks, programme checks and repository checks.

The template content should remain configurable by constitutional consequence. Templates must not create decisions, Authority, approval, adoption or effect.

## 6. Reusable Validation Patterns

| Pattern | Reusable control |
|---|---|
| Identifier integrity | Count, uniqueness and expected sequence for proposition and decision identifiers. |
| Traceability bijection | Every proposition has exactly one traceability row; no traceability row points to a missing proposition. |
| Candidate parity | Candidate propositions match the reviewed and Founder-amended source exactly. |
| Approved-body parity | Approved constitutional body matches the approved candidate body, excluding governed status metadata. |
| Founder amendment test | Every issued amendment appears exactly where directed and nowhere else. |
| Status-transition test | Draft, Candidate, approved instrument, adoption and effect statuses remain distinct. |
| Authority test | No new Platform Authority or silent extension, transfer or allocation. |
| Scope test | One constitutional subject; excluded and deferred subjects remain absent. |
| Non-effect test | Authorization, drafting, review, approval, repository action and programme synchronization create no unintended adoption or effect. |
| Protected-file test | Hash or compare higher-order and completed instruments that must remain unchanged. |
| Link test | Resolve new and changed links; report pre-existing link debt separately rather than silently altering it. |
| Markdown hygiene | Heading consistency, trailing whitespace, EOF and `git diff --check`. |
| Worktree isolation | Explicit changed-file allowlist, staged-file count and separation from unrelated worktree entries. |
| Programme consistency | Dashboard, current focus, current position, stage status, dependencies and change log agree. |
| Register consistency | Approval status, record, date, adoption status and effect status agree across registers. |

## 7. Reusable Governance Controls

The following controls demonstrated durable value:

- one bounded constitutional subject per work package;
- planning before constitutional drafting;
- attributable Founder authorization before work begins;
- evidence before interpretation;
- no doctrine from repeated practice alone;
- explicit uncertainty and reserved Founder questions;
- Authority discipline;
- proposition-level traceability;
- separate recommendation and decision records;
- exact recording of Founder amendments;
- Candidate freeze before approval;
- explicit separation of approval, adoption and constitutional effect;
- no status by filename, repository action, implementation or programme sequence;
- historical preservation;
- no successor authorization by implication;
- programme and register synchronization after attributable status change;
- validation against a declared change boundary.

## 8. Recommended Reusable Lifecycle

### Phase 0 — Programme Position and Eligibility

- confirm the Master Programme position;
- identify the constitutional subject;
- confirm dependencies and blockers;
- confirm that planning is the authorized next action.

**Gate:** planning may begin; drafting remains unauthorized.

### Phase 1 — Bounded Planning

- define identity, purpose, scope, exclusions, sources, dependencies, deliverables and gates;
- identify Founder authorization questions;
- produce dependency verification.

**Gate:** planning accepted for Founder authorization.

### Phase 2 — Founder Work-Package Authorization

- record identifier, title, scope, structure and drafting authorization;
- validate every authorization condition;
- synchronize programme state only if required.

**Gate:** constitutional drafting explicitly authorized.

### Phase 3 — Founder Review Drafting

- draft one constitutional subject;
- assign proposition identifiers;
- produce traceability, self-validation and decision material;
- preserve exclusions and deferred matters.

**Gate:** technical review confirms readiness for Founder constitutional review.

### Phase 4 — Founder Constitutional Decisions

- present each unresolved question independently;
- record selected option, disposition, amendment, rationale, date and affected propositions;
- amend only as directed.

**Gate:** every proposition has an attributable disposition.

### Phase 5 — Founder Approval Candidate

- freeze the Founder-approved proposition body;
- verify proposition and traceability parity;
- preserve historical Review Draft and Decision Record;
- state that the Candidate is not adopted or effective.

**Gate:** constitutional review confirms readiness for Founder approval.

### Phase 6 — Founder Approval Closure

- record attributable Founder approval;
- preserve the Candidate as approval evidence under the canonical model later approved;
- issue the Founder Approved Constitutional Instrument;
- validate body identity, statuses, boundaries and non-effects;
- synchronize affected registers and programme tracking.

**Gate:** Founder approval is recorded. Adoption and effect remain separate unless explicitly declared.

### Phase 7 — Adoption and Constitutional Effect

- occur only through separately attributable Governance Authority action;
- identify adoption, constitutional effect and any applicable dates explicitly;
- do not infer status from approval closure or programme synchronization.

**Gate:** determined by later approved governance; CGP-02C.4 itself demonstrates that approval may close without adoption or effect.

### Phase 8 — Programme Transition

- confirm closure evidence;
- identify but do not infer the next subject;
- require new planning and Founder authorization before successor drafting.

**Gate:** Master Programme and dependency records agree; no successor work begins without authorization.

## 9. Standardization Recommendation

### Recommendation

Create a programme-wide constitutional work-package production standard after CGP-02 is completed and the CGP-03 entry dependency is satisfied.

The standard is justified because CGP-02C.4 exposed recurring constitutional risks that templates alone cannot govern reliably:

- authorization being mistaken for approval;
- approval being mistaken for adoption or effect;
- recommendations being mistaken for decisions;
- recording functions being mistaken for Authority;
- document status drifting across artefacts;
- historical records being overwritten;
- programme sequence being mistaken for authorization;
- validation noise obscuring package-specific defects.

The future standard should codify minimum evidence and boundaries while permitting proportionate document consolidation. It should not require every optional artefact for every package.

### Important restraint

This recommendation does not authorize the standard, assign its identifier, select its exact title, place it in the Master Programme or authorize drafting. Those are future Founder planning decisions.

## 11. Suggested CGP Programme Location

The best constitutional home is within **CGP-03 — Governance Documentation & Traceability Standard**, because the proposed subject primarily governs documentation lifecycle, evidence, traceability, status integrity and controlled production.

Subject to future Founder authorization, it could become the first bounded CGP-03 drafting package under a provisional identifier such as:

> **CGP-03A — Constitutional Work Package Production, Approval and Traceability**

The identifier and title are suggestions only. They are not established.

Recommended repository locations, if later authorized:

- planning and authorization evidence: `docs/programme/`;
- constitutional standard and its review artefacts: `docs/governance/principles/`;
- reusable programme templates and validation checklists: `docs/programme/`.

CGP-03 remains blocked until CGP-02 satisfies its governed completion dependency. This retrospective does not change that status.

## 12. Open Standardization Decisions

A future planning package should ask the Founder:

1. whether the standard belongs within CGP-03;
2. whether `CGP-03A` and the provisional title are acceptable;
3. whether the Candidate becomes the approval record or remains immutable beside a separate Founder Approval Record;
4. which artefacts are universally mandatory and which are consequence-dependent;
5. when independent constitutional review is required;
6. whether body-integrity hashing is mandatory or one permitted integrity mechanism;
7. how pre-existing repository validation debt must be reported;
8. whether programme synchronization is required after every status transition or only governed milestones;
9. whether drafting is authorized after planning review.

No question is answered by this retrospective.

## 13. Retrospective Conclusion

CGP-02C.4 provides a strong reference model for future constitutional production. Its durable core is not the number of documents produced; it is the attributable chain of bounded planning, explicit authorization, traceable drafting, separate Founder decisions, candidate freeze, explicit approval, preserved history and non-inferred adoption or effect.

Standardization is recommended, but only after CGP-02 completion and through a separately authorized CGP-03 work package.
