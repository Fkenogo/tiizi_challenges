## 4. Duplication Assessment

No duplicated doctrine requiring deletion or redrafting was identified.

The following repeated patterns are intentional:

- subject-specific anti-inference clauses;
- subject-specific no-new-Authority clauses;
- subject-specific attribution requirements;
- historically intelligible treatment for each constitutional act; and
- explicit deferral of implementation and accountability.

They protect separate constitutional boundaries. Consolidation may remove purely editorial repetition only if:

- every proposition remains traceable;
- no protection is narrowed;
- no package’s recorded approval state is upgraded;
- no Founder decision is reinterpreted; and
- no deferred matter is silently resolved.

## 5. Remaining Constitutional Doctrine

Validation Rules is the only evidenced remaining substantive doctrine.

The remaining treatment must distinguish:

- constitutional validation from review;
- constitutional validation from approval;
- constitutional validation from adoption and constitutional effect;
- minimum constitutional assurance from operational tests;
- unresolved validation findings from inferred failure or approval; and
- evidence integrity from technical tooling.

This matrix identifies the missing subject but supplies none of its constitutional answers.
