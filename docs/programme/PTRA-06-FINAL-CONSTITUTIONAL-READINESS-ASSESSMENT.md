## 8. Final Drafting Recommendation

### 8.1 Is CGP-02C.12 required?

**Yes.**

A bounded work package for **Validation Rules** is required because:

- it is frozen Blueprint deliverable 16;
- GAP-16 remains open;
- recurring validation reports are observed programme practice, not approved constitutional doctrine;
- no prior package defines the constitutional validation minimum; and
- consolidation cannot legitimately invent that missing doctrine.

The identifier `CGP-02C.12`, title, scope and drafting authorization remain unapproved until a separate planning and Founder authorization lifecycle occurs.

### 8.2 Is another bounded constitutional package required?

**No additional subject is presently evidenced beyond Validation Rules.**

No separate package is recommended for deliverables 17 through 20 because they are integration outputs, not independent constitutional subjects.

### 8.3 Should the programme transition directly into consolidation?

**No.**

Direct consolidation would leave deliverable 16 and GAP-16 unresolved and would risk elevating recurring validation practice into doctrine without attributable review.

After Validation Rules completes bounded closure, the programme should perform one governed transition assessment and, if ready, proceed to one whole-instrument consolidation programme covering deliverables 17 through 20.

## 9. Required Sequence

The evidence-based sequence is:

1. synchronize the stale C.10 and C.11 authorization evidence through status-only documentary maintenance;
2. prepare planning and Founder authorization evidence for proposed CGP-02C.12 — Validation Rules;
3. if separately authorized, complete one bounded Validation Rules work package;
4. conduct a post-C.12 transition and integration-readiness assessment;
5. if separately authorized, consolidate deliverables 1 through 16 into one CGP-02 Founder Review instrument with deliverables 17 through 20; and
6. conduct whole-instrument review, approval, adoption and programme transition under separately attributable governance.

This sequence is a recommendation only.
