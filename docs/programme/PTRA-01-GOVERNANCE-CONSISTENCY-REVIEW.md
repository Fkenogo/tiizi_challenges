# PTRA-01 — Governance Consistency Review

**Reviewed programme:** CGP-02 through CGP-02C.6

**Document type:** Programme assessment evidence

**Status:** Review complete; no governance changed

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This review tests the completed CGP-02 work packages for duplication, overlap, contradiction and boundary drift. It does not reopen a Founder decision, amend a proposition or create constitutional doctrine.

## 2. Package Relationship Review

| Package | Constitutional subject | Principal dependency boundary | Consistency finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| CGP-02C.1 | Amendment and review principles; constitutional change boundary | Establishes principles and defers detailed lifecycle, classification, approval, effect, traceability and validation | Consistent; later packages apply rather than redefine its principles. |
| CGP-02C.2A | Governance Lifecycle | Distinguishes lifecycle terms without approval, adoption or effect rules | Consistent with C.1 and C.4; conceptual lifecycle remains separate from constitutional effect. |
| CGP-02C.2B | Amendment Classification | Classifies by consequence without procedure or review thresholds | Consistent with CHB-02 and CHB-04. |

| Topic appearing across packages | Assessment | Required control |

No redundant package is identified. The overlaps are dependency interfaces created by the Blueprint sequence.

## 5. Boundary Drift Review

No package:

- introduced implementation or repository workflow;
- allocated a role, permission or accountability relationship;
- created a Platform Authority;
- adopted itself through completion;
- changed an earlier package’s propositions;
- resolved a later Blueprint subject silently;
- authorized its successor; or
- treated observed practice as constitutional doctrine.

Potential future drift risks are:

1. treating C.4’s Founder Approved instrument as adopted;
2. treating C.1, C.2, C.3, C.5 or C.6 candidates as effective governance;
3. merging approval records, amendment traces and impact-review records;
4. allowing Conflict Review to decide an instrument-specific conflict;
5. turning cross-reference impact identification into automatic synchronization;
6. turning recurring validation practice into doctrine without Founder review; and
7. using Blueprint order as authorization.

## 6. Status Coherence

The package statuses differ but do not conflict:

- CGP-02C.1 and CGP-02C.2 candidates remain proposed and unapproved constitutional governance;
- CGP-02C.3, CGP-02C.5 and CGP-02C.6 have attributable Founder approval for their candidates but remain unadopted and without effect;
- CGP-02C.4 has a Founder Approved instrument but remains unadopted and without effect; and
- CGP-02 as a whole remains In Progress.

This asymmetry requires explicit preservation during final CGP-02 consolidation. It is not a current contradiction.

## 7. Methodology Observations

Recurring evidence patterns include bounded authorization, single-subject drafting, proposition traceability, decision separation, hash preservation, candidate parity, explicit non-effects, completion reporting and programme synchronization.

These patterns are suitable for later methodology evaluation. This review does not approve them as a standard, reactivate the deferred Governance Review Standard proposal or authorize a constitutional-production standard.

## 8. Consistency Verdict

**Consistent with controlled integration risks.**

No correction to a completed package is required before the next planning activity. Later packages must preserve the identified interfaces, status distinctions and explicit deferrals.
