## 5. Dependency Assessment

### 5.1 Governance Index and Status Integrity

This subject depends on:

- Approval Governance for the distinction between approval and its recording;
- Adoption and Constitutional Effect for current constitutional status;
- Amendment Traceability for change-specific records;
- Dependent-Governance Impact Review for cross-reference impact without automatic synchronization;
- Conflict Review and Escalation for unresolved inconsistency;
- Supersession and Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules for status distinctions; and
- Historical Preservation for the relationship between current representations and historically applicable governance.

CGP-02C.10 satisfies the final substantive prerequisite. The subject is ready for planning.

### 5.2 Validation Rules

Validation Rules depend on the complete constitutional subject architecture, including Governance Index and Status Integrity. Validation cannot coherently test status-representation integrity before that integrity boundary exists.

Validation Rules are therefore not ready to precede deliverable 15.

### 5.3 Whole-Instrument Integration

Integration depends on completed bounded treatment for deliverables 1–16. It must preserve:

- proposition wording and attribution;
- each package’s recorded approval status;
- all deferred and unresolved matters;
- all explicit non-effects;
- historical intelligibility; and
- the distinction between package-level decisions and whole-instrument approval.

Integration is not ready before deliverables 15 and 16.
