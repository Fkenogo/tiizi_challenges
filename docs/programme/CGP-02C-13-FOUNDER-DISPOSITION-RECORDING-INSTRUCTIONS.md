## 1. Purpose

These instructions govern the completion of the existing Founder disposition fields for the 45 constitutional observations.

They do not supply, infer or recommend a disposition.

## 2. Permitted Disposition Entries

Select one disposition for each observation:

- **Accept**
- **Reject**
- **Defer**
- **Needs clarification**

No unchecked option, omission, silence or incomplete entry is to be interpreted as a Founder decision.

## 3. Required Founder Entries

For each observation:

1. select exactly one disposition;
2. enter Founder rationale;
3. select exactly one amendment-follow-up response, Yes or No; and
4. enter a future amendment programme reference only where a separate programme reference exists.

The future amendment programme reference remains blank where no separately established reference exists.

## 4. Recording Integrity

When recording a Founder disposition:

- preserve the Observation ID;
- preserve the classification;
- preserve every proposition ID;
- preserve the neutral summary;
- preserve the supporting evidence;
- preserve the batch assignment; and
- record only the Founder-supplied disposition and accompanying fields.

Do not reinterpret a Founder entry or infer additional constitutional meaning.

## 5. Incomplete Entries

An observation remains unresolved where:

- no disposition is selected;
- more than one disposition is selected;
- the Founder requires clarification before disposition; or
- an attributable decision has not been supplied.

Amendment follow-up and a future amendment programme reference do not themselves amend constitutional content or authorize later work.

## 6. Recording Non-Effects

Completing this register does not:

- amend, merge, delete or rewrite a proposition;
- establish an amendment programme;
- create constitutional approval, adoption or effect;
- update the Master Programme; or
- update the Consolidated Decision Register.
