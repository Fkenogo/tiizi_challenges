# PTRA-02 — Governance Consistency Review

**Reviewed programme:** CGP-02 through CGP-02C.7

**Document type:** Programme assessment evidence

**Status:** Review complete; no governance changed

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This review tests the completed CGP-02 work packages for duplication, overlap, contradiction, dependency failure and boundary drift. It does not reopen a Founder decision, amend a proposition or create constitutional doctrine.

## 5. Boundary Drift Review

No completed package:

- introduced implementation or repository workflow;
- allocated a role, permission or accountability relationship;
- created a Platform Authority;
- adopted itself through completion;
- changed an earlier package’s propositions;
- resolved a later Blueprint subject silently;
- treated conflict escalation as a delegation of Governance Authority;
- treated conflict disposition as supersession;
- authorized its successor; or
- treated observed practice as constitutional doctrine.

Potential future drift risks are:

1. treating C.4’s Founder Approved instrument as adopted;
2. treating bounded candidates as effective governance;
3. merging approval records, amendment traces, impact-review records and conflict records;
4. treating conflict disposition as automatic replacement;
5. collapsing supersession into retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
6. treating current-index representation as the act of supersession;
7. turning recurring validation practice into doctrine without Founder review; and
8. using Blueprint order as authorization.

## 7. Status Coherence

The package statuses differ but do not conflict:

- CGP-02C.1 and CGP-02C.2 candidates remain proposed and unapproved constitutional governance;
- CGP-02C.3, CGP-02C.5, CGP-02C.6 and CGP-02C.7 have attributable Founder approval for their candidates but remain unadopted and without effect;
- CGP-02C.4 has a Founder Approved instrument but remains unadopted and without effect; and
- CGP-02 as a whole remains In Progress.

This asymmetry requires explicit preservation during later consolidation. It is not a current contradiction.

## 8. Governance Methodology Evidence

Recurring evidence patterns include:

- evidence-led discovery;
- frozen, single-subject planning;
- dependency verification;
- attributable Founder work-package authorization;
- proposition-level traceability;
- technical Founder Review before constitutional decision;
- decision packs separated from decision records;
- hash preservation and candidate parity;
- explicit deferrals and non-effects;
- milestone validation; and
- programme and Decision Register synchronization at bounded closure.

These patterns may be evaluated later as reusable programme methodology. This review does not approve a methodology standard, reactivate GRS-01, create constitutional doctrine or authorize a new programme.

## 9. Consistency Verdict

**Consistent with controlled integration risks.**

No correction to a completed package is required before the next planning activity. Future Supersession Rules planning must preserve the identified distinctions and status boundaries.
