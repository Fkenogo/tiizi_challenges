# PTRA-03 — Governance Consistency Review

**Reviewed programme:** CGP-02 through CGP-02C.8

**Document type:** Programme assessment evidence

**Status:** Review complete; no governance changed

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This review tests CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.8 for contradiction, duplication, overlap, dependency failure, architectural gap and boundary drift. It does not reopen a decision, amend a proposition or create constitutional doctrine.

## 2. Package Relationship Review

| Package | Constitutional subject | Principal boundary | Consistency finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| CGP-02C.1 | Amendment and review principles; constitutional change boundary | Establishes general principles and defers detailed subjects | Consistent; later packages apply rather than redefine it. |
| CGP-02C.2A | Governance Lifecycle | Distinguishes lifecycle terms without approval, adoption or effect rules | Consistent with C.1 and C.4. |

## 3. Duplication and Overlap

| Repeated topic | Assessment | Consolidation control |
|---|---|---|
| Governance Authority | Intentional dependency across approval, adoption, conflict and supersession. | Preserve one approved Authority model and each subject-specific non-transfer boundary. |
| Attribution | Applied to approval, trace, disposition and supersession for different purposes. | Retain the distinct constitutional act or evidence being attributed. |
| Evidence and records | Approval, trace, impact, conflict and supersession evidence are separate records. | Do not merge their purposes or treat any record as constitutive unless approved doctrine says so. |
| Constitutional status | Lifecycle supplies vocabulary; adoption/effect and supersession apply distinct treatments. | Final integration must preserve sequence and non-equivalence. |
| Historical intelligibility | A general principle is applied in trace, impact, conflict and supersession. | Dedicated preservation may consolidate shared requirements without deleting subject-specific safeguards. |
| Anti-inference | Repeated to prevent labels, records, implementation and repository actions from creating status. | Consolidate common language only if every existing protection remains. |
| Review | General principles, proportionality, impact review and conflict review serve different scopes. | Retain the scope and evidence distinction for each review type. |

No bounded package is redundant. The repetition reflects shared constitutional controls applied to different subjects.

## 4. Constitutional Consistency

| Review area | Finding |

**Contradiction verdict:** None identified.

## 5. Boundary Drift Review

No completed package:

- created implementation or repository workflow;
- created a Platform Authority;
- allocated accountability, a role or permission;
- adopted itself through approval or bounded completion;
- treated a record, label, index or implementation act as Governance Authority;
- silently resolved a later Blueprint subject;
- superseded an actual instrument;
- erased historical governance;
- authorized its successor; or
- elevated observed practice into doctrine solely through repetition.

## 6. Architectural Gaps

The current gaps correspond to the frozen Blueprint rather than defects:

- no treatment yet defines retirement, withdrawal or rejection;
- historical preservation is distributed but not complete as a dedicated subject;
- governance-index and status integrity remain deferred;
- validation doctrine remains deferred; and
- final cross-cutting consolidation, review and self-validation remain outstanding.

## 7. Consolidation Risks

| Risk | Required future control |
|---|---|
| Different package statuses flattened into one implied approval status | Preserve each source status until attributable whole-instrument approval and adoption. |
| Repeated non-effects removed as editorial duplication | Test whether each statement protects a distinct subject boundary before consolidation. |
| Vocabulary harmonization changes Founder-approved meaning | Require exact source trace and attributable amendment for substantive change. |
| Candidate propositions combined without decision trace | Maintain proposition-to-source and decision-to-proposition mappings. |
| Cross-cutting sections introduced before substantive completion | Complete deliverables 13–16 first. |

## 8. Reusable Methodology Evidence

Recurring programme patterns remain visible:

- frozen single-subject planning;
- dependency verification;
- attributable Founder work-package authorization;
- proposition-level traceability;
- separate technical review and constitutional decision;
- decision packs separated from decision records;
- hash and wording parity;
- explicit exclusions, deferrals and non-effects;
- milestone validation; and
- programme and register synchronization at bounded closure.

These patterns may later be considered as reusable programme methodology. This review does not approve a methodology standard, reactivate GRS-01 or authorize any work.

## 9. Consistency Verdict

**Consistent with controlled integration requirements.**

No corrective amendment to CGP-02C.1 through CGP-02C.8 is required before a later planning activity for the next Blueprint subject.
