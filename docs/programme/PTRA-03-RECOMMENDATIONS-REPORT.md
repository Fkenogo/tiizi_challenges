# PTRA-03 — Recommendations Report

**Programme:** Tiizi Version 2 — Stage E0

**Status:** Non-governing recommendations; no authorization

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Recommendation Summary

Accept PTRA-03 as programme evidence. After acceptance, the next governed activity should be preparation of a separate planning and Founder authorization package for **Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules**, Blueprint deliverable 13.

This recommendation establishes no identifier, scope, decomposition, Founder question or drafting authorization.

## 2. Why This Subject Is Next

Retirement, Withdrawal and Rejection Rules is next because:

1. it is the next ordered deliverable in the frozen [CGP-02 Drafting Blueprint](../governance/principles/04-CGP-02-DRAFTING-BLUEPRINT.md);
2. CGP-02C.8 expressly distinguishes those treatments from supersession without defining them;
3. lifecycle, approval, adoption, traceability and historical-integrity dependencies now exist;
4. defining status-ending treatments before Historical Preservation allows later preservation treatment to assess the full set of constitutional status changes; and
5. deferring index and validation treatment preserves the distinction between constitutional acts, documentary representations and operational checks.

Blueprint order and dependency readiness support planning only.

## 3. Recommended Planning Boundary

A future planning package should remain bounded to the constitutional subject identified by deliverable 13. It should preserve:

- the distinct treatment of retirement, withdrawal and rejection;
- the difference between status-ending treatment and supersession;
- the approval, adoption and constitutional-effect boundaries;
- Governance Authority discipline;
- attribution and historical intelligibility;
- explicit anti-inference protections; and
- deferral of implementation, status-index synchronization and validation doctrine.

These are boundary recommendations, not constitutional propositions or answers.

## 4. Recommended Explicit Exclusions

Future planning should exclude:

- retiring, withdrawing or rejecting an actual instrument;
- changing the status of any named governance;
- redefining supersession, amendment, approval, adoption or constitutional effect;
- defining historical storage, retention, archival or repository procedure;
- defining governance-index synchronization;
- defining validation doctrine;
- allocating a recorder, reviewer, decision-maker or accountable relationship;
- creating a subject-specific Platform Authority;
- implementation or technical format; and
- authorizing any later Blueprint subject.

Future planning should verify, without reopening:

## 6. Consolidation Recommendation

Do not begin whole-instrument consolidation yet. Complete the remaining substantive and dedicated subjects first.

After deliverables 13–16, a separately governed integration activity should:

- assemble sections in Blueprint order;
- preserve proposition and decision traceability;
- retain source document statuses;
- harmonize terminology without changing constitutional meaning;
- identify true duplication separately from intentional dependency reuse;
- consolidate deferred matters and non-effects; and
- prepare deliverables 17–20.

This recommendation does not authorize that integration activity.

## 7. Reusable Methodology Recommendation

Retain the recurring authorization, traceability, parity, boundary and validation patterns as programme evidence. Consider programme-wide standardization only through separately authorized programme governance. Do not create a new constitutional or programme standard through PTRA-03.
