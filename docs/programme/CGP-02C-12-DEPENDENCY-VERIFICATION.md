## 3. Dependency Conclusions

The dependencies establish that:

- Validation Rules is the only remaining substantive Blueprint subject;
- validation must remain distinct from review, approval, adoption, constitutional effect and status representation;
- constitutional consequence may influence the validation inquiry without creating thresholds, tiers or mechanics during planning;
- traceability, dependency, conflict, status and history evidence may inform validation without being redefined;
- recurring validation reports and mechanical checks are observed programme practice, not an approved canonical constitutional minimum;
- the C.10 and C.11 documentary prerequisite is closed;
- Validation Rules must remain Tiizi-specific and technology-neutral;
- operational, repository, engineering, reusable and cross-project validation controls remain outside scope;
- the developing Founder Engineering Framework supplies no Tiizi doctrine, dependency or authorization; and
- no existing package or accepted assessment authorizes drafting.

These conclusions bound planning only and establish no constitutional answer.
