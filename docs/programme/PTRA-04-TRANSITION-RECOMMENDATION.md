# PTRA-04 — Transition Recommendation

**Programme:** Tiizi Version 2 — Stage E0

**Status:** Non-governing recommendation; no authorization

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Recommendation Summary

Preserve CGP-02 and Stage E0 as In Progress and CGP-03 as blocked. Do not authorize another drafting package until the CGP-02C.9 authorization-record inconsistency is reconciled through attributable documentary governance.

After that prerequisite, complete the remaining frozen Blueprint sequence without redesign:

1. Historical Preservation;
2. Governance Index and Status Integrity;
3. Validation Rules;
4. whole-instrument integration covering Blueprint deliverables 17–20;
5. complete-instrument review and attributable Founder approval;
6. separately attributable adoption and constitutional-effect treatment; and
7. CGP-02 programme transition and dependency synchronization.

## 2. Step 0 — Documentary Prerequisite

Reconcile the status of:

- [CGP-02C.9 Founder Authorization Record](CGP-02C-9-FOUNDER-AUTHORIZATION-RECORD.md); and
- [CGP-02C.9 Authorization Validation Report](CGP-02C-9-AUTHORIZATION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md)

against the attributable authorization evidence and the current Master Programme.

The corrective action must be status synchronization only unless attributable evidence requires otherwise. PTRA-04 does not authorize or perform it.

## 3. Step 1 — Historical Preservation

Prepare a separately governed planning and authorization package only after the documentary prerequisite.

Historical Preservation should remain one bounded constitutional subject. It should consolidate the common historical-integrity foundation while remaining distinct from amendment traceability, status treatments, storage, retention, archival workflow and implementation.

## 4. Step 2 — Governance Index and Status Integrity

Proceed only after Historical Preservation has completed its bounded milestone.

This subject should govern constitutional integrity of current-status representation without allowing an index, register, manifest, cross-reference or repository action to exercise Governance Authority or create status.

## 5. Step 3 — Validation Rules

Proceed only after the preceding substantive subjects are complete.

Validation Rules should establish only the constitutional minimum needed to assess identity, scope, Authority, traceability, dependencies, status and non-effects. Tooling, commands, repository workflow and accountable-role allocation must remain outside scope.

## 6. Step 4 — Whole-Instrument Integration

After deliverables 14–16, use one separately governed integration activity for Blueprint deliverables 17–20.

The activity should:

- assemble the complete CGP-02 draft in Blueprint order;
- preserve every proposition’s source and decision status;
- distinguish unapproved candidates, candidate-level Founder approvals and the C.4 Founder Approved Instrument;
- harmonize terminology only where meaning remains unchanged;
- consolidate genuine duplication while retaining subject-specific safeguards;
- carry forward every actual deferral;
- disposition all 18 gaps, 8 conflicts and 9 duplicate clusters;
- produce Governance Status and Decision Trace;
- present only genuinely unresolved final Founder Review matters; and
- perform full-draft self-validation.

Integration must not be used to approve, adopt or make any source proposition effective.

## 7. Step 5 — Complete-Instrument Review and Approval

The integrated standard should undergo:

1. technical and constitutional consistency review;
2. correction of genuine integration defects through attributable governance;
3. final Founder Review;
4. an attributable Founder decision record; and
5. preparation of a whole-instrument Founder Approval Candidate.

Existing package-level decisions supply traceable evidence but do not substitute for whole-instrument approval.

## 8. Step 6 — Adoption and Constitutional Effect

Founder approval must remain separate from adoption and constitutional effect in accordance with CGP-02C.4.

No adoption, effective date, application date or constitutional effect should be inferred from completion, approval metadata, publication, index synchronization or programme transition.

## 9. Step 7 — Programme Transition

Only after the approved CGP-02 instrument has completed its attributable adoption treatment should programme records be synchronized to determine:

- whether CGP-02 is Complete;
- whether CGP-03 is unblocked;
- the next Stage E0 activity; and
- any required index, register, manifest or historical-reference updates.

Stage E0 completion remains dependent on all Stage E0 deliverables and gates, not CGP-02 alone.

## 10. Recommended Immediate Next Action

The immediate next action should be a documentary status-reconciliation decision for the CGP-02C.9 authorization evidence. After that reconciliation and separate acceptance of PTRA-04 as programme evidence, the programme may consider preparation of planning evidence for Historical Preservation.

This recommendation does not create that planning package, assign an identifier, formulate Founder questions or authorize drafting.

- CGP-02 remains In Progress.
- CGP-03 remains blocked.
- The Master Programme remains unchanged.
- The Consolidated Decision Register remains unchanged.

## 12. Non-Effects
