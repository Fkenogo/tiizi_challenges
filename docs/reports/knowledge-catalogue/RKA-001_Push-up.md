# 5. Technique Module

## Starting Position

Hands placed slightly wider than shoulder width, body aligned from head to heels, shoulders positioned over wrists, core engaged.

## Execution

Lower the body under control until the chest approaches the floor while maintaining a neutral spine.

Press through the hands to return to the starting position.

## Breathing

Inhale during the lowering phase.

Exhale during the pressing phase.

## Tempo

Controlled by default.

Suggested learning tempo:

2 seconds down

1 second pause

1 second up

## Completion Criteria

Full controlled repetition.

Elbows extend without locking aggressively.

Body alignment maintained throughout.

# 6. Coaching Module

Primary Coaching Cues

- Keep your body in one straight line.
- Brace your core.
- Press the floor away.
- Keep your neck neutral.
- Control the descent.

Common Errors

- Sagging hips
- Raised hips
- Partial range of motion
- Excessive elbow flare
- Holding the breath

# The Significance of RKA-001

I think we've just created something more important than the Push-up record itself.

We've created the **Tiizi Reference Knowledge Asset Standard**.

Every future asset—whether it's a Squat, a Walking Workout, a Mindfulness Session, a Nutrition Concept or a Recovery Protocol—should be able to follow this same modular structure.

## My Recommendation Before We Continue

Rather than immediately authoring the remaining Push-family exercises, I recommend a short **validation workshop**using RKA-001. Review it from four perspectives:

- **Product:** Does it support the user experience you envision?
- **Exercise Science:** Are the technical and coaching sections complete and appropriately scoped?
- **Engineering:** Can every module be represented cleanly in the knowledge model without duplication?
- **AI & Personalisation:** Does it provide enough structured information to support explainable recommendations?

If RKA-001 passes those four perspectives, it becomes the permanent benchmark for every subsequent Knowledge Asset in Tiizi. From there, knowledge production becomes a disciplined process of applying the same standard consistently across the catalogue rather than redesigning the model for each new concept.
