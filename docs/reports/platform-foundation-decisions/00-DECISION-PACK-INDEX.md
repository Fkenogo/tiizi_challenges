# Tiizi Version 2 Founder Governance Decision Pack

## Approved direction

Tiizi is a group-first challenge and motivation platform that helps people pursue meaningful goals together through measurable activities, mutual encouragement and shared progress. Fitness and wellness are initial domains, not the permanent platform boundary. Group challenges are the primary product mode.

This pack presents pending founder choices. Recommended defaults are evidence-based recommendations, not approvals.

## Evidence categories

- **Repository fact:** observed code, rules, services, screens, collections or tests.
- **Documented intent:** a statement in existing V2 knowledge/governance documents.
- **Approved direction:** the product direction supplied for this task.
- **Recommended default:** analyst recommendation awaiting founder approval.
- **Uncertainty:** production/deployment or business intent not established by repository evidence.
